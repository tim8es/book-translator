## Chapter 147: Chapter 147

There were no shops open on Christmas Day, so Draco and I ended up just soaking each other up beneath the quilts, limbs all braided together and alternately smiling at each other and just *staring* at each other. There was such a sense of *awe* radiating from us both that it put us in almost a *trance.* And I couldn’t be bothered to wonder how *‘unsettling’* it might’ve been for us to just be looking at each other in silence for so long.

*We’re about to be a whole new level of unbearable,* Draco chuckled internally, grey eyes *sparkling* with amusement and wonder. A grin broke across my cheeks.

*Yeah, reckon so. Think Ron and Mione’ll be narked that we beat them to it?*

He snorted, reaching a hand into my hair, just behind my ear and stroking it.

*What, like it’s a race?*

So *pleased,* and *shocked* and *amazed,* fucking *elated* that all I could do was pull my grin up to his and kiss him. He sucked me down like a *tonic,* but it was then our mutual *hunger* registered and I was sort of struck with the realisation that we’d been in bed since breakfast and it was nearly *lunchtime.*

While we were both slightly *sad* to lose that physical connection as we rolled out of bed to redress, the soulbond was like an actual, *tangible* fucking thing now, and it was that much less painful to be parted from each other. Instead of the dining hall, though, Draco led me to the greenhouse towards the back of the villa for lunch, insisting the staff would bring our food wherever we chose to eat.

It’d been overcast when he’d given me the tour, so the clear blue sky and joyous sunshine through the glass walls and ceiling were *stunning* on all the leafy plants and vibrant flowers that dominated nearly every *inch* of space in the greenhouse. A small wrought-iron table and chairs were pressed against the glass of one wall, though, right next to the little inlaid stream that ran through the floor, bubbling and burbling past us as we settled into the little sitting nook.

“I *swear,* I’d *never leave* if I had a place like this,” I remarked quietly as I leaned in to sniff a carnation that was reaching towards me. Draco leaned back in his own chair, crossing one knee over the other and folding his long fingers in his lap as he eyed me.

“We can move in if you’d like,” he answered just as quietly. And I stiffened in shock. Turned wide eyes back over to him as he smirked softly.

“B-but your *mother–”*

“We have plenty of other houses like this across the world. Italy, Spain, Germany, Austria. If you like this one so much, we can live here.”

I gaped at him. It wasn’t much of a countryside cottage with farm animals, but to be surrounded by such *splendour…*

“I-I…” Cleared my throat and swallowed, felt some of the blood leave my cheeks. “N-never really been *good* with, erm… *Wealth,* I feel like… I’d be, just…”

Draco raised an eyebrow. “Out of place?”

Silently thanking the forces that be for the ease of communication allowed by the soulbond *again,* I nodded. Chuckled nervously, and the door that led to the rest of the villa swung open. Glancing towards the sound, I found a butler bearing two plates with domed metal plate covers, weaving through plants towards us.

My tongue got slightly stuck to the roof of my mouth as the stately older gentlemen hopped over the stream and set the plates on our table before us.

“Voici votre déjeuner. Apprécier,” he said, and I felt Draco’s attention on me as the butler in question removed the plate covers to reveal steaming bowls of soup, an assortment of bread and ramekins of sauce, silverware, and… *Chestnuts?*

“Merci,” Draco intoned as he sat up, but I couldn’t get over the chestnuts. A Muggle Christmas song my aunt used to play echoed in the back of my head, and I was sort of amused and *stunned* to see them represented in *Wizarding France.*

“So fucking weird,” I chuckled to myself as I plucked a spoon off the tray and made to try a taste of the soup. Of *course,* it was *heavenly.* An appreciative moan eased through my nose upon my first swallow, and I felt Draco’s heart swell to see me enjoying food, *again.* I smirked up at him.

“So easily pleased,” he teased as he reached for his own spoon. “Is that why you’re with me?”

Smirk *firmly* on his face, he offered a spoonful of soup up to his own lips as a scowl crept across my brow.

“Only *you* could taste *French cuisine* and deem it *average,”* I shot back as I dove into my lunch. I was *starving,* after all, as was he, and with a snort from my *husband,* we resolved into silence to consume our meal.

*Fucking hell,* everything was *delicious.* The sauces that’d been provided were *incredible* on the bits of bread, but adding them to the soup and stirring them in created an *entirely new dish,* and the chestnuts were just a pleasant way to balance out the savoury with *sweet.* So rich with hearty flavours, I felt warmth nestling into my core and spreading through all my limbs. By the time I’d devoured what we’d been given, Draco was still patiently consuming, so I cast my attention around the greenhouse.

It really was *stunning,* just like everything else he’d shown me in France. There was just so much *green,* and orange and violet and pink and yellow and red, like a floral *rainbow* interspersed through the green, but then my eyes were drawn down to a flash of *gold,* and I stared at the koi fish that swam up the stream in the floor with *awe.*

There were several, but two in particular seemed oddly affectionate as they chased each other around in the water, swirling spirals together and eventually rushing to keep up with their school. Shining like metal in the stream, and I was so *full* and *happy* and *enraptured* that all I could do was sit back in my seat and drink in the colourful details of our sunlit room.

With Draco around my ring finger, in my heart and in my *soul,* there was… *Nothing* I wanted. No lingering hole in me, and though it felt *very fucking strange* to not be ignoring an unmet *need,* it was… *Fuck me,* it was bloody *welcome.* And knowing I’d never *want for anything again…* My attention slid back over to him, spooning the last of his soup into his mouth, and he glanced back up at me.

Set his bowl down, dabbed his lips with his napkin and *smiled.* Knowing exactly how I was feeling, and I was suddenly on the verge of fucking *tears.*Just like he’d been at breakfast, a strangled sort of sound choked out of me, and I leaned forward on the small table to lower my brow into my hands.

“Corny,” he said quietly, and I *hacked* a sob-like chuckle as I felt gentle fingers curl around my elbow. *“Je t’adore,* Harry.”

Blinking through tears, pushing them down my cheeks, I peered up at him. Lowered a hand from my brow as I contended with the *nauseating* volume of emotion flooding through me to take his hand.

*“Je t’adore,”* I echoed (poorly) back to him in a whisper, which was all I could manage as my throat stopped up. And he *smiled.*

Peace. Pure, uninterrupted *peace* pealed *choruses* through my chest, into my limbs and into *Draco,* and my tears dried. No space for pain, no allowance for *grief,* and I think it was *that moment* that I was finally… *Finally* able to *let go* of all the agony of the war I’d been clinging to. Like it was my whole *identity,* I didn’t know *who* I was without the ache. But seeing Draco *smile* at me like that, feeling this… This all-consuming *peace* flooding through my veins, I…

Like in the museum, when I’d first felt *glad to be alive,* Draco gave me *normal.* Allowance, acceptance for my survival, a *reason.* Not a purpose, not a *goal,* but *validation.* I was allowed to just… *Be.* Be *his.* I felt slightly *faint* to feel so unencumbered, so *free.* Head light, limbs *buzzing,* I squeezed his hand and swiped my tears off my cheeks with my other.

“Happy?” he asked, even though he knew the answer. And I smiled all the brighter.

“Happiest.”
