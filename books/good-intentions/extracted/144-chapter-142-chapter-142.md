*Mother-in-law,* I shot back, and he exhaled a voiceless laugh through his nose.

*Sorry I can’t give you a father, too.*

*God, now I really wanna tell him. Imagine the look on his face.*

“Oh, *honestly,* you could at least include me in the conversation if you’re going to talk around me.”

“We were just imagining father’s reaction,” Draco explained, and although Narcissa went slightly *pale* as she froze, the image of Lucius’s *outrage* nearly had me on my fucking *knees, weeping* with laughter. And Draco finally joined, like he couldn’t *help* it as he chortled into a fist.

Narcissa was looking at us like we were *mad* as I folded to brace myself against my thighs, abs *burning* with effort as I recalled the show I’d put on in the vault. All dressed up in gaudy fucking baubles and pretending to be Lucius.

*If I catch you so much as looking at him again, I’ll have your hide, is that clear?*

I’d never heard her laugh before, and I think neither had *Draco,* because as she lifted a hand to cover her chuckle, he went *silent*. *Savoured* the sound, and I was awash in his *incredulity* as her chuckling cascaded into *giggling.*

All of a sudden, he pulled her into another hug. And my heart tightened in my chest as her laughter cut off with a gasp. She went stiff in his arms for a moment, but to see this, to *feel* this through him… I sniffed and replaced my glasses on my face just as her expression fractured.

Only a breath, she hesitated with shock before wrapping her arms back around him, buried her face in his shoulder and *wept.*

Felt like *I* would cry as I watched, because this was *all he’d ever wanted.* A mother who celebrated with him, laughed with him, wept with him. This was the first moment Draco ever counted his own mother as a *friend.* And I was *devastated* to witness it.

Just the tiniest *suggestion* of a pull was all I needed to wrap my arms around them *both.* And Narcissa spared an arm for me, brought me into *another* group hug, and I’d never felt so… I didn’t know what name to give this emotion, but *family.*

*There’s nothing I want more in the entire fucking world than to call you family,* he’d said.

And here it was.

“Love being *soulbound* to you,” he intoned. And I smiled.

“You *know* that officiant is fucking creaming her pants at having your autograph, right?”

“Let her. Would’ve signed her fucking *tits* for marrying us.”

More laughter as I drew him tighter to my side. *God,* the *rapture* of being Harry’s *husband.* Not just his husband, but his *soulbound.*

*Never thought my life would turn out this way. Always expected horrible, and here I am in heaven.*

Mother returned at that moment, just as I felt Harry’s heart *thud* at my internal assessment.

“Here we are,” she began as she joined us, bearing a tray. And we righted ourselves, sitting up proper to meet her approach to the ottoman before us. Set it down and withdrew her own tea before she backed up to the adjoining chair and carefully climbed into it. Her hair was all free of the curlers now, face still bare and wearing a robe as she smiled at us. “Happy Christmas.”

“Happy fucking Christmas,” Harry answered as he leaned forward for his coffee. I chuckled, but wasn’t surprised when he grabbed my tea, too, and leaned back to offer it to me. I took it in my free hand, and he settled back into my other arm as the hearth crackled before us.

“Happy Christmas, Draco,” was her reply, and I lifted my tea back up like a toast. She gasped suddenly, and Harry lifted his head off my shoulder as she lunged from her chair. “Oh, I have *gifts* for you!”
