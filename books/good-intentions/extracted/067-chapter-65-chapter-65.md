## Chapter 65: Chapter 65

I just about fell asleep right fucking *there,* with Draco still inside me and legs *aching* from the effort. But he eventually managed to manoeuvre me onto the bed properly, cast a *plethora* of cleansing spells over us both, and curled up with me beneath the sheets. I was asleep the second my head hit the pillow. And, to my great relief, I didn’t dream of Draco in compromising scenarios with any other *Slytherins.*

The morning eventually came, and once we’d made it down to the Great Hall, our breakfast was interrupted with the morning post, and Draco received a letter… *Right* into his plate.

“Bloody bird,” he muttered as he plucked the envelope out of his food, shook off some egg, and flipped it to see the writing on the front. I couldn’t help but be curious, and he answered without me even asking. “My mother.”

He tore the envelope open, and I suddenly felt strange peering over his shoulder, so I seized my mug of coffee off the table and took a hearty swig. *Fuck,* *that’s good.* I tried my best not to acknowledge the swell of pain in my chest at the distinct lack of *anyone* still left alive to write to as he flipped the letter over to read the back.

When Draco swore under his breath, it was a welcome, albeit *less-than-ideal* interruption to my grief as I set my mug back down on the table.

“What?”

A grimace contorted his features as he shoved the letter into his pocket, and deftly withdrew a little knut-sized rhinestone. Set it on the table and ran a hand through his hair.

“She thinks I’ll… Likely have a follow-up hearing at some point.”

“Oh.” *Oh. The Ministry wants to track his progress.*“Couldn’t they just go through McGonagall though?”

I palmed the rhinestone off the table and eyed it as he heaved a sigh.

“Shacklebolt’s a very… *Hands-on* Minister. She’s probably right.”

A frown drew my brow tight as I pocketed the little glittering bit of glass. I trusted Kingsley, honestly, bureaucrat though he was, and having seen how his *first* hearing had gone…

“It’ll be fine. I mean… You’re not just dating *some bloke,”* I joked, and hoped it’d land. Thankfully, Draco turned a smirk over to me, and reached over to ruffle my hair. “Y-you should ask her about France.”

It was at that moment Ron and Hermione decided to join us, and I saw Draco’s grey eyes sparkling like *quicksilver* for a moment before we addressed the adjoining parties.

“Morning,” Hermione greeted as she dropped into the bench opposite us, and categorically began assembling her own plate.

“Morning,” I replied. But Ron drew my attention away, because bloke was *clearly* fucking *wound up.* “Alright?”

He jerked a dismissive nod, frowned, and ran his nails over his jaw. Anxious as *fuck. What the hell…?*

The rest of our peers started filtering in, and I was lost in a chorus of *‘morning’s*, but made a note to ask him about it later.

Sort of *forgot* about it in the wake of *warmth* that swelled through me. Eating breakfast with my boyfriend, my *friends,* just felt so fucking *normal,* I wanted to cry. Neville and Luna had a brief, private choking-crying-laughing moment over some inside joke, which neither was inclined to share with the group, but it was… *Nice.*

*Everything’s a bit alright, isn’t it?* Draco’s words echoed in my head as I turned a smile up at him. He caught my eye over the rim of his tea, and returned my smile with a *blinding* one of his own. Like he knew *exactly* what I was thinking. How I was *feeling.*

Back in the same classroom, same people, same task, that feeling of *normal* started to feel a bit… *Monotonous,* and as grateful as I was for regularity, for *stability,* I couldn’t help but internally gripe at the *mundanity* of it all. I’d spent seventeen fucking *years* not knowing what to expect next. Never knowing what horror or event would arise next, and just going day-to-day with no reason to expect *anything* ever going awry was… Weird.

By lunch, I remembered Ron’s anxiety, and cornered him in the hall just outside the classroom. Sent Draco an affirming dip of my chin as he hung back with a question in his eyes, and he curiously left me to cling to Ron’s sleeve as the rest of our peers drifted back towards the Hall.

“What’s up?” he wondered as I released him, but wouldn’t quite meet my eyes.

“You tell me,” I insisted, crossing my arms over my abdomen. And *finally,* he sent me a grimace.

“I-I dunno how to tell you this, mate, but… You’re fucking *awful* at keeping secrets, and there’s… I can’t *tell* you because she’s too fucking *perceptive,* Harry, if I told *you–”*

*Hermione. This has to do with Hermione. He’s not wrong, I’m a terrible liar, but what could he be hoping to keep from–?*

A hasty back-track in my mind gave me the answer I was looking for, and Ron must’ve seen it on my face, because he *groaned.*

“Harry, you can’t say *anything.* Don’t even *think* about it in her presence, I swear she’s a fucking *mind-reader!”*

*Hermione’s birthday is this week. He’s planning a surprise party. Oh fuck.*

“Shit, *shit… FUCK,* I can’t even be *around* her now…!” I began to pace, arms unlocking from around my stomach to scrub at my temples. “Why’d you have to look so fucking *aggravated* at breakfast??”

*“I’m* not the planner, *she is!!* You think this shit is *easy,* dating Mione?? Woman’s gonna plan her own fucking *wedding,* I swear to f–”

*Wedding?* I halted, hands dropping from my temples to stare at Ron as he went *ginger-fucking-red. Holy shit.*

“W-*wedding…?”*

He sort of *gaped*at me for a moment, and I think I probably did the same to him, but he eventually said:

“P-probably, yeah.”

*Holy shit. Ron’s thinking about bloody*proposing.

*“Really?”*

He nodded, gaping mouth snapping shut as his flush spread to his neck. Dropped his gaze to the floor and scratched behind his ear.

“Y-*you* know her, she’s… *Fuck me,* she’s fucking *incredible.”* A hesitant glance up at me. “C-can’t imagine life *without* her.”

My throat went suddenly tight, *my best fucking friends, Christ…* Without really deciding to, I closed the few steps between us and pulled Ron into a bone-crushing hug. He stiffened in my arms for a moment before reciprocating, and my thoughts and feelings were just fucking *all over*the bloody place, all I could say was:

“W-words.” Swallowed. “Good. Great. *Wicked.*”

And he coughed a laugh.

“Yeah?”

*Fuck,* I was crying. Throat tighter than I wanted, eyes prickling as I considered just how *in love* they were. How in love *I* was. Where *that* might lead.

*“Fuck,* yeah.”
