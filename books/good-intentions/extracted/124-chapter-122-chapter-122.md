## Chapter 122: Chapter 122

I couldn’t fucking *think* as *Narcissa Malfoy* kissed me. *Kissed* me! Left little wet spots on my cheeks from her tears, but the gesture was so *maternal,* so… So *Molly Weasley,* I sort of choked on my shock.

*Guess there’s a mum beneath that icy exterior after all.*

She pulled away to sop up her tears, muttering something about a *mess,* but I glanced up at Draco. He looked, indeed he *felt* just as *stupefied* as I did.

“Well! Dinner’s not getting any hotter. Shall we?”

Just like *that,* she started around us, leaving us to follow. Without question, without any sort of *follow-up,* she just… Waltzed back the way I’d come towards the dining room. And Draco finally returned my glance.

“Well. That’s… That’s that, then, I guess.” An incredulous laugh huffed through my lips, and he cleared his throat. “Dinner?”

I nodded, still a bit dazed as he pulled me along to attend dinner.

*Jesus Christ,* the dining hall (for I saw it was much larger than just a *room*) could’ve sat at *least a hundred people.* It stretched what seemed like a *mile* to the head where Narcissa sat. Could’ve laid out an enormous banquet, but as we sat opposite each other next to Draco’s mum, more staff appeared bearing plates.

Plates of… I couldn’t really tell *what.* Looked fancy as *fuck,* and smelled *divine,* but as I reached for one of the many forks (*why the fuck is there so much goddamn silverware??*) Draco reached out to stop me.

“Harry, just… Go slowly, alright? There’s bound to be, like, seven hundred courses, so don’t fill up if you can help it.”

I nodded, and delved in for my first bite.

*Oh my god.* Whatever the fuck it was, it looked and *tasted* like *edible art.* It was a small portion, so I found myself, indeed, eating very slowly. Savouring each bite as Draco and his mother chatted. He caught her up to date with how we started dating, and throughout the messy retelling, the second and *third* courses passed. Each as *divine* as the last.

*Christ, I’d never leave if I had a place like this to my name.*

It was by the fourth that Draco finally began recounting his recent… Misfortune. I felt my brow darken as I forked a tiny little rose-shaped potato and shoved it in my mouth.

“...but *clearly,* I survived. Harry saved me.” My eyes swept up to his, and he was wearing half a smile as I chewed. “Made a bloody mess of it, but here we are.”

My lips twitched an echo of his half-smile, and Narcissa reached out to lay a hand over mine.

“Thank you, Harry. Truly, I… I don’t know what would’ve become of me if Draco hadn’t found you.”

*Found me.* Even though, in this instance, *I’d* been the one who found *him,* I swallowed as I took her meaning. And offered her a bit more of a smile, even though the events set a heaviness on my lungs to recall.

“Nor me,” I answered. And she pinched a smile back at me, blue eyes going wet again. A little brush against my ankle, and I turned back to Draco, looking wistful and *pleased.* And I extended my leg for him, hooking my foot around his as we resumed our *fourth course.*

*Bloody hell,* it was a lot of food. Draco hadn’t been lying. By the seventh or eighth course (I genuinely lost count), I was taking meagre bites. Just to *taste,* because *fuck me, everything* was *heavenly.* Narcissa even let us partake in some *wine,* and though I’d never had wine before, I found it… Palatable. Bit bitter, bit *strong,* but sweet and rich enough to craft a blend of flavours in my mouth with the food that sent my *head spinning.*

*Fucking rich people, my god.*

“I’m just gonna show Harry round, and then we’ll be off to bed. You kept my room the same, haven’t you?”

I passed one last swipe of my napkin over my lips as Draco stood.

“Yes, everything’s the same. I believe the staff brought Harry’s things to the first guest room, but…” She glanced at me, something like… *Mischief* playing around her eyes. “Don’t let me come between two young *lovers*.”

Draco groaned, and I choked on a startled laugh, cheeks going *hot.* Without another word, he was at my side and tugging me from the room.

“Th-thanks for the food!” I called behind us as I stumbled after him. “Everything was great! It was–” My voice cut off with an *oof* as I collided with the doorway, and then I was tumbling along out into the hall. *“Fuckin’* hell…”

Draco snickered, but laced his fingers through mine, and then we were on the move.

“Dunno *where* she learned to talk like that, I swear, after the war she just decided ‘hey, I’m gonna loosen up,’ but had *no idea* how to do that.”

I cackled as he turned a corner and led me up a grand staircase. I was *so fucking full,* I wasn’t sure I was in the *mood* for a whole tour, but he was just so bloody *eager,* I couldn’t help but follow. Down another corridor walled with more art than I could comprehend as ensconced lamps illuminated our journey, lighting as we passed.

“You’re not *really* gonna show me the *whole place,* are you? Thing’s fucking *enormous!”*

He snorted. “Nah, just figured a walk would help us digest. Though there *might* be *one* thing I want to show you…”

With a suggestive grin back at me, he opened a door and led me through to, again, an *enormous* room. Just as my eyes were drinking in the smattering of *huge* fucking chandeliers dripping from the muraled ceiling, I realised this must be a *ballroom.*

“Christ,” I muttered as I craned my neck back to appreciate the details.

“Used to host all manner of social events, back when… Well, we were still in good *social standing.*”

I could just imagine the spacious room full of colourfully dressed witches and wizards, twirling in pairs in the centre or chatting on the outskirts. Butlers and maids drifting around with trays of food and drink, and one sour little blond likely hiding in a corner or under a table. Here, too, like every other room in the villa, the art seemed *alive*. Not moving portraits, but living nonetheless. Girls in frilly dresses, dancing around a bonfire, or sweeping landscapes of gorgeous sunsets over the moors.

*No wonder Draco has a penchant for art. He’s been surrounded by it his whole life.*

“This isn’t what I wanted to show you, though.”

He caught my hand again and led me towards one of the walls, lined with drapes and curtains and as he pushed past them, I saw the entire wall was *glass,* and all the glass were *doors,* and he invited us through one into the brisk night air and *behind* the villa was…

*“Oh…”* All the air in my lungs left in a *whoosh* with that word. The glittering night sky offered the perfect backdrop to a *massive* courtyard, illuminated by standing lamps interspersed along walkways. Rosebushes and hydrangeas, even a *gorgeous* wisteria tree dressed it up in soft pinks and purples, periwinkles and violets and reds. And dominating the centre was a *mammoth* fucking fountain, boasting a stone satyr playing a reed flute for an elegant serpent.

It was… *Stunning.*

Draco pulled me into him by the waist, and I felt his heart *thrumming* down the link.

“You like it?”

Unable to form words, I nodded. We were on a long balcony, hedged by a short balustrade that offered just enough of a view to drop my jaw. And as my wide eyes drank in the details, they danced just far enough towards the back of the courtyard to make out the beginning of a *hedge maze.*

*Fucking Christ, the Malfoys must’ve been like*royalty.

I turned my astonishment up to my silver *prince,* and found him already smiling at me.

“It’s like a f-*fairytale,”* I muttered, and swallowed because *what the fuck was that?? Like a ‘fairytale’?? God, I sound so cheesy and lame and–*

Draco pulled me into a kiss, interrupting my internal cringe, and I *evaporated* into his mouth. The gorgeous scenery, the *delicious* meal, the exorbitant beauty surrounding us and his *lips* on mine all coalesced into *fireworks* in my brain, and when Draco finally released me, I saw the reflection of my eyes in his. Just so subtly *alight* to catch in his own.

“Every day with you is like a waking dream,” he said softly, fingers finding my jaw as my throat went tight. And I leaned in to claim his lips once more.

“Let’s go to bed,” I eventually whispered into his mouth, and felt him smile against me.

*Bliss.*
