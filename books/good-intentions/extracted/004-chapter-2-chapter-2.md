## Chapter 2: Chapter 2

The interviews and the interrogations were already mind-numbing to say the least, but even round the Weasley’s dinner table, I was elsewhere. Still in the sitting room of the Manor, at that, watching seven years of repressed feelings flooding out of Malfoy’s lips like a pinprick in a water balloon that just kept tearing wider.

“Harry?” A hand on my thigh. I blinked up at the worried expressions that seemed to be waiting for me, down to the hand, and followed its arm up to Ginny’s face. “Are you alright?”

“Been like that since the Manor,” Hermione quietly offered as she twirled another mouthful of pasta onto her fork. “Won’t tell me what he said.”

“It’s fine,” I half-heartedly lied as I forced myself to keep eating. Ginny’s hand squeezed once, and I tried not to flinch. “Doesn’t matter.”

*Doesn’t matter,* he’d repeated. Over and over, *doesn’t matter because it’s me.* *I wouldn’t have thought twice about a post-war confession from anyone else, but of course it bloody matters when it’s Malfoy!* I ate a bite of pasta, but didn’t really taste it. Wasn’t really there, even though I was trying.

Ron cleared his throat gruffly. “You know you can talk to us, mate. About anything.”

Surprise that anything could pause Ron’s inhalation of food was what lifted my eyes to his. A sudden spike of anger lashed through me though at the cautious way everyone was watching me, and I grit my teeth.

“I appreciate it, really, but if I get asked if I’m alright one more time, I’m gonna lose it.”

Ginny’s grip on my thigh eased, and then finally disappeared. I frowned at my food, regret welling up to replace my anger. *I know they’re not trying to irritate me, but I can’t help I’m already so fucking irritable. How could he just drop something like that on me and*run???

“You’ll have an easier time figuring out Yotley’s Riddle than getting us to stop worrying about you, Harry,” Molly chimed from the end of the table. “It’s a lot you’ve been through, and we all love you.”

A chorus of agreement rippled down the long table, and I wanted to scream. I was working myself up into a strop, I knew it, but I couldn’t *stop.*

“Sorry, I…” I stood, suddenly unable to be the object of scrutiny anymore. “I just need… Time.”

Several concerned eyes followed me out, but it only made me walk faster. Around the table to the back door and out into the garden. And I kept going, out into the night. Not really sure where, but my legs needed to *move.*

Months ago, this would’ve been a horrible idea, wandering out into the night alone. But now that all was said and done, I almost wished someone would spring out of the reeds and fight me. I wanted to fight something. Wanted to rip something apart with my *teeth.* By the time the Burrow’s lights were small behind me, I was *seething.*

*I have no fucking idea how to like someone.* At least that was something we had in common. The few times I’d been in relationships – including the current one with Ginny – I had no idea what to do. How to act. But that confusion had never once gotten so convoluted that I’d ended up *bullying* the people I liked! I had to admit it made sense, since Malfoy wasn’t particularly pleasant to people in general, and had purposefully targeted me. In a twisted, fucked-up sort of way, it was perfectly on-brand for what I’d imagine Malfoy *flirting* looked like.

*I was fucking jealous, alright?* It hadn’t made a lick of sense in the moment, but now that I’d had several days to mull over it, I was starting to understand. I mightn’t have had a dad, but to have someone like *Lucius* watching my every movement and holding me to *his* standards… A shudder went through my limbs as I held my arms to myself. As ‘fucking awful’ (as Malfoy put it) as my life had been, it pained me to acknowledge that it couldn’t have been much easier for him. And if he’d really fancied me the whole time, what a fucking cherry on top of the disaster that was the last seven years.

A wretched sort of sound coughed through my lips and I decided to sit. Right where I was, with the dew seeping into my trousers and likely slicking me with mud. I had always wondered why he hadn’t sold me out when we’d ended up in the Manor, but the pieces were slowly clicking into place.

It made a little *too* much sense, honestly. That’s what was so bloody terrifying about it all. Treating me like shit, because it was fucking *Malfoy,* and if the person he liked didn’t like him back, of *course* he would make their life miserable. Not that it was excusable, but… It made too much damn sense.

*I should’ve ran him down afterward. How the hell can you just leave after setting off a bomb like that?? Although… To be fair, he has always been a bloody coward, so the fact that he even had the balls to say all that to me in the first place…*

An uncomfortable sort of respect formed in my chest as I recalled the expression he’d worn. Desperate, chaotic, terrified as all hell, and just so fucking *sad,* I was sure he’d cried his eyes out afterward. It was weird, thinking about Malfoy crying. Bit too reminiscent of the bathroom in sixth year. A knell of pain panged through me to my core as I considered the idea that he’d *realised* his feelings for me by then, only to have me hex him to *ribbons* on the tile.

*It’s only fair, he did break my nose.* An agitated groan had me scrubbing my fingers against my scalp. WHY did it have to be bloody fucking *Malfoy* of all people!? He was a git and a bully and a prick and he–

*He fancies me.*

The agitated tautness of my muscles all went slack as I fell back onto the wet earth, staring blankly up at the stars. If he hadn’t been so distraught, I would’ve thought he was taking the piss. It was almost too wild for me to believe. And it made *so much goddamn sense.*

*If that dickhead ends up being the final straw to break my sanity, I’ll kill him.*

---

The parchment in my hands was trembling just as badly as I was. I’d ripped it open anxiously when I’d seen it was from the Ministry, but now that the contents of the letter were unfolding before me, I couldn’t stop fucking *shaking.*

*Shit.*

I’d thought myself lucky to evade Azkaban, what with my father and all the other *adult* Death Eaters being rounded up after the war, but this didn’t bode well. Was I to be tried as an adult? I'd escaped the initial manhunt thanks to my mother, but in some ways, I knew this was only a matter of time.

My eyes scanned the letter, but I couldn’t make them focus on the words. I’d gotten to ‘hereby summoned’ and my vision had gone glassy. I slumped down onto the hallway settee, fingers immediately fishing my wand out of my pocket. *Calm down, they wouldn’t have let you have your wand back if they were just going to confiscate it.*

The months I’d been without it were like a nightmare I couldn’t wake up from. Sure, mother had given me hers to use, but just the sense that I was missing a part of myself was a special kind of hell. Having it back in my hand was a great comfort, but the letter currently rustling in my trembling hand was diminishing that comfort as I forced my eyes to focus.

A hearing. I’d been summoned for a hearing. It wouldn’t be for several days, but it was so like the Ministry to give such short notice for something like this. I’d have those handful of days to fret myself bald, and if I didn’t show up, I’d be held in contempt. Which was just as deadly as if I’d have been a proper Death Eater.

At my mother’s trial, after it’d come to light that she lied to the Dark Lord and was exempt from punishment, I’d heard she’d pleaded for me. Begged for my freedom, and I could just imagine all the nonsense about ‘just a boy’ and ‘following orders.’ The fact that they’d waited until now to summon me was a testament to her persuasion, but obviously my doubts were well-founded. After what I’d done to the Bell girl, not to mention the cabinet…

A violent shudder interrupted my subtle trembling, and my fingers fisted on the letter, crumpling it slightly. *No use trying to run. Time for your comeuppance, Draco.*

With a stuttering sigh, I squished the letter into a ball and gave it a hearty Incendia. In a way, I supposed this was a solution to my moving-out issue. Be it Azkaban or some other magical prison, if I could get away from this place and my *mother,* it was like a twisted fucking answer to my prayer.

My grip nearly snapped my wand in half as I considered the possibility that I’d be sentenced to the same prison that currently held my father.

*This is what I get.*

---

Keeping out of the public eye was already proving difficult, but when news of Malfoy’s hearing reached me, I considered it. I was slowly going insane regardless, so the idea that I could show up and attend the event was only slightly crazier than normal.

*I wonder if they’d even listen to me if I went and defended him,* I thought idly as I kicked my feet over the edge of the bed. *Can’t imagine the prescription of Harry Potter himself would be swept under the rug, especially concerning someone I’m familiar with. They’d listen to me, right?*

A startled laugh choked out of me as I stared blankly at the ceiling. *Why do I feel like I owe him? Sure, he lied to protect me when I'd been taken by those Snatchers, but one butchered confession later and I'm considering*defending him in court?? *I need to get my head looked at.*

With a sigh, I rolled out of bed and made my way downstairs. 12 Grimmauld place still felt painfully fucking empty without the Order members and my friends (*Sirius,* my mind helpfully reminded me) but being alone was a blessing nowadays. As I curved around the bottom of the steps towards the kitchen, a glance at the small cupboard beneath the stairs sent my throat tightening. Considering how lonely a childhood I’d had, one would think I’d hate being alone now. But Hermione and Ginny, even *Dean* were all just being disconcertingly worrisome about me, and it’d started to drive me insane. Not to mention the public reaction to just my general fucking appearance.

Ginny was still somewhat upset that I chose to talk to her brother more than her, but she couldn’t *really* blame me. I’d been his friend way longer, and Ron was never one to press. It’s the main reason he’d become one of the precious few I felt like I sort of*could* talk to. Which was weird enough, seeing as we’d never been particularly open about things like… *Feelings.* But it wasn’t the mushy, overprotective bullshite the girls tried to pull anytime I opened up. Anything I said could be relayed to them, but to have to see that *pity* in their eyes one more time…

I thrust a cabinet open angrily, scanning my pantry for something to snack on and selecting an unopened package of Licorice Wands thoughtlessly. It wasn’t so much that it aggravated me to have people worrying about me, it was just that they all were walking on eggshells around me now – save Ron. It was as uncomfortable as it was familiar.

“Never wanted to be a fucking celebrity,” I griped to myself around my first bite of a Licorice Wand as I leaned back against the counter. The afternoon light was slowly dying, washing the kitchen in warm oranges and blues through the window.

*And therein lies the rub,* I thought grimly. If I did manage to haul my arse to Malfoy’s hearing, there’d be pictures of us on the cover of every news outlet within seconds. If only I was someone else, could *be* someone else, just for a night–

I nearly choked on my swallow as the answer found me, and a self-deprecating laugh followed the back of it. *Of course. You’re a fucking wizard, Harry! What’s a little Polyjuice potion to a wizard?*It was still a little strange to think of myself as an *adult,* but Apothecaries would have no reason not to sell it to me. Whether they’d have any in stock was a bridge I could cross when I came to it. With the date being only days away, I didn’t have the time to wait and brew it myself, but it wasn't like this would be the *first time* that I'd sneak into the Ministry in disguise, either.

*Mulpepper’s better have some. If I have to try Knockturn, I might as well take the Cloak.*
