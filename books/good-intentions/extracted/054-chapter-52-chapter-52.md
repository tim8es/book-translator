“Seems sort of silly to, y’know… Be sharing a bed and still have to come back here to change.”

“Come on, I’m starving.”

I tried not to stumble as he led me along in the direction of Hogsmeade, but I couldn’t deny I was anxious as *hell.* Especially as we passed a handful of wandering Hufflepuffs who immediately all bent their heads together to whisper. “Y-you know, I don’t think you ever *did* tell me why…? Why did you come to my hearing?”

I flinched a smile. *God,* recalling my horrendous confession was painful even *now.*

“Really didn’t come out the way I’d meant it to. But then, when does *anything?”*

Around a corner, down a flight of stairs, and down another hall we continued. Of course, Harry saw straight through me and didn’t look the least bit convinced, but if this was going to be the rest of my life (and I damn-well hoped it *would* be) I’d have to get used to it sooner or later. *Public appearances with my celebrity boyfriend. And I winced again. “Y-yeah, ahm… Got too curious, snuck down with the cloak.”

My cheeks warmed slightly as I returned my gaze to watch where we were going.
