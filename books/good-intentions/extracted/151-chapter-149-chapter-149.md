## Chapter 149: Chapter 149

After we pieced ourselves back together, I took one last turn around the room, appreciating all of Draco’s childhood things with an *ache* in my chest like *nostalgia,* but… Not. Maybe his nostalgia combined with my *resentment* for the childhood I’d survived, but eventually he led me from the room, and the way our emotions influenced each others’, the way we were able to *reach into* each other… Curiosity pulled me back to the library.

I sorely regretted not asking the bureaux officials more about the soulbond this morning, but I’d been half-awake and *seething,* and then half-awake and *shocked,* too *elated* to prioritise my thoughts. But for all the seemingly *endless* fucking shelves of the villa’s collection, not a single mention of soulbonds. We wound up wandering back to the dining hall for dinner as I contended with my disappointment.

*It’s obviously not forbidden knowledge, there’s legit legal procedures for it. Maybe it’s just so obscure and uncommon that it’s not written about much.*

Draco’s hand in mine tightened as we strolled through the open dining hall doors.

*Our collection is just an amalgam of things my father deemed important and things that caught my mother’s interest. I’d’ve been surprised if you had found anything.*

A disgruntled little *‘hmph’* as we took our seats, Narcissa eyeing us suggestively as she sipped her wine.

“Rather long conversation,” she teased as she set her goblet down. Predictably, my cheeks went hot, Draco rolled his eyes but it was sort of… *Nice.* Teasing was such *friend* behaviour, I had to bite back a smile as we were brought plates of food.

*In-fucking-credible* food, as always. Usually when I ate, there were dishes I wouldn’t try, vegetables I’d eat first to get them out of the way, but *everything* *was delicious.* Even foods I’d previously *hated* were prepared in such a way that I was savouring every bite. And, like I’d come to expect, a *swell* of love poured into me from across the table as Draco watched me eat.

Felt so… I didn’t even *know,* to be fed such delicious food, to be outfitted in such *exorbitant* clothes and supplied with such various *finery.* By the time I’d forced the last course down my throat into my aching stomach, I was *begging* for sleep. Whether it was how full I was, or the emotional toll our visit to his *nursery* had been, I was *exhausted.*

Seemed Draco was, too, because he didn’t have much to say as he followed me back to the room. And just like that, Christmas Day was over.

After a second night of sharing a dream, I was *lethally* curious to know what all soulbonds entailed. It was so *strange,* to have a dream constructed of two independent subconsciouses, and we were *wicked* clumsy with it, so the next day, I insisted we return to the Place Cachée so we could visit the bookshop.

Boxing Day, and the street was *packed.* Though the sky was similarly clear and blue, the sun bright overhead, the wind was *wicked* cold, and I tugged Draco through the crowd by a hand, eager to find a warm reprieve.

*Why don’t they just spell the whole street warm?* I wondered incredulously, and felt him chuckle through the soulbond.

*It’s not usually this cold,* he supplied as we burst through the busy doors to *Mag…* The bookstore. *Think you’re stealing the sun’s warmth.*

Casting an affectionate smirk askance at him,*Why am I still so cold then?*

*Magillard’s Plumes et Tome,* Draco supplied, was not quite as packed as the street, but still a tight squeeze to peruse the shelves. He broke away, never more than a thought away, to divide and conquer the collection, with me giving up far before him because *right, everything is in fucking French.*

He huffed a laugh as I returned to his side, but it was then that the stares, the *whispers* registered, and I offered our onlookers a shaky smile as I curled closer into his side. Wrapped around his arm and pressed my chin to his shoulder.

*Maybe ask an employee?*

Without further prodding, Draco tore away from the shelf he was thumbing through to approach the desk. A short conversation my mind struggled to keep up with later, and I was met with disappointment.

*It’s just as well. Must be bloody well rare.*

He offered me a consolatory kiss to my temple, fingers weaving into my hair as I laced my hand with his. An idea took hold in his head just then, though, and I pried my head from beneath his lips to quirk a brow up at him. A smirk answered me.

*Come on, know-it-all.*

I took Harry back to the Maison Capenoir. I knew he could read my intentions, my emotions and coherent thoughts, but I hoped the finer details of my reason for this visit would elude him as we stepped through the doors.

Thankfully, seemingly reading my purpose, he busied himself in glittering cufflinks as the presiding signeur approached me, still wearing his *ridiculous* moustache and a smile.

“Bonjour,” I began, glancing back at Harry, who was pouring over glass cases of jewellery. “Er… Je voudrais commander un nouveau costume.” My French was clumsy, slow and likely *accented* with my native tongue, and I *fucking knew* Harry was half-listening. But as I narrowed my attention to the signeur, he met my fervour. *“Brillant,”* I whispered, hoping Harry was too consumed in the *sparkly* things he was pouring over.

Thankfully, the signeur frowning up at me smiled, glanced sideways at Harry, and winked at me.

“Oui. Je connais juste le truc.”

I bit back the *pure fucking relief* that threatened to flood me, because they’d already taken his measurements, and I *knew* they could construct a *magnificent* suit for him, complete with all the *sparkly bits,*just like I knew he liked.

“Merci beaucoup,” I dipped my chin in a nod, and the signeur tapped his nose conspiratorially, and slipped away back into the shop. And I wandered back up to Harry’s back.

“See something you like?” He jumped, and I let that *relief* fucking *flow.* He hadn’t heard, thank *god,* and turned a startled smile back at me.

“N-no, I… I was just…”*Appreciating,* he finished as he glanced back at the case. I followed his gaze, found *cuff guards,* fucking collar attachments, and snorted a laugh.

“You don’t even know what those *are,”* I teased, and he frowned up at me.

“And what?” he shot back, and my relief *closed* on itself. Full circle. I’d distracted him, and he was following along. Didn’t even *know* what I’d done for him. I pulled him into my hip, and his frown softened.

“Home?” I asked, and his softened grin softened *further* into a *smile.*

“Home.”
