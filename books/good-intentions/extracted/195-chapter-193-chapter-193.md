## Chapter 193: Chapter 193

Chapter Notes

Living with Draco had taught me two things. One: he was a bit of a princess. Thinking about things like cleaning and organising didn’t come naturally to him, which didn’t really bother me since it only took half a thought for me to keep up with the home, but also two: there was an innate sense of *pride* I took in being the one to do it all. Which worked out sort of perfectly.

Growing up with my aunt and uncle, things like cooking and cleaning and chores were expected under threat of punishment. But when it was my *own* home, my *own* laundry and dishes and countertops, it was a labour of love. Not that I *did* any labour. By the time Spring had rolled around to bless us with even more predictable English rain, I’d grown wholly dependent on my wandless magic.

So *maybe* I was a bit of a princess, too. But it’s not like I’d *prove* anything by getting down on my knees to scrub the floors when I could just will them clean. That, and I’d figured out my own little *hacks* to fast-track everything Molly had taught me about cooking.

The cooking, Draco almost always wanted to participate in. Even if he was just standing behind me, arms round my waist as he watched me. Or sat at the kitchen island, chin in his hand as he doused me in wave after wave of *affection.* It could be a bit distracting sometimes, but so long as there wasn’t a pot boiling or some other time-sensitive process in motion, I… Wasn’t wholly *against*being distracted.

One afternoon, after we’d spent nearly a full day in the shops and loaded up a small tote enchanted with an extension charm, I insisted we visit Flourish & Blotts to buy a few cookbooks. Just for ideas, now that I had a better handle on our magical kitchen. By then, it was mid-April and I’d just about run out of meals I knew how to make myself.

Draco indulged me, even picked out a few new books about magical marine life for himself before we were back out in Diagon Alley. Every cobblestone and store seemed unchanged, despite all that’d happened since I first stepped foot here. Made me oddly nostalgic, and even though we’d been making regular trips back here, the crowd was still *painfully* obvious in their curious observation of us. I almost didn’t even notice anymore.

We’d stopped to treat ourselves to some ice cream from a street vendor when something caught Draco’s eye. The line wasn’t long, but I felt it when he froze, and peered around him to see what’d caught his interest. It was, of all things, a small orange kitten lounging in the window of the Magical Menagerie. Watching the passersby with interest, but seemingly nonplussed by any of the shop visitors who might’ve given it any attention.

A little swell of emotion gripped Draco’s heart, and I smiled. Nudged him out of line, and he didn’t even glance back at me as he led me from the vendor to the window, and only released my hand to crouch before it.

*Oh my god, it’s so fucking cute* flashed across his mind, and I grinned. Crouched next to him, and its wide, blue eyes glanced back and forth between us. With a sideways glance at his *precious* little smile, I lifted a hesitant hand to touch the glass. The kitten leaned in to sniff my fingers, as if it could smell me through the glass, and then *smacked* its little front paws against it like it could *catch me.* And I chuckled.

*This one?*

An *exhilarating* flourish of joy burst out of Draco as he twisted a hopeful glance at me.

*Really? Can we keep it?*

“Of course. It was my idea in the first place anyway.”

He pressed his lips together as he turned back towards it, and it’d laid back down to lick its paws. Sure, it was a month and a half until his birthday, but the timing seemed somewhat serendipitous to me. It was, as he’d said, *really fucking adorable.* And another chuckle escaped me as it stretched out and rolled onto its back.

“Today? Right now?” he pressed, and I jostled his shoulder with mine.

“Come on, before someone else scoops it up.”

Before Draco, I’d always considered myself more of a dog person. Excited to see any animal that was excited to see me. I wasn’t sure why or *how,* but ever since he’d stepped into my life, some gradual change in me had envisioned a *cat* when I’d pictured our future. And as we flooded into the Menagerie, I stopped even really *wondering,* because I’d never felt the sort of *glee* that was now pouring out of Draco.

*“Hi!!”* he greeted the cat as he came around to crouch by the inside of the window. And it flipped onto its other side, studying him curiously as he extended a hand for it to sniff. *God,* I didn’t even *know* he could be this *adorable* when presented with a fluffy animal. Made me want to *fill* our cottage with them, just to see that precious little smile on his face, this fluttering joy coursing through him. I cast my gaze around the shop for the shopkeeper, only to find she was already approaching us.

“This one’s just come in, you’ve got a good eye,” the older brunette lauded as she sidled up to us. “She’s got all her Healer work done, and has been a gem with potty training the week she’s been here. Are you two… Just looking?”

Draco wasn’t listening. He was… *Giggling,* because apparently his scent had been agreeable and the kitten was letting him pet her.

“Oh my god, *hi sweetheart,”* he crooned quietly as he cautiously began trying to pick her up. She seemed confused if anything, but not entirely against the notion as he straightened with her in his arms.

“I think I might be in for a *spat* if she doesn’t come home with us. Can we… Get the lot? I dunno what all we might need for a *cat,* but…”

I trailed off, hoping the shopkeeper (whose nametag I now saw read *Hannah)* would fill in the blanks, which she apparently did as she smiled at me.

“Of course. If you’ll follow me, I can get the paperwork started and we can load you up with the essentials.”

“Fantastic.”

---

I now had *two* reasons for living. Harry, the central gravity of my *life,* my home and solace and entire fucking heart, and *now…*

Saoirse, we decided to name her, (alright, *I* named her) was a perfect *angel* as we brought her home. The shop lady had given us a crate to carry her in, but I just couldn’t *stand* the idea of shoving her in a little box, so she rode all the way back to the cottage in my arms. Alternately looking around curiously and sniffing me, and I was *in love.*

I’d tucked her head into my chest when we Apparated, but once we were safely in our living room, I set her down carefully on the living room bay window bench to let her take in her surroundings, and nearly broke down in fucking *tears* at how adorable she was. So *little* and *soft, god,* she was so soft, I almost couldn’t keep my hands *off of her.* And she was so patient with me, allowed all my little peppered kisses and explored like she’d never thought she *couldn’t.*

Harry even laid down on the floor with her once she’d hopped down to investigate her new home, let her sniff his face, let her crawl up onto his chest and I’d thought I’d been the happiest I could be *before her.* With Harry at my side, *inside* me in every way, but I’d never known, never *expected* I could be *this happy.* This *enamoured.*

Harry peered up at me from the floor, petting Saoirse as she settled down onto his chest and purring happily, and even upside down I read his smirk. Of course, I *immediately* knew what it meant.

“Oh, fuck off!” I grumbled as I settled down into a cross-legged seat above his head, and he chuckled.

“Didn’t say owt,” he teased, though he’d picked his head back up to watch her big, blue eyes casting about the room again. She looked like she might leap off him at any moment to continue her journey of discovery when Harry started scratching under her chin, and her gorgeous big eyes slid shut as she leaned her head back to allow it. Purring so loud, I *knew* it’d inevitably wake me up one of these days. A thick little chuckle huffed out of me.

Eventually, she did jump off him again, scarpering under the couch to explore again, and Harry sat up. Twisted to face me and *smiled.*

*Love you so much* he thought, reached for my hand, and I gave it to him. *Happy early birthday.*

I scoffed. *Oh shut up. This doesn’t count.*

His attention turned suddenly *very* intrigued as he leaned forward over his elbows.

“Tell me, then,” he began, voice low in his chest. “What *do* you want?”

*I have everything that I want* echoed in my head, but I considered. Was there *anything* that could even add a *nugget* onto the perfect happiness I now found myself in? Any way to *improve* upon such *utter perfection?*

Unbidden, an image flashed across my mind. And just like that, we both had our answer. A very specific image, one that had my cheeks (and elsewhere) filling with blood, one that he’d first offered at the soirée dansante involving garters and stockings and maybe, just *maybe* a *leash…*

Harry swallowed, cheeks mirroring my own in their colour as he sat up a bit straighter.

“Oh,” he said quietly. Blinked at me as I waited his response. Which happened to be a nervous chuckle, paired with a “I-I *think* I can manage that.”

The notion had me swelling like a fucking *balloon* in my jeans, but it was just then Saoirse called for us with a tiny, *piteous* little mew, and I leapt to attend her.

But that image we’d shared was *burned* into my fucking eyelids. He was the only gift I ever really wanted, and if he was willing to wrap himself up as such in ribbons and bows and *leather, god…*

My birthday suddenly couldn’t come *soon enough.*
