## Chapter 168: Chapter 168

Chapter Notes

After the first *perfect* night back at the castle, we awoke to a mutual sort of *softness.* Like how Harry would get sometimes after he’d submitted deeper than usual to me. Open, hushed, gentle. *Quiet* as we pried ourselves from the bed for a shower. The warm water was *heavenly,* and it made a little sense to me the more I thought about why we were both in this… This *mood.*

Every new layer we discovered with the soulbond, the deeper we sank into each other. It was like the most intimate opposite of *lonely* one could possibly *feel.* So totally whole and content and at *peace* as I massaged my hair products into Harry’s scalp. Before last night, we’d been able to send each other *surges* of *love,* of *calm,* but now it was like the keyhole we used to use to communicate these things was now just an open *door.* No need to think to *send* anything, it was all *around* us. *In* us. Harry never *wasn’t* loving me, and I was never *not feeling it.*

*God, I hope there’s a dictionary of terminology to describe these experiences because it’s so tricky to understand when I don’t have the language for it.*

*I know what you mean,* Harry agreed as I gently tilted his head back beneath the spray. *Need, like, metaphysical words to describe this shit. Hopefully Hermione finds something.*

Had I ever lived *alone* in my head? As we dressed side-by-side, I *genuinely* struggled to remember what it’d been like to be so cut-off from everyone else. From *Harry.* For weeks now, he’d been slowly but surely infiltrating my soul, so *intrinsic* within me that my past life of solitude seemed like it’d happened to someone *else.*

For one of the last times, we donned our uniforms, green and red like *Christmas* as we strolled silently to the Great Hall. Souls just sort of *humming* together, and it felt *wonderful.*

“Morning!” Hermione greeted us cheerfully as we took our usual seats. Ron grunted his own greeting as Harry reached for the pitcher of coffee. “Excited to cram for the exam?”

She was *far* too awake so early, but a soft scoff sounded to my right as I flipped the upside-down mug on its saucer before my place setting to prepare myself some tea.

“Ecstatic,” he answered quietly just before lifting his own cup of *dirt water* to his lips. But as it slid over his tongue, it… It was *heavenly.* He groaned, silently thankful for *‘proper coffee,’* even though he hadn’t *hated* espresso. So *different* to taste it on his tongue that I was suddenly… *Curious.* He eyed me dubiously before offering me his mug, and I tentatively accepted it.

Sure, it *smelled lovely,* rich and warm but as I carefully lifted it to my *own* lips, the single little sip I sampled was just as bitter and *awful* as it’d ever been. I felt my face screw up in disgust as I handed it back to my chuckling husband.

“So *weird,* having different tastebuds,” I considered. Oh… No, that was out *loud,* because I caught Ron giving us a strange look. “Tastes *wonderful* in Harry’s mouth, but it’s *awful* in mine,” I explained.

“Y-you can *taste* what each other tastes?” Harry hummed in answer as he stole another swig of his coffee, like *roasted chocolate* on his tongue as I chased the bitter aftertaste from mine with a sip of my own still-brewing tea. *“Bloody* hell, when you said you shared *feelings,* I thought you just meant, like… *Happy, sad,* that sort of shite.”

“That, too,” Harry allowed as he set his mug down to start compiling breakfast for himself.

“I read something about that,” Hermione intoned curiously as she leaned over her elbows on the table. “I actually *did* find something in the library, and it’s a bit too much to summarise over breakfast, but… You two can share *sensations,* too, can’t you?”

Her eyes were shining a bit too *mischievously* for us to miss the subtext in her question, and I wouldn’t have known which one of us it was that blushed if I hadn’t seen Harry’s cheeks pink.

“‘S not even *nine* yet, Mione, can you at least let me wake up before you go grilling us about our *sex life?”*

*“Eugh,”* Ron moaned around a mouthful of eggs as he held a fist to his lips. Hermione just cackled quietly to herself, and I admit a snort of my own escaped my careful rein as I spread jam on a bit of toast for myself. “Maybe don’t ask *at all.”*

Poor bloke looked a bit *nauseous* as he forced himself to finish his mouthful thickly, and we settled into friendly silence as the morning inched on. Eventually, the rest of the eighth years drifted to the Gryffindor table, equally groggy after weeks of likely sleeping in, and only muttered greetings interrupted the peaceful silence as we ate. Towards the end of the meal period, though, McGonagall paused on her way up the aisle towards the back entrance, and Harry and I stiffened at her presence.

“Oh, good, you’re all here,” she began, and half of us twisted to wait expectantly for what she had to say. “You’ve three days before the first round of your NEWTs begins, but you needn’t return to the classroom anymore. There will be practice exams to be found there, if you so choose, but it’s up to you how you decide to prepare at this point. I expect I’ll see several of you in the library.” A little implicative quirk of her eyebrow as she eyed us over the tops of her glasses. “Mister Malfoy, Mister Potter, if you would find me in my office once you’ve finished here, there’s a bit of *business* to attend to.”

I expected to feel a little flurry of anxiety, a jolt of adrenaline, but as several eyes at the table turned or leaned to glance back at us, all I felt was… Calm. *Safe.*

“Yes, headmistress,” I said evenly, and she nodded.

“Good morning.”

And she strolled away, out of the Hall.

*Wonder if we’ve been summoned by the Ministry.*

---

Draco wasn’t fretting as we walked through the castle halls towards McGonagall’s office after breakfast. Wasn’t worried, little more than *curious,* and I squeezed his hand in mine. Smiled up at the smile he shot me.

*Not like they can forcibly divorce us or dissolve the soulbond. Never needed their permission anyway.*

*I have everything I want.*

I found if I *focused,* I could differentiate whose thoughts were whose, but it didn’t really *matter* to me as we ascended the staircase to her office doors. And they were already propped open.

“Kingsley,” I breathed as I recognised the figure leaned against McGonagall’s desk, where she sat with fingers steepled before her as he stood to his full height with a smile.

“Hello, Harry,” he said, and I was compelled to leave Draco’s side to *hug* the man. He wasn’t in his ministry robes, he was just here as *Kingsley.* And it’d been too long since I’d seen him as anything but the Minister of Magic. “Good to see you, lad.”

“You, too,” I huffed incredulously as he returned my hug. Patted my back familiarly and then we separated. “What are you doing here?”

He quirked an eyebrow at me as he glanced back at Draco, hands still on my biceps.

“Just… Making a *social call,* of sorts. I hope you don’t mind.”

A little swell of intrigue as he released my arms, and Draco sidled up to take my hand again.

“Of course not. What, er… What can we do for you?”

Kingsley glanced back at McGonagall, and then leaned back against her desk.

“Well… First of all, *congratulations* are in order. The paperwork from the bureaux crossed my desk this morning.” Draco’s hand tightened around mine, and I smiled blithely.

“Thanks. I– we weren’t sure what the proper procedure was for just coming back *married and soulbound.”* A little chuckle huffed through my lips, echoed by his, but a small frown formed between his brows as he glanced down at our hands. “There’s… *More,* isn’t there?”

Kingsley sighed, and passed a hand over his mouth.

“It’s only that… Well, you’ve put me in a rather tricky position, Harry.” The first eddy of *anxiety* drifted through us as we heard him out. “I understand that you two love each other… *Very* much, but… I don’t have to *worry* about you, do I?”

*Worry about me flying off the rails again. Killing people who hurt you.* I felt my brow darken.

“I get it. I’m… *Really* sorry I put you in this position, Kingsley, and I appreciate you letting me off the hook. With the soulbond in full effect, I can confidently say no further harm will come to Draco, so it won’t come to that again.” My attention dropped to McGonagall’s desk as my throat tightened. “I-I *understand* I don’t have the authority to play judge, jury and ex-*executioner,* regardless of saviour status.”

Another sigh as Draco leaned into me. Shoulder to elbow, soul to soul, hearts beating in tandem, and I found the nerve to lift my gaze back to the man before us. Who was eyeing me sympathetically.

“It relieves me to hear you say that, Harry. But even if no *harm* ever comes to Draco again, there may likely be, er… *Outrage* in the form of media publications. I don’t need to worry about the lives of seedy reporters looking to stoke a reaction for attention, right?”

An image of the Prophet going up in *flames* in Draco’s hand rose behind my eyes, and I bit back the little edge of *anger* that accompanied it to recall the horrible things that article had said. I glanced sideways at Draco before responding, because I *absolutely* felt justified defending him, *protecting* him, whatever that might mean, but his soft eyes studied mine as we worked in tandem to temper my answer.

“I don’t want to inspire fear, or pose a threat to anyone who might question our relationship.” I spoke slowly as Draco helped me say what I *meant,* instead of what I *felt.* And strove to meet Kingsley’s eye steadily. “And I… I’d like to *promise* nothing like that will ever happen again, but I… I’m just *mad* about this idiot, and while I might be a *legal adult,* I’m still just a teenager. So if… If you need to assign a–” *Supervisor? You mean babysitter?* I sent Draco a scowl, but couldn’t deny he had a fair point as I let him finish my sentence through my lips. “A *s-supervisor,* or something, I dunno. A PR rep, to help me brace for what I might hear or read or *learn,* I would… Understand.”

Kingsley nodded, brow crumpled contemplatively as his gaze slipped into a middle-distance, chewing on our words.

*I won’t be the only one suffering the consequences of this if he takes us up on this.*

I felt the subtle degree Draco’s heart warmed, just like how it felt when he *smiled,* but knew he was keeping his expression firmly neutral.

*I know.*

“I appreciate your candidness. And you raise a good point. Perhaps an… *Advisor* of sorts would benefit you?”

Kingsley’s attention focused back on me as I swallowed. But it was Draco who spoke now, with so much more ease of care as I followed his train of thought.

“W-we all *know* Harry to be a… *Passionate* person, and given how his abilities have enhanced since the war, your concerns make perfect sense.” A certain *petulance* soured in my chest, but this was such a *delicate* conversation to have, I let it go. None of this was going on record, nothing *official,* but we were still speaking with the *Minister of Magic.*His opinion of us would inevitably affect his ruling should we be– “We’re being summoned soon, aren’t we?”

A grimace stained his features now as he glanced between the two of us. And the little flutter of anxiety that’d made itself known in us responded to it.

“I have reason to believe I’ll be pushed to do so, yes.” We shared another glance before he went on. “Which is why I wanted to have this conversation with you two *now.* Thank you, Draco, for your insight. I think… A sort of *monitor* of Harry’s progress and development would ease the minds of the Wizengamot, especially after the lax ruling that followed his, er… Well, *killing spree.”*

He huffed an uncomfortable sort of laugh and adjusted his seat against the lip of McGonagall’s desk. The stoic figure behind which was sat remained curiously *silent* in the presence of this discussion.

*Divine intervention is more like it,* I grumbled internally, and felt a subtle elbow against my arm.

*You could’ve been convicted, Harry,* came his silent retort, and I bit back a scoff. *Behave.*

“I ap-appreciate you taking the time to discuss this privately, Kingsley. I’m… *Sorry,* again, for… *Letting my emotions* get the better of me.” I spoke through gritted teeth as Draco carefully vetted my wording, because he *was* right, I’d let my emotions take control. But like *I’d* said, I was still just a bloody teenager! And Draco had been *literally kidnapped and tortured,* *what the fuck was I supposed to do??*

A wave of calm submerged my soul, and a sigh eased out of me. And Kingsley smiled at us curiously, trading his gaze between the two of us as a… Almost *teasing* sort of curl twisted his lips.

“You two make a fine couple,” he remarked. And I was sort of *shocked* at his observation. Must’ve shown on my face, because he cut a little nose-laugh. “I feel *much* better, seeing you two like this. You know the last *soulbond* in the UK was in the *seventeen hundreds?”*

Kingsley finally pushed off McGonagall’s desk, and adjusted his collar as he twisted back to address her. And I felt Draco hone in on my soul again, reading my state at its core, *wanting* me to feel attended and cared for. I took a measure of comfort for it as I listened to the conversation before us.

“Thanks again, Minerva. Sorry to barge in on you like this. You understand, don’t you?”

A polite little smile formed on McGonagall’s lips as she lowered her steepled hands to her lap.

“Of course, Minister. You’re always welcome, regardless of the circumstances or reason. Is there anything else I can do for you?”

He glanced back at us, at *both* of us, a certain sort of satisfaction in his eyes.

“No, I’ll get out of your hair.” And he took us in his hands, one shoulder each as he smiled at us. “The world can go fuck itself, you two will go down in *history.”*

We *both* went still with shock at hearing him *swear,* but he gave us a final parting jostle, a last glance back at McGonagall, and then stepped around us and *left.* He just… *Left,* on *that note.*

*What the fuck…?*
