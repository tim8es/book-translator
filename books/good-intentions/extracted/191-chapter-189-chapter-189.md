## Chapter 189: Chapter 189

“You aren’t *really* getting horses, right? It’s just a metaphor for freedom?” Ron supposed as we wandered the perimeter of the field. February was barely creeping up on us but the difference of the warmth of sunshine and the dappled shade of the surrounding woods was noticeable even through the foliage.

“I think Harry’s aim is like, an animal sanctuary? So no, the horses are likely very much real.”

Ron sniffed and shoved his hands in his pockets.

“Awful lot of work for two blokes.” I snorted, stepping around a patch of wildflowers.

“Might be if we were *actually* gonna do *manual labour.”*

“Still, animals need *enrichment.* You two planning to spend a lot of time at home to give them all attention?”

I inhaled deeply of the earthy air, savouring the sound of birdsong deeper in our woods.

“I think it’s exactly what Harry and I need, honestly.”

The cottage was several acres away by now, but I still sensed Harry and Hermione step out the back garden gate into the field, and paused to turn towards it. They were discussing the logistics of hosting the wedding *here,* and I felt Harry’s attention searching for me, too, as he sent me a silent

*Mind the grass.*

I relayed the message to Ron, and he frowned, but it wasn’t a moment later the entire field rippled outward from the house as Harry shore the blades to a much more manageable length.

“Bloody hell,” Ron muttered as the wave of loose grass grew closer to us, and I foresaw the impending shower of plantlife just in time to understand Harry’s warning. It was easier than I thought it’d be to reach into his seemingly infinite well of magic, even across the clearing, and draw a fraction of it into myself. Lifted a hand just in time to erect a shield to protect us both from the flurry of grass sent our way, though Ron still flinched.

I was sort of sad to see the wildflowers mixed in with the grass get shorn short, but also considered why *I* hadn’t thought to do that earlier. Traversing the landscape would be *much* easier now. As the sudden torrent of grass and flowers and twigs settled harmlessly behind us, I lowered my hand.

“You alright?” I smirked over at Ron, who was just coming out of a protective stance to baulk at me.

“Never get used to that,” he murmured, checking himself for stray blades of grass. And I chuckled.

“They’re talking about hosting here.” I could barely make out two bodies venturing out from the back garden into the land, and smiled to myself. “Save an awful lot of money on a venue, I s’pose.”

Ron grunted. “Mione’d plan her own death if she could. Woman loves being in control.”

Before I could stop myself, a suggestive little hum escaped me, and earned me a surprised side-eye. Long enough I was about to apologise for my insinuation, but his surprise lent itself to *me* when he… *Laughed.*

“Set myself up for that one, I guess.” Amusement huffed out of me as we resumed walking, retreating back to the cottage now as he added quietly: “Seriously, though, you have… *No idea.”*

*That,* I laughed at.

The days were growing longer, I realised as I peered out the sitting room window at the lavender and peach sunset. Winter’s chill would recede, hopefully some wildlife would emerge. I was particularly interested to see baby rabbits peeping out of the warren at the edge of the garden, but Ron’s birthday was a mere month away now, and I sipped my hot chocolate to chase down the weird nostalgia the notion knotted in my throat.

After would come Draco’s. The wedding. *My* birthday. We were all getting older, officially out in the world and living our *adult lives.* I’d agreed to hold off on my animal search until after the wedding, since it wouldn’t do to have construction going on were we to host it *here.* The prospect of a *cat,* however…

“Hey,” came Draco’s soft voice as he quietly padded into the room. I smiled up at him, and he came to join me on the couch. “You’re really considering having it here?”

He twisted to face me, pulling one leg up onto the seat and leaning back onto the backrest over an arm. One he extended towards me to twirl his fingers through my hair, and I leaned into it.

“Seems your mum’s visit convinced her of its potential, and she’s almost won Hermione over. God knows how *she’s* managed to sway her of *anything,* but if it’s what she wants, it’s fine with me. Is that… Alright?”

I watched Draco’s face carefully as it blossomed into an easy smile.

“Of course. I happen to have a wealth of experience hosting parties, you know.”

A hum as I took another sip of my hot chocolate. I still sensed a nuance of *reluctance* within him, but it was subdued enough that I could tell he was trying to ignore it. If I just pushed with the *gentlest* pressure, I could glean that he didn’t *want* to shy away from the prospect of a crowd, of attention, of *children* running around and screaming and causing havoc. A grin tugged on my lips at his imagination of such a thing, and I leaned over to set my mug on the ottoman.

Draco welcomed me with open arms as I reoriented myself on the couch to lean back into him. Drew the blanket over myself up over his legs, too, as he wrapped me up in himself and pressed a kiss to my cheek. We took a deep breath in unison, not even really meaning to as we relaxed into each other, and exhaled on a synchronised sigh.

I was so fucking happy it felt like my heart was *breaking.* Like it was just *ripping apart,* warm and safe and happy in the arms of the love of my fucking *life.* It was actually slightly *painful,* how hard my chest was gripping my heart, and I felt a little nudge against my soul. Driven by a desire to *check in,* Draco was silently asking me to induce Fieridem. And with a swallow, I allowed it.

The volume of emotion I was feeling seemed to surge with me as I let my soul take his into itself. Into *myself,* into *me. Us.*

Not in anything like language, he searched the breadth of me. Stretched himself into my very crevices, expanded until I felt him in every *inch* of my being, and I distantly noticed my eyes had slid shut. It felt *wonderful,* so blissfully euphoric I almost whimpered as he reached into me, *through* me. Like the pure, undiluted *togetherness* I always felt with his prick in my arse. The most ecstatic version of a hug.

He gently prodded my soul’s wounds, a bit, and I felt myself twitch in his arms. My breath hitched in my chest, but I let him. The concept behind the word *H**ealer* echoed somewhere in his mind as he assessed the state of them, and I realised what he was doing.

It’d been almost exactly a month since Fern’s first visit. She’d popped by a few times since then, but the Healer’s card hadn’t been touched since I’d placed it on the mantelpiece. Draco was as aware of this as I was, and was ascertaining just how much we could accomplish without… Healer *Worten.*

Images of Hogwarts’s hospital wing flashed through my head, sterile bandages and vile potions and cots, and I felt myself wince again. Felt his arms tighten around me, because who’d ever had a *pleasant* experience in a medical facility? It was reluctance that drove our procrastination, and I couldn’t help but agree as I let Draco delicately reach into the wound named *parents.*

It was a familiar ache – the oldest – but didn’t hurt any less as I felt him soundly pumping *love* into the sore emptiness. It was so different than any other loss, because I barely had *scraps* to cling to of them. Snape’s memories, others’ stories, the few brief miracles that’d allowed me to meet them. All flashed behind my eyes like a montage as they spilled out of the injury he flooded with *love.*

*Love.* It was all I really had of either of them. Lost time and missed opportunities, every birthday they’d missed, every milestone. And now they’d miss my *wedding.* A pitiful little sob escaped me before I could bite it back, and I felt Draco flinch back. Pressed the soul-equivalent of a *kiss* to the wound as he retreated, not out of me, but elsewhere. He seemed determined to love me to *death* as he sought out the corrupted corner of my soul Riddle’s own used to inhabit.

Memories of fifth year rushed back to me as he cautiously reached the edge of the stain. When Riddle had learned of our connection, *used* it to manipulate me, to *torture* me, and though he was dead and gone, the hatred that used to live here seemed to echo through it in retaliation to Draco’s love. Like a habit, like a *reflex,* and I saw a flicker of me punching my reflection for just a half a heartbeat.

All those years. All that *time,* his Horcrux had been *bleeding* into my soul. It’d taken most of its influence with it when he’d died, but still the scar remained. Just like the ones on my brow, on my chest. Scarred and tender. Draco’s love felt *warm* as he spread it over the blackened wretch of its remains, like a balm, but I wasn’t sure this was something a Healer could fix. That *he* could fix.

*“Might never go away,”* I thought defeatedly. While I appreciated his efforts, even with the soulbond, even with *Fieridem,* it’d been a part of me for *seventeen years.* And it might’ve taken even longer to fully rid myself of it, if that was even a possibility.

*“Doesn’t mean I won’t still try to help,”*he answered in that soft, wind-chime soul-voice of his. And I smiled.

Draco didn’t push this time. Not like before in the library when he’d discovered this… Blight. Didn’t force his love into it hard enough to bring me to tears, just kissed it, *accepted* it, and retreated to search elsewhere.

It was strange feeling him map my soul. Because it was my own, it wasn’t really something I could look at *myself,* but experiencing it with him through our bond was like holding up a mirror. Dozens of little rips and tears and wounds with as many names dotted my soul like a pox, leaking pain and memories and all seemed to shrink as Draco’s passed over them. But I knew what he was looking for. And braced myself.

The wound named Sirius was by far the worst. The most painful. Not warped and rotten like the Horcrux’s, but raw. Red and wide and bleeding, even after all the love he’d *already* poured into it. And I couldn’t help but fidget in his arms as he carefully, *expertly* slid into it.

I could’ve pushed him out. Shut myself off from him, seized the wound back to nurse it myself, but I… Let him in. Let him soak himself in the runoff, the agony, the trauma. My breath was coming a bit too fast, a strange physical sensation that couldn’t quite rip me out of the Fieridem we were sharing, but I was quickly distracted from it as every smile Sirius had ever given me flashed through my head.

Every hug. Every affectionate ruffle of my hair. Every clap on the shoulder. I would never, *ever* stop missing him, and it *hurt.* If ever I’d had a real version of a father, Sirius had been it. The *only one.* My only family I’d ever had before Draco, and his final smile to me as he drifted into the veil slammed into the front of my brain and I gasped.

I’d gone rigid, Draco’s tight grip on me told me as much. Heaving and blinking, though I couldn’t see much. Warmth ran trails down my cheeks, and I tried to relax back into him, but that *smile…* It’d said so much. And never enough. *Never.*

“I’m sorry,” he said quietly, his thumb rubbing soothing passes over my bicep as I finally started to ease back into him. I was crying, even though he’d withdrawn his attention to just *hold me,* that final, sorrowful, parting smile was like a brand behind my eyelids. “I’m sorry, Harry.”

What a Healer might do that Draco couldn’t, I didn’t know. Perhaps they could use Necromancy? Bring Sirius back to life? But I couldn’t bet on much of a life for him, if what I’d heard of the Dark Art was accurate. No, there was no way I’d ever see him again, unless…

Unless…

My eyes screwed shut as I fought to control my breathing, Draco’s warmth soaking into my back as I wrestled the lid back onto *hope.* I *couldn’t* use the stone to summon Sirius, I just *couldn’t…*

To have to look him in the eyes, knowing he was dead and I’d have to go *my whole life* without him was just too painful. He was the one person I could never, *ever* summon, because I knew the risks. I’d waste away talking to him, fall into the same pitfall the Peverell brother had when he’d fell prey to the stone all those centuries ago. I swallowed thickly as Draco pressed another kiss to my hair.

“You’re alright,” he murmured, and I inhaled deeply with the flood of *love* and *gentle care* he pulsed into me. But another sob broke free of my careful rein.

And it was then I resolved to finally reach out to the Healer.
