## Chapter 56: Chapter 56

Chapter Notes

The longer dinner went on, the more I was included in the conversation, the more I was convinced there wasn’t *anything* Harry couldn’t do. Couldn’t achieve if he wanted it. It was like having a fucking *god* in my pocket.

Speaking of pockets, I curiously delved into my own to see what trinkets I’d swiped today. And groaned when I recognised what I withdrew.

“You’re fucking *kidding* me,” I moaned as I set the tiny salt shaker on the table. *Rosmerta’s gonna kill me.*

Harry laughed incredulously, took it off the table and studied it.

“No *way.* Oh, this is *so* going in the drawer.” And pocketed it himself, still chuckling.

“What’s that?” Neville wondered, wiping frosting off the corner of his mouth with his napkin. I rolled my eyes as Harry eagerly perked up and launched into his explanation.

“Right, so Draco does this *thing* where he snatches little odds and ends and pockets them without even *noticing,* and… Well, ever since I called him out on it, he’s been giving me the things he finds in his pockets.”

“Aw,” Luna crooned, leaning into her palm dreamily. “That’s *adorable.”*

“Is *not,”* I hissed back, angrily chasing a raspberry around on my plate. “I’m a *thief!"*

Tonight was one of the rare nights Harry had decided to eat dessert, and I didn’t have to wonder if it was because he was so pleased to have his friends slowly *adjusting* to me, so I didn’t rush him from the table. If he wanted to linger, I’d linger with him all night. But I’d still gripe when *anyone* called me any synonym of *cute.*

“Mostly, it’s just… Rocks and seashells and shit, but this has to be the first thing you’ve *actually stolen* from anywhere.”

I sent him a sneer, but he was just grinning so *brightly* at me, I couldn’t force myself to hold it on my face. My cheeks warmed.

“I don’t do it on purpose! I *swear,* I dunno where I picked up the habit, but now there’s shit in my pockets *everyday.”*

“There’s *always* been shit in your pockets. Used to see you stealing erasers and bits of tile and thought you might’ve been concocting some elaborate fucking *potion*, only to find out you didn’t even know you were doing it.”

I narrowed my eyes at him. “You know, like once a day you forget you wear *fucking glasses* and try to touch your eye *through them?*Honestly, how long have you worn glasses and you still forget?”

He straightened in his seat, sort of gaping at me as Hermione coughed another laugh. Harry sent her a betrayed sort of indignant expression, and she only laughed harder.

“He’s right, you do,” she confirmed, and I nodded.

“See?”

*“God,* you two are gonna make me *sick,”* Neville complained, but there was amusement in the curve of his lips.

“Just wait,” Ron murmured into his mug of hot cocoa, and earned a chuckle from me.

*I could get used to this.*

Eventually, Harry complained about being too full and I didn’t need further encouragement to leave together. We bid his friends good night and retreated back to the eighth year dorms, but as we reached the boy’s hall, he paused outside my door.

“You w-wanna grab some clothes…?”

*Right. I’m moving in.* I swallowed.

“S-sure.”

I let us both in, and Harry sort of marvelled at all the clothes I withdrew onto the bed.

“Too much?”

Turning his gaze from the growing pile to me, his open mouth snapped shut and he shook his head.

“It’s not that, I just… H-how experienced are you with Legillimency?”

I frowned, tossing the black jeans in my hand onto the pile.

“Not at all. Why?”

Harry drifted to my bed, sort of passed a hand over the pile of clothes, and sat next to them, facing me.

“I wanna show you something.”

*Legillimency? He wants me to read his mind…?* Curiously, I withdrew my wand. It wasn’t a complicated spell or wand pattern, but I was still a bit hesitant.

“Are you sure?”

“I-if you’d rather not, you don’t have to. It can be… Pretty jarring to be inside someone’s head.” He smiled weakly. “But I trust you, and I *think* I’ve had enough experience to be able to… Show you only what I want you to see.”

My lips pursed as I considered it. If I accidentally saw something he didn’t want me to see, of course I’d feel *bad,* but… Even somewhat knowing some of the worst things I could encounter, I wasn’t afraid. I wanted every part of him, no matter how damaged or mangled. So I aimed my wand at him.

“A-alright.” He shut his eyes, brow knit in concentration, but an easy smile on his lips. And I uttered the spell.

Harry hadn’t been lying, but *jarring* was a bit of an understatement. I was *in his head,* and I could sense him trying to focus while also being aware of my presence, and he sort of… Led me through a maelstrom of impressions and images that flurried around us until the scenery finally resolved to a homey little living room. Almost like a cottage, where Harry was reclining long-ways on the couch, levitating a book before him as he sipped a glass of wine.

“Harry, have you seen my blue jumper?”

*That’s my voice.* Without looking up, Harry called back.

“I think it’s under the bed?” Took another sip of wine, turned the page.

A warm fire roared in the hearth, gentle snow falling outside and I was *entranced.* Bookshelves, pictures, Quidditch memorabilia, *memories* littered the room, and shock sort of struck me as my eyes landed on a cat curled up on a footrest before the fireplace.

Suddenly, who should come prancing down the stairs but *me,* in all my naked glory. Holding said jumper and hair damp.

“How’d it end up *there?”* This version of me wondered as Harry smiled up at him.

“Might’ve… *Kicked* it. Sorry.”

Naked-me sighed, ran a hand through his hair, and came into the room. Knelt briefly to pet the cat as Harry took another sip of his wine.

“Want some wine?” Harry’s book landed softly in his lap as he shifted to sit up more, and fabricated-me stood to approach the couch.

“What’re we drinking to?”

He knelt beside Harry, lifting the bottle from the rug to read the label.

“First snow of the season? Cannons beat the Magpies? I’m in a warm home with the love of my life and all is right with the world? Take your pick.”

This other me smirked, lifted the open bottle to his lips and necked a couple gulps. And Harry reached out to invite his elbow up higher, urging him to drink more.

“Mm!” Harry laughed as the other Draco finally lowered the bottle from his lips, passed a hand over his mouth and grinned. “Trying to get me drunk, Mister Potter? Tut tut.”

“But you’re so *funny* when you’re drunk!” Harry whined, but was mollified with a kiss. A kiss that turned into a *snog* as he wrapped his arms around my twin and allowed himself to be lifted up to his feet.

A phonograph flicked on in the corner of the room, filling the room with dulcet, romantic brass as the two began to sway together on the rug.

“Come to bed,” the other Draco muttered against Harry’s lips. A ripple of a tremor went down his spine as he nodded, and the two scarpered off towards the stairs I’d come down, giggling like teenagers. And then I was back in my body.

I couldn’t move. My eyes prickled, my throat felt tight as Harry blushed up at me sheepishly from my bed.

“I-I want that,” he said quietly. “Normal.”

My wand-hand slowly lowered to my side, and then just fucking dropped it. I was kneeling before him before I’d even made the decision to move. And his face swam in my vision as I gazed up at him.

“Me too,” I croaked, throat thick with emotion. And it was then I realised I was crying. Harry smiled down at me, reached out to swipe moisture across my cheek, and I just sort of… Leaned into his crotch and *wept.*

He carded his hands through my hair as I clung to his legs, and I was *overcome.* I hadn’t been lying when I said I wanted forever with him, but had been unable to picture it until now. And realising he’d envisioned a *future* with me when I’d admitted it, just… *Just*–

A *horrid* little sob escaped me as I soaked his jeans with my tears. Waking up next to him every day, fucking him *stupid, every single night,* learning all the ridiculous little muggle rituals he’d picked up in childhood and adopting a *cat…* It was too much.

Harry let me cry as long as I could. As long as I *needed to,* because *fuck me,* I’d never thought *anyone* would ever want me like… Like *that.* Like *this.* Like Harry wanted me. *Harry wants me forever.* He whispered he loved me so many times I lost count, and I was all at once drowning in that *agonising* bliss I’d once wept into this very bed.

In that one little glimpse, I saw our entire *lives* stretching out before us. A hand in mine to help navigate the unforgiving Wizarding world when my past was so irrevocably stained with blood, and not just any hand, but *Harry’s.* The boy I’d tormented for *years* because I couldn’t *stand* how taken I was with him. The Boy Who Lived. The *man who loved me.*

I felt like I was *actually* going to *fucking combust* with the sheer enormity of the love I felt swirling through me. My love for him, already *impossibly* large, and then the semblance of an idea of *his love for me.*

*Here lies Draco Malfoy.*

*He was loved to death.*
