Shacklebolt shut the door behind himself, and in the silence that followed, the anxiety that’d been building in Harry and I *flourished.*

*Fuck.*

McGonagall eyed us for a moment, as if she just *wanted* us to agonise, but eventually spoke.

“You can’t blame him, honestly. Harry, I understand why you did what you did, but…” She tilted her head to the side, brow pinching like *sympathy* as we silently supplied the end of her sentence. *But holy shit, you killed all those people.* She sighed. “I know this conversation has likely derailed your focus, but I would like to *implore* you to study well. There *may* be exceptions made should any of your peers, including you, *fail* your NEWTs, but I would rather not have to do the paperwork to justify you *retaking* it. So…”

He nodded.

“Right. I-I understand. We won’t disappoint you, headmistress.”

A tight smile and nod and we were bid on our way. And as we walked to the library together, Harry’s recollection of his prior visit to the Ministry (while I’d been in hospital) replayed behind his eyes. I silently attended.

The hushed whispers, the tension in the air as Kingsley Wandered him. Finding he’d hardly been using his wand *at all* the previous months, and the questions regarding what had happened. The Aurors that’d responded to the call gave their testimonies, and I felt the echo of the memory of Harry’s gut clenching to hear the details with such description.

*I killed them all. And I didn’t feel the least bit bad about it. Didn’t even really think about it, didn’t– Well, yes, actually, I did mean to do it. When I realised you’d been hurt, I had nothing but murder in my heart.*

My hand squeezed around his as we turned a corner closer to the library.

*You’re not a bad person, Harry. Honestly, I wouldn’t even have called that an overreaction. If I’d found you in that state, and had the abilities you possess, I might’ve even done something worse.*

He turned a dubious, quirked eyebrow up at me.

*Worse than killing them?*

*You gave them all quick, merciful deaths.* Harry’s brow slacked as my words rang in his head. *I would’ve taken my time, personally.*

Even just *imagining* finding him tied to a chair, wand snapped in half and beaten, *bleeding* nearly had me *trembling* with cold rage.

*I could’ve just incapacitated them until help arrived. Should’ve if we’re being honest.* The library doors were already wide open as we strolled through. *Never really been a think first, act later sort of bloke, though.*

I felt a smirk pull at my mouth as we searched for our peers. Paced further into the quiet room to glance down the aisles when they weren’t readily apparent.

*Mind, I’m not condoning murder,* I tacked on. *Just… I understand. You’re not a bad person.*

*Murderer* echoed quietly in Harry’s mind, and I pulled him to a halt against one of the ends of the shelves. He wouldn’t look up at me, though, as… *Shame* of all things festered in his chest. *Spent so long trying not to be like him.*

A montage of impressions were fed into my head, nearly too fast for me to comprehend, but they must’ve nestled into my subconscious because I *understood* the story they told. The Dark Lord in his head, feeding him *lies* and *manipulations* to try to encourage Harry to give in, to acknowledge their–

*Similarities? You’ve got to be fucking joking.* Finally, he did glance up at me, shame contorting his features into this sheepish little guilty look that made my heart *twist.* And I pulled him down an empty aisle.

A *flash* of my aunt *screaming* under his Cruciatus Curse moments after Sirius’s death, and I whirled him back against the shelves. Took his face in my hands and forced him to look at me. For a long moment, I couldn’t *think* through the onslaught of memories his anxiety was compiling for me. A *symphony* of screams, Quirrell’s, Hermione’s, Ron’s, Harry’s *own,* and an *image,* clarifying second by second of… *Me.* Splayed out on the bathroom floor and leaking *red* from too many injuries for him to *comprehend.*

“Harry,” I said quietly, realising his breath was suddenly coming in short little gasps, *panic* rising out of him to dance with the guilt, the shame, the *horror.* So I repeated it a bit louder. “Harry.”

*Sorry, I’m sorry, I’m so sorry* played on a loop in him, like it wasn’t even a conscious *thought,* but a symptom of the malady affecting his soul. He was *spiralling* down an endless hole of *regret,* and it was starting to affect *me,* so much *I* still regretted, so I… Did the only thing I could think of. I kissed him.

A gasp sucked into his nose as I pressed my lips soundly to his. And just like that, his head went quiet. The montage flickered to darkness, and he lifted his hands to take my waist. Pulled me tighter into himself, and I let him. Pressed him back against the shelves and kissed him like I *should’ve,* all those months ago when I’d last pinned him to these shelves.

With his mind silent like this, I found it much easier to think. And decided to use that awareness to reach into him, followed that stream of *ache* to its source, much like when I’d found the wound called *Sirius* in him, but *this…*

*Bloody hell,* I didn’t even know where to *begin* to fix this. To *help,* but I *desperately* had to try. It was like a corner of his soul was just… *Black.* If souls could take physical shape, if they could be described in terms like *colours,* this piece of him I’d never accessed before was… *Wrong.* And as I touched it with my own, it became suddenly all-too-clear what I was perceiving.

Harry had been a *Horcrux* for the first seventeen years of his life. Had harboured a piece of the Dark Lord’s *soul* until it’d been forcibly ejected at the price of his *life.* A little *flinch* of pain passed through us both as I investigated this… This *corruption,* because I couldn’t deny the familiarity that came with the sensation. This was *his doing.* I knew it just as clearly as I knew we’d stopped kissing.

The lingering effects of *dark magic. Cursed* magic, like the scar on his brow, his chest, *this* must’ve been the spot *he’d* previously occupied. And even though the Horcrux was absent, its damage remained. *That’s* why Harry tried to make himself small. Why he felt so strongly about proving he was *dissimilar* to the Dark Lord. Why he felt his guilt so heavily. A new facet of him washed over me, to *know this,* to feel it and realise it and *see it* was just so–

My own mind went similarly blank. I think I might’ve had my eyes shut, or was just so lost in this un-physical sensation that my vision went dark. There, in that butchered corner of his soul, I *loved him.* Actively, *aggressively* loved him. Loved that *stain,* that *scar,* loved it so hard it *hurt,* and distantly heard a little pained *whimper* as Harry’s knees buckled.

I didn’t know what I was doing. If I was even doing the *right thing,* or could *ever* be enough to even *slightly* help, but as he slid to the floor, I followed to my knees. Felt so *strange,* so *wrong* to perceive what one might’ve called a *weakness* in such an all-powerful being. But equally *honoured* to be granted an audience with it. I *kissed* it, even though it *burned,* not hot like Harry’s magical core, not like fire but like… Like *soul-deep pain* so acute it was *hot.*

*“Draco!”* The sound of my name, paired with a hand on my shoulder *jostling* me tore me out of this almost *trance-*like state I’d put myself into. And I blinked down at Harry, who was *weeping* beneath me as we sat and knelt on the library floor. My startled attention twisted to see who’d interrupted me, and it was none other than the clairvoyant witch herself, looking panicked as *hell.* “What the fuck is *happening?”*

Harry braced himself like he might try to sit up, but as I cast my gaze around at our attending audience, I realised we’d made a scene. Not only had the eighth years found us, but other students lingered in the aisle, watching, *whispering* with avid attention.

“I-I…” My voice came out crackly from disuse, and I cleared my throat. “I… *Think* I’m healing Harry’s psyche,” was my answer as Hermione glanced back at Ron, frown firm on his ginger brow as he studied us. Harry sniffed and hastily wiped his cheeks dry as I turned back to him. “Or at least I was… *Trying.”*

Luna knelt on our other side, but I only spared her a glance because my hands were still on his face and he was *crying.*

*Shit, I’m sorry,* I shot at him reflexively.

“Alright, Harry?” she asked, and I stroked his cheeks. Brushed his hair off his forehead, *touched* him gently. And his stuttering exhale was interrupted with a short cough.

“F-fine, Luna,” he lied. Shot me a glance and quietly requested my help sitting up more. So I did. “J-just… Erm… Caught me in a b-bit of *spontaneous therapy.”*

He cleared his throat, and a soft scoff escaped me.

“Alright, nothing to see here!” Neville started herding our audience away from the aisle, and gratitude swelled in me to see him shooing them away. “Bunch of fucking *busybodies.”*

I swiped a few more tears off Harry’s cheeks, the corners of his eyes as he glanced up at Luna, then Hermione, and finally me.

*You’re gonna love me to death, I swear.*
