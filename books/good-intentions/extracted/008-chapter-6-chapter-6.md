## Chapter 6: Chapter 6

*Harry Potter is in my fucking room. And he’s… Giggling?*

I really couldn’t believe what I was hearing, what I was *seeing.* But I hadn’t left him an inch to hide from me, testing his bluff, and he was *red as hell*.

All those years, watching him from afar, getting into scraps just for the excuse to get to touch him and now he was *here.* In touching distance— in *kissing*distance. My gaze dropped to his bloody mouth again as he pressed his lips together to smother his laughter. It was positively dizzying.

And he was asking me to *act like I like him?* Fucking hell, what did that even *mean?* Still, a small part of me counted it as a victory. The only reason I’d run in the first place was because I was assuming he’d be upset, *disgusted,* that a guy had a crush on him. And that that guy happened to be *me…*

“S-sorry,” Harry spat hastily. “I’ll-I’ll stop, I promise.”

My gut dropped for some reason, but this was… Good. Right? He agreed to stop fucking *teasing* me so long as I stopped running from him. He wanted to talk to me. To be *normal*with me. Harry cleared his throat, still red as a sunrise before a storm.

“S-so we’re… We’re good, yeah?”

I nodded, still grappling with the fact that *I’d touched him,*and he hadn’t flinched away. Hadn’t moved out of the way or pushed my hand.

“Yeah,” I finally uttered on a ghost of a breath. “Fine.”

*Not fine. Not anything close to fine.*

“R-right… Well… Good night.”

He wriggled sideways out from between me and the door and left through a crack, but I was stuck in place, just staring blankly at the wood.

*What the bloody fuck just happened??*

---

The next morning, I was feeling so hungover from a lack of sleep (thanks to a certain blond git) that I had suddenly found myself seated in class without remembering how I’d got there. I was barely there as it was, nearly nodding off in my palm when a word broke through the lingering drowsiness.

“M-morning.”

It wasn’t a particularly surprising word, one I’d no doubt heard a million times in my life, but the voice that fumbled it now… I turned to blink at the pink-cheeked blond to my left.

“Morning,” I croaked back. He was methodically withdrawing his effects from his book bag, laying them out on his desk with a precision that betrayed discomfort. And I couldn’t help but grin. “Not so painful, is it?”

Malfoy threw a scowl my way, but the effect was ruined by the pink in his cheeks going suddenly redder. And I smiled a bit wider.

“Sod off,” he half-heartedly muttered, but before he turned away from me, I happened to glimpse what I could’ve sworn was a hint of a *smile* tugging at the corner of his lips.

*Imagine that. A real smile on his face, not a sneer.*Only inconsiderate, teasing responses formed in my mind so I just shut the hell up and leaned over the edge of my seat to rifle through my own bag.

Suppose this was his version of “acting normal” around me, and I was grateful. Uneasy as it was, this sort of performative civility was… Nice. *I could get used to this,* I mused as Malfoy flipped his Transfig textbook open again and cleared his throat.

Since this was a supplemental class, I didn’t feel uneasy about withdrawing my Potions textbook to help me with the worksheet I was meant to be doing. I did try to answer what I could without it, but whenever I was particularly stumped, the text was helpful. Our only goal this term was to prepare for our NEWTs after all, so it was useful to find the gaps in my knowledge even if it was annoying having to flip through the book every fourth question.

How the hell they’d figured out what exactly each individual needed particular work on, I hadn’t the foggiest. But when I cast a glance up at Hermione’s back and saw her with three textbooks stacked next to what must’ve been three feet of parchment, a wry smile spread across my lips. Of course, ever the overachiever, she’d likely just decided to put herself back through the entire seventh year. It was just as well Ron didn’t have her in elbowing distance to help him with his own work. I glanced over my shoulder to see him towards the back, staring mournfully down at a blank parchment of his own. A snicker worked through my lips as he happened to glance up at the same moment and caught my eye.

“*Help me,”* he mouthed, and I gestured hopelessly at my own worksheet like we *both* needed Hermione to get through this, and he smirked.

It was short-lived, though, when he glanced pointedly to my left and raised his eyebrows as if to say *least I don’t have to sit next to him.* I shrugged noncommittally and finally turned back round to my work. Malfoy was distracting sometimes, but now that we were on speaking terms, it wasn’t… Unpleasant. I risked another cautious glance his way, and it was like he now had some sort of sixth sense for when I was looking at him, because his ears pinked.

He didn’t look at me, but I saw the way his eyes went from skimming the text before him to stuck in place. Like he didn’t want to look back at me, but couldn’t quite read. *God,* I was tempted to tease him. Despite the fact I’d promised not to, he just made it too fucking easy. Knowing I could easily just lean across the aisle and casually ask to borrow his ink or something, and that he’d immediately start stammering and going red was *too much power.*

*He’d never have said a word if he’d known we’d have to be trapped together in this room for four months afterward,* I mused as I finally allowed my attention to return to the work I was neglecting. *And I would’ve just gone on completely oblivious to the now-obvious fact that the git fancies me.*

My quill twirled idly in my fingers as I wondered what the actual reality of liking me meant for him. It’s not that I really felt one way or another about gay folks, but I couldn’t really recall any other I’d ever met. At least, none that I knew were gay when I met them. Were gay crushes like straight crushes? Did he think about… *Kissing* me?

Recalling the way he’d stared at my mouth, I realised *yes, he definitely thinks about kissing me.* I felt a flush of my own rise to my cheeks as I pretended to flip through my textbook for an answer. *Maybe there was once a parchment among his things that had “Draco Potter” in big bubble letters with little love hearts all around it.*

The idea of him doodling our names together did a number of inconsistent things to me. At first, I started to chuckle under my breath but picturing the words *Draco Potter* snagged my laughter in my throat so it came out as a strangled sort of cough, which I covered with a hand. Now it was my turn to be overly sensitive to him looking at me.

He was better at ignoring me, though, because I couldn’t help but glance back at him as I slowly lowered my hand from my mouth. Malfoy quirked an eyebrow at me and I, feeling like an utter prat, just stared back.

*“What?”* he mouthed at me, and I felt like I’d just pressed my cheeks between two sides of a panini press.

“*Nothing,*” I mouthed back, furiously nailing my attention back onto my work.

*Bloody hell, what have I gotten myself into?*

---

Harry was acting… Strange. I didn’t know what to make of it, but if the git was going to request I act *‘normal,'*(whatever the hell that meant) then he was doing a bang-up job doing the same. He didn’t speak to me all through morning lessons, and come lunch, I *tried* not to run from him again as we all left for the Great Hall. But forcing my legs to walk at a ‘normal’ pace was proving excruciating.

He, thankfully, didn’t catch up to talk to me. Didn’t call for my attention or shoot me any parting remarks. And I was gratefully alone at the Slytherin table before anymore of his curious behaviour could twist my brain around itself.

Halfway through delicately picking at the Shepherd’s Pie I’d selected for myself, I happened to glance up to see a weird interaction with Harry and that ginger girl he’d been going out with. Seemed she’d pulled him aside from his meal and was having some manner of conversation with him beneath one of the windows. Even from across the Hall, I could see the desperation in her body language, and the discomfort in his. And just as I was taking another curious bite, she *leaned in and kissed him on the cheek.*

My mouthful slid thickly down my throat as I watched her retreat. Harry seemed too stunned to move, which I thought odd. Hadn’t they been going out for nearly a year now? He rubbed at his cheek uneasily before returning to his seat, and I only realised I’d been staring when he happened to glance up and catch my eye. Instantly, my attention was back on my meal as I felt my cheeks warm.

*Acting aside, this term is gonna be unbearable regardless. Fucking hell, if only Lockhart hadn’t thought he could go up against the Chosen One and landed himself in St. Mungo’s, I could’ve asked him to use some of that memory magic to wipe the git out of my fucking head!*

I angrily devoured the rest of my meal, cursing my circumstances and myself. Falling for a straight boy wasn’t a trivial issue, but it wasn’t the worst one. *It just had to be Harry fucking Potter!*

There really was nothing for it. He knew, and if everyone’s avoidant behaviour was any indication, he hadn’t told anyone, and all that stood in the way of me getting out of this damn place was passing my NEWTs like the rest of the eighth years. I could focus on that if it meant escaping the memories that littered these hallways.

*And those damn green eyes.*
