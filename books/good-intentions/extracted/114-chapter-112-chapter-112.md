## Chapter 112: Chapter 112

Chapter Notes

All through our shared shower, through breakfast, I was here and there and *everywhere.* Torn in *thirds*, here with Draco, in the art room with my unfinished gift for him, and still in bed on top of an *adorably* giggly fucking blonde. *Christ,* I was so in love with him, I wanted to *eat him alive.*

As distracted as I was, I didn’t miss Draco’s anxiety. He was nearly ripping his hair out, so often was he running a hand through it, and I was sort of pleased and *surprised* that I could pick up on his moods while being as dense as I was *and* as distracted. Didn’t really need to ask why, though, because after breakfast, he made some hasty excuse about needing to ‘pop out for a bit,’ and I grinned to myself. *Back to Hogsmeade then? Guess he found something he liked yesterday.*

With a parting kiss (or two... maybe three), he left, giving me the *perfect* excuse to work on *his* gift. Luna had given me some really good pointers on how to use the paints I’d selected, and I already had the background done. This time, though, I stopped by the library to borrow a book that would help me with the… *Details.*

I was probably more worried about the outcome than I should’ve been. I *knew* Draco would fucking *swoon* no matter what it looked like, because it’d be coming from *me,* and it was his favourite fucking sea creature. As I sat before the easel Luna’d shown me how to use, canvas set up and accoutrements strewn around me, I tried to shake off my stress. And withdrew the book to flip to the appropriate page.

*Fucking Marmites.* I smirked down at the illustration, because without even reading the description, I knew they were one of the more unexciting magical sea creatures. There was a pretty clear reason why he loved them so much, despite their boring aspects and abilities, because they were… Honestly *really* pretty. Like little ghostly, glowing squids.

My smirk eased into a genuine smile as I began spreading out my paints on a palette. The same whites, blues, and purples as yesterday, but with the book open in my lap, I had a clearer plan for how to portray them. I was only attempting *one,*and it’d hopefully turn out fairly accurate, but as I selected a wide brush and dipped it into the white, I decided to lean more towards abstract than accurate.

Art wasn’t even *close* to a forte of mine, let alone a *talent,* but I silently reminded myself he’d love it no matter what. Besides, didn’t the saying go *‘beauty is in the eye of the beholder’?* I took it to mean that art was *subjective,* so I didn’t hesitate… *Too* much, as I touched my brush to the surface I’d left blank in the centre.

*Fuck me, the things I’m willing to do for this man.*

---

After a quick pitstop in our room to bundle up more, I was out in the daylight in record time. This was the last day of the last weekend before the holidays after all, and even if the ring I’d glimpsed lacked a twin, I was determined to at least *look.* Maybe the antique store we’d dipped into briefly had *other* options.

Ambling down an alley towards the main strip, I was just recalling the *stack* of Muggle money we’d both left untouched in his nightstand cabinet when pain *exploded* on the back of my head, and everything went dark.

The next thing I knew was… *Also* pain. Back of my head, neck, shoulders, wrists, tailbone, *fuck,* I was a *wreck.* A grimace pulled at my face as I took stock of what I could sense. I was sitting down, arms bent behind the back of the chair I was in, wrists *screaming* from their bindings and *sounds…*

“Look who finally decided to join us.” A voice. I squinted into dim light, pulled my head up from where it’d been drooped to peer at my surroundings. And the reality of my situation suddenly caught up to me.

My head was *swimming* with pain, but I managed to make out seven… No, *eight* people, in a large room with no windows and only one door. Long, pitiful lamps hung parallel to the ceiling, suspended on wires and a grimy sort of fluorescence that implied *Muggle.* The entire *room* was sort of grimy, strewn with tables and stools and *people.* Standing to approach me, all presenting what I assumed they intended to be *malice.* A scoff coughed out of my lips as my head rolled back.

“Not like I had much of a choice,” I decided to say. My mouth tasted sour, *fuck,* my head hurt. I tried my bindings, and was only rewarded with a *flash* of pain that made me hiss. A set of echoing footsteps approached me, and I swivelled my head back around to appraise the person who now crouched before me, wearing amusement.

“Sarcastic as ever, *Malfoy.”* Something about this man’s face was familiar to me, but I couldn’t place it. “Let’s see how long you can keep it up.”

A sneer pulled at my lips, one I hadn’t worn in… Shit, I couldn’t even *remember.*

“The fuck do you *want?”* I demanded, shifting my shoulders to try to ease the ache of their prolonged position. *How long have I been out…?*

He snorted. “Isn’t that obvious?” It was then I noted the Dark Mark on his exposed forearm, sleeves pushed up to his elbows as he leaned them on his knees. And as I cast my dizzy attention back up at our… *Audience,* my eyes fell on a familiar face towards the back. Sat on a stool, foot propped up on its footstand and teeth worrying a finger as he avoided my gaze… Another scoff pushed out of me.

*Fucking Theo.*

“Glaringly,” I directed right at him, and he pulled his hand away from his mouth. But still *wouldn’t look at me.* So I sent my smirk back to the semi-familiar man before me. “Bit late for *revenge,* isn’t it? Dark Lord’s *dead,* or hadn’t you heard? Unless you’ve got some Dark Magic than can *bring someone back from the–”*

I was interrupted as another figure shot towards me out of my peripherals and landed a *solid* hit against my jaw. My neck *snapped* to the side with the impact, head ringing with pain as I tasted blood.

*Shit.*

“And *whose* fault is that?” Another voice. Accusatory, *spitting* rage, but unfamiliar. “Your fucking *boyfriend’s,* that’s who.”

“Easy, Mannen. You’ll have your turn.” I spat the blood that’d welled in my mouth, and groaned in pain as my chin was seized. Made to look back at the man crouched before me, and scowled. “You see, some things… Aren’t adding up.” I jerked my chin out of his grasp, wincing as my jaw *burned* from the punch I’d been dealt. But he released me. “Do you know what the Ministry did to us *minors* after the war?”

I sent another glance up around those attending, recognition sinking into me as more than a few faces registered. But the one before me went on.

“We weren’t of age to be tried like our *parents.* So instead of sentencing us to *Azkaban,* they did something *even worse.”* *Worse than Azkaban? The fuck is he on about?* I frowned, and his expression dipped into something I wanted to call *loathing.* “They *stripped us of our wands.* Made us no better than fucking *Squibs.”* He spat on the floor at my feet. And my heart sank into my *gut* as he delved a hand into his vest, and withdrew– “So *why,* pray tell… Were you allowed to keep *yours?”*

My eyes danced along the length of hawthorn he braced between his hands. Twirled it in his fingers as panic crept up my spine.

“I was pardoned,” I said softly, unwilling to let my voice waver. *Years* of abuse, courtesy of good old dad, were steadily adding cards to my hand as I measured the extent of my disadvantage. How I could worm my way out of *this* one.

“Why.” Not a question. Not like he *didn’t know.*

“My mother–”

“Your *mother,”* he interrupted with a sneer, and lowered himself to his knees. “Did quite a lot of damage, or didn’t you know?” My frown returned, because I *knew* she’d pleaded for my pardon, must’ve made quite a good argument, but– “Gave up *every name in the book* for you. Precious little *Draco.”*

He traded my wand into one hand, and touched the tip under my chin. My heart *lurched* as I let him tilt my head back. *She ratted out the rest of the Death Eaters in exchange for my pardon?* It was the first I’d heard of it, but as a swallow forced itself down my stretched-out throat, it *made sense.*

*Fucking hell.*

“What’s that got to do with me?”

My voice came out fairly steadily, despite the *death* pressing at my throat, and the man before me sneered. Withdrew my wand and stood, pacing away with a sigh like this was *painful.* And I found my attention unerringly drawn back to Theo. He’d stood, too, and was back to worrying his nail. Only now he was *staring* at me.

“Bit *serendipitous* for you to wind up with the Chosen Boy after everything went to *shit,”* he spat, and whirled back towards me. *“Sources* tell us you’d been *pining* after the little shit for *years.”* A snicker echoed through the crowd as Theo stiffened in my peripherals. But as their leader, I presumed, closed in on me again, wand still in his hand, my attention was for him alone. “Tell us, *Draco…”* He reached his free hand for my hair, fisted it and forced my head back to look up at him as he bent over me. Another wince flashed across my face as I met his ire. “Did you, perhaps *plan* on the Dark Lord’s failure?”

*Did I plan on it? Has anything* ever *gone to plan?* I scoffed again, and felt the skin of my wrists tear as I tried my bindings again. Warmth spilled down my hands.

*“Plan?* You give me too much credit.” This answer didn’t seem to please my interrogator as he thrust my head away like it *disgusted* him. Backed away and sent a glance at the other man who’d hit me. *“Hoped,* though,” I added in a mumble.

Like the *brute* I took him to be, this slightly familiar man doubled back towards me to send a heel between my legs. Knocked the *breath* out of me as I folded over, *nauseating* pain wracking my body as I choked on a sob.

*They’re going to kill me.*

“Bully for you, mate, cheers,” he scoffed, but I was *swimming* in agony. “Got everything you wanted, didn’t you? Mudbloods liberated, Dark Lord *dead*, got the *guy…*” An incredulous huff of laughter as I managed to scowl up at him. And he spread his arms wide towards me. “Everything’s coming up *Malfoy,*innit?”

One hand still gripping my wand, he brought it before him to fist it in both hands, and my stomach *convulsed* as he snapped it in two.

*No.*

*No, fuck.*

*Shit.*

“There,” he said, tossed the two halves onto the floor between us. And I stared down at them. The *halves* of my fucking *wand. No, nononono…* “Now you’re just like us.”

He stepped over the discarded hawthorn to lean a foot against the seat of my chair, and I spread my legs instinctively as my shoulders pulled back against it. *Narrowly* avoiding another heel to the balls as he leaned over his knee on an elbow towards me. *Grinning.*

“Welcome to the *fucking* club,” he spat, shoved my chair away as he pushed off it. “Someone would like a *word* with you.”

He paced back into the room, and my head was just *pounding.* Jaw burning, wrists *screaming,* and my nethers weren’t in too great a shape, either, as who should step into the light but Theo fucking Nott. Hesitant, but *angry.* Definitely angry.

A flash of *regret,* though, as he eyed my harm. *No fucking way. No, it’s not possible. He can’t possibly still feel that way about me.* But as he came to kneel before me, his betrayal won out as *rage* leaked over his expression.

“You’re such an *idiot,”* he began. “Why *him?* Of *all* the fucking people, Draco…” *Oh my fucking god. He* does… Theo reached out a hand and it was then I glimpsed the glint of metal in his fist as he sliced a clean cut down the centre of my chest. Barely grazing my skin, and then flipped my jumper open. “Just *look!* Look at what he *did to you.”*

I didn’t. I just breathed. *Pitied* him, as his mournful fury flashed between my chest and my eyes.

“I know, I was there,” I croaked. Swallowed. *He’s gonna kill me. They’re gonna make him kill me.*

*“Fuck,* Draco, you… You can’t *seriously–”*

“I’m in love with him,” I cut him off. And he sobered slightly. “Always have been. Always will be. There’s nothing you can do about it. *Any* of you can do about it, so if you’ve got some *point* to make, then *make it.”*

Theo’s expression screwed up. Not quite disgust, not quite a *grimace,* but more like… Like *pain.*

*Shit, they’re gonna make me break my promise.*

“You *love* him?” Theo’s breath started coming more urgently, more *unhinged* as he adjusted his grip on his blade. And I shifted against the seat I was in, *shit, shit, fuck, no, not like this, not like* this…! “He fucking *killed–* And you… You’re in *love–??”* He rose to his feet, fucking *heaving,* and I tried my bindings again. Winced, again, as they cut deeper into my wrists, but this couldn’t be *it.*

*I'll never let them take me from you,* I'd said.

Pure *wheezing,* Theo lifted the knife in his hand back up between us, this *deranged* fucking agony in his eyes as he aimed it at me. And everything in me went still.

*Harry’s gonna paint this room with their blood and raze the planet to cinders.*

“You love your *precious Saviour* so much, you can fucking *match him!”*

My eyes slid shut as he lunged at me. And sent one final, silent apology to my entire heart.

*Nothing ever happens like I expect it will.*
