## Chapter 93: Chapter 93

“Draco Lucius Malfoy,” Shacklebolt began, earning a wince as he used my full name, *again*. I stiffened in the single chair at the centre of the room as dozens of eyes appraised me, and all at once felt that same skin-crawling *exposure* I’d felt the last time I was here. “You’ve been summoned… Just to check in.” He smiled at me, leaned over his podium and laced his fingers together. “How’ve you been?”

I expected this, honestly. After last time, I’d learned just how *amenable* a creature this new Minister was, but it was no less unsettling to witness the second time.

“F-fine, Minister,” I croaked. Mother shifted in her seat in my peripherals, but I’d already granted her a glance. And now my focus was *dedicated* to Shacklebolt.

“Excellent,” he adulated, clapping his hands together like it actually *pleased* him that I’d been doing well. “Might I borrow your wand?”

My skin went cold, by no fault of the ventilation in the room as I shakily delved a hand into my pocket.

“O-of course.” My fingers gripped hawthorn as I pulled it free, and an auror stationed at the front of the stand approached to deftly pluck it from my fingers. Felt like she’d plucked my *stomach* out of my gut as she turned to approach the podium again, and held it aloft to the Minister.

“Much appreciated, let’s just take a quick gander.”

I swallowed, lowered my twitching hand back to my lap as he pulled out a *Wander.* *A fucking Wander. Is it that serious…?* The cylindrical instrument slid against his stand and he gently placed my wand down into it. And it was at *that fucking moment* I recalled all the… The *prepping spells* I’d used recently.

*Jesus Christ.*

A roll of parchment began feeding out of it towards Shacklebolt as the device read all my most recent spells. And I sank into my chair, elbow finding the armrest to offer my hand up to my brow. My cheeks were on fucking *fire* as his eyes danced over the various spells I’d cast.

“Oh,” he said. Brows shooting skyward as he read. I wanted to fucking *die.* Wanted the floor to open up beneath me and *swallow* me as he fed the parchment towards himself. *Christ, this is so much worse than I ever could’ve imagined.* “Wow.”

Kingsley cleared his throat as he continued pulling the parchment towards him, and I couldn’t meet *any* of the fucking eyes on me. *Especially* not mother’s. My heart was racing in my throat, in my fingertips, in my fucking *ears* as the Wander poured out more of my recent, erm… *History.*

“Oh, you’ve cast a Patronus! Congratulations!” I still couldn’t look up at him, humiliated as I was, but managed to keep my leg from bouncing as he continued down the list.

I hadn’t cast anything *unsavoury,* so I hoped against hope that my wand would be returned to me, but the more I thought about it… Yeah, losing my wand had been traumatic. But knowing it’d been *Harry* using it… When he’d returned it to me, I wanted to fucking *cry* the second my fingers touched hawthorn, but the fact that he’d *given me his first–*

“Well, I don’t see anything here meriting further questioning.” He plucked my wand out of the Wander as I glanced back up at him, lowered it down to the same auror, and she frowned at me. Hesitated, and then strutted over to give it back. I breathed a little easier, fingers gripping the wood slightly tighter than necessary, and the auror returned to her post. “You’ve been… *Getting along* with your peers, then?”

I winced as I took his meaning, but straightened in my seat to respond.

“Y-yes, Minister,” was my subdued answer.

*God, the Minister of Magic knows I have frequent sex. Please, please just let me die.*

“Good, good.” He cleared his throat again, seized the entirety of the Wander’s product and *Incendia’d* it. My eyes went wide as the flames danced before his eyes. “Apologies for pulling you out of school. Minerva?”

The witch in question rose from the stands, hands politely folded before her as she waited.

“I appreciate you coming on such short notice. You shouldn’t expect this again,” a curt glare back at the attending Wizengamot before he continued. “But your flexibility is much appreciated.” She dipped her head fractionally, and my attention snapped back onto the Minister as he extinguished the burning page before him. He met my eyes for a brief second, something like *wonder* shining in them, before he declared: “Dismissed.”

*That’s it…?*

Hours later, I was curled up in the library, in *Draco’s chair,* holding a tome to my chest as I wrangled with my emotions. It hadn’t been difficult to find a book he’d read before about magical sea creatures, but it was stiff and pointy in my arms when all I wanted was *warm and soft.*

*Draco.*

The anxiety was still running circles around my brain, but at the very centre sat an *ache.* An ache the book couldn’t ease, the trinkets in my drawer couldn’t ease, even his *smell* on my pillows. I’d tried these first, but it was in the library that I finally fell apart.

*God, I hope he’s okay.* It felt unnatural not to seek him out, to let the tether between us pull me to his side, but I’d told myself to *trust McGonagall* and I could just as easily agonise in the library as I could in bed. Nearly every three minutes, though, I had to talk myself out of tearing tooth and nail to the Ministry just to make sure Draco was alright.

*McGonagall wouldn’t let anything bad happen to him. He hasn’t done anything wrong.* I winced as my teeth broke skin, worrying my lip as I reluctantly recalled all he *had* done wrong before this term started. And I started the process all over again, telling myself there was nothing I could do that McGonagall couldn’t, that he was safe in her presence, that I’d only get in trouble for leaving school midday. It wasn’t even lunch yet, and yet there I sat, curled up in his chair with a fucking book in my arms.

I waited like that all morning, vacillating between anxiety and pure fucking *terror* until the lunch bell actually chimed. Didn’t really have much of an appetite, so I had no plans of getting out of this chair, but my spiralling was interrupted when I was approached.

By the *entire fucking year.*

“Harry,” Hermione said softly as she came to kneel before me. Ron hung back, hands in his pockets as the rest of our peers appeared. A hand on my ankle as my wide eyes accounted for my audience. “You alright?”

Just like that, I was on the brink of fucking *tears.* If they pulled him out of school, god forbid, if they’d changed their mind and sent him to *prison–* I shook my head, tears welling in my eyes as I clung to the book. Hermione glanced back at the rest of them before leaning forward to pull me into her arms.

“Whatever you need, mate,” Ron began as I fell to *pieces* in Hermione’s arms. I glanced up at him, tears pouring down my face. “Just tell us what to do.”

*What can be done?* I buried my face in her curls, sobs wracking my shoulders as I hitched in gasping breaths. Still, seeing *everyone* show up to help gripped my quaking heart in a fist. *They care about him, too,* I realised. Because even if it was only *me* coming apart at the seams in his absence, here they all were, waiting on *me* to give them an order. To make a choice. And my stomach *lurched* at the distance that’d been stretched between us.

“We’re going to the Ministry, right?” Ginny supposed. *Ginny.* I tore my face out of Hermione’s shoulder to peer up at her, and the arms around me eased. Released me as she glanced back at the redhead, too.

“Last time we broke in, there was weeks of planning involved. Potion brewing, schematics, failsafes. We don’t have that sort of time,” Ron answered, frown pinching his brow as Ginny glanced back and forth between us.

“Well we don’t have to *break in,*” she insisted, eyeing the rest of the group. “You *really think* it’ll be *that hard* for us to just *walk in?”*

Neville tipped his chin to the side as if to say *fair point,* and I happened to catch an awkward glance between Seamus and Dean. But it was Luna who answered.

“Don’t think there’s much we can do anyway,” she supposed, eyes trained on me. “If the Wizengamot deems him a threat and we break him out, we’ll all be on the run. Not that I find that *likely,* but it’s not like a group of teenagers are above the law.”

A sob coughed out of me as she spoke my worst fears into existence. Hermione winced, and I suddenly couldn’t sit still anymore. Wrenching myself from the chair, I stepped around her as she rose onto her feet, and I didn’t even fucking *know* what I was going to do, where I was going, but if Kingsley could be reasoned with, I had to try. The group parted for me as I wove through them, back down the aisle towards the door to the library and they were hot on my heels. Unsure as I was what I would do, just knowing they would back me up had my steps pacing slightly faster.

*If I have to voluntarily be held in contempt for coming to his rescue, so be it. It wouldn’t be the first time, anyway, and at least this time it’s a choice I’m making for myself.*

I swiped my tears from my cheeks with a sleeve as I imparted the book still in my grip on a table as we passed, determined to find a way to fix this. If it was the last thing I’d ever fucking do, I was getting Draco back.
