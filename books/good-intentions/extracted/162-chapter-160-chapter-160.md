## Chapter 160: Chapter 160

Chapter Notes

Regret for even daring to *remember* my childhood piano lessons had me groaning as Harry dragged me through the halls of the villa after breakfast. Of course, I’d do *anything* for him, but this particular favour set a scowl firmly on my brow as I recalled Mrs. Fortesque, the *horrid* old bitch who’d taught me to hate the instrument. Could almost see her waspish face behind my eyelids as I let Harry lead me to the gallery.

“Oh come *on,* I’m not gonna rap your knuckles, drama queen,” he tutted as he let us through the doors. With the sunlight glittering through the windows, the art and instruments all propped up against the walls that echoed his voice back to us, he sent me that *manipulative,* doe-eyed pleading expression, and I rolled my eyes with a sigh.

“Harry, it’s been *years.* You’re lucky I love you so much, but don’t get your hopes up.”

His face broke into a grin as he released my hand, drifting to the grand piano I’d had the pleasure of cleaning my own blood off of more than once. Fingertips sliding across the glossy, erected top board as I reluctantly approached the bench.

“It doesn’t have to be anything fancy, I just want to hear you *play.”*

“Yeah, yeah,” I grumbled to myself, recalling a flash of pain across the back of my hand that had me rubbing them together. Harry wandered around the instrument, appreciating the exposed strings and sound board as I forced my hands to release each other. Reached for the fallboard flat over the keys and hesitantly flipped it up.

It wasn’t one of the more traumatic memories from my life, but I could almost *feel* the shadow of my father’s presence lingering around the room, dimming it slightly with his judgement, his carrot and stick of approval and punishment. Felt like a fucking fool for ever yearning for that carrot, but as my fingers touched ivory for the first time since, my attention snapped up to Harry skeptically as he waited. Patient, eager, *smiling.*

With another sigh (that likely wouldn’t be my last), I pulled out the bench and stepped around it. Settled down onto the leather, sort of *braced* for an impact I knew wouldn’t come but I couldn’t help it. My fingers found the A minor position most comfortably, and I sent Harry one last glance like a silent *must I?* But he wasn’t waiting to correct mistakes, likely didn’t even have the background to recognise them if I *made* mistakes, and as I tried to recall a single piece in A minor, I just… Let go of any expectations for myself.

Music shelf empty, no *prescription* for what to play, I felt pretty out of my depth, but it was… Oddly *freeing* to just gently press on the sustain pedal and let my fingers dance up the scale to let the ear adjust to the key. Clear, crisp notes rang into the acoustic room, and Harry’s heart fluttered in his chest just to hear the tonic chord laid out.

*So easily pleased,* I smirked up at him, and he pinked slightly. Drawing up the scraps of music theory that lingered in the roots of my mind, I set an arpeggiating foundation with my left hand and just sort of… Made something up with my right on the spot. Knew enough about chords and cadences to clumsily assemble something so similar to *music.*

Tonic, subdominant, dominant, I led the sombre minor into hopeful major, and felt another flurry of *wonder* in Harry’s heart. Felt like I was *playing* *his heart,* hammers falling into his chest instead of the strings within the instrument’s guts. And I sort of wondered how much control I could wield over that stuttering rhythm with just music. So I broke away from the repetitive arpeggios in my left hand as I dragged the melody down towards the lower end of the keyboard.

Dark, heavy chords to offset the hopeful beginning, and I was suddenly reminded of the song that’d played when I swept Harry across the floor at the soirée dansante. It hadn’t been in a minor key, but I let some of it influence the sound I constructed and wondered if he recognised it. If I was recalling and reconstituting it accurately enough that he *could.*

Right at the cusp of a cadence, I teased him with a sudden misdirection and felt his heart *tighten* in his chest. Just a pitch or two lower than where I’d been leading the ear, and smirked up at him as I wove the melody back up to the middle of the keyboard. Somehow, with nothing more exciting than a deceptive cadence, I had him in the palm of my hand.

It was sort of *fascinating* to hear what I composed through his ears simultaneously as I played. Like a subtle filter over my own mind as I finally let myself return to tonic. My fingers were slightly looser now, having warmed to the keys and as I surprised both Harry *and myself* by deciding to undertake a *B section,* something subtle in me... *Opened.* I was sort of just curious to see how far I could take it, as out of practice as I was, as *easily pleased* as my rapt audience was, but then and there, for the first time in my fucking *life,* I think, I actually *played.*

These weren’t rehearsed patterns, memorised passages or regurgitated drills, this was *music.* Created out of thin air, given form and breath and *life,* and as my right hand reached up to the higher end to decorate a little echo of the motive I’d invented, a giddy little chuckle burst out of me. Who the fuck did I think I *was,*fucking *Mozart?* Composing on the spot and crafting chord progressions based purely on where my ear wanted me to go, fucking *embellishing* and expanding on the theme I’d just pulled out of my arse not moments ago.

I felt rather silly, but not in the *put-on-the-spot* sort of way I’d always felt sitting before these keys. More like… How *Harry* had made me *snort* with laughter as he’d dressed himself up in *ridiculous* amounts of robes and furs and jewellery in the vault, twirling and mocking my father and the *instant* I realised I was *having fun playing piano,* I–

My fingers stopped. I sort of froze, the last chord lingering in the air as my eyes were drawn up to Harry. And seeing the plain *awe* on his face, I realised what I’d just *done.*

After *years* of not going anywhere *near* a piano, it’d all just… Come back to me. Not the pieces I’d learned, but the *feeling.* And it forced a swallow down my throat as the room finally went silent to consider that I might’ve had a *knack* for music, might’ve pursued it myself if I hadn’t been taught to *hate* it from such a young age. Really *recognising* that Harry had, without even *meaning* to, given me back something I believed my father had *ruined,* and my throat tightened.

“Wow,” Harry finally breathed. I felt a corner of my lips twitch to hear that word on his tongue *again,* but as I glanced back down at my hands, I realised they were trembling ever so slightly. Fisted them and lowered them to brace against the bench as he drifted around the piano to my side. “You’re *amazing.”*

I sent a dubious glance up at him, scooted aside to let him sit with me and scoffed.

“Wasn’t *that* good, you’re just *easily impressed.”* He jostled my shoulder with his, and while he wasn’t exactly a *music critic,* his praise still brought a pleased flush to my face. And it was at that moment a quiet sound drew my attention over my shoulder to the door.

Of course, live piano had summoned my mother, and she stood in the doorway with… With *tears* in her eyes. Hands wrapped together against her chest as Harry followed my gaze. And I was all at once reminded how I’d *never* heard music in our house until father was gone. His absence had allowed something in her to be reborn, and *Harry…* Harry had watered a seed so deeply rooted in *hate* with *enough love* to make it flourish into something completely *new.*

“That was… *Beautiful,* Draco,” mother adulated quietly. And I swallowed as Harry’s attention returned to me. Pure *beaming* as I felt my cheeks burn hotter.

“Th-thanks, mum.”
