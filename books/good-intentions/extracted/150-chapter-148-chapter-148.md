## Chapter 148: Chapter 148

I’d probably end up regretting it, but so long as we were being *unbelievably* sappy, I decided to show Harry a room I’d avoided before. It wasn’t locked; I’d just conveniently ‘*forgotten’*about that end of the hallway during our tour. The hallway he now followed me to, hand in mine and some tune I didn’t recognise playing in his head.

Just before the door, I hesitated. Twisted to eye him, and he just waited with a smile. Blithely curious to see *anything* I wanted to show him, but he didn’t even *know…* With a sigh, I twisted the handle and led him through.

Predictably, he *melted* at what was tucked away here. The first thing he approached was the crib, and I released his hand to scratch behind my ear.

“Oh my *god,”* he breathed, eyes drawn to the shelves of toys and children’s books beside it. And I cleared my throat, pocketing my hands as he pawed through them.

“After the, er… *Nursery* was upgraded into my bedroom, all the leftover shit was shoved in here. Gave my mum like, what… Four years of me to herself? And then it was *father’s house,* father’s rules, grooming me for polite society as the heir to the Malfoy estate.”

I could tell Harry was distantly listening to me, but he was just *enraptured* with all my stuffed animals and childhood toys. So I shut up, cocked my head to the side as I watched him thumbing through the spines of books, delicately selecting the very same little toy dragon featured in the photo album. Reverently held it to himself and when he turned to look back at me, his eyes were glistening.

“N-never had stuff like this growing up,” he said quietly, and addressed his next words down to the dragon. “Made my own toys out of… *Rubbish* and… Told *myself* stories whenever I–”

Flashes of a little blond baby danced across Harry’s mind, holding the toy he now held, and I felt my throat tighten. Knowing he’d dreamt of *me* in his pitiful little cupboard as children made me want to hunt down his aunt and uncle and do *despicable* things to them, to do everything they’d ever done to Harry back to them a *hundredfold,* but the hatred for them that welled in me popped like a bubble as something in Harry’s imagination *pricked* it like a needle.

That little blond baby turned its face up to Harry in his head, and its eyes were *green.* Emerald green, just like his. Smiling and giggling and reaching its chubby little arms out like it wanted to be picked up. The image faded away before I could come up with anything to say, any *thought* could form.

Hesitantly now, Harry looked back up at me. An apologetic little smile playing about his lips as the lingering impression of *our child* burned itself into my eyelids.

“Sorry, I…” He swallowed, set the toy back on its spot on the shelf and cleared his throat. “I-I’d be one *paranoid* fucking f-father, I just know it.”

*He’d be a perfect father* assembled in my head before I could censor myself. And Harry went stiff. *Me, on the other hand…*

The spot in *his* head labelled ‘father’ was decidedly blank, but my own was… Corrupt. Like a great bloody iron nail had been hammered into me over time, the association with the concept was like rotten *blood*. Like a stain, a curse, a wound that’d never scarred. Only festered and infected, *father…* Even the *idea* of lending myself to that title felt like stepping straight into that pool of filth, made my *skin crawl,* and it was only when Harry’s warm hands closed on my cheeks that I realised I’d started to *panic.*

“Hey,” he said softly, and I blinked down at him. When had my breathing turned so *uneven?* My panic was reflected in his eyes, in the crease of his brow, and I tried to swallow. “Hey, you’re alright.”

A *flood* of warmth washed over me, and I let my eyes shut for a moment as it washed away what leaked out of that *wound* in me. It was still leaking, just a steady drip of *dread,* but Harry’s grip on my face tensed slightly, and I sucked in a gasp to feel that warmth pour *into* that wound. Garish and ragged and *ugly,* and I wanted to *stop him,* to prevent him soiling himself with that *disgusting* fucking stain, but he was flooding himself into it so purposefully, so *precisely*that I was helpless to do anything but stand there and take it.

Hands on my face angled it down to touch my brow to his, and I found my arms just in time to take his wrists. Held them, screwed my eyes shut and tried to *breathe.* *Jesus,* it wasn’t *fair* that he was able to access even the darkest corners of me, like a surgeon prying me open and flooding out the infection, it fucking *hurt.* But the more he urged into that injury, the more I realised it’d been aching *this whole time.* Since before I ever met him, it’d been bleeding *hatred* into me, and while his *all-consuming* presence in my life had nearly made me *forget* it was even *there,* it…

Warmth spilled down my cheeks as flashes of *Lucius Malfoy* were dragged out of me like a montage. My grip on the wrists in my hands tightened, because this was something *no one* should see. No one but me, because it was *my burden* to carry, my ache, my *disease.* The sneer I’d adopted, the unattainable approval, the near-constant *disgust* and *disappointment,* every instance of him raising a hand to me and I *pleaded* for it to stop.

*No more, please,* but Harry was *adamant.* *Determined* to find the root of this poisonous plant and rip it out of the *soil* of my brain. *Please, stop, please–*

It wasn’t like I *wanted* to keep it, wasn’t *sentimental* or whatever, but just feeling it being split in *two,* letting him *see* the extent of my *damage* was just… Not how I wanted to spend Christmas Day. So he paused. The images flickered to darkness. And my knees gave out.

Harry helped me down to the floor, an apology keening in his chest as he held me, gathered me into his arms as I split *wide* the fuck open. His soul wrapped itself around mine, *pulsing* love and peace and calm into me as I wept.

*Wanna rip him out of you* he said silently, and though that wound named *father* in me wasn’t actively leaking, he’d ripped it wider and it *smarted* like a *bastard.* Harry carefully extracted himself out of it, leaving something like a little *kiss* against it like a plaster, and I breathed a bit easier. A *helluva* lot easier. *Fuck,* I hadn’t even really *acknowledged* how deep that wound went, but to feel it even *halfway healed* was like… Like a lead *weight* off my shoulders. And I found myself wondering how *different* my life would’ve been, how different *I*would’ve been if I’d been born to another man.

*You’d be such a better father than him,* Harry asserted, but I couldn’t help but doubt it. Unless I could *literally forget* all the hardwiring, the *brainwashing* and *manipulation* and *trauma,* if I could become a *different fucking person,* maybe, but otherwise…

*You’ve too much faith in me* was my answer. And the arms around me *squeezed.*

*You learned how not to be a dad by example. More experience than me, I don’t even have a shitty example to compare to.*

Sirius flashed through my mind, just for a *split second,* and Harry winced. I instantly regretted it, and was already sorry, but he–

*It’s alright,* he assured me, even though the wound named *Sirius* tore a little wider in him. I was *fascinated,* because if he could reach the wound named *father…* *Draco, no.*

I speared into him, and it was the first time I’d *ever reached* into him. It was *so strange,* but I was astounded to find the blinding epicentre of his magic near *painful* to behold. Even though I perceived the wound I sought, I was driven out by the sheer *enormity* of his power, back into myself and *gasping.*

*Holy shit.*

Harry *groaned* against my shoulder, and buried his face into my chest.

*Holy shit,* he agreed, and I tried again. Delicately, *hesitantly,* but more prepared this time for the *incredible* intensity of the raw fucking magic that was blistering in his core. Felt like thousand-kilometre winds, like opening a 300 celsius oven, nearly tore the *surface of my soul* off to be in its presence, but I let it wash over me. Bathed in it, let it sink into me, and felt like some of that *wild magic* sank into *me.*

*You– you live like this?* I wondered, *incredulous* as I pressed against the pressure, and he huffed a laugh into my chest.

*Welcome to my life,* and the blistering *storm* sort of… Quieted. Subdued however slightly, and I took it to mean he was *holding back.*Allowing me in, and I ventured further.

Even though soul-to-soul, things like sight and sound were mere *impressions,* his soul was like *liquid light.* Like… Like the fucking *sun,* and I felt a certain amount of *gratitude* for the rings I’d found in our vault. He was the sun. And I was his moon, drinking him in all these years and just now learning how to *reflect.*

Carefully, almost *painfully,* he held himself contained to allow me to reach the *Sirius* wound. It was wide, and fresh, and *leaking,* just like mine had been. And gauging the strain, the *effort* with which he was allowing me in so deep, I delved into it.

I *felt* how it *hurt* to let me in, but he did. And knowing he must’ve felt how it *ached* to push himself into *my* wound… I pressed harder. If he could take it, so could I. And, similarly, flashes of Sirius poured forth. But these were *happy* memories, memories I didn’t want to take from him. A story of a boy and his dead father’s best friend, seeing Harry and *recognising James–*

*Nice one, James!* echoed in my head a heartbeat before I was pushed out. I couldn’t begin to fathom what that meant, but found myself back in my body before I could wonder. Panting and crying, *shaking* in Harry’s arms as he held me.

*Sorry,* he said, and I shook my head.

*Why don’t we resurrect him sometime?*

“No,” he said almost before I could complete the thought, pulling himself out of my arms to take my shoulders and level a *lethal* fucking stare at me. I felt my own eyes go wide as he shook his head. “No, I…” Swallowed. Glanced down at my throat. “I c-*can’t…* I can’t…”

His severe expression fractured as the moisture in his eyes spilled down his cheeks, and I instinctively pulled him back into my arms.

*Alright, we don’t have to,* I allowed. But I felt the *swell* of *anger,* of *pain* that welled in Harry, the *longing,* the *missing…* He missed Sirius so hard it made *me* cry. But he didn’t want to see him…? I couldn’t make sense of it, but resigned myself to it for the time being. We had *forever* to understand each other, after all, and with the soulbond in place, it was an *inevitability.*

He’d inevitably see all *my* damage, and I was just perfectly fucking *eager* to learn all of *his.*

*Never been loved like this.*
