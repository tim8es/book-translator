## Chapter 188: Chapter 188

Chapter Notes

Little by little, the house came alive. Bits and pieces of us added to the generic furnishings it came with. A painting up on a blank spot of wall. A Chudley Cannons poster I only *slightly* protested put up in the study. A vase of lilies on the entryway console. And the more life we breathed into the walls, the more *Harry* seemed to come alive. He’d never had much of *anything* of his own, so now to have an entire *place* of *us* was like spreading *gasoline* onto the candle wick of his soul.

We hired contractors to come take a look at our land, assess it for the potential Harry saw in it and were given a price estimate. It was nothing we couldn’t afford, and they left with a promise to have blueprints by the end of the week. And by then it’d been nearly a month since we’d graduated, which seemed to be the limit of how long his friends could go without seeing him.

“Harry!” Beaming, Hermione leapt forward as soon as the gate swung open for her, and pulled him into her arms. And behind followed a grinning ginger to wrap his arms around the both of them.

“Bloody hell, it’s good to see you two,” he answered. I politely hung back, hands clasped behind me but smiling to see their affectionate display.

“Can’t get rid of us, mate,” Ron chuckled, and then caught my eye over Harry’s shoulder.

“God, your hair’s getting long,” the witch noted as they released each other, and Ron stepped around her to offer me a hand.

“Y-yeah, I… Can’t remember the last time I cut it.”

“Malfoy,” Ron greeted me as we shook hands, just enough teasing glint in his eye to make me smirk.

“Not for much longer.”

Speaking of hair, *his* ginger mop was pristinely tidied on his head, short enough that it gave his features this sort of… *Maturity* I hadn’t expected. It was strange, recognising time had passed. Seeing Harry every day had made the change gradual enough I hadn’t noticed, but even when Hermione turned her blazing smile back at me, there was a certain *sharpness* to her face I wasn’t sure had been there before. It’d only been a month! Was I seeing things…?

“Come in, come in!” Harry led the way back to the front door, and we followed, but not before Hermione had pulled me into my *own* rib-crushing hug. Made my lungs tighten in them to hear her quiet little *‘It’s good to see you, too, Draco,’* and then we were heading inside.

We gave them a tour of the cottage, to the tune of Hermione’s little gasps and *‘aww’s* until finally we found ourselves on the back porch, where I left them to put the kettle on. But not before I’d dropped a kiss onto Harry’s bunched up cheek. His happiness for seeing his friends was *infectious,* and we shared a grin before I retreated inside the house.

I half-listened as I whipped us up some tea (coffee for Harry, of course) and was pleased to hear Ron grudgingly forgive him for withholding the Resurrection Stone. I glimpsed a very approving smile on Hermione’s face through his eyes, though, so I had to assume she’d assisted their reconciliation behind closed doors. And was silently grateful.

After a bit more conversation about an apartment they were looking into, Hermione’s application for a Ministry internship, and a general update on the Weasley clutch, I rejoined them bearing a tray of mugs and accoutrements. They’d pulled the deck chairs into a little semicircle around the tea table and had left a seat for me right next to Harry.

“So, Narcissa said she’d been by,” Hermione ventured as she plucked a cup of tea off the tray and brought it to her lips. Harry and I shared a glance.

“Yeah, she, erm… Had a bunch of questions about the wedding, but we figured we’d leave it to the women.”

He gave a nervous little laugh as he took his coffee, and Ron snorted.

“Right you are.”

He and I took our own tea and reclined back almost in unison, and I was a bit unsettled at the way we mirrored each other, but it was then I realised we’d be standing side-by-side at the end of the aisle. Watching the loves of our lives walk down towards us. And was quietly pleased as I took a sip.

“Well, she and I have been working on a guest list via correspondence, all of our friends and family, but is there anyone you’d like to invite we mightn’t have thought of?”

Harry sipped his coffee contemplatively, and I felt the wound called *Sirius* exhale a gust of cold into his soul, but his face betrayed nothing.

“Hagrid?” he suggested with a shrug. “He’s missed a *load* during his year abroad, but I’m sure he wouldn’t miss it for the world.”

Hermione smiled brightly.

“Way ahead of you. I’ve taken the initiative of adding Minerva to the list, too, I hope you don’t mind.” Harry’s and my eyebrows shot up in unison.

*“Minerva?”* he scoffed incredulously, and Hermione tilted her chin indignantly.

“What? We’re not *students* anymore, we’re *adults.* And adults address each other by their first names.”

Ron’s pale eyebrows lifted to crinkle his brow as he took a hearty sip of tea, like he wasn’t inclined to engage in the topic *at all.*

“R-right, well… That’s… Fine with us,” Harry glanced at me for my agreement, and I couldn’t disagree. She’d clearly stuck her neck out for me with the Ministry to get me to come back, so I couldn’t say she wouldn’t be welcome at our wedding.

*Our wedding.* Such romantic, flowery images answered my internal comment in Harry’s head. And he smiled at me. Reached for me, and I gave him my hand and a smile of my own. Raining flower petals, live music, sunshine and smiles and *warmth.* As corny as it was, I couldn’t help but be *unreasonably* excited.

“Oh, I’m so *excited!”* Hermione agreed, glancing back and forth between us like she was on the verge of *tears.* “You said you wanted to wear something *sparkly,* right Harry? Have you looked into any options? I think I’ve been doing Ginny’s head in with dress-talk.”

A giddy little giggle escaped her as Harry’s bliss came to a screeching halt.

“O-oh, erm… Well, n-not exactly…”

“Harry!” Hermione admonished, set her mug down in her lap and leaned forward seriously, and I bit back a snicker to see Ron roll his eyes affectionately. “It’s only *months* away! Do you even know what colour you want to wear? Are we to match in white? We have to take it into account to correspond with the colour story of the entire *event,* and if you’re wanting something *bold–”*

“I think he’d look dashing in green,” I chimed in, because her rant was starting to make Harry’s head spin, and he *had* looked *incredible* in green when he’d worn it in our dream. Glittering emeralds emblazoned onto his jacket, his tie, *god,* the way it’d brought out his *eyes…*

“Green…” Hermione hummed, eyes going unfocused as a frown scrunched her brow. And at that moment, Ron cleared his throat and leaned forward to set his mug back onto the table.

“Draco, mate, why don’t you show me round the grounds?”

I sent Harry a glance.

*Can I leave you alone with her?*

He gave me a small smirk.

*Guess we’ll find out. Go on.*

---

“You should know, Narcissa sort of *hinted* at the possibility that you two would let us hold the event *here,”* Hermione warned as she sipped her tea.

“I don’t see why not,” I shrugged. As we’d *both kept telling people,* the details hardly mattered. We could have our wedding in the Hog’s Head and we’d be happy. But Hermione fixed me with an incredulous stare.

“Really. Outside? You’d be alright with an *outdoors wedding.”*

I winced. Had that been the wrong thing to say?

“I-I mean, if *you* would, but I w-wouldn’t want to–”

“No, no it’s fine, so long as the *weather*is. Maybe we could put up some canopies…”

She got that calculating far-away look in her eyes again, and my brain sort of *throbbed* in my head as Draco’s question of if he could *leave me with her* echoed in my ears. And I sighed. Stood and rounded the table to claim the seat Ron’d sat in beside her.

“I want you to have the *perfect* day. Ron does, too. The rest is just noise.” Hermione turned a sympathetic smile over at me, and reached out to take my hand. “And I *might*be able to assist with the weather thing.”

A scoff broke on her lips, but her eyes never lost their sincerity.

“You know… Narcissa seems like she genuinely loves you.” I swallowed as she studied me. “Do you… I mean, Draco said you call her *mum…”*

My attention dropped to our clasped hands as I considered Draco’s mum. And I felt him gently attend me as he pointed out where the developers had indicated our orchards would grow best to Ron.

“I… Y-yeah, erm… Sh-she asked me to, but it’s…” I cautiously peered back up at her patient curiosity. “It’s… *N-nice.* She’s a lot more like Molly than I thought she’d be.”

An awkward sort of laugh coughed out of me before I could swallow it back, and Hermione’s lips pressed together into a tight smile.

“You know, I was *worried* there’d be tension at the wedding, what with both mums being there and… What with Molly and-and *Bellatrix–”*

“Andromeda,” I suddenly remembered. “We should invite Andromeda.”

Confusion slacked Hermione’s features for a moment before understanding replaced it.

“Of course. She can bring Teddy.”

The mention of Remus and Tonks’s son wrought a fist around my heart, but I tried to smile.

“Y-yeah. That’d be lovely.”

*Bloody hell,* Draco moaned silently, and I bit back a chuckle.

*Relax,* I shot back. *It’ll be perfect.*
