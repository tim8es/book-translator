## Chapter 183: Chapter 183

Breakfast was quiet. Seemed everyone had slept in like we had, because the majority of students were already heading to class by the time we’d assembled at the Gryffindor table. But none of us had much to say.

After we ate, we began an equally silent procession back up to the dorms to get ready. Pack and clean and prepare to leave. Whether we’d ever return was uncertain, but we’d never return as *students,* and that was enough to make it feel… Sort of *final.*

The group reassembled in the common room, just sort of staring at each other and the furniture like a final goodbye. Hermione’s birthday party seemed to linger in every corner, the echoes of laughter and hoots and whistles as we took each others’ measure. The air was heavy, but there was no reason to linger. Nothing keeping us here anymore.

We *could’ve* stayed and waited out the school year. McGonagall had made it clear that they didn’t need us to vacate the Room of Requirement anytime soon, but I think we all heard the call to the future. A distant song that seemed to sing all our names, beckoning us towards the bright horizon and away from the dark past that clung to the walls of the castle. It hadn’t been easy to return, for any of us if I could guess, but Hogwarts would always feel like a home away from home. And as I slid my hand into Harry’s, he turned bright eyes up at me, and I knew he felt the same.

We led our peers out into the halls. Not rushing, but not wandering. Slowly and with a purpose, we strolled through the castle for the last time. I heard a feminine sniff behind us, indicating one of the women was in tears, but not necessarily feeling any sort of way about it. It was a significant moment, after all.

As we reached the main strip of classrooms that would lead to the courtyard, something unexpected happened. A door opened, a student peered out at us. Suddenly several other students were at her back, forcing the door wider as they watched us leave. First years, wide-eyed with awe as Harry gave a polite little wave. And then another door on the opposite side creaked open. Third years.

Slytherins. Ravenclaws, Hufflepuffs, Gryffindors. Students of all houses and years peeked out of their rooms as we strolled by, until the hall was neatly lined with gawking students. My throat felt a little tight for seeing their astonished observation, and Harry’s hand tightened in mine.

How *terrified* I’d been to return. How mortified to see him again, to face the judgement of my peers, to endure the *scrutiny.* But this, this was… Different. Of course, *everything* about this term had been different than I’d expected, and I assumed our quietly whispering audience now was more so for Harry’s sake than anything else, but of course, there was the rest of the Golden Trio trailing behind us. Them, and the rest of our peers who’d fought and won the battle. They were war heroes, and I was *Harry Potter’s husband.*

A slightly prickly sensation went over my skin as we made our way through the quietly filling halls, all at once feeling out-of-place and *exactly* where I should be. Right by his side, the moon to reflect his blinding sun, and although there still might’ve been those who saw me as unfitting in their company, I couldn’t feel anything other than *right* in Harry’s orbit. He was the central gravity of my entire *world.* Being without him would’ve felt wrong, I reminded myself as I tried to ignore the expressions on the faces around us.

Even a few professors watched us leave. I was just about to start fretting that they’d *applaud* us when who should round a corner but Ginny Weasley herself. Slightly out of breath like she’d run here, and rosy complexion to match. She only paused for a moment before she stepped right into our path, and we paused.

Casting a glance from Harry to me, and he peered up at me like he was asking *permission.* Like I had any say over his actions, but it still warmed my heart to know I was his priority as I released his hand. And the ginger took this as a green light to *launch* herself at him.

“I’m so proud of you, Harry,” she said, voice tight with emotion as I politely stood aside. Sort of grated at my *nerves* to see him in her arms, but I knew this was important. So I packed it in as he cautiously returned her embrace.

“Th-thanks, Gin.” She sniffed and extracted herself from his arms, and I breathed a little easier. “‘M sure you’ll pass, too.”

She smiled sort of sadly, but there was amusement shining in her eyes as I glanced back at Ron and Hermione, the former of which seemed oddly emotional as the latter spared me an assessing glance. Like she was gauging just how long I planned on letting Harry’s ex put her paws all over him. I had to smirk.

“Not just about the NEWTs, idiot,” she cajoled, and Harry cut a short laugh.

“Right. Thanks.” She nodded, and patted his shoulder once more, as if she were about to move on to make the rounds of her other goodbyes, but he stopped her. “F-for everything, Gin. I mean it, you… I-I would’ve *hexed* me a *dozen* times over by now.”

She laughed, voice thick with tears as she sent me another glance. One that was now laced with… Was that *respect?*

“Plenty of time for that,” she teased, and Harry ducked his head with another forced chuckle. Gravitated back to my side to let her pull Ron into another hug, and I took his hand again as we waited.

The youngest Weasley had plenty of parting words for our peers, and as Harry and I politely stood aside to allow it, a peaky-looking second-year Gryffindor stumbled up to us. Wide, brown eyes only for Harry as he seemed to work up the nerve to speak. And we waited patiently.

“Oh, um… Hi there,” Harry began when the boy didn’t immediately talk. His eyebrows shot up in surprise to be spoken to, but then he seemed to finally cough it up.

“Th-thanks for being gay,” he exhaled in a rush. And immediately the pallor of his cheeks filled with blood. “I-I mean, er… That-that’s a weird thing to say, right? I didn’t– I mean–”

Harry chuckled good-naturedly.

“No, no, I… I mean, I don’t really know what to say to that, but… Y-yeah.”

The nameless Gryffindor smiled, shoulders going slack with relief to see Harry was just as awkward and ineloquent as he. And I covered my snicker with a hand, even as Harry cast me a long-suffering glare.

“D’you…? I mean… I-I s’pose I could, like… *S-sign* something or…?”

Instantly, the boy’s grin fell back into shock.

*“Really?”* he nearly shouted, and Harry giggled again. And nodded. “Oh my *god,* yes, *please!”*

The boy fished a bit of parchment and a quill out of his bag slung across his body, and shakily handed them to Harry, who cleared his throat and released my hand to take them.

“Right, er… N-never given an autograph before, heh… Um… What’s your name?”

“Roddy,” he answered quickly, eyes *sparkling* like he’d *swoon* at any second.

“Nice to meet you, Roddy,” he said as he scrawled something in his *horrendous* handwriting on the parchment.

*Not above autographs, I see,* I teased, and he jostled a chuckle out of me with an elbow to the rib.

*“Wow,”* Roddy gushed as he accepted the signed bit of parchment back reverently. “Thanks, Mr. Potter!”

Harry’s spine went rod-straight as he dashed back into the crowd. And it was just then that Ginny concluded her goodbyes. When she came back to the head of the group, though, she didn’t address Harry again. She addressed *me.*

“It’s… *Really* nice to see Harry so happy. I guess you’re to thank for that.” Not the most *enthusiastic* thanks, but I was just astonished that she was thanking me *at all.* “Don’t be a stranger, alright? Just because we used to date doesn’t mean you’re not welcome in… In my life.”

*Fucking women,* Harry quietly commented as Ginny extended a hand towards me. And as I cautiously took it, something like my *own* respect settled into my chest.

“Thanks. I won’t.”

She gave me a nod, a firm handshake, and then stood aside to let us leave. Harry and I exchanged a bewildered glance before we finally did start walking again, but I was still lost in thought about that interaction as we came upon the courtyard finally. Didn’t even realise McGonagall was waiting for us until Harry released my hand again to throw himself into the Headmistress’s waiting arms.

“Congratulations, Harry,” she said, voice similarly thick with tears as she held him to her. *Bloody Christ, how many people are waiting to hug you?*

Harry chortled thickly into the witch’s robes as the rest of our year stepped up to wrap their arms around them as well. *God, another group hug.*

I barely bit back a groan at the *cheesiness,* but just as Luna brought up the rear, she twisted to glance back at me. And *smiled,* twitched her head toward the group in invitation, and I rolled my eyes with a sigh. Let her pull me into it and clamped down on the internal *cringe* at myself.

This was par for the course as a Gryffindor’s *husband,*after all.

---

I Apparated us straight to the stoop of Grimmauld place as soon as we were outside the Apparation wards. Malfoy Manor was too stained with trauma for either of us to tolerate, and we’d have to find an *agent* to find us a bit of land and a cottage suiting both our tastes, but for now, we’d decided to settle in here. At least for the time being.

I couldn’t help recalling the summer I’d spent here as I let us in. Self-isolating and going *mad* in the aftermath of Draco’s confession. I led us upstairs to the bedroom I’d been using – never felt like *my room* – and offloaded all I was carrying. Our trunks were much lighter without all the school supplies, even though it felt so *weird* to be… *Done* with school.

“Well… It’s not much, but… This is it.”

Draco took a slow, observant turn around the room, fingertips trailing over the pictures and desk, the wallpaper, the window. Almost *reverent,* like he knew what this place meant to me. And my fucking *bones* started to itch, because even though these walls had seen some happy memories, the rooms themselves were… Too *empty.*

He came up to me, took my hand in his and watched himself thumb my knuckles.

“I’m… I hate that this… I mean…” Draco’s intention was obvious enough, and I gave his fingers a squeeze.

“It’s alright. We’re only here for a little while, right?”

Finally, he turned his grey gaze up to me, an apology burning behind them. And I tried to smile.

“We should keep the place in your name. Buy out Godric’s Hollow if you want, just… Just find a place of our own.”

*A place of our own,* I repeated internally, taking a measure of comfort from the idea.

“Yeah.”

---

We were just *kids.* Fumbling through the world, but not having to do it *alone…* I held Harry’s hand a bit tighter as we sat across the desk from the real estate agent who was presently carding through files of houses. She was a bit older than us, but not *old.* Clearly recognised who we were, and was taking extra care to find places that fit our criteria.

No cupboards under stairs. No basements. Plenty of windows and light, lots of land, at least four bedrooms. With our considerable combined estates, we could afford it.

“Let’s see here… Alright. I think I have a suitable amount of places to consider.” She stacked the selected files together and tapped them against the desk before presenting them to us. “Just touch the photos, and it’ll give you a virtual tour.”

Harry glanced at me with a swallow before pulling the stack towards us. It wasn’t *enormous,* but he was sort of *overwhelmed* with the amount. And I was somewhat surprised that so many places checked all our boxes. So I helped him open the first one, and touched the photo to set the tour playing on the parchment.

It was a quaint little house, somewhat removed from the nearby neighbourhood, but as the scene panned out, we saw just how close to the other houses it was. And Harry quickly shut the file and pulled the next one towards us.

*Last thing I need is people going to the media saying ‘my neighbour is Harry Potter!’* I couldn’t help but agree as he touched the photo in this second file to set it going.

The seclusion made enough sense, hell I didn’t want to live near people either if I could help it. God only knew what sort of gossip it would spawn to have an *ex-Death Eater* in their wizarding midst. But this place barely had a *garden,* let alone enough land to give his animals room to roam. He didn’t want them for any agrarian purpose, nor just to have *pets.* And I sort of understood why he wouldn’t be satisfied by just a cat or dog.

Indoor pets, at least in *his* mind, existed only for their owners’ amusement. Just to be an object of affection and attention. He wanted animals to give them a *good life.* Like pseudo-children, he wanted them to have access to space and air and sunlight. To take care of them as they made their days as they would. And I couldn’t help but love him a little more for it.

As he shut the file and pulled the next one towards us, I considered this *‘sanctuary’* he wanted to create. Would we fill it with any ill-gotten grouse he happened to find away from their nests? Would he reach out to shelters to take in animals on the kill list? I found it likely as he touched the photo to set it moving.

This one… This one had *land. Acres* of it. And while the price was a fair bit higher than the previous ones, it went on to check an *awful* lot of boxes. My throat went sort of tight to picture our life in this home, fencing in the goats, building a coop for the chickens, a stable for the horses. It could accommodate it all. And Harry flipped the file closed, but didn’t slide it away as he lifted his attention to address *Janine,* our bespoke agent.

“Can we tour this one?”

She smiled, fingers unlacing before her to pluck the file off the desk. Turned it towards herself and opened it to glance at the address.

“A fine choice, Mr. Potter. Of course. Please, do take a gander at the rest in case there’s any else you’d like to see while we’re out in the country.”

Harry pulled the next file towards us. And I still felt like nothing but a *child,* playing at being an adult.

*We can do this,* Harry assured me, and I leaned over to touch my shoulder to his.

*With you, I can do anything.*
