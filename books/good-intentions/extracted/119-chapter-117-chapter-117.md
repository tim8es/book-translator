## Chapter 117: Chapter 117

“Why were your eyes glowing?” Draco quietly wondered several minutes after I’d insisted my way onto the small hospital cot with him. In each others’ arms, it was still a tight fit, but there was just no way I could spend another second doing anything less than touching as much of him as I could. I lifted my head off his chest to frown up at him.

“Were they?” He nodded, fingers running through the hair on the back of my head gently, and I carefully replaced my cheek on his clavicle. “I dunno. Didn’t do it on purpose.”

Draco hummed, turned a kiss to the top of my head, and hissed a wince into my hair. Instantly, I was pulling myself up to study his face, searching for the source of his pain.

“Sorry, my… Neck’s still a bit sore,” he grimaced up at me, and replaced the hand that’d been on my arm for his shoulder.

“Here, sit up.”

Leaving the bed to help him sit up, I invited him forward a bit to sit behind him, and… *Tried* to massage his neck and shoulders. Wasn’t really sure what I was doing, but he groaned like I was doing alright, and let his head fall forward.

*Love,* I recalled the way I’d poured pure love into him and he’d healed. It was what’d saved my life as a baby, and how I’d been able to save *his* life, so I shut my eyes and tried to recall the way it’d felt to let it flow from my heart to my hands. And when I felt his muscles relax beneath them, I blinked up at him again.

“That’s brilliant,” he sighed.

*My hands are glowing.*

Experimentally, I slid them from his shoulders up the back of his neck, and he *groaned.* The same hands that’d been painted in death not *days ago.*

“Wicked,” I breathed, sort of fucking *awestruck* at this new ability I’d learned. Hadn’t even really meant to teach myself, all I’d known was Draco’d been hurt, and I loved him too much to allow his pain. I pulled them back down his shoulders, to his back, and he curled forward easily under my touch. All the way down the length of his spine to his lower back, and tried to push *more* love into him. And they glowed *brighter.*

I could see the shape of my bones, the shadows of my veins through the skin as I worked my way back up to his ribs. Slid around them and pulled him back into me, and he went *limp* against my chest. Curiously, I pulled his shirt up to see how the little slice down his chest was healing, and found a much smaller version of the one I’d left on him. But as I held his shirt up in one hand, the other passed over the tiny scar and it *vanished.*

*Bloody hell.*

I’d never really given much thought to what all I could achieve if I *tried.* Confidence in my abilities had built up over time, so nowadays if I had an idea, I just attempted to do it, but as I felt Draco take a slow, *deep* breath against me, my hand started drifting up to his face. And only then did he lift a hand of his own to take my wrist.

My awe sort of fractured with the smirk that pulled my lips, because I’d been about to heal his forehead, but *obviously* he liked it too much. So I just let him pull my hand away and lift his head to peer at it. Let him splay my fingers and turn it over to run his own over the back of my hand.

“Just like that,” he said quietly. “Your eyes.”

I hummed equally quietly, now holding my other hand firmly over his heart to pump love directly into the throbbing muscle. And it started *racing* beneath it.

“What does it feel like?” I wondered as he laced his fingers through mine. Felt like I was *holding his heart* in my hand as his lungs started working to catch up.

*“H-hurts,”* he whimpered quietly, but just as I was about to pull away from his chest, he pressed his other hand against it and held it there. “Hurts w-*wonderful.”*

My own heart swelled up into my throat as I pressed my lips behind his ear. So solid and warm and *safe* in my arms, I had to shut my eyes against how brightly my hands shone. I forced every last *drop* of love I felt for him into my extremities, and my whole *body* might’ve started glowing, the way I had to squeeze my eyes shut.

I held him as tightly as I dared, feeling the desperate little sobs working out of him and for what felt like *forever,* I let myself be *overwhelmed.*

Early on, my reflexive magic had only ever responded to heightened emotion. Whether I was feeling particularly angry, or lonely, or afraid. And the more it happened, the more familiar the feeling became, the easier it got. I’d never really been able to induce vicious feelings in myself to draw on my raw magic, but now that I had a *good* one… It was like the first tangible grip I’d ever managed around that blistering core within me.

My lungs sucked in a gasp as I felt the sheer *enormity* of what I was touching. What I was *grasping, squeezing* as hard as I *could.* Draco’s hands on mine were nearly breaking *bone* as he gasped in my arms, but I couldn’t let go. After nearly having *lost him,* the terrible fear and grief I’d felt were *eclipsed* like… Like candles before the fucking *sun.* And only when his voice broke on a mangled sort of *cry* did I let the light start to trickle out of me.

I hoped it’d make its home in his body. In his chest, an invulnerable barrier around his heart. It was only then that I realised I’d shed a few tears of my own, but coming down off the high of using so much powerful magic had left me slightly jittery. Trembling as I cautiously peered my eyes open again.

Draco’s death grip on my hand loosened slightly as the light faded from my wrists, my palms, leaving only my fingertips as I watched. And craned my neck forward to peer at the one on his chest. He let me pry that one off his skin, and I blinked down at the distinct, hand-shaped mark I’d left. I’d put the light *into him,* *into Draco,* and he drew another stuttering gasp as I watched it fade.

I wasn’t really sure *what* I’d done, but as his chest dimmed back to its normal quality, so did my hands and the hair on the back of my neck stood on end. It was like I’d just heard *music* for the first time, like I suddenly had a new *sense,* and the information that sense picked up first was… *Lovely.* I blinked a few more tears out of my eyes as I let my head fall back against the headboard, because ringing strong and clear and true to this new sense of mine was…

*His heart.* I could *feel* it beating, *racing* in his chest. In a way that went deeper than just feeling his pulse. It was like it was in *my* chest, and feeding *ecstasy* into my bloodstream. All I could do was *breathe* as I adjusted to this new feeling, this strange *euphoria* that made my head feel slightly lighter. Without knowing the finer details of it, I felt sure that I’d… *Connected* us somehow. How I’d managed that, and what all it’d entail, I couldn’t say, but Draco’s quiet sobbing finally hushed to stuttering breath as he lay against me.

*“Love you, Harry,”* he whispered, and I pulled him tighter into my arms. Pressed a tender to kiss to the diagonal scar across his brow, now no more than a few millimetres thick and *white.* Interrupted his pale eyebrow in one spot as another glistening tear slipped down his cheek.

By way of an answer, I hesitantly *reached* for this newfound sensation. Sent my attention towards it and… Sort of *caressed* it. And felt his heart flutter in return. A smile pulled at my lips as I eased us back down onto the small cot, never releasing him for a single heartbeat.

*I’m never letting him go.*

---

Felt like Harry’d nearly *loved me to death.* Like he’d submerged me in a *geyser* of love and while it’d ached, *burned,* I let it wash over me. Let it *fill* me until it overflowed from my eyes. Felt like he’d set every inch of me down to my *soul* on fucking *fire.*

Once my thunderous pulse finally started to quiet down after his *onslaught,* a strange sort of… *Presence* lingered in my chest. Like my heart had stared at the sun too long and went slightly blind, the *shape* of Harry’s love seemed to have singed into the tissue. And when I breathed those three words, that lingering warmth seemed to *purr* in my ribs.

I wanted to ask what he’d done, but the sheer *comfort* I felt in his arms, the purring in my chest, lulled me to sleep like an order. One I was helpless to follow as I realised not even an *ounce* of tension remained in my body.

I dreamt of him. My subconscious fabricated what I might’ve witnessed if I’d been awake for it, because it was that same dingy room, only this time, when Harry burst through the doors, we immediately locked eyes.

Like someone had cranked a *dial* inside him, he started to glow. Eyes first, then his fingertips and it spread through his whole body until he was *blinding* to look at. My attackers were only shying away in the corners, but as he started towards me from the opposite end of the room, one by one, they vanished into red *mist.*

I wasn’t afraid. I was in *awe* as he effortlessly stepped over rivulets of blood, claiming lives without lifting a *finger* and radiant as a *god.* I’d felt the pull to worship him when I’d first had the privilege of properly bedding him, but now in the presence of the fearsome, *glorious* power at his disposal, all I felt was…

*Love.* *This is the man I love. The man who bites his lips and stammers so awkwardly and hates tea but loves coffee. The one who keens so beautifully for me when I’m inside him.*

It wasn’t surprise, because I’d *known* he was bloody powerful, but seeing it even in a dream, I felt like I… Like everything I knew Harry to be was being proven to me. He came to kneel before me as the red vapour in the room condensed to splatter us with a gentle rain of blood, but I hardly noticed, because he *smiled* at me.

“Found you,” he said, voice all at once soft and *enormous.*

“I’m so sorry,” was my reply. “So sorry I left, I never should’ve *left–”*

“Hey,” he interrupted, reaching a bloody hand for my face to cup my cheek. And I leaned into it. “‘S not your fault. You’re safe now. I’m not going *anywhere.”*
