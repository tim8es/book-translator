## Chapter 87

Seeing Harry so excited to tell his friends about our discovery was just as adorable as it was… Well, kind of embarrassing. As soon as we entered the Great Hall that evening and saw a familiar brunette and redhead at the Gryffindor table, he was hauling me toward it, nearly bouncing as he did.

“Guys!” he called as we called as we closed in on them, and I had to press my lips together to keep from laughing at him. “Guys, guess what happened.”

He only released my hand to climb into the bench opposite them, seeming to startle the two out of some reverie they’d been in as I more patiently joined.

“Er…” Ron began, but Harry blazed ahead.

“Nevermind, you’ll never guess. Draco just cast his first Patronus!”

Hermione smiled widely at us, and I felt warmth creep into my cheeks as I settled onto the bench beside Harry. Who was nearly *vibrating*with excitement.

“Congratulations! That one can be tricky,” she adulated. “What was it?”

Harry and I exchanged a glance, and he was fucking *beaming.*

“D’you wanna tell them, or shall I?” He was so *eager,* so proud and happy that my cheeks went a bit hotter. And I gestured for him to go ahead, since he seemed like he wanted to. So it was with a sparkle in his gorgeous eyes that he turned back to his friends. “It was a–a *stag.”*

Hermione slapped a hand over her mouth, eyes going wide as Ron’s pale eyebrows nearly flew off his head. And I awkwardly scratched behind my ear. *God,* I didn’t know why I was so bloody *nervous,*but I still couldn’t stop smiling.

“Oh my *god,”* the witch exulted as she slowly lowered her hand from her gaping mouth. “That’s… That’s *adorable.”*

For once in my fucking *life,* I didn’t immediately lash out for being called a synonym of *cute.* My ears went hot as I dropped my gaze to watch my fingers pick at each other.

“A bloody *waste,* you mean,” Ron teased, easing forward on his elbows to smirk as I frowned up at him. “I was hoping it’d be a ferret.”

My shoe connected with his shin beneath the table, but his wince only slightly interrupted his cackling. Hermione rolled her eyes and gave his shoulder a half-hearted slap, but I wasn’t *really* mad. Couldn’t be much of anything other than pure fucking *ecstatic.* Ron muttered a good-natured apology, but before I could offer anything like my forgiveness, Harry’d taken my face in his hands and twisted it to kiss me. And like *instinct,* I wrapped my arms around his waist and pulled him into me.

Any reactions to our affection was lost to the fucking *bliss* Harry’s mouth sucked me into. I think I was sort of *swallowing his tongue,* though, because eventually what cut through the *rapture* of his lips was a

“*Bloody* hell,” as we disentangled our tongues from each other. Who should it be but a sheepish-looking Dean Thomas hesitantly approaching our end of the table. “They always like that?”

Ron snickered around his mouthful as I released Harry’s waist, and we exchanged a meaningful glance.

“Only on days that end in ‘Y.’ How’s it?” Hermione welcomed as he carefully lowered himself to Ron’s right.

“Alright. You two have a nice night?” Dean smirked sort of implicatively at the two as he plucked a drumstick from a bowl of various pieces of chicken. The redhead went noticeably red, but Hermione just grinned.

“Best birthday *ever.* Did you all end up playing Spin the Bottle?”

Harry and I were suddenly *very* engrossed in assembling our own meals as Dean sort of choked on his mouthful.

“E-er… Yeah, for a bit. Reckon it’s m-more fun in larger groups.” Hermione hummed, as if she didn’t even *need*the details to piece together what happened, but then Dean addressed Harry. “Thanks for that tonic this morning, by the way. Think I’d still be in bed if not for it.”

“Course, mate,” he offered noncommittally, and it was then it sort of clicked for me that Dean must’ve asked Harry to keep what happened a secret. Not that I’d have gone trouncing about the bloody castle spouting what I *knew,* but he could’ve inserted a subtle-enough jab if he’d had a mind to tease the poor bloke.

I was beginning to wonder if timing was just a weakness of the Gryffindor house, though, because it was at that moment Seamus happened to wander through the Great Hall doors. Took one glance at who we were sat with, went red as *fuck,* and spun on his heel to fucking *bolt.* And I bit back a snicker.

Passed right by a confused-looking Neville and Luna as they walked in, but given Dean’s unusual company with us, I began preparing myself for what would likely be a very similar conversation with the chestnut-skinned bloke. And even though a subtle reluctance to get involved laced the corners of my mind, I couldn’t spare more than a passing thought for it because I had Harry by my side, and that meant all was well.

*He’s my happy memory. He’s my whole fucking heart is what he is. Bloody hell.*

---

Draco must’ve been a bloody *mind-reader,* because by the time dinner was winding down, he casually assembled me a plate consisting of a slice of custard tart with a scoop of ice cream, even spooned a few blueberries on top and slid it over to me. Like he just *knew* I’d want a sweet excuse to linger at the table, because Dean hadn’t sat with us yet all term and I could only assume why he’d chosen to now.

Sure enough, as our other dining companions split off to their own evening activities, Dean lingered with us. Feigning nonchalance (poorly) and rosy in the cheeks. So we just waited, likely equally intrigued to see how this would go.

“Er…” he began, glancing up at us with a swallow. “S-so about last night…”

“You’re alright,” Draco cut in as he dragged a strawberry through the chocolate frosting of the slice of cake he’d selected for himself. “We won’t tell anyone, I mean.”

Dean *visibly* relaxed as Draco popped the berry between his lips, and I pulled the vanilla-coated tines of my fork through my own. Unbothered, and not even really pretending to be.

“Thanks,” he breathed, but almost instantly tensed back up again. Stared down at the table and dragged his thumbnail through the wood grain. “Th-that’s not really what I’m… *Worried* about, though…” He fidgeted uncomfortably for a moment as he sorted through his thoughts, and we just waited patiently. Eventually though, it all came out in a rush. “I-I dunno what to say to him now. Like, w-was that just a… Like, a *drunken* thing? Dunno if he was even that *drunk,* anyway, and it’s not like… I don’t think we’d have done that *sober,* but does that just mean it meant *nothing?* Am I just supposed to pretend like it was *nothing?”*

As calmly as I could, I set my fork down. Hoping to ease some of Dean’s tension, but *blimey, what a fucking predicament.*

“Did it mean nothing to you?”

Dean’s mouth flapped open and closed for a moment, eyes sort of fluttering like he wanted to blink at me but couldn’t before he eventually forced the words out.

“N-no. No, it-it didn’t mean… *Nothing.”*

“Well there you go, then,” Draco supposed, as if he’d answered his own question. “Just be honest with him, I reckon.”

Dean frowned back down at his hands, like obviously *he knew* what he had to do, he was just *loathe* to do it. And all of a sudden, the whole thing seemed… Rather *silly.*

“Look,” I sighed, leaning forward on my elbows. “Whether you’d both been drunk or not, it *happened.* Merits at *least* a conversation, if not a good shag.”

Draco choked on his next bite as I sent him cackling, but I just grinned at the way Dean stiffened. Gaped at me like I’d just admitted that I knew how their night had ended up.

“I’m-I’m not gonna *shag Seamus,”* he hissed at me, suddenly redder in the face than I’d ever *seen* him. But *clearly* he was thinking about it now, because his gaze sort of slipped into a middle-distance as his eyes went wide.

*We’ll see about that.*
