## Chapter 74: Chapter 74

Draco talked me out of a *number* of silly purchases, but I did walk out of Zonko's with a last-minute gift for Hermione. It was just a little quill with a fluffy bauble on the end that giggled quietly when written with, and given her knack for losing quills in her hair, I figured she might get a laugh.

“Think there’s any chance she’ll say no?” Draco wondered as we wandered back towards the castle, skipping the alleyway tryst this time.

“I doubt it. She’s definitely the pragmatic one of the two, but I’ve seen Ron draw a romantic side out of her on more than one occasion.”

“Are you suggesting one can’t be both pragmatic *and* romantic?” he teased, and I jostled him with an elbow. He caught my hand and laced our fingers together.

“I just mean, they… They’re good together. They balance each other out, without being too dissimilar.”

A hum, and then we were walking in companionable silence for a while. It was so *nice* and *normal,* and I couldn’t help remembering the first time he visited my room just to chat, and how *weird* it’d felt to be normal with him. Now it was the best thing in the *world.*

Just as I was about to comment on the pleasant weather, a familiar ginger started coming into focus, strolling towards us down the hill and my steps faltered. I swore under my breath, but didn’t pause as Ginny made a beeline for us. She’d been little more than *awkward* around us since my confession, which was as encouraging as it was uncomfortable, but the closer she got, the more the determination on her face registered. And I realised she wasn’t just walking to Hogsmeade, she was coming *to us.*

“Hey,” she finally said when she was in hearing distance. And we paused. I shared a glance with Draco, who was wearing that *all you* sort of passivity, and bit back a groan.

“H-hey,” I answered shakily, hand tightening around Draco’s as I forced a smile onto my face.

She eyed us for a second, tucked her hair behind her ear and glanced down at our hands.

“So… Ron’s having me spread the word. Tonight, eight o’clock in the common room. George is smuggling in some food and drinks, I guess, but remember to knock before you go in so they know it’s not *them.*”

“R-right.” I shifted my weight uneasily, hoping Draco was memorising these instructions because I was just so *bloody nervous*, the way she was studying us. “Just… Just us and George?”

She jerked a nod, chewed the inside of her cheek, and leaned into a hip.

“I’m not… *Cross,* just so you know.” Here, she seemed to address Draco, albeit somewhat reluctantly. “Harry told me he’s in love with you.” With a wince, I checked his reaction. He seemed… Well, sort of *shocked.*

“He did…?”

Ginny hummed, shoved her hands into the pockets of her coat.

“It’s none of my business, in fact I’d like to know *less* than I already do, but… For what it’s worth, congratulations.” I couldn’t believe what I was *hearing.* “If you can win his heart, you must be some sort of alright.”

She looked like the words tasted *sour* in her mouth, but she twitched a smile. I wanted to hug her, but decided against it when Draco squeezed my hand back.

“Th-thanks,” he said at length. “Sorry for, erm… Like, *loads* of shit.”

Her pinched smile warmed a little, a bit more genuine, as she glanced back at me.

“You seem… Happy.” Speechless. Utterly speechless, I nodded, throat going tight. And her eyes softened. “I’m glad.”

With that, she continued down towards Hogsmeade, hands still in her pockets and wind whipping at her fiery locks. I didn’t miss the final glance she sent Draco as she walked past us, but the look in her eyes… I couldn’t decipher it.

*“Fuckin’* women, man,” was how Draco decided to break the silence once she was gone. Shook his head, sort of *awestruck*, as he turned his attention back to me. And he *smiled.*

“Y-yeah,” I agreed, still slightly emotional as I tried to ease my throat with a swallow. “Weasley women.”

As we resumed our return to the castle, my head was a bustling intersection of thoughts. Molly would be *gutted* that I wasn’t marrying into the family, but it’s not like I’d ever be too far. Maybe one day they’d let me invite *Draco* to the Burrow for the hols. Imagining him in that environment was *hilarious,* but the humour didn’t quite reach me. Nerves all fraying, I pictured what it’d be like to *introduce* him to them. His father had given the Weasleys a world of trouble before he was imprisoned.

It’d almost be like introducing him to my *parents. My parents,* I recalled, the Resurrection Stone safely buried in my desk drawer with all the other gifts Draco had given me.

*One day.*
