## Chapter 76: Chapter 76

Chapter Notes

I think I sort of *wrecked* Harry’s legs a bit, which I did feel bad about, but he agreed to sit in my lap in the corner of the common room so I wasn’t too gutted. Handed Hermione’s gift off to Luna to wrap and add to the pile in the opposite corner, and as we snuggled tighter together, I was just sort of… In *awe* of the proceedings.

Birthdays had never been a pleasant affair for myself. Each year I was held to new expectations, *‘you’re this age now, Draco, you need to act your age,’* no matter how old I was. And so it became a tradition of learning what new ways I was disappointing father, what new shortcomings and flaws I had to work on. So to see everyone in such high spirits, tittering and giggling and dressing the room up in streamers and such was… Strange.

Not like I’d never witnessed pleasant birthday parties for my peers before, but to be included in the environment like this, even just from the corner was… Nice. Alien, but nice.

At one point, I was sort of stunned to see a door carve itself onto a wall where there had previously been none, only to find yet *another* ginger waltz through, hauling a sack of goodies. Ginny was the first to greet George, threw her arms around the man as he settled the bag onto the floor. He happened to glance our way over his sister’s shoulder to find us curled up together, and a smug smile was our silent greeting. Harry curled a bit tighter in my lap, and I smirked at the flush that rose to his cheeks.

*I’m sure Ron’s threatened to hex him if he tells Ginny about the bet, too.*

The rest of the attending eighth years gathered round to help unload the sack, and I was surprised to find a Weasley’s idea of *food and drinks* was a full-on *meal* and…

“Wicked!” Seamus rejoiced as he held a bottle of *Firewhiskey* aloft. “Thank *fuck* that secret passageway still works.”

“*Really?*” Ginny disagreed, hands on her hips. “You brought *alcohol* to *Hermione Granger’s birthday party?”*

George shrugged innocently.

“She doesn’t have to drink. Besides, after everything, there’s not even a *slight* chance I’m not taking advantage of a reason to celebrate. We’re about to have another sister, Gin!”

This seemed to mollify the youngest Weasley, because when she whirled to help set up the food table, the roll of her eyes disagreed with the *very* pleased grin on her lips.

I felt sort of bad for not helping, but having Harry in my arms and *knowing* he’d feel obliged to help, too, if he was the only one left seated kept me firmly in the chair we shared. My arms wrapped a bit tighter around him as my chin found his shoulder.

“Have I fucked all the stress out?” I asked quietly in his ear, and felt him stiffen in my arms. He half-turned to glance back at me, a delightful pink in his cheeks earning my smile.

“R-reckon so,” was his equally quiet answer as he turned back to watch his friends. Nestled a bit firmer back into me, and I pressed a kiss to the side of his neck. Seamus and Dean were already sharing shots of Firewhiskey, arms looped around each others’ as they threw them back in one. And my brow furrowed to recall the man's curious line of questions earlier in Hogsmeade.

“Seamus found me in Zonko's while you were gone,” I breathed, intrigued to see what Harry made of it.

“Oh?”

“Asked me how I knew I was gay.”

This time, he didn’t just stiffen in my arms, he *wrenched* himself from them to twist at the waist. Studied my face like I’d just admitted to having a basement full of *bodies.*

“You’re kidding,” he hissed, and I shook my head. “*Bloody* hell, woman’s a *menace…”* Harry relaxed back in my arms, but my whirling mind was sort of spinning all towards the same conclusion as he leaned his head back on my shoulder to murmur up into my ear: “Mione’s betting on him and Dean, too.”

A shout of laughter burst out of me, and Harry shushed me hastily, but *he* started chuckling quietly, too, and we just set each other off.

“The hell’s so bloody *funny?”* George wondered as he strolled past our chair to add to Hermione’s pile of gifts. Harry was the first to quell his humour as he cleared his throat.

“N-nothing. Hermione’s just clairvoyant as all *fuck,* apparently.”

The elder Weasley quirked an eyebrow at us, jerked his chin in a gesture that said *‘you mean about you two?’* And I shook my head. Inclined it towards the chuckling pair of *friends* now being swatted away from the food that’d been set out. George turned to follow my implication, and he grinned mischievously.

“Ron’s a fucking idiot for ever betting *against* her, honestly.”

Harry laughed again, and then it was time. Somehow, everyone seemed to go quiet at the same moment as the room held its breath. We all watched the door for a heartbeat… Two heartbeats… The knob clicked open, and then it swung inward.

A chaotic blend of *“Surprise!”* and *“Happy birthday!”*amounted to a rather confusing outcry as Hermione stepped through the doors. Even if she’d been anticipating the party, the cacophony that welcomed her would’ve startled anyone, and she jumped.

*“Bloody* hell,” she finally said once she scanned the interior of the room. “Nevermind a *surprise,* are you all trying to give me a *heart attack?”*

A chorus of laughter answered her question as she began the rounds of congratulatory hugs. And in behind her trailed Ron, looking peakier than ever but even from across the room, his eyes were *bright.*

It wasn’t until Hermione had assembled a plate for herself and sat on a couch that the rest of us were allowed to touch the food, but Harry seemed content to just wait. So we waited, and I tried to surreptitiously massage his legs through his jeans, rested my hands in his lap and gently squeezed every once in a while. I wasn’t quite hungry yet, or so I could convince myself when he seemed to be enjoying my touch.

Luna was the first one to make a move towards presents. Bounded over to the pile and selected a strangely-shaped parcel from the bunch.

“Here, open this one first,” she insisted as she brought it to Hermione. The brunette witch passed her plate off to Ron, who for *once,* didn’t seem to have an appetite. Dabbed her lips with her napkin, wiped her hands off, and accepted it.

“Thanks, Luna.” The Ravenclaw settled onto her heels before Hermione as she tore the package open, and… *None* of us could’ve told her what Luna had given her.

“It’s a protective charm against Wrackspurts!” she proudly proclaimed as Hermione held the strange stick aloft. All manner of charms had been affixed to it on the ends of strings, chains, cords. It made a pleasant sound when jostled, but for much else than a windchime, it seemed altogether… Rather *useless.*

Hermione beamed nonetheless.

“Thanks, Luna. Can’t have my brain going fuzzy while I’m trying to squeeze a whole year into one term, can I?”

The kneeling blond grinned back widely, and shuffled aside to sit against the foot of the couch. George volunteered next.

It was sort of bittersweet to see the growing pile of gifts at Hermione’s feet. Not that I’d ever *wanted* for anything, never *needed* anything growing up in the lap of luxury, but the only gifts I could recall from my family were only ever meant to boost my status. Magical theory years beyond my level, pointless Wizard opulence in the forms of clothes and baubles and shoes. Anything to lead me down the path towards Pureblood Excellence (trademark) and never *anything* so… *Personal.*

As Hermione unboxed a handmade scrapbook from Ginny, her eyes welled with tears. Flipped through pages of memories, glued in or scripted in or taped-on photographs. And my own throat went tight. There wasn’t yet a *single* gift that wasn’t saturated in meaning. In history, in the personalities of their givers. Even Harry’s explanation for his last-minute quill (as if the witch were ever wanting of quills) made her laugh. She was so *known,* so… So *loved* by her friends.

*That’s* what was different. ‘Friendship’ (if it could be called that) amongst Slytherins was only ever based on *respect.* It’s why I’d performed my expected duties as a Pureblood, as a *Malfoy,* because I never even thought to *want* them to like me. If I didn’t have their respect, I might as well have not existed to them. Every item that accumulated at her feet, beside her on the couch, was given out of *love.*

Neville’s hand-painted mug, (“It’ll sing when your tea’s going cold”) Dean’s hand-knit scarf, (“I didn’t know you could knit!”) Seamus’s bottle of hand-fired beads (“Only blew meself up once!”). Hands and hearts and intentions woven into every one. And I was suddenly *dreadfully* relieved I hadn’t tried to choose a gift for her.

Would’ve felt strange, for obvious reasons, but now I knew even if I’d *tried,* I’d have fucked it up. Would’ve gotten her a top-of-the-line something-or-other, and it would’ve stuck out like a sore thumb amongst the heap of *love* surrounding her. Even if I’d delved into the Malfoy reserves to give her something pricey, something *shiny,* it… It just would’ve been wrong.

Feeling strangely emotional, I pressed another kiss to Harry’s neck. He took one of my hands off his lap to lace his own fingers through, and I gripped back. He wasn’t just a lung for me, he was the anchor for my very *essence,* and as I exhaled a sigh over his shoulder, I felt myself sink into him. Bury itself between his ribs, and it was the first time I’d ever really *recognised* what *home* felt like.

Eventually, Hermione’s gifts dwindled, and by the last one, Ron was fidgeting like he’d been *hexed.* Running his fingers through his hair, scratching his jaw, his elbow, behind his ear. And I was *maddeningly* curious to see how this would play out.

Hermione already looked near tears at all the love that’d been laid upon her, but as she was sending one final *thank-you* to each of her friends, I saw Ron lock eyes with his brother. And said brother gave a short jerk of a nod, casually wove between chairs to stand behind the couch and conjured a camera. A *camera.*

*Bloody hell, didn’t think Ron was capable of this sort of planning.*

The ginger in question cleared his throat, earning the attention of the room as he hoisted himself up onto his feet. And sent a final glance over at us; no, at *Harry,* who gave him an encouraging nod, returned by a sideways smile.

“Thanks, everyone, for helping make this a special evening. Course, we all *know* how bloody *amazing* Mione is, but… I-I feel *especially* lucky to love her.” His throat bobbed with a swallow as he glanced up at his brother once. But then his attention zeroed in on Hermione like she was the only one in the *room.* “I’ve *no* idea how you put up with all my bullshit. Truly.” She chuckled, but her voice was still teetering on the brink of tears. “Dunno why I wasted so much *time* all these years. Feel like a git for ever thinking you were annoying, because you’re *actually fucking brilliant.”*

Luna leaned into the side of Neville’s knee, still sat on the floor before the couch he shared with Seamus and Dean, and it was the only movement in the room as we all watched Ron spill his fucking *guts.*

“You… You make me feel like I can be… *Anything,* do *anything,* and I… C-can’t imagine my life without you in it.” Hermione hiccuped, swiped a few tears from her eyes, and smiled up at him. And he smiled right back. “So… In the interest of *not* wasting anymore time, er…”

As awkward as I’d only ever seen *Harry,* he lowered himself to one knee. Hermione sucked in a stuttering gasp as he fumbled for his wand, clumsily conjured a small box, and held it aloft. And for reasons I couldn’t yet comprehend, *my* heart skipped a beat in my chest.

“W-will you m-*marry* me…?” The box popped open in his hands, sapphire catching the light like I’d only seen a handful of other jewels do, and my eyes went wide. *Holy shit, where the fuck did he find a ring like*that??

For the second time tonight, the entire room seemed to hold its breath as Hermione covered her mouth and nose in both hands. Eyes *flooding* tears down her face as she glanced from the ring back up to Ron’s *terrified,* expectant face. Hopeful, yes, but downright *mortified.*

She choked a sob into her hands and nodded, and a tension I hadn’t even realised had gathered in my chest eased out in a sigh as she threw herself from the couch to kneel before Ron and pull him into her arms.

“Oh thank *god,”* he released in a rush as he threw his arms back around her. And her sobbing laughter was quickly smothered by applause. *Cheers*, even.

I was all at once surprised and *not* surprised as Harry eased forward off my lap to the floor, and crawled towards the couple. Threw his arms around the pair of them, and it was then I realised he was *also* crying. They both accepted him into the embrace, an arm each, as the three of them dissolved into happy tears.

My lap was slightly cold for his absence, but my heart swelled in my chest to see them together like this. Harry loved his friends *so much,* and it wasn’t even slightly painful anymore to witness. It was… *Warm.* Alright, perhaps a bit *painfully* warm as my heart tightened in my chest, but there wasn’t a *drop* of jealousy in me.

*I want to learn love like this.*
