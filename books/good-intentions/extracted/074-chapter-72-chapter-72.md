In class that morning, Draco penguined me a little figurine of a ballerina. I felt bad for whoever was missing a figurine, but was fascinated by the craftsmanship. Plucked it off the corner of my desk and held it up to study it. She was posing with her front leg bent at the knee, back leg long and pointed with an arm in the air and the other curved out to the side. Her tutu was actual fabric, but the ceramic of her body was painted with details that *must’ve* been applied with a fucking *single hair.* Her expression was almost… *Euphoric.*

It was beautiful, and I gave Draco a grin as I tucked it into my own pocket. Pleasure flushed his cheeks as he returned to his work, and I tried to do the same. As keyed up as Ron had been over breakfast, though, I couldn’t help but feel his anxiety radiating from behind me like a physical touch. A glance back at him told me all I needed to know: his jaw was a raw sort of red from where he’d been scratching it.

As perceptive a witch as Hermione was, there was no doubt in my mind she’d picked up on his nervous behaviour. Her birthday coming up was just the perfect excuse, too, and I didn’t doubt his proposal would come as the intended surprise it’d be.

*How he can worry she’ll say no is insane. I mean, I’d be going round the bend, too, but Christ they’ve been obsessed with each other for nearly a fucking decade by now. If it comes down to wedding costs, I suppose my untouched wealth will finally have a purpose.*

The Weasleys weren’t poor. Despite the stigma that’d followed the family of gingers, to be able to support *seven fucking children* was no easy feat, I was sure. Even with the hand-me-downs, sending all seven to school and providing rooms and clothes and food and *gifts* for them all their entire lives had to have cost a *fortune.* And a familiar twinge of jealousy pinched my heart to think about the home life *I’d* left.

*There’s another thing Draco doesn’t know about me. At least, I assume he doesn’t know.* I glanced over at him, working studiously and *are my love bites even worse today? His throat is nearly purple.* A quiet snort sounded in my throat to recall the way he’d referred to my life as *fucking awful* in his initial confession. He hadn’t even known the *half* of it.

As I turned back to my textbook, I tried to make my eyes follow the words. Some bloke with a distinctly wizard-sounding name *(seriously, half of these have to be pseudonyms)* making some discovery or other in the eighteenth century… *Why did I tell him about my death that night he first visited my room?*

I hadn’t thought about it in weeks, honestly, but now, I did. It’d been like *instinct* to hide the truth from Ron and Hermione, just like they’d never really know how bad Vernon could get. Like it was *embarrassing* how *‘fucking awful’* my life had been. It’d been initial relief when Ron and his brothers had come to save me from them summer before second year, but I’d never forget the following *dread* that they’d glimpsed the horrors of life with the Dursleys. In the end, I’d downplayed it, said I’d started a fight with Dudley and *that’s* why they’d punished me. Like it was just a typical, neurotic caregiver’s response to an unruly child.

So much of my life I’d hidden from them. My *best friends.* It just came so naturally to me, the shame of my truth so deeply ingrained into me that even my *godfather* never really knew what happened behind closed doors. I stewed in the puzzling reality of my own secrecy for a while, but in the end, concluded it was because my childhood environment had never once taught me *trust.* To my aunt and uncle and cousin, I’d only ever been an inconvenient *nuisance,* so the idea of sharing the *worst* of me was so opposite to my natural instinct that it’d never even *occurred* to me that things like that *should* be said out loud.

The bell signalling lunch broke through my reverie, and I was sort of astounded that the morning had passed so quickly. *Was I lost in thought that whole time? Again?? God fucking dammit…*

Draco and I held hands all the way to the Great Hall, but he didn’t speak to me. I was still sorting through a maelstrom of thoughts and emotions, and distantly, I noted he could probably read as much on my face. *I’ve purposefully not thought about the Dursleys in years, but… Would it even make a difference to bring it up now? Does it even matter?*

The constant anxiety of knowing powerful people were gunning for my life, the trauma each year at Hogwarts had dealt me, killing Quirrel at eleven, Ginny nearly dying the next year, going up against a werewolf and then a *horde* of Dementors third year, watching Cedric die in front of me and Voldemort coming back to life fourth, the war, and every year having to return to the Dursley’s abuse between *relentless* fucking school terms, it was no fucking *wonder* I didn’t trust the way things were seeming to–

“Harry?” I snapped back into my body at the Gryffindor table, *nauseatingly* jarring, to see Hermione leaning over her plate with concern all over her face. And as I blinked around, I saw Ron’s features pinched similarly. A hand on the small of my back, rubbing little circles against my shirt.

“I…” Clearing my throat, I decided it *did* matter. Sent a glance sideways at Draco to see his features carefully neutral and open, but eyes *burning* with worry. “I sh-should tell you guys…” Returning my attention to the couple across from us, I forced a swallow down my throat and wiped my palms, suddenly moist, against my lap. “I mean… Not-not like it *matters,* but I’d… I’d just rather you guys know, I…” A wince as I felt warmth creep into my cheeks. “There’s a lot you don’t kn-*know* about me. Things I-I dunno if I’ll *ever* be able to… To *tell* you, but… Bad things. That’ve happened to me. That I’ve… N-never said.”

The hand on my back stilled as Hermione leaned back on the bench to exchange a glance with Ron. The lift of his faint orange brows seemed to imply *this one’s all yours* as Hermione returned a calculating frown back at me. And I felt the rush of blood out of my head as my ears went cold.

“We would… *Never* expect you to talk about things you don’t want to, Harry. And, I mean, it’s not like you *owe* us your life story, but… For what it’s worth, we’d still be your best friends regardless.” A small smile pulled her lips sideways, frown still creasing her brow as I tried to return it. “Course, if you ever *wanted* to tell us anything, we’d listen. You know that, right?”

The hand on my back slid around to the opposite side of my waist and *gripped.*

“Y-yeah, I know. Thanks.” Ron scratched at his jaw for the *millionth* time today. “Just feel so fucking *stupid,* like I know all the *reasons* why I’ve never wanted to say anything, and I *also* know why they’re all *ridiculous* reasons, but I still…” I glanced down at my plate, saw it notably empty, and felt the blood rush back into my head as my face warmed. “A-anyway…”

Clearing my throat, I began purposefully collecting food onto my empty plate like doing something gave me a purpose. A way to redirect my mental energy from cringing at myself, but then Draco leaned in to press a kiss to my hair and my heart fluttered in my chest.

“You’re alright, mate,” Ron muttered quietly just before taking an *enormous* forkful of spaghetti into his mouth. And *thank god for Ron,* his dismissive, easy nature loosened my stiff muscles just enough for me to cough a laugh.

“Love you guys,” I returned just as quietly, and Hermione reached across the table to take one of my hands. My eyes snicked up to hers, and she didn’t even really have to *say* it, the way she was looking at me.

“We love you, too, Harry.”

My throat went tight, eyes prickling as I mirrored her smile back to her.

*What did I ever do to deserve them?*

*There’s a lot you don’t know about me. Bad things that’ve happened to me that I’ve never said.* Harry’s words made it *awfully* hard to concentrate the rest of the day as they bounced around the inside of my skull. They didn’t know he’d *died,* for one, or that he nearly hadn’t come *back,* and I held that secret tighter in my ribs as I considered all the *other* things he’d never said out loud.

*Why tell me but not them?* I couldn’t help but wonder, because we’d only just been on *speaking* terms when that truth had burst through his lips. *I’ll have to ask him later tonight.*

Dinner came and went as usual, Harry was putting on a good show of nonchalance, like nothing had happened during lunch, but by the time we were climbing up the stairs to our room, I’d decided patience was the only option. He didn’t owe *me* his life story, either, and I was grateful for the single nugget he’d shared, but if his confession to Ron and Hermione hinted at any *more* confessions, I would gladly receive them. *Wanted* them, but I wouldn’t ask.

Harry showered as I dressed down for bed. Decided the black briefs I’d worn all day were fitting pyjamas as I laid on the bed, arms crossed behind my head. *His life’s been even worse than I thought,* I painfully acknowledged as I twisted my head to eye the drawer where all my nicknacks were accumulating. Alongside a *Deathly fucking Hallow. Christ, was he serious about me meeting his dead parents…?*

Purposefully putting the stone out of my mind, I returned my attention to the ceiling as my gaze went unfocused. Harry was out of the shower as fast as ever (when using his own products) and I was just… There. Willing, but not asking. Waiting, but not expecting as I heard his towel drop to the floor. He forewent the wardrobe, sat on the edge of the bed and when I finally zeroed in on the side of his face, he was worrying a fingernail.

*Talk to me,* I wanted to say. How many times had I requested his admissions by now? But given how today had gone, I just laid there. Watched him as my heart gripped in my chest.

“You can ask,” he eventually said. Quietly, barely above a whisper, and sent my mind catapulting out of my head.

“I want to know,” was my answer. Because I did. It wasn’t a question, though, I *hadn’t* asked. Just informed him of my curiosity, and whether he’d take that as an invitation or a fact, I left up to him. His finger lowered from his mouth as he stared ahead, and I *desperately* wanted to watch his face straight-on, to read his thoughts in his expression, but all I did was scoot closer so my thigh touched his back. *Contact,* as I waited.

Eventually, he twisted to glance down at me. The setting of his mouth betrayed worry, but his eyes… Those *monstrously* green eyes were pinched with horror. Vacant horror, like the second he spoke, I’d vanish and he’d never see me again. It made my throat tight to see the way his eyebrows pushed together, but then he ripped his face away from me to stare down at his feet. Heard his nails clicking together as he fidgeted.

“Dunno why it’s so fucking *humiliating* when it’s… ‘S not even things *I did,* it was shit done *to me.* Makes no fucking sense.” A hand lifted to scrub at his brow, removed his glasses to let his other hand pinch his eyes together as he leaned forward over his knees. “Not like I *abused myself,* *Christ…*”

Slowly, I curled up to sit behind him. Leaned back on my hands as I prepared my lovesick heart for injury. *This is gonna hurt.*

Harry sighed. “Y-you know I was… Raised by my aunt and un-*uncle?”* I hummed in assent, nearly reaching out to stroke his back, but withheld my touch.

“Muggles,” I said. And he scoffed, passed a hand over his eyes and went on.

“The worst kind. They–” His voice cut off as he glanced back at me, and I saw the wince in his features. “C-can you… *Not* look at me…?” Instantly, just like before our first time, I tore my eyes away. Leaned my cheek against his back and shut them. Felt the stuttering exhale through his ribs. “They, erm… H-*hated* me. First eleven years of my *life,* I w-wished I’d never been *born.*” A loud swallow through his back as my heart lifted an inch towards my throat. “Even though they set me up in a fucking *cupboard,* starved me, b-beat me, I… I just wished I’d disappear so *their* lives could be better.”

The scar across my chest *stung* like a fresh slash through my lungs. Like it’d sliced my *heart,* and I was bleeding out. And all at once, the *floodgates* opened.

“I… I att–*attempted*, second year.” Harry’s voice grew small. “L-learning I wasn’t only hated my own *blood,* but that there were people out there trying to *kill* me… I j-just thought I’d m-make it *easier* for everyone.” A swallow. A shuddering sigh. “Wished he’d killed *me*when he killed my parents, and I…” A sob-like sort of laugh. “Got a beating when it didn’t work.”

Like a passenger in my own fucking *body,* I wrapped my arms around Harry’s waist. Gripped him as tight as I *dared.* And he sniffed. *Oh fuck.*

“And every *fucking year,* I’d come back here and be subjected to *unmitigated* horrors, only to return to that *fucking* house, and–” He stuttered in a gasp as his voice tightened, puffed out an exhale and I felt him scrub at his face. My eyes screwed shut even tighter against the stinging that threatened tears. “*God,* I’ve never known a moment’s *peace,* honestly…”

*Second year. Twelve fucking years old.* I’d contended with the possible benefits of my own death too many times to *count,* but had never made a move towards it. Never actually *tried.* An unbidden sob coughed through my lips as I held him to me.

I didn’t say anything. What was there *to* say? Harry wept quietly in my arms as I wrangled with my own grief. Seemed he’d liberated all the secrets he could for the moment, though, as he leaned back against me. And I welcomed him onto the mattress as he shifted to lay against me. *On* me, clinging to me just as tightly as I clung to him.

“I love you, Harry,” was all I could manage as warmth streaked wet rivulets down my temples. *God,* it fucking *hurt.*
