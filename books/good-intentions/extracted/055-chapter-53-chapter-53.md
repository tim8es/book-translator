## Chapter 53: Chapter 53

“No, really, if you don’t tell your own story, someone else inevitably will, and what if they get it wrong? What if they *irrevocably* fuck it up, and that’s what ends up being taught in future history classes?”

I resisted rolling my eyes as we closed in on the mouth of Hogsmeade, equally unwilling to consider writing an *autobiography* like Draco was suggesting I do as I was to consider what drivel some random journalist would come up with to tell my story. There was so much I didn’t want *anyone* to know, but it grated my bones against each other to imagine what *would* end up in… *History books. Shit.*

I winced. “Y-you’re probably right, but… *God,* I just wish everyone would fucking *forget the whole thing.* Wish *I* could.”

Draco’s grip in my hand went tight and *sweaty* as we drew our first round of stares, stepping between two stores toward the main strip of Hogsmeade. And I tried to keep the conversation going, to keep him present and distracted and *calm* as we sought out sustenance.

“You should write it for me,” I suggested, and he turned an incredulous frown down at me. “I’ll dictate to you and you ghost write for me.”

The crowd parted for us, people halted to stare and whisper, but there was *nothing* that could stop us loving each other and that was the only thing that even held any merit for worry. So I just kept my paces steady and easy, didn’t acknowledge any of the attention, as he’d said, and hoped he’d take inspiration from me a second time and be *brave,* like I knew he could be.

“I dunno if you’ve picked up on this, but I’m sort of *just as shite* at words as you, knobhead,” he teased, but his attention was torn between me and our onlookers. So I just kicked the nonchalance up several notches, breezing past the bounds of believable straight to fucking *spiteful.*

“I’m sure between the *two* of us, we could manage to rub our single brain cells responsible for communication together and come up with something.”

He laughed, and I felt my heart *soar.* Straight out my chest as he shot me a smile that was all pure fucking *love. God, I’d go back through the war all over again for him.*

“You’re very optimistic. But I suppose I knew that about you. Only the most ridiculously optimistic could hope to stand up to the most powerful dark wizard of all time at the ripe age of *eleven* and live to tell the tale.”

I shrugged, not bothering to wonder if anyone was picking up on our conversation as we strolled through the throng.

“I *am* the Boy Who Lived, you know, not necessarily the Boy Who Had Any Grasp of His Abilities Whatsoever.”

Draco laughed *again,* and I wanted to swing him into a dip and snog the *shit* out of him right there, in the midst of the many whispering and staring witches and wizards that stood in attendance of our journey.

“You’d only be that much more of an *unbearable prick* if you’d known what you were capable of this whole time.”

*That* earned a laugh from me, and I suddenly realised I was *buzzing.* Every nerve was *on fucking fire,* because I was *holding hands* with my *boyfriend* in *public* and we were *laughing together.* God, I’d never felt like such a normal fucking teenager before in my *life,* and it was *incredible.*

Of course, it’d have to be just at that fucking moment that I heard the tell-tale sound of a camera shutter behind us, and I flinched. Draco halted with me as I sent a grimace behind us to see a middle aged wizard slowly lowering a camera, staring at us sort of *awestruck.* And I was just about to do something stupid when Draco nudged my shoulder with his, inviting my gaze to snap back up to his.

“Still hungry?” he asked, and I heard the subtext clear as day: *he’s not worth it.* And I nodded, trying to swallow my grimace as we continued down the street.

*God, the Prophet’s gonna have a field day.*

Eventually, we made it to the Three Broomsticks and found it thankfully empty. Christ, what time even was it? My stomach was *aching*, so it must’ve been well past lunch. Had we spent more time languoring in my bed than I’d realised? It’d just been so nice to lay with Draco, *holding* him inside me even as he held me, I hadn’t even realised the day had gotten so late.

We paced inside the small pub to a two-seated table on the far wall, and I agreeably let Draco have the seat that faced the door. Didn’t even really think about it, but he sent me this *look,* like he was just remembering I was in love with him as pink spread across his cheeks. And then Madam Rosmerta swept into our circle, sort of glancing back and forth between us like she didn’t know *what* to think. But she took our orders politely and then returned, likely to gossip with the other staff as they prepared our food.

“Fucking *fame,”* I groaned into my palm as I leaned over the table on an elbow. And Draco laughed nervously.

“Yeah… Kinda surprised you’re not *used* to it by now.” I was just about to shoot back with some more aggravation when I saw the expression on his face. And I shut up. “I-I… *Realise* that this is, like… A reality for you, everywhere you go.” He swallowed, toyed with his fingernails and wouldn’t look up at me. “W-wouldn’t be here if I… If I didn’t want this for… *Forever.*”

I straightened in my seat, head sort of light and spinning as his words sunk into my bones. *Forever…? I mean, sure no one plans on breaking up with someone they’ve just started dating, but bloody hell…*

*I invited him to move into my room.*

“Y-yeah?” Finally, he sent a glance up at me, grey eyes sort of *vulnerable,* bordering on *pained* as he dropped them back to his nails.

“Yeah. Never…” He swallowed, pink cheeks going *red.* “Never w-*wanted* anything so bad. Never thought about the *future* like…” With a *deep* inhale, he finished: “Like I think about my future with y-*you.”*

Rosmerta chose then to return with our drinks, tea and coffee respectively, before slinking back to wherever she went. And I was just fucking *gone.*

*Draco wants to spend his life with me. Like, for real. Like… Like, like m-marriage…?*

All of a sudden, I saw it. I’d just been sort of taking it day by day, learning new things about myself and reassessing the past, but now I sent my sights *ahead.* Saw myself waking up in his arms every day, getting a place, making his tea just how he liked it every morning, ignoring his protests when my kisses tasted of coffee, borrowing clothes and *smelling the same,* fucking whenever we pleased on *every surface,* navigating adulthood together, attending weddings together and baby showers and making new friends together, contending with reporters and Ministry officials as a *unit,* and I…

Me, in white robes, Draco in black, stood before an altar as a Ministry officiant declared us *h–*

I choked on absolutely *nothing,* all of a sudden back in the Three Broomsticks on a Sunday in September, sat across from my *boyfriend Draco Malfoy,* and hacking up a fucking *lung.*

“Harry!?” Draco leaned across the table, reaching for me, panic on his face as I spluttered nothing but *air.* *Christ, he’s already so many steps ahead of me, and I keep forgetting…!*

“‘M alright,” I managed between coughs as I buried my mouth in the crook of my elbow. Tried chasing it down with coffee, but it was so bloody *hot,* I spluttered that, too. *“Shit.”*

After a hasty cleansing spell on the table, I somewhat regained control of my lungs as Draco made to stand, to help me. And I waved him away, clearing my throat in a good show of being alright. He hesitantly sat back down, concern still plain on his features.

“Did I say something… *Wrong?”*

I shook my head, wiping my mouth with a hand as the rest of our *lives together* played like a fucking *montage* behind my eyes.

*I want that. Christ, when was the last time I wanted* anything? *Other than for shitty things to stop happening, actually* wanting *something…*

“J-just…” I began, cleared my throat as my breathing returned to normal. “N-never knew I could love this much.” Tried for a smile, but my throat was still a bit raw, so it might’ve been a grimace. “Never even knew this was an *option.”*

Draco’s worry finally eased into something like pain, but he was *smiling.* Smiling like he might fucking *cry*as his body language relaxed.

“Me neither,” he admitted. “Never thought karma would *allow* me to be this happy.”

Draco, wandering our apartment naked because he preferred to air dry after a shower, stealing my leftovers and eating them standing up in our kitchen, grinning when I caught him and pulling me into a gravy-saturated kiss, casually chatting about the state of our garden as the days got colder–

“I-I want that,” I said. Out of nowhere, with no context for the conversation, but perhaps he *knew me* well enough to infer what I meant. “F-forever.”

His chin wobbled slightly just before he snapped a hand out to seize the front of my jumper, pulling me across the table to crush his lips to mine.

Draco, bitching at me for using the last of one of his many hair tinctures, but knowing he preferred to run his hands through my hair when it was silky like his, wondering where his favourite jumper was and interrupting my reading to ask, my wince when I remembered I’d kicked it under the bed on accident, grocery shopping together, making *memories…*

I whimpered into his mouth, so overcome with emotion that my throat *hurt* as he finally released me back into my own seat. Tried soothing it with another sip of coffee, but he was just *watching* me. Like he couldn’t fucking believe what he was seeing. And I wanted to show him everything I imagined.

*Later,* I promised myself. *Let’s see how adept he is with Legillimency.*
