## Chapter 7: Chapter 7

Chapter Notes

> This one's a doozy, y'all. I'll be the first to admit I suck at slow-burn, but Imma need everyone to strap in for some serious trauma-bonding. You're welcome <3

Ginny’s words swirled around my head as I lay in bed that night. I was knackered, but I just couldn’t put the conversation from my mind. And then to have looked up and seen Malfoy looking positively *sick…*

*I understand that you need time, but I’m not going to wait around forever for you to make up your mind. So if things have changed, just tell me. I’ll always love you, Harry, but I can’t just sit on the sidelines waiting for you to figure it out.*

It was unfair of me, I knew, to have been pushing her away all summer. And now that we were back at school together, to *keep* doing it… A wave of hot shame lashed through my throat, and I swallowed. Not like I could expect her to keep waiting for me, anyway. I sighed as I shifted onto my side, worrying my lip with my teeth and not really seeing my pillows.

Learning how Malfoy had felt all this time definitely threw a spanner into the works. And the way I’d (definitely) latched onto the idea by now had reluctantly taught me the pattern I hadn’t seen in my love life: always, *always* it was someone else liking me first. And me just accepting whatever I was offered.

A dry huff pushed through my nose, not quite a laugh, as I recalled the way Romilda had thought to pursue me. Reckon if she’d tried another way, at another time in my life, I would’ve ended up dating *her,* too. Had I *ever* pursued anyone, really? There was the time I asked Cho to the Yule Ball, but her rejection could hardly count as anything but poor timing.

Ginny was brilliant. Funny and brave and perfect for me, by all rights. But she deserved someone who was better at *liking someone.* As Malfoy had said, we were both downright shit at it. It was no seven years of tormenting, but the way I’d been avoiding her wasn’t fair to her.

I wanted to be everything she wanted. Everything she *needed.* But whenever she tried to get me to talk about the war or my *feelings* or anything, I’d just clam up. I already knew how much she and the others worried about me, so how could I hope to ever open up about… Those things? I’d only have to see that damn pity in their eyes again, their empty suggestions, half-arsed condolences and fretting because there wasn’t a damn thing any of them could do. And I knew they’d try. Especially Ginny.

*Bollocks, what sort of boyfriend can I be when I can’t even talk about the things that matter with my girlfriend??*

Just as I was moaning about my shortcomings, there came a subdued knock on my door and I sat up, frowning curiously. A quick tidying spell later, and I was opening it for one guilty-looking Slytherin.

“Oh…” Malfoy glanced up at me like a puppy who’d been caught eating something he shouldn’t have. “Hi.”

“Hi.” Frowning down at my chest, he shifted his weight and I was on the verge of asking what he wanted when he said: “C-can I come in…?”

I dunno why, but my stomach flipped at the fact that he’d *sought me out.* A far cry from running from me, and decidedly outside the realms of ‘normal,’ but I supposed this was the sort of thing one does when they fancied someone. So I stepped aside and silently invited him in.

Suddenly glad I’d taken the brief second to tidy up, I awkwardly sat on my bed, legs crossed beneath me as he cast his gaze around my room.

“Saviour suite, is it?” His eyes caught on the door to my private washroom, and I smirked.

“Something like that I guess.”

Finally his attention landed on my desk, and he awkwardly pulled the chair out to face me, and then lowered himself stiffly into the seat. It was no less disarming seeing him in pyjamas as it had been the first time, but I was proud of myself for not staring this time.

“So… What’s up?”

It was like coaxing a nervous cat out of a cage, the way he’d look up at me, hold my gaze for a moment and then glance away. All the while wringing his fingers together and growing increasingly pink in the cheeks.

“I-I just… Wanted to talk to you,” he said at length.

“Right…” When he didn’t continue, I wondered if he meant he’d just wanted to *chat. Blimey, a chat with Malfoy. Will the surprises never end?*

“Erm…” He looked so damn uncomfortable and out of place in my room, I almost laughed, but valiantly held it in. “H-how was your summer?”

This wasn’t what I expected him to ask, but as I eyed him dubiously, I considered all the questions I had for him. Perhaps this was a golden opportunity, since *he’d* come to *me.*

“Fine, suppose. Didn’t leave the house much.”

Malfoy nodded. “Me neither.”

It was so weird, and so normal, and so weird that it was normal, and so normal that it was *weird.* He wasn’t being hostile, he was just… Catching up?

“Can I ask you something?”

He turned a suddenly very piercing gaze up at me.

“So long as it’s not ‘why do I fancy you.’”

I felt my shoulders slump a centimetre, and noted the flutter in his expression as he perceived it, but decided it was something I could learn further down the line. He was doing impressively well at being nice, and I didn’t want to scare him off. So I pushed that one to the back of my mind.

“What’s it like being gay?”

His eyebrows shot up in unison, and he scoffed, but there was a warmth on the back of it. Like *laughter.* I’d never heard him laugh, and something about the way his unease disintegrated made my stomach flip again.

“Rubbish,” he answered, reaching up to scratch behind his ear. “Not-not that anyone *knows,* persay, but… Lads always assume I’m coming onto them when they find out.” I nodded, not commenting on the current situation he now found himself in, and watched his throat work as he tried to continue. “O-older gay men can be awful creeps, too.”

A subtle shudder went through his shoulders, and I felt a tight ring of sympathy pull around my heart. *Not like being straight is much easier, but I hadn’t considered what* liking men *might be like, and all that would entail.*

“What’s it like being straight?” he returned, and I blew out my cheeks.

“Awful.” Malfoy *actually laughed* at that, and I thought my brain might leak out of my ears. “Women are…” My lips pressed into a tight line as I considered all the *‘luck’* I’d had with women. And Malfoy waited patiently, openly curious. Seeing his face without a single line of scorn on it was… “Confusing. Infuriating. Mercurial.”

“Mercurial?” Malfoy turned the corners of his lips down, eyebrows shooting up again. “Didn’t know you had a vocabulary like that.”

I smirked at the back-handed compliment, a pit of unease growing in my gut even as the ease of our conversation lit something warm in my chest.

“Hang about Hermione long enough, it rubs off on you.” He grunted at that, and something about his shoulders seemed to relax. Even pulled a foot up onto my chair, resting his chin on his knee and studying his sock as he pulled at a loose string.

“So…” I winced as he glanced back up at me through fair lashes. “D-do you want to…” Not wanting to see his reaction to my question, I turned my grimace down at my lap. “T-talk about the w-*war*?”

Could’ve heard a pin drop in the silence that followed my question, and I braced myself for what his answer might be. If it hadn’t been so quiet, I mightn’t have heard his response, it was so soft.

“Do you?”

Having heard how different his side of things was at the Manor had left me damnably curious, and I figured I didn’t have to worry about seeing *pity* on his face, so I cautiously returned my attention up to his wide, wary eyes.

“R-reckon we sh-should.”

*Fuck me, this is going swimmingly. Can’t I get through one whole sentence without stuttering??* I saw a flicker of guardedness flash across Malfoy’s face, but then he lowered his knee, spreading out to cross his legs over my desk chair as he mirrored me.

“I’m… ‘m sorry about all the shit he made you do,” I decided to begin, because an apology was an easy way to begin a difficult conversation, right? As he nodded, though, a sort of vacancy came over his expression and I wasn’t sure if I’d said the right thing.

“You don’t know the half of it,” he said hollowly, and swallowed. I was… *Probably* being nosey, but I was just so damn curious, I carefully slid off my bed to sit at his feet, legs still crossed and peering up at him. Since I’d put myself into his line of sight, he met my eyes and the shell-shock I saw there tightened the string around my heart.

Always, when I’d heard my friends talk about the war, their faces would contort in grief. Tears and wobbly voices, but Malfoy’s was just… Empty. It was like looking in a goddamn mirror, and it was suddenly so easy to *do this,* because it was an expression I’d seen on my own face too many times to count. It made me want to–

“M-my father,” he continued, seeing me wait so intently, “always thought I was too, erm… Soft.” He managed a half of a weary smirk, but I only listened. And he took a shuddering breath as he went on. “So… He… He’d v-volunteer me to–” He paused, voice seeming to catch in his throat as he looked anywhere but at me, and I ventured to shift my gaze back to my lap in the fear that my attention was too overwhelming. “T-*torture* people… To toughen me up.”

I felt my eyes go wide at the image his words conjured in my head.

“Christ,” I whispered under my breath.

“All the *fucking time,*” he breathed. “I was sick the first time.” His weak scoff lifted my eyes back to his face, and he seemed very, very far away. “And the second time. And the third time.”

“That’s… *So* fucked up.” He suddenly seemed to remember I was there, and turned a fluttering glance down at me. “I’m… *Shit,* Drac–” His name caught in my throat, and I swallowed. Those grey eyes went wide at me just as a hint of life rose back to his pale cheeks, and I started to apologise, but–

“‘S fine,” he said hastily, clearing his throat. “Rather that than my *father’s* name, honestly.”

His lips twitched like he was trying to smile, but being altogether unsuccessful. So with a deep breath, I tried again.

“D-Draco.”

For the first time in my fucking life, he *smiled* at me. Not a sneer, not a smirk, but a genuine smile, eyes bunching at the corners, the whole bit. And it did several funny things to me. This time, I cleared my own throat, and was desperately searching for a way to fill the awkward silence, when he did it for me.

“Sorry about… Everything *you* had to go through, too.”

*A night for firsts,* I realised in a distant corner of my head because I couldn’t recall a single other time anyone had ever just said *sorry that shit happened to you.*It was always *'thank you'* or *'well done,'*not a single damn *'that must've sucked.'* My throat tightened, and I dropped my eyes back to study my pyjama pants.

“Y-yeah…” And seeing the horror, the vacancy, the shellshock on Mal- *Draco’s* face, somehow my lips came as loose as my lungs. “Don’t know the half of it, either.”

*Is this it? Is this really it? For some reason, talking to Ginny, Hermione, even Ron feels impossible, but just one glimpse into his trauma, and I’m ready to talk??*

He just waited silently as I cycled through my emotions, but eventually I swallowed thickly and forced the words out.

“Of all the shit that I went through, fucking *killing a professor* when I was eleven, losing Sirius, the battle…” Once the first word was out, they all just fountained out of my lips like a faucet and I somehow found the wherewithal to lift my eyes back to his. And spoke softly. “Dying was probably the worst.”

I couldn’t begin to understand *why now, why him,* but he still wasn’t running. His eyes did go wide, his lips opened slightly, and the fact that it was *Draco bloody Malfoy* to be the first to hear this confession was so wrong. But I didn’t want to stop.

“Wait… But you– You didn’t *die,* you’re right here in front of me!” His brow cinched in confusion, and I smiled dryly. It didn’t make much sense to me either, but beyond a doubt, I *knew*–

“I did.” His protest died in his mouth as mine released the most *cursed fucking admission* I’d ever said out loud. “I walked into those woods *knowing* I was going to die. Just…” Draco snapped his teeth shut. “Didn’t realise I’d get the choice to come back.”

I shrugged noncommittally, as if I hadn’t just finally shared that secret with a single other soul. To the Ministry officials, the reporters, even fucking Ron and Hermione, I’d only ever said I didn’t know what happened, that Voldemort’s Horcrux must’ve taken the blow because the spell hadn’t worked. But I knew it did. The evidence of it was permanently etched into my chest.

“H-how…?”

“Dunno. I was given a choice.” I swallowed again, as if it’d keep the real kicker down, but we were already here and he wasn’t running and I was feeling lighter and lighter by the second, so I let it out: “Didn’t want to at first.”

True horror peeked through Draco’s shock as he blinked down at me, and I suddenly couldn’t look at him anymore.

“I was j-just… *Tired,* y’know? Tired of fighting, of losing everyone, of hearing screams and seeing death, and I just… Didn’t want to come back.”

A shuddering sigh escaped me as I finally, *finally* expunged what was well and truly the most fucked-up thing about it all. Because if Draco was gonna be brave enough to confess his years-buried feelings for me to my face, and brave enough to come back to school and weather his peerage, the Gryffindor in me wasn’t about to be shown up. If I was honest with myself, though, it hadn’t come down to bravery in the moment. I *wanted* to tell him. Wanted him to know someone else out there was just as torn up and wrecked and irreversibly changed from the war, and he *still hadn’t run,* so I wasn’t really regretting it, but there was a sticky feeling forming in my ribs as the silence dragged on.

Just as I happened to glance back up at him, only catching a moment’s glimpse of *pure fucking heartbreak,* he slid off the chair, dropped to his knees, and pulled me into a heart-wrenching hug. I blinked.

“I–” *Oh, shit. Have I said too much?* Draco’s voice was full of foreign emotion as he crushed my shoulders to his chest. “I’m *so fucking sorry. God,* I can’t… Can’t even *imagine.*”

My mental faculties all stuttered to a halt as I registered the fact that *Draco Malfoy is hugging me.* And I, strangely, didn’t want him to let go.

“‘S alright,” I managed, awkwardly bringing my hands up to his back. “‘M here, aren’t I?”

His shoulders lurched under my touch, and perhaps it was the strangeness of it all, but I… *Wrapped my arms back around him.* He was nearly in my lap as I pulled our chests together, feeling his stumbling heartbeat through his ribs.

*This is so much better than fighting,* that distant part of my mind said as I sat on the floor of my dorm in Draco Malfoy’s arms. *We could’ve been doing this the whole time if things hadn’t worked out the way they did.*

Eventually, he released me from his embrace, and I delicately returned my arms to my lap. As he sat back on his heels, I noted a faint pinkness to his eyes and nose. His eyes were wet, but no tears had streaked down his splotchy cheeks.

“S-sorry,” he said, shaking his head as if he’d just come back to his senses. And I flashed him the best smile I could manage.

“You’re alright,” I croaked. And cleared my throat. “Reckon I needed that.”

His lips fluttered in a similarly unconvincing smile, and he sniffed hard. Just then, a yawn overtook me as my body remembered just how little sleep I’d gotten the previous night.

“I’ll… I’ll let you get some sleep.” He clambered to his feet somewhat gracelessly, using my chair for support, and I did the same with my bed.

“Right,” was the best I could come up with. Draco wiped his palms on his hips, looking around like he might’ve brought something to forget leaving.

“Right,” was his succinct answer as he turned for the door. Before he left, though, hand on the doorknob, he turned back to me. “Th-thanks for… Talking to me, or whatever.”

I nodded, feeling oddly lightheaded and dizzy from the sudden turn of events.

“Same, I guess.” He nodded in return. “G-good night?”

“Y-yeah. Good night.”

And he left.
