## Chapter 173: Chapter 173

“You know,” I said to Draco in our dream that night as we sat, side by side on the swingset of my local playground in Little Whinging. Idly swaying in the last golden rays of sunset. “I never really *thought* about what a soul was before. Obviously, I knew they existed, because Riddle found a way to tear his own into *sevenths,* but never gave it any deep thought. It’s… Sort of *mad* to be learning in such a *hands-on* way now.”

Draco hummed contemplatively, leaning his head against his own swing’s chain as he thought.

“We could go to school our whole lives and probably never learn *everything* there is to know about magic. Christ, we’re still *discovering* shit about it.”

A gruff laugh huffed out of me as I leaned back, face to the colourful evening sky as I held myself up by the chains of my swing.

“Reckon there’s shit about soulbonds that hasn’t even been *recorded,* honestly,” I mused as I swung gently. And Draco flipped around in my peripherals to lay his stomach against the swing, attention firmly on my face as I twisted it towards him. “Ancient though it might be, we’re making *cutting edge* discoveries, you and I.”

He mirrored my smirk back at me, and dropped his gaze to the gravel to fuck with it.

“D’you… D’you really think there’s a… *R-risk,* with us? With Fieridem, I mean.”

A sigh escaped me, because wasn’t that the fucking *question* as I released the swing’s chains to fold backward over the swing and touch my head to the gravel.

“I mean… I’ve never felt like you were *disappearing,* any of the times we’ve done it. You’re always *there,* and *real,* y’know?” I reached below my head to run my fingers through the soft gravel like he was, and scooted down the swing so it was at my mid-back. “Probably got a stronger soul than you realise, honestly.”

I felt Draco’s *shock* at my assessment, and twisted an upside-down glance at him.

“H-how do you figure?”

How indeed. Swivelling around to brace my stomach against the swing like he was, I tried to put my thoughts in order. But they started spilling out of me before I could correct them all.

“You’ve insisted me into submission *several* times by now, you haven’t broken any of the times you’ve touched my soul, you’re still *intact* after experiencing a *wealth* of Fieridem, you’re able to handle *wielding my magic,* you–”

“Stop,” he interrupted, hand clapped to his brow as I paused. “Stop, you… You made your point.” Our swings slowed to a halt as I waited for him to process my assessment. And he swallowed. “Y-you’re not going *easy* on me… Right?”

The pure *terror* fluttering out of him expressed itself on his face, and I reached for him. He met me with his own hand, and I grasped it, pulled him close as our swings would allow.

“Draco, you survived horrors that would’ve broken some of the *strongest* of men.” His eyes guttered as they dipped to my lips once, and he exhaled a stuttering sigh as they returned to mine. “You’re not weak.”

An *amalgam* of emotions raced through his subconscious to hear me say this, and I waited for the process. Sort of rattled my *own* brain to be carried along such a string of dichotomous feelings, but I didn’t fight it. Let myself be carried, because I was sure if I even *wanted* to resist, our shared dream would fracture. And neither of us wanted to dream alone ever again.

“R-right,” he eventually wheezed. Gripped my hand tighter and met my gaze again. “M-maybe *weakened,* but… Holding.”

I felt a small smile pull at my lips at his internal assessment. And nodded.

“No one knows the mettle of the sword that’s never been tested,” I said. Dunno where I came up with that, or where I *heard* it, but it felt right. “And *bloody hell,* *you’ve* been tested.”

A self-deprecating chuckle squeezed out of him as he pulled my hand up to his lips. Pressed a kiss to my knuckles and then let me swing sideways away from him.

“You would know, I s’pose.”

Earned a chuckle of my own as I eyed him subtly.

“Yeah. I *would.”*

Thankfully, none of our peers were there to pressure us to attend the mutual, silent study session, but I still felt anxiety shifting around Harry’s ribs as we ate.

*Some last-ditch effort to cram isn’t gonna help anything,* I reminded him, because I was *right,* and he *knew* I was right, but still sent me an apologetic smile around his mouthful once I’d made the observation.

*I know. It’s just… NEWTs, y’know? The final exam, the last hurdle before we’re out of this place… Forever.* He turned a sombre expression back down to his plate as he swallowed. *It’s… Weird, thinking about how tomorrow will be the last day we spend in the castle as students.*

I leaned forward on an elbow against the table as I chewed. Watched him carefully as he assembled another bite.

*It’s the closest to home you’ve ever felt.* Harry paused, fork in his mouth as my assertion washed over him. *It only makes sense that it feels wrong to be leaving it. But you’ve a whole nother home waiting for you.*

Slowly, carefully, he pulled his fork out of that mouth and sent me another hesitant glance.

*Goats and chickens and horses?*

I smiled.

*Whatever you want. Me, the cottage, the land, the animals, the cat. Home and family forever.*

He chewed. Studied me, and I felt his observation soul-deep. Scoping out my… My *trust* of all things. Trust in him, in our ability to create said future, in *happiness.* He’d shown me visions of a future I’d never been able to even *imagine,* but my imagination had run off with them. And I watched his posture relax slightly as he found that *future* of *us* in me. And mirrored my smile back as he swallowed again.

*Never been able to picture a good life before you.*

My own swallow slid down painfully tight as I absorbed his words. His murder of a professor out of self-defence first year, his face-off with a *bloody basilisk* second, with dementors in third, with the *literal Dark Lord* fourth, the war in fifth year, *sixth year…* Of *course* he’d never been able to picture a good life. And his recollection of our imagined future rose behind my eyes.

Him reading on the couch. The hearth burning, snow drifting outside. Me strolling into the living room in nothing but skin, inquiring about my jumper. Our snog, our *dance.* My invitation back to bed. My throat *barely* permitted another swallow down it as I looked at him. At Harry. My husband, my soulbound, my *life.* My entire *heart,* for *far* longer than he even *knew.* And knowing I’d get to *keep him,* just like I’d asked in my mangled fucking proposal. A cough burst out of me, and I dropped my fork to raise a fist to my lips to catch it.

Harry’s eyes were soft. His *soul* was soft as he watched me put myself back together. He was *so in love* with me, it felt like a *lightning bolt* through my heart, my *soul.* I was on the verge of *tears* as he pulled a predictable fucking *smirk* at me and forked another couple asparagus into his mouth.

*Draco Potter sounds fucking ridiculous,* he said, and my next cough was a *laugh.*

*Yeah. S’pose I’ll just have to deal with it.*

And he kissed me. Mouth full of food, slightly chuckling against my lips as he *burned* his love into my soul. Like a *brand,* like a… Like a *scar,* like the only disfigurement I’d ever, *ever wanted.*

*Love you so so much.*
