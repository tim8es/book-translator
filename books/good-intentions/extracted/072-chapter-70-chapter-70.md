## Chapter 70: Chapter 70

Chapter Notes

I surprised *myself* by pulling Draco away from the throng of our peers on their return to the classroom. Hand firmly in mine, I led him towards the courtyard and out into the daylight. And *giggling,* he went with me.

The sunshine was *heavenly* in the coolness of the Autumn air, and I whipped out my wand to summon our brooms. Wherever they’d ended up after the last time, I turned back to face him, hands clasped behind my back and wand still in hand as I grinned at him.

“What’s this? *Harry Potter,* *skiving?”*

A chuckle burst through my lips as he pocketed his hands, easy smirk on his face and throat *abused. God,* he was so gorgeous.

“Used to do *much* worse, you know,” I cackled, halting my wandering so he could catch up to me. And he paused toe-to-toe with me, smirking curiously down at me.

“Oh yeah? Like what?”

I shrugged. “Brewed Polyjuice potion to sneak into Slytherin second year.”

Draco’s features slacked with shock.

“Bollocks.”

I heard the whoosh before I saw it, but when I twisted to catch my broom, his clattered to the stone. And I turned a smug smile back at him.

“Grilled you about the *heir of Slytherin* as Goyle.”

Draco sputtered on *nothing,* and I laughed.

“You *didn’t.”*

“Ron was there, too. Cr–” My voice died as I recalled Crabbe’s untimely end. And a swallow wrought down my throat. “N-nevermind.”

I stepped around him to collect his broom from the ground, and when I straightened to offer it back to him, he was just as dumbfounded as I’d left him.

*“Bastard,”* he accused as he thrust an arm out to accept his broom. And I smirked again.

“Come on.”

We took to the skies. Draco just sort of followed where I flew, and I didn’t really have a plan. So we ended up circling the West Tower idly as I swivelled to sit sideways on my broom.

“So,” I began as he twisted his legs to mirror me. “Ron and Mione.”

“Yeah,” he assented, reaching up to tug at his earlobe. “Figured that’s what you were talking about.”

*“God,* it’s so mad. I mean, *inevitable,* but *Christ…”*

Draco braced his hands against his broomstick, dangling his feet over the open air as I sighed.

“When’s her birthday?”

“Saturday.”

“Mm.”

Seeing him, windswept and flushed, swinging his legs over the edge of his broom sent my stomach twisting for some reason. I wanted to ask him how he felt about it, his *opinions* on marriage, since he’d come from an *arranged marriage*, but only pressed my lips together. Wasn’t sure these were answers I *wanted.* If he wanted to tell me, of course I’d listen, but to actually *ask…*

“So he’s… *Definitely* proposing this Saturday?”

He peered back up at me, eyes curious but *vulnerable* somehow. And I swallowed.

“Y-yeah,” I choked. “Got the ring ready and all.”

Draco hummed again, frowned down at the ground. And I suddenly wanted solid earth beneath my feet, so I angled my broom downward, and started a steady descent. And he followed. All the way to the Quidditch Pitch, where we dismounted. I threw myself back onto the grass, staring up at the sky and inhaling the scent of clean earth as he settled back beside me.

“You happy for them?” he asked. Genuinely, like there was a chance I *wouldn’t* be happy for my best friends getting hitched. But I thought about it.

“I just… *Yes,* but…” My feelings wouldn’t assemble themselves into thoughts, so it was with a sigh that I rolled onto my side, one arm beneath my head as Draco mirrored me. With no thoughts to form into *words,* I just let my mouth talk for me. “It’s like, I just can’t even *imagine* betting on happiness so surely. My whole life I’ve spent on the edge of my fucking *seat,* waiting for the next terrible thing to happen. Bracing for things to get *worse*, and to be able to accept that they *won’t* is just…” I huffed another sigh as Draco’s eyes softened. “Dunno how to reasonably expect… *Good.*

“I-I *want* good,” I continued. *“God,* I want good. Want mundane and easy and fucking *normal,* but I just… I don’t *trust* it, so for him to be planning on *happiness* is just… Like… Y’know?”

Draco scooted closer to me on the short grass, pushed a hand through his hair and pierced me with a stare.

“Yeah,” he said at length. “I’m sort of figuring it out for myself, too.” He frowned into a middle-distance, reached out for my hand like he didn’t realise he was doing it, and spoke carefully. “I… Don’t think it’s necessarily about expecting nothing bad to ever happen again. That’s sort of ridiculous, because life just isn’t *like that.* But… It’s more like…” Here, his eyes refocused on mine. “Ron’s *choosing* happiness. He’s decided to take matters into his own hands and *make it happen.”*

I released a breath I hadn’t realised I’d been holding as his words sunk into me.

“Y-yeah,” I choked, suddenly caught in a flurry of emotion. “S’pose you’re right.”

He smiled, stroked the back of my hand with his thumb, and I felt a smile of my own answer his. *Ron’s making happiness. Fucking incredible.*

*Regardless of the form it takes, happiness is a choice I can get behind.*

---

The rest of the day, Harry was sort of… *Soft*. Spoke softly, smiled softly, touched gently. After our conversation on the pitch, I couldn’t help but wonder what was going on in that gorgeous head of his, but didn’t have the heart to ask. Just seeing him so… *Pleased? Peaceful?* Whatever it was, it was *deathly* adorable and I found myself being drawn into the warm glow radiating from him. It was like I’d somehow answered a question he never knew to ask.

“Are you *sure* you don’t want *any* sort of healing?” he asked at dinner as I, once again, hissed upon sitting. And I flinched a smile at him.

“‘M fine, I promise.” He sighed, and took my hand off the table.

“Least let me do this,” he grumbled quietly. And again, *gently*rotated my wrist to face my palm upward. Withdrew his wand and muttered a healing spell over my little half-moon cuts. I was sort of sad to see them go, but they’d begun to itch, so I let him do my other hand.

“Cheers.”

He was sunset-pink when he nodded, pocketed his wand again, and began assembling his plate. It was at that moment our peers started filing into the slowly filling Great Hall. Ron and Hermione took their usual seats, the latter clearly giving us disapproving silence for having skipped, but I couldn’t quite gather any regret for my afternoon with Harry. His presence was *always* bliss.

“Alright,” Ron grunted, a wild sort of fraying about his eyes, and Harry just hummed in reply. *Blimey, I don’t know him at all like how I know Harry, but Ron looks positively frazzled.*

I sort of pitied the bloke, but at the same time, couldn’t help but respect him. To have been through hell and back and *still* choose happiness was a resilience I envied. If Harry hadn’t taken all the steps towards me, I mightn't have ever had the courage to go for what I wanted. Wouldn’t have had the *suspension of belief* to delude myself into hoping this was even *possible.*

Harry’s quiet softness started to infect me through the course of dinner, and by the time we were back in the common room, the fire was too inviting not to sit by. As if the warmth he’d lent me pulled like to like, we sat on the loveseat closest to the heat, Harry sort of in my lap as I looped an arm around his shoulders.

“Love you,” I whispered as he curled his head into my chest. And pressed a kiss to that soft brown hair.

“Love you more,” was his subdued response, which earned a snort from me.

“Not in a million years.” His shoulders shook once with a quiet laugh, and he hooked an arm around my waist.

“Reckon between me and you, we could craft a new Philosopher’s Stone and then we’d just have to wait and see.”

The door from the rest of the castle swung open, and Harry didn’t even lift his head off my chest to see Neville and Luna stride through. They acknowledged us, and I smiled. Couldn’t *not* smile, drowning in Harry’s scent as I was.

*I think I actually prefer when he doesn’t smell like me. He smells like home.*

“Night,” Luna waved as they disappeared up the steps, and I sent her a little finger-wave as Harry took a deep inhale for the sigh he loosed against me.

“Neither of us has ever really had a say over our lives, have we?”

I went sort of stiff at Harry’s words, returning my hand to idly run through his hair.

“Can’t imagine there’s much *anyone* can control about what happens to them, but… Specifically you and I? It’s a resounding *fuck* no.” He huffed another little laugh as I heard a door shut upstairs. And the longer he remained silent, the more curious I grew. “Talk to me.”

“It’s just…” Another sigh. “As a child, you can’t *wait* to become an adult so you can make your own choices. But from the prophecy to Dumbledore’s *puppeteering,* I… Here I am, a proper adult, and I have *no fucking idea* how to exercise my will. What that even *looks* like.”

I frowned. “Puppeteering?”

“Y-yeah, I… Literally didn’t even know the half of it until *just* before walking into the forest that night, but… He’d essentially been, like… *Grooming* me to be sacrificed.” He swallowed loudly, and my mind went silent as the fucking *grave.* “Knew everything from the get-go, planned it all out, lied to me about... *So* much, but I… I-I get it, I mean… He was trying to save the *world,* and what’s one life weighed against the many?”

*Holy shit… I knew father hated the man, and only tried to get him fired to push his own agendas, but… Still, to allow all that shit to happen to a*child…

“That’s… *So* fucked up,” I eventually croaked. “So… *Slytherin.”*

Harry coughed a weak laugh. “Yeah, you’re not wrong. He fucking *knew Sirius was innocent,* and let him *rot* in Azkaban for–”

His voice cut off as the tone tightened, and I pulled him closer to me. *He knew I’d been trying to kill him. Knew I’d let the Death Eaters into the castle, that I wouldn’t be able to do it, that Snape would do it for him, that Harry would witness his death…* My mind whirled a *nauseating* tumult as it raced to process all the manipulations our former Headmaster had woven. Not quite a villain, but *decidedly* south of *good.*

*“Fucking* hell,” was all I could manage as another piece of Harry slipped into place. As heartbreaking as it was to consider, I wanted *every* piece. Every facet of trauma, of pain and betrayal and harm and I think for the first time in my fucking *life,* I… Wanted to share mine. I eyed my left arm, resting on the armrest as a frown crumpled my brow.

Sort of numb, sort of *absent,* I reached my left arm to the hand around Harry, and tugged my sleeve up. Frowned down at the brand in my skin. And Harry loosed the arm around me to trace delicate fingertips over the design.

“My mother cried the night I got this,” I said quietly. Swallowed thickly. “Not… Not in front of any of them, but later in my r-room. She thought I was asleep.” Harry’s fingers paused, but didn’t stop. “Begged my father not to let him give me the kill order.” A beat. “She had a bruised jaw the next day.”

The fingers on my skin suddenly gripped my arm. Not tight, but firm. And I flexed my hand, recalling the subdued resignation in mother’s eyes after he’d hit her.

“She loves you a lot,” was his hushed response. And I felt my brow cinch even tighter. She’d been making strides lately, but talking about this just invited the bitterness to swell in me.

“S’pose it’s just a *Malfoy trait* to be shit at showing it.” A pang of *I-don’t-want-to-talk-about-this* nearly knocked the *breath* out of me, but Harry’s thumb started stroking small circles into my arm, and I remembered *who* I was telling. And I swallowed again. “She just… *Stood there* when he made me t-torture those people. Cleaned up my sick afterward. Just… Just *let him.”*

The tightening of my throat was starting to affect my speech, and Harry pulled himself up into my lap fully, wrapped his arms around my neck and laid his head against mine.

“‘M sure they’d let me kill him,” he supposed, and I coughed a laugh as I wrapped my arms back around him. “Kingsley’d understand.”

*I love him so goddamn much.*
