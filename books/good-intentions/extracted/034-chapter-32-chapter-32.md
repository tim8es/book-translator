## Chapter 32: Chapter 32

Honestly, I couldn’t wrap my fucking head around it. The fact that not only had Gran- *Hermione* and *Ron known* Harry had a thing for me, but they were already acting like it was the most normal thing in the *world* that we were dating. Like it’d been a long time coming, like this was just another fucking *Thursday.* Felt like my heart was gonna crawl up my *throat.*

When the bell for lunch rang, I was gathering my shit to put it all away and nearly *actually choked* on my heart when Hermione bounded up to us confidently.

“Hey, Draco,” she started, loud enough for anyone to hear. And a few people paused. “Wanna eat lunch with us?”

I gaped at her, *painfully* aware of the stares we earned, but strove to absorb some of the pure fucking *Gryffindor* in the air as I glanced over at Harry. His lips were pressed together, likely to keep from smiling (though it didn’t fucking work) as he nodded encouragingly. And I cleared my throat, hoping to dislodge my heart from it.

“A-are you sure…? I wouldn’t want to… *Impose.”* My voice grew quieter the more I spoke, but even though it was one of the Golden Trio herself extending the invitation, I wasn’t about to just jump at the opportunity to sit at a House table I didn’t belong to. And was suddenly reminded of my hearing at the Ministry, how fucking *exposed* I felt.

“I mean, you don’t *have* to, but you’re welcome to. Right, Harry?”

Harry nodded a *bit* too eagerly, eyes bright as stars.

“That’s a great idea. You’ll sit with us, right?”

I couldn’t help but check the reactions around us and warmth rose to my cheeks as I noted the way a few lingered in the doorway to hear my answer while others just openly *watched.* Like they couldn’t really believe what they were seeing. So it was with an anxious pull at my earlobe that I returned my attention up to Hermione’s expectant face.

“I-I suppose…”

Her smile was all satisfaction as she gave a nod of approval. “Excellent. Let’s go.” Not even bothering to *pretend* to care what anyone else thought, she all but *hauled* me out of my chair and I stumbled to keep pace as she led me from the room, weaving through the attending throng like they all weren’t bloody *staring* at us. But as I sent a *help-me* sort of glance back at Harry, I saw that *ridiculous* grin illuminating his face as Ron leaned in to whisper something in his ear. And he shook his head affectionately.

*Bloody helluva plan, Hermione.*

Once we were out in the corridor, she surprised me even further by *wrapping an arm around my elbow* and chattering to me animatedly as we walked. Like she was just *thriving* off of the drama she was causing. And I… Couldn’t deny, even if it was a bit at my expense, it was… *Amusing.*

“...not that it’d’ve been *fair* to the other players, but to not even give him a *chance* to try out is even more unfair, don’t you think?”

My mind raced to catch up to what she was saying as I endeavoured to ignore our gawking audience. *Players? Wait, try out…? Quidditch. Got it.*

“D’you think he’d even want to *play* again this year, though? ‘S not like we’ll be here the full year anyway.”

She huffed a little sigh. “That’s not the point. The point is they didn’t even give him the *choice,* even if he had turned them down. This could’ve been the last chance for him to play for Gryffindor!”

“Reckon it’d be more unfair to the *other houses* if they had let him on,” Ron supposed as he sidled up to Hermione’s other side, hands in his pockets. And he sent me a sidelong smirk. “‘Less Slytherin has some *hidden ace Seeker* up their sleeve?”

I scoffed, because he surely wasn’t referring to *me -*I’d only ever decided to play to get closer to Harry anyway - but was feeling particularly pleased with myself for being able to carry a conversation at *all,* the way the crowd parted and stared and *whispered* as the Golden Trio fucking *escorted me* to the Great Hall. And Harry sort of drifted up to my other side, alerting all my senses to his presence as I looked over at him.

He was just pleased as *punch,* but his fingers were fidgeting with the newest little rock I’d gifted him like he wasn’t sure what to do with his hands. Not to mention the shade of his *ears…*

“He knows I’m always down for a friendly game of one-on-one, don’t you, Harry?” He turned a surprised but suddenly mischievous smile back at me. “A little Seeker’s game?”

“You know I’d *slaughter* you,” he supposed, quirking an eyebrow at me. “But if you *insist* on getting your arse handed to you, I’m game.”

“Typical Gryffindor *arrogance,* what if I’ve been practising this *whole time?**”* *Banter. We’re bantering in broad fucking public view.*Harry smirked.

“One way to find out, I suppose.”

His eyes sparkled with challenge, and against all *possible, realistic, believable* odds, I fell a little more in love with him.

---

The shock that followed us to the Gryffindor table was understandable, really. We were including Draco without even like, any preliminary steps. Like we’d secretly all been friends this whole time. Which was probably like seeing a Dementor make a daisy chain, but I couldn’t be bothered to care. Didn’t even really *acknowledge* it, because I was on top of the fucking *world.*

Elation wouldn’t let my smile leave my face for even a heartbeat as we settled into the far end of the table, casual as anything. Well, Draco was stiff as a board, but he was putting on a good show by holding conversation. He lost a bit of colour in his cheeks when we started eating, since there came with it a natural lull in conversation, but he was bracketed in by me on his right and Ron and Hermione across from us, so I hoped he’d take some inspiration from our nonchalance and just *relax.*

I paused at one point, though, because I realised I’d selected a sandwich that had too much going on and was picking it apart with a fork to separate its contents by category, just generally being a fucking *freak,* and glanced over at Draco.

He was watching me, of course. Little smile on his lips, fork loose in his hand as he slowly chewed his mouthful. A flush rose to my cheeks, but I was starting to smile back when Hermione cleared her throat, snapping both of our attentions up to her. She raised her eyebrows pointedly at us, and leaned across the table to hiss:

*“Very fucking subtle,*” and I tried not to look too shocked that she’d *swore.* Hermione *never* swore. But as she eased back, pressing her lips together and looking altogether fucking *complacent,* Ron groaned and a little burst of laughter coughed through my lips.

“Is this what it’s like being around *us?**”* he asked her, forking another *half a potato* into his mouth and looking mournfully at her.

“Not at all, I don’t think. I mean, no one bats an *eye* when I do *this,*” and she took his face and smacked a kiss on his cheek, turning it bright red beneath her lips.

“Alright, alright, fuck you *very* much, I’m trying to *eat,*” I grumbled as I turned back to my disassembled sandwich. And Draco snorted, leaning over his plate on an elbow.

“Don’t wanna make this into a competition with us, anyway, I reckon,” he supposed quietly as he took another bite of salmon. And the way he chewed around his grin, I *leapt* on board.

“Absolutely *not,* have you *met us?* I mean… Sweet and *unbearably in love* as you two are, I can think of like *thirty* ways to make you lose your appetites off the top of my *head.*”

Draco choked on his mouthful, lifting an elbow to cough/laugh into it as Hermione’s cheeks flushed to match Ron’s. And it wasn’t until that *very moment* that I let myself acknowledge *this. This* is what it’d look like to include Draco in the dynamic. I glanced from Draco, laughing into his elbow, to Hermione as she began stammering some excuse, Ron’s affectionate exasperation, and fucking *memorised* it.

*Didn’t think I’d ever be this happy again after everything.*

*Christ, I think I* am *falling in love with him.*
