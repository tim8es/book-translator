## Chapter 49: Chapter 49

Harry was just as flushed as he’d *ever been* as he sat up, leaning back on his hands as he surveyed the Black Lake. And I carefully curled up beside him, pulling my feet towards me to sit cross-legged as I eyed him.

“T-tell me more about… Sea creatures,” he requested. And I about *launched* myself at him, but held back. Pressed the lips that were *aching* for his together as I scrambled through all the trivia I held in my head.

“Erm… Well, did you know sharkskin is rough because it’s covered in teeth?”

He turned a bemused expression toward me, and I smiled.

“Liar,” he supposed, and I shook my head.

“They’re called *dermal denticles.* How fucking ironic is that?”

He scoffed, lips curling into a half-smile, and it knocked all the air out of my fucking *lungs. Christ, when do I get used to him smiling at me?*

“Aht!” he shouted suddenly, pointing at my hand, and I frowned down at it. My hand had unconsciously curled around something, and I lifted it to reveal what I’d grabbed, and it was…

*“Jesus,”* I breathed as I eyed the little ammonite fossil in my hand. I *swear* I hadn’t meant to grab it, hadn’t even really *seen* it, but the evidence of his vindication was undeniable in my palm. I turned a grimace up at his smug fucking face, clicked my tongue, and threw it at him. It hit his chest, and he chuckled as he chased it down to study it himself. *“Fucking* hell…”

He giggled a bit more as he turned it over in his hand, and then pocketed it. Returned a *blinding* fucking smile up to me, and any annoyance I might’ve been feeling fucking *evaporated.* And I smiled back at him.

“You *really* don’t notice you’re doing it?”

I shook my head, sort of twisting my smile to the side bashfully.

“God, I wonder how much shit I’ve just flat-out *stolen.”* Harry laughed, full and bright and easy despite my horror. *What am I, a fucking kleptomaniac?*

“So fucking *mental,”* he chortled, and I wanted to throw something *else* at him. But I settled for a fistful of grass. *“Oi!”*

He kept laughing, ripping a handful of grass out of the earth to hurl back at me, and I dissolved into laughter. Our grass-fight kept up for a good long while before he just *seized* the front of my jumper and crushed his lips to mine.

*Oh, fuck me. I don’t deserve this. I don’t deserve*him.

*But I’ll fucking take it.*

Mid-afternoon invited us back to the Great Hall, and we walked together, brooms in hand, sort of just *talking* as the castle welcomed us back.

“You *have* to learn how to play Guess Who,” I insisted as we navigated a busy hallway. Despite the stares and the whispers, I was *intent* on teaching Draco about Muggle boardgames. “There’s, like, a range of people you make assumptions about, and each person *gets* a person and you have to try to guess who the other person’s person is by asking questions about them.”

Draco smiled at me, reached to take my hand, and then pocketed it.

“If *I* learn Guess Who, you *have* to learn Boxies.” I quirked an eyebrow at him as he continued. “It’s a game with like a chessboard, but the pieces are… Sort of *independent.* And there’s *plenty* of ways of cheating.”

His smile turned into a sly grin as I huffed.

“Such a *Slytherin,* to suggest *cheating* at a game I don’t even know how to *play.*”

Draco chuckled, and the sound earned my smile. And I couldn’t help but remember we were being watched, observed as my life *always* *fucking was,* but found I really didn’t care. If they saw us being *flirty* with each other, let them. It’s not like the rumours it’d spawn would be *inaccurate.*

We rounded the corner into the Great Hall, and chatted a bit more as we reached the Gryffindor table, and I was pleased not to have winced so hard when we sat, but as we started assembling our plates, the inevitable happened. None other than *Neville Longbottom* paused on his way out of the Great Hall, eyeing us incredulously as I let my attention tear away from Draco. And Neville looked positively *pale.*

“H-hey,” I said carefully. And he looked at me like I’d grown a second *head.*

“What the *fuck* is this?” he asked, like we hadn’t been sitting together for *days.* “I get trying to be *inclusive,* but you two…”

He glanced back and forth between us for a moment as Draco and I exchanged a glance. And I stood with a sigh.

“Look, Neville, I’ll explain everything tonight in the common room, alright? Can you… Actually, can you spread the word? I’d… L-like a word with everyone.”

Feeling the weight of my words in the way Neville’s features slacked, I sucked down a swallow as he nodded.

“Right. Sure, Harry. Just…” He sent another *loathsome* fucking glance at Draco before returning a much more forgiving one at me. “Okay.”

And he paced away. I let out another sigh as I dropped back onto the bench, *minimally* painful, and resumed my meal. Draco said nothing as I sampled the dishes I’d selected.

“Want me there?” he eventually asked, and I twisted a glance at him, fork in my mouth. And I uneasily swallowed the carrot I’d tried.

“E-er… I… Th-think it might be b-*better* if I…”

He nodded, smiling softly as his throat worked.

“‘S fine. Whatever… Whatever you think’s best.”

And I offered the best smile I could manage back.

“Th-thanks.”

*Fucking hell.*
