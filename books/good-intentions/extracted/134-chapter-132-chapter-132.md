## Chapter 132: Chapter 132

Chapter Notes

Being in *France* with the *love of my fucking life* was really like a fairytale. Like a *dream,* like a *movie,* because as wrapped up as we were in each other, there was still *fucking France* outside the doors. Plenty to see within the walls of the villa, gorgeous grounds he walked me around, and the *Place Cachée* waiting for every visit Narcissa could insist us into. On Christmas Eve, we were huddled around warm mugs of varying drinks, just inside the window of the cafe we’d come to several times by now. Hot chocolate for me, oolong and cream for Draco, and Narcissa with a *Café Au Lait*as we watched the gentle snow falling over the darkening evening.

“We *have* to go to the *soirée dansante*on New Year’s Eve,” she exhaled resolutely over her steaming cup. And Draco caught my eye.

“Big fancy ball,” he answered my unspoken question, and my heart *leapt* in my chest.

“W-we’re going *dancing?”*

He smirked, crossing his leg over his opposite knee as he sipped his own tea.

“Oh, yes!” Narcissa exulted, leaning forward to replace her cup in its saucer. And as my attention slid over to her, I saw my own *thrill* reflected in her bright blue eyes. “Dancing and drinking and eating, making *merry* as the new year begins. Cusp of the century!”

The scenario I’d imagined in the villa’s ballroom resurfaced, with witches twirling in colourful, sparkling gowns alongside formally dressed wizards, live music, trays upon *trays* of various dishes, and suddenly I couldn’t fucking *wait.*

But my heart dropped before I could say as much.

“I-I, er… Dunno how to d-dance,” I admitted quietly, sinking back into my seat as my cheeks went warm. Recalling the only other time I'd even *tried,* and how *horribly* that'd turned out as Draco exchanged a glance with his mother, and then back to me.

“I’ll teach you,” he volunteered, leaning forward to similarly set his cup in its saucer. And the warmth in my cheeks changed from humiliation to *pleasure* as a smile crept into them.

“You’re going to teach me to *dance?”*

He grinned, eyes *sparkling* with amusement. “If it’s a *possible feat.”*

Narcissa clicked her tongue and shoved at his shoulder as he cackled, and I just glared.

“What Draco *means* to say is he’d be *honoured*. Ballroom dancing really isn’t so difficult if you have a capable partner.”

Draco sniffed, a *very* pleased smile on his lips as his cheeks went slightly pink.

“Harry’s rather *proficient* at following instructions, too, aren’t you, love?”

Just like *that,* the pleased flush in my cheeks shifted *right* back to humiliation as Narcissa coughed a laugh into her sleeve.

“Hope your dress shoes come with *steel toes,”* I hissed back at him, and chased my embarrassment down with another gulp of hot chocolate. *Fuck me,* but French hot chocolate was *literally* like straight-up molten *chocolate.* None of that watered-down shite that came in the little packets. A *surge* of affection flooded me, and I inhaled a gasp through my nose as I lowered the cup from my lips.

Draco was just pure *beaming* at me, and though I was still a bit *miffed* at his reference, I couldn’t help but offer a small smile back.

*Draco’s taking me dancing. What a fucking life.*

---

Back out in the lazily drifting snow, Harry *finally* bullied me into taking him to the Eiffel Tower. After our venture into Muggle London, I was *slightly* more amenable to Muggle *Paris,* but it was bloody *Christmas Eve,* and tourists were fucking *flocking.* A bit of disillusionment, though, and I cleared a little corner of the observation platform for us. The faint snow was even *fainter* out here, outside the Place Cachée, and though it was still fairly chilly, we were bundled and huddled together and Harry was filling me with such *blistering bliss,* I hardly even felt the chill.

*“Wow,”* he intoned, craning his neck back up at the tower as I glanced over at him. Green eyes *sparkling* with all the lights reflecting in them, pure *awe* in the curve of his smile as an astounded chuckle ghosted in the air before his lips.

Snowflakes had caught in his hair, in his long eyelashes as his flushed cheeks bunched in a smile. Emerald eyes *shining* with awe, and just like *that,* all my reservations for making this anything less than a ridiculous fucking *cliche* *just vanished.*

I’d *sworn* to myself I wouldn’t do this. Not like this, not so… So *typical,* so *predictable,* but seeing him like this, so happy and *gorgeous* and picturesque as the breeze swept snowflakes from his hair only to replace them with *more,* like a delicate snowy crown, I…

I fucking *folded.*

The hand that held his tucked our clasped appendages into my coat pocket as I watched him. Just fucking *floored* with how lucky I was. How *beautiful* he was, the love of my *life,* my entire fucking *world.* And as he turned his chuckling amazement over at me, the wide awe of his eyes softened.

“Draco?”

Throat suddenly tight, I forced a swallow down. Felt my heart skip a beat in my chest, and knew he felt it, too, because concern flashed across his brow.

“N-never get enough of you,” I breathed, echoing his own words back at him, and the worried crease between his eyebrows relaxed. An easy smile spread across his lips as he faced me more fully, and my heart was just *vibrating* in my throat as I invited his fingers deeper into my pocket. To the deepest corner, where– “C-can I keep you?”

His grin grew wider, even as he searched my face, because *fuck,* I was *buzzing* with adrenaline. Terrified and exhilarated and *nervous,* and I knew he could feel it, but then his fingers found what I’d been leading him to, and his grin rearranged itself into *surprise.*

Cold digits fished out my *pocket nicknack,* and I let him. Released his hand with a stuttering sigh as he withdrew what I’d been carting around for *days,* *just in case.* Sparkling green eyes flashed down from mine to his hand as he pulled it out into the air. And I watched as his cold, red fingers slowly uncurled.

I knew what it looked like well enough, had spent *hours* staring at it, contemplating if this was the right thing to do, if we were *ready,* if it was too *soon…* So I didn’t spare it more than a glance before returning my attention to watch his face.

He just… Stared at it. Blinked a few times, like he’d never seen a *fucking ring* before. And as the silence stretched longer, it played fucking *hell* on my nerves. *Oh my god, please say something, just speak, anything, even if you laugh at me, please…!*

*Finally,* after what felt like *eternity,* he blinked up at me. Features slack, eyes *wide* and curiously calm as he stared at me.

“I-is this…?”

I felt a wince pinch my features as I glanced back down at the ring in his hand. But when I found the nerve to meet his gaze again, I nodded. *No going back now. Jesus fuck me, shit, if he says no, I’m throwing myself in the fucking Seine.*

A little flurry of emotion leapt in his chest, but his expression suddenly went fucking *stern.*

“Kneel.”

I scoffed incredulously as he pointed at the ground. *Of course he wants the whole fucking display.* But as I glanced down, I realised I’d do fucking *anything* if the bastard would just *fucking marry me.* So I lowered myself to a knee. Right there in front of the *fucking Eiffel Tower. Bloody hell, this is corny as all fuck.*

But Harry’s heart *swelled* to see me kowtow to him. And I nearly fucking *passed out* to think I was doing *all this* just for him to *reject me.*

“Say it.”

A thick swallow worked down my throat, and I glanced down at his hand again. Still held aloft, gathering snowflakes as he waited. And as my eyes hesitantly danced back up to his, I forced it up my throat like *blood.*

“Marry me,” I exhaled in a rush, breath clouding in the cold air and drifting away. And even though this was so fucking *cliche,* so *painfully* fucking overdone, I suddenly couldn’t help but be anything other than *gut-wrenchingly honest.* “I-I’m serious about forever. And… And I think *you* might be, too, so… If-if you’ll *have* me, which I hope to *god* you will, I’d… There’s nothing I want more in the *entire fucking world* than to call you f-*family.”*

I braced myself. I held my breath. I watched and waited and *agonised* as Harry stared down at me. Didn’t blink for so long, his eyes watered in the cold air, and dripped down his cheeks. *Please,* I wanted to say. Wanted to *beg* him. *Please, please, PLEASE say yes.*

He dropped to his knees before me, landing with a *crunch* against the snow as he stared up at me. Wide-eyed and weeping and suddenly an *incredible* fucking swell of emotion flooded down the bond. Nearly knocked me *over* as it rammed into me, but his fingers curled around the ring in my peripherals.

*“Family,”* he said quietly. And my throat tightened. All I could do was nod, and my head was fucking *spinning* with the combination of my own *wild* fucking anxiety and his *consuming* swell of emotions. He looked back down at his hand, gently unfurled his fingers and eyed the ring. *Fuck, does he not like it? Did he want a diamond? Is there some Muggle tradition here I’ve neglected? What if–*

My tumultuous thoughts stuttered to a halt as he offered the ring onto his ringfinger. Held it aloft and *stared.* And my own heart *lurched* to meet the *tsunami* of emotions he was feeding into me. When he finally turned his verdant attention back to me, the tears in his eyes caught the light of the tower like *fireflies,* and he… He *smiled.*

“Took you long enough, *prick,”* he croaked, and I huffed a laugh as pure fucking *relief* flooded my body like a goddamn *orgasm.* And I sniffed, realising only then that I was crying.

“S-sorry,” I managed to wheeze back, delving into my other pocket to withdraw the matching ring. Took his hand and slid it onto his finger to show him how they nestled together. And he glanced down at it as I pushed it onto him, showed him how perfectly they fit. *Just like us.* “I-I’m just a bloody *chicken,* I guess.”

The rings illuminated in the light of his eyes just before he turned those glowing spheres back up to me. And I coughed another sob. Hand still in mine, he pulled my *moon* ring off, took mine, and looked down. I followed his gaze to watch him offer it onto my own *trembling* ringfinger. Whether it was the cold, or the *act* that had me shaking so bad, I didn’t know. But it fit like a fucking *glove.*

Harry smiled back up at me. And I just *fractured.* Right there in front of the fucking *biggest* tourist attraction in the country. And he pulled me into his arms, cold face against my neck as I wrapped my own arms back around him.

*“Draco,”* he sobbed into my neck as I tightened my grasp around him. “*Fuck,* I… Y-you’re *my family.”*

I could only nod into his thickly coated shoulder as we wept together. *Jesus fucking Christ,* I’d never been one for theatrics, for *showmanship,* but suddenly felt fucking *vindicated* for doing this *here.* *He said yes.*

Well… *No,* he hadn’t said it in so many words. Just put the ring on, and I suddenly *needed to hear it.*

*“S-say yes,”* I pleaded, *leagues* beyond fucking *gone,* but he sobbed a laugh against me.

*“Yes,”* he replied, equally fucking *gone* as our emotions swirled together into this *incomprehensibly* *euphoric*feeling. *“Yes, I’ll marry you. Yes, I love you. Yes, I want you f-forever, Draco.”*

*Hurts wonderful.*
