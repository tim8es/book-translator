## Chapter 121: Chapter 121

Chapter Notes

We finally touched down in Provence after night had fallen, even though we gained an hour. Draco’s anxiety paired with my excitement was making us *both* sort of jittery, but just as we were finally stretching our legs after emerging from the coach, the Malfoy villa stunned me silent.

*I dunno why I assumed it’d be anything less than fucking ginormous. Holy hell.*

It looked like a fucking *castle.* Albeit smaller than a *real* one, but with Hogwarts being my only real basis for comparison, it might as well have just been an actual castle.

“Wow,” I breathed as Draco unloaded our trunks behind me.

“Gaudy, isn’t it?” A bemused scoff burst through my lips.

“Yes, I suppose the *crown moulding* is a bit dated, and who even uses *pillars* anymore on their *fucking castles?”*

He snorted as the doors drew my attention, swinging open to reveal formally dressed staff. And my eyes just went wider and *wider* at the great host of butlers and maids that emerged, forming a *corridor* of bodies to lead us to the entrance. I sent a final, astonished glance over at Draco, and he smirked.

“Come on.”

An arm around mine, he led me up the gravel, and my head was *spinning* to drink in all the details. The men and women (thankfully *not* house elves) on our left and right bowed as we passed, muttering little french phrases, I assumed welcomes and greetings as we ascended the three steps before the front doors and through them.

My jaw actually dropped once we hit the foyer, because it was just the *fucking foyer,* and yet one of the most dazzling crystal chandeliers hung overhead, sending fragments of *rainbows* all over the room. A plush rug decorated the marble floor in whorling patterns, settees and short consoles bearing ridiculous vases of all sorts of flowers, art on the walls, but what had my eyes drifting shut despite the marvellous decorations was the *glorious smell* that wafted to us from somewhere within the… House? Mansion?

*“God,* what *is that?”*

Draco inhaled similarly, and released my arm in favour of my hand.

“French cuisine,” was his sighing answer. *“God,* it’s been *years* since I’ve been back here.”

He let me appreciate the vaulted ceiling and dazzling irises a bit more as the staff started carting our trunks through the foyer, past us and deeper into the structure. I was just sort of in*awe,* because as elegant as Malfoy Manor was, this seemed for all the *world* to be owned by completely different *people*. The art on the walls wasn’t conspicuous, it was *joyful.* The decor wasn’t pretentious and practical, it was sort of *flamboyant.* There was so much light and colour and *warmth*, imagining Lucius in these walls was all of a sudden *ridiculous,* and a giddy little bubble of laughter escaped me.

“There’ll be dinner soon, but…” I turned back to Draco, undoubtedly the most gorgeous thing among the beauty and finery. “I’d… I’d like a moment alone with my mother first, if that’s alright with you.”

I nodded. “Of course. Just… Give us a tug when you want me, alright?”

A stunning smile lit up Draco’s face as he gave an experimental pull on our link, and I stumbled a step closer on instinct, wearing a similar smile.

“Quite like having you at my beck and call,” he murmured just before lowering a *dizzying* fucking kiss to my lips. “I won’t be long.”

---

That length of elastic I used to feel between Harry and I was now a tangible fucking thing as I reluctantly left him in the entryway. It didn’t pull painfully, though, as I followed the familiar corridors to where I knew mother would be waiting for me. More like a tether, a grounding connection that reminded me he was only a thought away. And though I ran a hand through my hair more than once, tried to fix my collar to beat her to it, I took a measure of comfort from the warmth encasing my heart.

A glance through the open doors to the dining room sent my stomach twisting, but first things first. There was no staff waiting outside the sitting room, so I paused before the dark mahogany and braced myself. The last time I’d seen mother was at the Ministry, and our interaction had been more or less chaotic, but now… I was going to tell her *everything.*

The handles turned easily in my grip, and I pushed them into the room. The first thing that greeted me was… *Music. Mother is listening to music.* It was odd, I… Couldn’t recall the last time I’d heard music in any of our estates. But then I saw her head peering over the back of one of the sofas facing the hearth, and she leapt to her feet.

“Draco!”

I allowed myself a few steps further into the room, bracing myself for her to barrel into me, and as she lunged across the room, she didn’t disappoint. *Predictable woman,* I chuckled into her hair as I let my arms wind back around her. She smelled familiar, not quite like *home,* and the feeling of a *woman* in my arms was altogether strange, but these proceedings weren’t so unusual.

“Hello, mother.”

She *squeezed* me, and exhaled a sigh against my shoulder.

*“God,* it’s good to see you. How’ve you been?” Finally, she released me from her grip to study my face, and I sort of winced to realise what she’d see. “Draco?”

Her manicured nails unerringly found my brow, and swept my hair off of it. Alarm sang in her eyes, but I only took her wrist and tried to offer her a reassuring smile.

“I’m *fine,* mother. This is nothing.” She wasn’t having it, though, as the distress on her face resolved into something *hostile.*

“Who did this to you? Tell me, I want *names.”*

I couldn’t help but smile.

“Wouldn’t do you any good, they’re all *dead.”*

The murder in her eyes softened into something like *shock,* and she went slightly pale.

“Draco, you *didn’t–”*

“No! No, *god,* no. *Christ,* woman, who do you think I *am?”* Mother tutted disapprovingly, but let me remove her hand from my face. “Look, I’ll give you all the gory details and spoil your dinner all you like, but I… There’s someone I’d like you to m-meet first.”

Like I’d flipped a *switch*, her eyes lit up. I swallowed as she took my hand in both of hers, and hesitantly sent a little *tug* along to Harry. A wave of anxiety answered it, but I felt him growing nearer.

“Yes, I’ve been *dreadfully* eager to meet this new beaux of yours.” My brow furrowed in a frown at the way she delicately avoided the term *girlfriend.*

*She said she’s been avoiding the papers… There’s no way she*knew–

“R-right… It’s, erm… Actually someone you already *know,* but…” I took a deep breath, released mother’s hands and rocked back on my heels as I felt Harry turn the last corner of the corridor behind me. His quiet footsteps reached my ears as our twin anxieties seemed to play each other up. But then mother glanced behind me, and there was no going back. A little stuttering sigh escaped me as Harry hesitantly joined my side. “M-mother, this is my… M-my *boyfriend.*”

*Christ,* I was so bloody *nervous.* But a warm hand slid into mine, and I turned from mother’s surprise down to Harry and he was flushed to *hell.* He glanced up at me, offered me a wavering little smile, before turning back to mother.

“H-hi, Mrs. Malfoy,” he said formally, awkwardly stiff, and I was all at once reminded of how I’d felt meeting *his* parents.

“*Please,* Harry. Call me Narcissa,” she insisted, and I gripped his hand tighter. She hesitated a step closer, eyes dancing back and forth between us, and just when I was about to launch into a *horribly* mangled explanation of how we’d ended up together, she pulled us *both* into a hug round our necks. And I froze. “This is the most perfect Christmas present *ever.”*

The pinch to her voice registered, and I blinked. *Mother’s crying.* I’d *never* seen mother cry, only heard her the one time after I’d received the Dark Mark. Even when she bore silent witness to my *torture,* she’d remained as stoic as ever. The sound of her sob next to my ear was only familiar from the one memory, but it still made my throat tighten.

When she eventually released us, Harry seemed just as stunned as *I,* because we both just let her cradle our cheeks and offer the same teary smile to us both.

“Oh, I’m so *happy* for you,” she sniffed. “My son’s fallen in *love!”*

*My son’s fallen in love.* Nearly a decade, maybe longer, since I last felt like a *son.* Not a project, not a pawn, not an *embarrassment.*

“I-I’ve–” Harry cleared his throat as mother retracted her touch from us. “M-me too, I’m… W-we–”

Mother chuckled thickly, and took his face to kiss his cheek. *Kiss his cheek.*

*“Thank you,”* she intoned on another sob as she kissed his *other cheek.* Harry went positively *scarlet*. “I n-never got to say it, but *thank you,* Harry. For *everything.”*

He cleared his throat again as she released him, and gave a jerky sort of nod. *Fucking hell, this is not at ALL how I thought this would go.*
