## Chapter 88: Chapter 88

Chapter Notes

Harry stood guard outside the Hospital Wing as I snuck in to replace the pain potion I’d *borrowed,* and then we were strolling back to the dorms, hand-in-hand. His went a bit sweaty in mine as we walked, though, and I sent a curious, quirked brow over at him.

“Alright?” I asked when he wouldn’t meet my gaze. And finally, he sent a hesitant, fluttering smile up at me.

“Y-yeah, course, erm…” *So no.* He scratched the back of his neck, but when he met my eyes again, the floodgates opened. “I-I was thinking, well… I just… I love you a lot, and I-I’d really like it if–” He swallowed, cheeks cherry-red as he grimaced down at the floor. A few first-years dashed past us, but I hardly noticed. “D’you wanna meet my p-parents…?”

My steps stumbled to a halt, tugging Harry with me as I froze in the hallway. We’d briefly discussed it before, but *now…?*

“What… What brought this on?”

Flushed and bashful, he glanced back up at me.

“M-my f-*father’s* Patronus was a s-stag…” His voice trailed off with his explanation, but suddenly it clicked. *Holy hell. My Patronus is the same as James Potter’s.*

*Father would have a conniption.*

A smile crept into my lips as I tugged him closer back to my side. And he agreeably let himself be tugged, fear and hope warring in his expression as he studied my face.

“Yeah. Yeah, I’d love to.”

His throat worked a swallow down, but his eyes smiled back up at me, hope winning out for the moment.

“Y-yeah?”

I nodded. “Absolutely.”

Harry’s hand didn’t lose any moisture as he pulled me the rest of the way up to the dorms, through the common room and up the stairs to our room. Like if he didn’t force himself to do it, he’d chicken out. So I followed, pliant as anything as he let us into the room.

“Right, we… E-er, m-maybe we should, like…” He eyed my neck, wince twisting his features, and I instantly understood.

“Right.” I plunged my hand into my pocket and withdrew my wand, cast a few reluctant concealment spells over my love bites and gave him a twirl to assess me. He plucked a few stray hairs off my jumper, and I sort of chuckled to be clucked over like this. Like he was my *mother.* But I understood.

*Have to make a good impression. Fucking Christ, Harry’s parents.*

When he seemed satisfied, he jerked a nod and manhandled me back to sit on the bed. And with a nervous sigh, he pivoted to approach his desk. I watched his hand hesitate before the drawer, but then he pulled it open and trembling fingers reached for the Resurrection Stone.

*Two Deathly Hallows now. Bloody hell.*

His nerves were starting to affect me as he clumsily shoved the drawer closed and cast a glance back at me. I nodded, and he mirrored me, and then turned his back to me. He took a shuddering breath, and then a certain… Calm. Like he was concentrating.

*Oh god… Here we go.*

---

*Mum and dad.* Thrice turned in my sweaty palm, and I gripped it like it’d fly out of my hand. Sort of *prayed* it wouldn’t work, but then–

“Harry?”

My eyes flew open, and *instantly* filled with tears. *Fuck.*

“D-dad,” I said, like I had any idea what the word meant. James and Lily stood before me, ghostly figures interrupted by my desk, and I hesitated a step back. “H-hi.”

Those eyes, that nose… I’d seen them a handful of times, but the face I saw every day in the mirror seemed parsed between the two of them as they turned concerned frowns down at my hand. The stone. *Right.*

“Harry, what are you doing?” Lily wondered. *My mother. Mum.* I swallowed.

“H-hi, m-mum,” I managed. She turned a pinched worry up at me, and it fucking *wrecked* me as it evolved into *compassion.*

“Harry, love, this sort of thing… It’s *dangerous.”* All the emotions building up in my throat seemed to express themselves on her face as she took my measure. And I held stock fucking still.

“I know,” I assured her, hand fisting around the stone to shove it into my pocket. Fingers still gripping tight, but out of sight so we could have this… This *conversation.*

*Fuck me.*

“Harry, we *love you,”* James implored, hesitating a step towards me and drawing my eye. His hands reached like he’d touch me, but only fisted and dropped to his sides. “Dunno if we’ve ever actually *said* it, but…”

*Dad.*

“Y-yeah,” I croaked, throat tight. “L-love you, too.”

*Shit, this is harder than I’d thought.*

Jam– *Dad* sighed, glanced at mum, and the two seemed to wait for whatever I had to say. Why I’d summoned them. Because I actually *did* have a good reason, beyond just fucking *missing them.*

“There’s–” I cleared my throat, sniffed and swiped moisture from my eyes with my free hand and faced them again. “There’s s-someone I’d like you to m-meet.”

The bed creaked behind me, and then a hand in mine. I glanced up at Draco, who was altogether fucking *peaky* as he glanced between my parents. *My parents.* My hand tightened around his as I looked back at them. A dichotomous blend of mistrust, curiosity, and *wonder* coloured their translucent faces as I swallowed again.

“M-mum, dad, this… This is D-Draco… Malfoy.” I felt him glance at me, and met it for a brief moment before returning to the surprise on both their faces. “H-he’s my b-boyfriend.”

“Ple-pleasure to m-meet you,” he said, all at once formal and fucking *clumsy* as he stumbled over his words. And I took a bit of comfort for the fact that I wasn’t the only one feeling *supremely* out of my depth. L- *mum* rocked back on her heels slightly, eyeing our clasped hands as dad’s brow tightened.

“Boyfriend?” she wondered, eyes now searching my face. And I tried for a smile.

“Y-yeah, erm… I-I…” I sent another glance at Draco, who was just as pale as I’d *ever* seen him as he stood pin-straight beside me. But dad was looking more and more… Confused. “I l-love him a lot, just… W-wanted you to m-meet him.”

They exchanged a glance, and gripped hands like neither of them had *meant* to. And my throat tightened even further as they looked back at us.

“Well…” Mum cleared her throat. “It’s lovely to meet you, Draco.”

“Malfoy?” Dad wondered, still seeming *wholly* confused. “Lucius’s son?”

I felt Draco’s wince through our clasped hands, and he coughed an uncomfortable chuckle.

“Unfortunately,” he croaked. Gulped loudly, but my attention was *rapt* on my parents’ faces. *My parents.*

Dad seemed somewhat placated with his answer, because he glanced down at our clasped hands with a slightly more amenable look in his eyes.

“H-he’s got my Patronus, dad,” I said. Those hazel eyes flashed back up to mine, now full of surprise as I forced another swallow down my tight throat. “A s-stag.”

This surprise was offered back to Draco, and I saw the assessment as he took his measure. Shifted his weight as mum glanced up at him.

And to my *utter* fucking *shock,* he… He *laughed.* *Cackled,* like some inside joke had just come to fruition. And mum rolled her eyes, but leaned into him nonetheless. *God,* it was so *nice* to see them. To see them *together,* and *in love.* I wanted– *Jesus,*I *wanted–*

“*Shit,”* my dad chortled, wiping tears from the corners of his eyes. And my heart swelled in my chest. “Oh, *fuck,* bet Lucy just *loves* this.”

More laughter as I coughed a hesitant laugh of my own, glanced up at Draco. Some colour had returned to his cheeks as he met my glance, lips twitching like… Like a *smile.*

“W-wouldn’t know,” Draco supposed, dragged a hand through his hair. “Bastard’s in Azkaban.”

James’s laughter died in his throat as he righted himself.

“Right,” he conceded as he scratched the back of his neck. *Just like I do. Fucking hell.* “S’pose that makes sense.”

“I-I just…” I began again, voice wobbling the nearer I drew towards *sobs.* “H-he’s really important to me, and I… I just wanted him to meet you.” I sniffed. “Y-you’re not… Angry?”

Mum’s amusement softened into sympathy, and she reached out like she might touch me, too. But withdrew when she realised what she was doing.

“Course not, love,” she assured, curling dad’s arm closer to her side and *smiling.* “You should get rid of that stone, but… *God,* I’m so glad to see you.”

Ephemeral tears gathered in her eyes, and my own fucking *kicked* down the door to my own emotions. A sob coughed through my lips as my own tears burst forth.

“M-me too.”

Draco wrapped his arm around me, still holding my hand as he drew me into him. And I was all at once *swaddled* in more love than I knew what to *do* with. A kiss pressed to my hair, and my dad smiled.

“Good to see you smile, Harry,” he said softly. And I choked another sob.

“I-I m-*miss* you… *So*much,” I admitted thickly. And the tears gathered in my mum’s eyes spilled silvery lines down her face. “Doesn’t… Doesn’t get easier, y’know?”

Mum nodded, not even bothering to wipe her tears away.

“Harry, we love you *so, so* much.” She swallowed. “But you have to let go. You’ll find us again, but this… This *stone…”*

“I-I know,” I coughed, all at once sort of *amused* that she was *parenting* *me,* and simultaneously fucking *destroyed* from the encounter. And Draco pulled me tighter to his side. “I w-wanna know everything about you guys, I… I can’t *wait* to h-hang out.”

Dad wrought a mournful sort of laugh as he pulled mum into a sideways hug, mirroring the way Draco now held me. Pressed a kiss to her hair.

“Take your time,” he said. “We’ll have forever.”

I nodded, glanced up at Draco, who looked just as fucking *wrecked* as I felt. And reluctantly withdrew my hand from my pocket.

“Te-tell Sirius I said hi,” I wrought. Dad nodded, pulling mum’s head into his shoulder as she wept. *Wept.*

*Christ, this hurts.*

My smile fluttered, and I was just about to drop the stone when Draco–

“I-I’ll take good care of him,” he said, suddenly *much*more confident in his speech. “I promise.”

My parents smiled at him, and I wanted to watch his face, but my attention was just *drinking* in what might’ve been the last glimpse I got of them in this life as he held my head to his chest.

“We’ll hold you to that, Draco,” mum said, voice thick with emotion. And as they returned their simultaneous glances to me, I let the stone slip from my palm.

It clattered to the floor, mundane as anything as they vanished.

And I collapsed.
