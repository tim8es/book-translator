## Chapter 125: Chapter 125

Harry and I shared a bath, and he was just as *amused* with all the different spigots that poured different things as I’d been when we first came here. Made that pinch of nostalgia tighten around my heart to see him playing in the bubbles.

He let me scrub his hair clean, and moisturised, and *protected*, *god,* this might’ve been one of my favourite rituals of ours. Getting to massage his scalp, work his head at whatever angle I wanted, bending under my affections as I got to *play with his hair. Christ,* he was so *perfect.*

After our bath, we spent a few minutes drying off and Harry went to pry his toiletries from his trunk. And we brushed our teeth together, side by side before my sink, alternately grinning at each other and brushing. At one point, he managed to sneak a toothpastey kiss to my cheek, and I whined my dismay. When he only chuckled, it began this *horrendous* back-and-forth toothpaste war, at the end of which we might as well have just bathed *again.* But we were both red in the cheeks from hysterics as we rinsed our mouths and washed the foam from our bodies with a wet cloth.

Mother was waiting for us in the receiving room, all prim and proper herself with a tote of what I assumed to be *Bezants* tucked under her arm. And Harry was pure *brimming* with excitement as we stepped into the Floo.

*Place Cachée*was just as bustling as mother’d predicted as we emerged onto the street. Harry’s awe hummed in my chest as I led him out into the daylight, and he only withdrew his hand from mine to give a spinning assessment of our surroundings.

*“Wow,”* he breathed, and it was just then that an older French couple passing by recognised him. They stepped up to him, drawing his eye down as they spoke to him in rapid French, and he chuckled nervously. Sent me a pleading glance, but my mother was the one to shoo them away.

*“S'en aller!* Si grossier, qui t'a appris les bonnes manières?” she sighed as they hastened on their way, *tsked* and took Harry by the elbow. “Sorry, dear, the French have a rather… *Limited* social filter.”

I laughed as mother began leading him through the crowd, but when he sent me another pleading glance, I felt my heart *lurch* towards him. He pulled me to his side, and I was helpless to resist. As if I’d *want* to, I took his opposite hand and we bracketed him in from further persistent civilians.

“Erm… S-so *what…* What are these sh-shops?”

A devious grin pulled at my lips as I tugged Harry sideways towards a particular shop. I knew *just* where to start.

“Suivez-moi,” I said, and mother *giggled* to follow us. *Jesus, who is this woman?*

“Y-you speak French?”

Harry’s heart *leapt* with desire, and I shot him a smirk.

“Un *peu,”* I answered, using my other hand to show the universal gesture for *small.* And his cheeks pinked.

I led us to the biggest *couturier* on the strip, *Maison Capenoir,* and ushered Harry inside. He’d said he felt out-of-place as he beheld his reflection, so I was *determined* to dress him up in the finest French Wizarding robes *Bezants* could buy. A short Frenchman with a *ridiculous* moustache and a length of measuring tape over his shoulders stepped up to us, addressing mother as Harry took in his surroundings.

Not only suits and clothing of all sorts hung on hangers around us, but bolts of fabric, drawers of patterns and mirrors and mannequins. Glass displays full of buttons and cuffs, glittering under the spotlights that shone down on them.

“Oh,” he said quietly as mother conversed with the shop owner.

“You want to blend in?” I asked, because he already looked *gorgeous* in fucking *anything,* but as he glanced down at himself, simple jeans and jumper, he hesitantly peered up at me.

“Y-yes, please.”

I grinned.

Draco handed me off to the staff, and though the mirrors and glass cases *might’ve* rattled a bit for being torn from his side, I let them. They led me deeper into the store, up onto a short platform surrounded by three mirrors and took my measurements. *All* my measurements.

I’d never been *fitted*. For *anything.* It struck me as sort of *amusing* to recall all the baggy hand-me-downs I’d grown up in, but when the men and women seemed satisfied with mapping my entire *body,* they invited me to a settee. And I waited.

It didn’t take long, likely thanks to shit like sewing spells or whatever, but when they returned, they presented me with… A *ridiculously* lush suit.

“Oh, wow,” I murmured, hoping this interjection was universal. *Can I really just walk down the street in*that?

They insisted I try it on, though, so I let myself be ushered into a curtained changing… Spot. And stripped down to try it on.

It fit like a *glove,* and as I emerged, Draco and Narcissa were waiting on the other side. A swell of *awe* bled through the bond, and I felt my cheeks go hot as both blonds stepped up to me.

“My *god,* Harry, you… This was an *excellent* idea.”

She gripped Draco’s elbow as they took my measure, and I glanced down at myself. But a little *pull* from Draco had me stumbling forwards, back towards the platform with three mirrors, and he helped me up onto it. Like I’d *need* help, but he was just feeling such *rapturous awe* that I let him.

A stranger looked back at me. And I blinked. He had the same green eyes, same unruly hair and glasses and *scar,* but… The suit he wore somehow made the brunette disarray atop his head seem *suave.* Almost… *Styled,* as he turned this way and that, appraising me. I looked, for the first time in my *life,* like an *actual fucking celebrity.*

*Jesus Christ.*

Narcissa was muttering in French to the tailor as Draco caught my eye.

“I-is there… Anything less, erm… *F-formal* I could w-wear…?”

He quirked a smile, and I turned back to him. Reached for my hand, and I met him.

“You’re getting the *full fucking Monty,* Harry.”

When we finally left, we were carrying *bags. Plural.* Bags and *bags* of clothes for me, and I felt like a spoiled child, because I was wearing *plush* fucking cashmere, pleated trousers and even a *leather fucking belt.*

*Leather!*

I didn’t know how to *walk* in such fine materials, and Draco laughed at me. Reached over to ruffle my hair, and I frowned petulantly at him.

“You look *incredible,* love,” was his apology as he pressed a kiss to my temple. And even though I felt *so fucking weird*, walking around like I was *famous* (which I *was*… just never felt like it) I let my arm wind around his waist as his own came to rest across my shoulders. My pleased flush *still* hadn’t left my cheeks as we navigated the crowd up to our next stop.

Narcissa pivoted to face us, walking backwards with this beseeching expression on her face.

“Can we just… Pop in here for a moment? I haven’t been here in *ages,* and it’s one of my favourite shops.”

Coming to a halt outside of a busy establishment that said *Bonnetvolant Chapellerie* above the awning, Draco sighed.

“How about *you* go in, and we explore?”

Narcissa turned suddenly *stern.*

“You come to visit me just to *abandon*me? Is my company that atrocious?”

I glanced up at Draco just in time to see him roll his eyes.

“W-we can… We can spend just a *moment* in here, can’t we, Draco?”

He eyed me suspiciously, as if I were *siding* with her against him, but relented with a sigh.

“Fine.”

I couldn’t deny I was *curious. There’s a place here he doesn’t like…?* But as we followed a smug Narcissa through the doors, I realised why.

It was a… I didn’t *know* what. Seemed to be a *millinery,* but there were also rows of perfumes, jewellery, dainty little gloves. And the *smell…*

“Eugh,” escaped me quietly before I could help it. And Draco snorted.

“Exactly.”

It was like every witch who visited sampled some different perfume, and the blend of aromas wove together into an inescapable *cloud* of *too much.* Narcissa was already lost in the throng, and I resisted the urge to wrinkle my nose, much less *plug it.*

“How the hell do the employees not *faint?”*

“*No* idea,” he answered heartily as he pulled me aside to a corner, allowing the women (and the occasional man) to swarm the shop.

The glittering jewels and colourful hats *were* a spectacle, and I found myself slightly entranced despite the *assault* on my sinuses as we waited. Thankfully, Narcissa seemed to have a singular goal, and found us moments later with one small bag around her wrist.

“Right! On we go.”

Back out in the sunshine, Draco and I inhaled *deep* breaths of the fresh air in tandem. And then shared a chuckle as we followed his mum further up the street. Just sort of ambling, *strolling,* and it was *so fucking nice* to just be out on a *shopping date* with my boyfriend and his mum. In *Paris.* A thought suddenly occurred to me.

“Can we visit the tower?” Draco sent a dubious glance down at me as we wandered through the crowd.

“Bit *touristy,* Muggle Paris,” he griped, but I was *determined.* And set a petulant frown purposefully on my lip.

“*Please,* Draco?” I made my eyes flutter up at him, and he clicked his tongue.

“You *really* wanna see some great bloody *tower* made by *Muggles?”*

I nodded fervently as a pleasant breeze swept up the street.

“It’s *incredible,* don’t you think? To be able to accomplish a feat like that without magic.”

The reluctance in his eyes, in his *heart,* softened. And he sighed.

“Fine.” My heart leapt. “But not today.” And then sank. “We’ll come back, I promise. And I’ll show you *anything you want to see,* Harry Potter.”

My cheeks bunched in a grin as I sent a little pull along the cord connecting us. And he leaned down to press a kiss to my lips, just like I wanted.

*I’m so bloody in love with him, I think I might explode.*
