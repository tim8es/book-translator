## Chapter 99: Chapter 99

Chapter Notes

As we wandered down a road with the museum at our backs, my heart was *fighting* to get free of my ribs. Felt like I’d have a *heart attack,* I was so bloody happy. Didn’t know how to cope with the gorgeous brunette holding my hand, stealing pleased little glances at me as we traversed the dappled sidewalk beneath rows of trees.

“You know, I was gonna f-*follow* you, when I saw you being carried out of the woods,” I eventually said. Flinched a smile down at the alarm Harry shot me. “Thought you were dead, y’know.”

I glimpsed his alarm sour into something like *despair,* and internally cursed myself for stealing his gorgeous smile. Directed my attention down at the sidewalk before us as a swallow worked its way thickly down my throat.

“Didn’t last more than a second in the real world,” he assured me. “I was back before he’d… Before he’d even recovered from the recoil of his spell.” *Recoil?* I frowned at him, and he twitched an echo of a smile. “Knocked him on his *arse* to kill me.” A bark of laughter escaped me, despite myself. Imagining the Dark Lord *on his arse* was just too comical an image for me to even *picture.* And Harry smiled a little brighter. “S-spent *hours* on the other side, though. Weighing my options.” He shuddered a deep inhale as my smile sobered to watch him.

“Why *did* you come back?”

My heart tightened in my chest to ask this, but I just *had* to. If he’d been presented a *choice,* and had already *failed* an attempt of his own… I had to ask. The smile he turned up at me then was veiled in grief, but it was sort of… Empty. Like a facsimile of the grief he’d once worn.

“Just too bloody Gryffindor, I s’pose,” was his answer. And I tried for a smirk. It was a half-answer, but I could infer enough from it. His entire *life* was spent in preparation to slay the Dark Lord. Even with an exit so conveniently offered so near the end, all he’d known up to that point was *fulfill the prophecy.* And now…

“What… What do you want to do now?”

His lips pursed thoughtfully as he faced the sidewalk again, and he was silent for a while. There were undoubtedly *unlimited* possibilities for The Boy Who Lived, but I was curious about what *Harry* wanted to do. And when he finally answered, it was with a short sigh.

“Buy a bit of land in the country? Maybe… *Maybe* write an autobiography. Have goats and chickens and… And *horses.*” He smiled. “Host dinner parties for my friends, pick up some hobbies…” Smiled at *me.* “Come home to *you*every day.”

My throat was tight with affection, and I sniffed. Mirrored his smile back to him.

“I’d love that,” I answered thickly, and Harry’s eyes *sparkled.*

It was only then I realised we’d come up to the riverside, and as we leaned against the balustrade over the edge, a breeze swept over us, carrying the smells of water and Autumn and food, again. Nearly fucking *euphoric* as Harry looped his arm through mine and leaned his head against my shoulder. On pure *instinct,* I pressed a kiss to the top of his head and laid my own atop his.

“Thanks for coming out with me today,” he eventually said softly. A few ducks swooped before us, down to the water as boats drifted by, the sounds of people and traffic a distant backdrop behind us.

“I’d follow you *anywhere,* Harry.” And I meant it. Even to our fucking *deaths,* I’d follow him.

*Best date ever.*

---

Whether it was all the walking, or the emotionally heavy conversation, Draco and I found ourselves equally encroaching on exhaustion as we made our way back to the Leaky Cauldron. It’d been the best day of my fucking *life,* and I found myself silently wondering if today would replace my Patronus’s *happy memory.* If there was another layer to unlock with that magic if I could summon a happier memory than the one I’d been using.

It was near dinner time by the time we found ourselves back in the castle, but without even a glance of silent discussion, we were trudging back up to our room. My feet were nearly dragging, and Draco seemed just as knackered as he swung the door open for us. I was instantly stepping out of my shoes and pulling my jacket and jumper off. Loosened the button and zip of my jeans, but the bed was too inviting to worry about stripping completely. I was curled up beneath them and half-asleep by the time Draco joined me.

His arms evoked peace, and I quickly succumbed to sleep. And tonight’s dream seemed to be a reversal of the one I’d had about *him,* because this time *I* was small and terrified and locked in my aunt and uncle’s cupboard beneath the stairs. Hungry and weeping in the dark as I tried to claw the locked door open. Even just to get the *grate* open to let some light in, *anything…*

Arms wrapped around me suddenly, and I was pulled away from the door. I struggled initially, but then a voice in my ear:

“Shh,” as my back found a chest. “It’s alright, I’m right here.”

It sounded like a child’s voice, but somehow I knew *exactly* who it was. And in young Draco’s arms, I broke down in bitter tears.

“I-I’m s-*sorry,”* I sobbed, curling into him on my pitiful excuse for a bed. “Sorry they l-locked you in here with m-*me.”*

He clicked his tongue.

“So *silly,* Harry. I *asked* to be locked in here with you.”

Our dark surroundings suddenly adjusted to my eyes as I turned them, pouring tears, up at Draco. And found him no older than perhaps *ten.* I sniffed.

“Y-you *did?”*

He smiled and nodded firmly.

“Course. You’re the only place I want to be.” So simple, as if it were *obvious,* and I twitched a smile as I curled back into him, wrapped my short arms around him.

“Love you, Draco,” I wept into his shirt. Sniffed and sobbed. “Love you *so much.”*

He pulled me tighter into him, nearly crushing me, but *let him.*

“Love you so much, Harry.”

I wept myself awake.
