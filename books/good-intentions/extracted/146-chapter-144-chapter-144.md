## Chapter 144: Chapter 144

Chapter Notes

*You’d think I’d get used to delicious food after nearly a full week of it,* Harry mused as he savoured his breakfast. *God, imagine the school food at Beauxbatons.*

Watching him eat was *always* enthralling, but especially now that I was privy to his little internal commentary. I found myself wondering when the exact moment had been that we’d solidified the soulbond, but the instant I wondered, I knew.

Harry paused to put the timeline together with me. After he’d rescued me, in the hospital wing, must’ve been when he established it. And then after I’d shown mum the rings in the library when we’d first heard each others’ thoughts, it must’ve sank in a bit deeper. Kneeling before the Eiffel Tower *(yesterday)* and then our bathtub fuck inside each others’ heads. The *dream last night.*

*You gave yourself to me,* Harry concluded. *Up until that dream you’d only been taking what I was giving you, but then you… You let me have you.*

I nodded solemnly. It was the only answer that made sense. Sometime around two in the morning, Harry had asked for my *soul,* and I’d given it to him. So different than all the prior times when I’d just been *flooded* with his, and I felt like a goddamn *fool* for thinking soulbonds were a myth when we *shared a Patronus.* The literal embodiment of our souls *mirrored* each other, and the fact that soul magic beyond just a Patronus had never even *occurred* to me–

“That’s *really* unsettling, you know,” mother mused from behind her second mug of tea today. Harry and I interrupted our silent staring contest to glance at her, and he chuckled a laugh to realise what we’d been doing.

“Sorry. There’s… There’s a lot going on in our heads. Lots of information to contend with.”

She hummed vaguely as I returned to my breakfast, biting back a grin. And as Harry did the same, the implications of it all started to make swallowing difficult. He’d been *so scared* of losing me after he nearly *had,* that he’d imparted a piece of his *soul* into me. So he’d always know where I was, how I was feeling, he’d spared a *part* of himself because he loved me so much.

He really was the Dark Lord’s perfect antithesis, because while *he’d* been ripping his soul apart to escape *death,* Harry had walked into it *willingly,* and had now knit his own to *another* out of *love.* Not for fear of death, for fear of *losing me.* I’d won his heart and he’d *offered his soul.*

A quiet little *hack* of a sob had me dropping my fork to lean my forehead into a palm.

*Never been loved like this.*

“Draco?” mother asked, but all I could do was shake my head. If I tried to answer her right now, I just *knew* I’d fall apart. Was barely holding it together as it *was,* and I felt an ankle gently curl around mine.

“He’s alright,” Harry assured her, but I just couldn’t even *look* at him at the moment. “Big *crybaby,* my husband.”

I choked on a scoff, and pinched my eyes together between a finger and thumb to try to keep the tears in.

*Fuck you.*

*Don’t tempt me.*

Mother clicked her tongue, and as I finally managed to release my eyes to *glare* across the table, expecting to find a *smirk* on Harry’s face, he was just… Smiling. Chewing contently and *smiling at me.* Mother’s hand extended towards me in my peripherals, palm up, asking for mine. Even as I rolled my eyes, a tear slipped free and I swatted it away hastily before answering her silent request. Gently laid my hand in hers, and she gripped it.

“Happy, my love?” she asked, searching my face as I impatiently swiped another tear off my cheek. And I nodded. Sniffed *hard* and tried to blink away the lingering moisture in my eyes.

“Ha-*happiest,”* I croaked. Cleared my throat and sniffed again. *Not at the fucking table, Jesus Christ…*

Mother smiled at me, brow pinched like *she* might cry, too, and I couldn’t look at her anymore. Reached for my water and necked a *hearty* several gulps, willing my throat to loosen.

*God, I can’t believe our anniversary is Christmas fucking Day,* I moaned internally, and Harry chuckled.

*Always felt like an outsider when my friends would get presents and shite from their parents. Even being invited to the Weasley’s, being… Pseudo-adopted by them, never really felt like it was mine. But you’ve made it the best fucking holiday ever.*

*Corny,* I shot back as a corner of my lips twitched up at Harry. And he snorted.

*“You’re* the one all weepy because you’re so *happy,”* he returned to me. I buried my face back in my hand with a *groan,* because he… Well, he was *right.* “Reckon we’re allowed a *modicum* of corniness.”

Mother finally let me pry my hand out of hers, grumbling nonsense to myself as I polished off the rest of my breakfast. And she cut a sigh.

“Always been a bit of a *pessimist,* haven’t you, dear?” A delicate little snort. “Good luck clinging to that cynicism now that you’re *married to Harry fucking Potter.”*

I chortled at that. Couldn’t *help* it. Course, *she* was right, *too.* I had always planned for the worst, *expected* it to avoid disappointment when the world just continued to be inexorably cruel. But as I peered up at Harry, still smiling like… Like he was *too damn happy* to let the smugness show, but still smug as fuck, my *husband…*

Eyes locked on his, I lifted my glass to pull one last mouthful of water, and his eyes dipped to my throat, cheeks going flush as his smile slowly dropped. Set the glass back down on the table, and offered my napkin up to my lips before replacing it beside my plate.

“Happy Christmas, mum, but I need a word with my *husband.”*

I stood, studiously ignoring my mother’s reaction as I held Harry’s eye. And he ambled to his feet, cheeks *conspicuously* red as he more clumsily followed me out of the dining hall. She chuckled quietly, said something to herself that I didn’t quite catch, but I didn’t care. We were hand-in-hand and pacing away into the villa in *seconds.*
