## Chapter 3: Chapter 3

I miraculously hadn’t fretted myself bald by the time my hearing rolled around, but I was damn-near losing my mind. And mother wasn’t helping in the slightest by tittering over my appearance, smoothing my hair back and my collars down for the umpteenth time and reminding me to speak politely. After nearly fifteen minutes of this, I finally caved.

“Enough, mother!” My hand collided with her wrist as I swatted her away from my ear. “I’m not a fucking child, I know how to sit and behave.”

She held her wrist to her chest as if I’d actually hurt her, and suddenly I didn’t mind letting my irritation show. I rolled my eyes, turning away from my reflection and the shock on her face.

“If this is the behaviour the Wizengamot can expect, you’ll find yourself following in your father’s footsteps, mark my words!”

“The Wizengamot isn’t going to be inspecting the state of my robes, mother! I’m not going to Azkaban for a single fly-away hair.”

She clicked her tongue, and my resolve to hold my own was spiderweb thin, and wearing. Our combined anxieties were well and truly draining me, and I just *knew* I’d be knackered by the time I walked out of the Ministry, regardless of where my destination ended up being.

We Floo’d directly to the Atrium, and while I was no Saviour of the Wizarding World, the looks we got were obvious enough. I winced as we began our solemn trek towards the hearing room, willing the brunette from my mind. *Relax, this is going to be fucking excrutiating but at least you’ll never have to see him again.*A few reporters lept to action at seeing the last two free Malfoys, but mother elegantly side-stepped them all. Once in the waiting room, she gave me one more thorough once-over, earning a groan from me.

“Mother!” She withdrew her touch, lower lip quivering slightly, and I sighed. “If I see father, I’ll tell him exactly where to shove it.”

Lips pursed, eyes wet, she gave a soft sniff and tossed her hair back behind her shoulders. She made no answer, but gave me a hard look for a few heartbeats, and I wasn’t really sure what she saw. But it was hard to care beneath the black dread that was accumulating in my gut as she purposefully turned towards the double doors and pushed them inward.

The Wizengamot’s yammering quieted to whispers as we stepped in. Atop a little platform in the centre, a seat had been prepared for me, directly facing none other than Minister of Magic, Kingsley Shacklebolt. Mother gave my elbow a squeeze before excusing herself to one of the pews that lined the circular room, and I hesitantly approached the chair, making sure my robes were arranged decorously around me as cold sweat began to gather under my arms.

“Thank you for joining us today, Draco,” Shacklebolt began, calling for silence from the attending members. Surprise at hearing him use my first name was only surpassed by shock at him *thanking* me. As if I’d had any say in the matter, but what did it serve him to be *nice* to me? “How’s your summer been?"

He leaned forward on his podium, smiling at me like we were *friends. What the fuck?*

“Er… F-fine, Minister.”

He nodded. “Good, good. That’s good to hear. You’ve been adjusting well, then?”

I cast a searching glance through the crowd to find my mother, who seemed just as surprised as I was. Not that anyone but I would be able to tell – her features were frozen in that icy, stoic expression I’d seen her hone over years being married to my father.

“I… Suppose?”

*What the hell is going on?*

“As well as can be expected, right?” He grinned knowingly at me, and I surreptitiously tried pinching my thigh to make sure I wasn’t dreaming. When I felt the sting of pain, I tried twisting my wince into a sheepish grin in answer, which thankfully seemed good enough for him because he carried on: “Well, you’re not being tried today, so no need to worry about that. I asked you to come here because we’ve been working with Headmistress McGonagall to form a sort of… *Supplementary*term for the students whose education was interrupted by the war.”

Suddenly, I felt sort of hollow between the ears. Like my entire brain had evaporated into mist, leaving only an icy breeze that seemed to whoosh through my skull.

“What?”

It wasn’t a particularly polite way of phrasing my question, but it was out before I could swallow it back. Shacklebolt gestured to my audience, and a familiar figure rose to her feet.

“The Minister is correct, Mr. Malfoy,” came McGonagall’s reedy, Scottish voice. “Your class of Seventh Years never achieved your NEWTs, and finding employment without them can prove difficult.” She eyed me over the rim of her glasses, tilting her chin down in that condescending way that made the whooshing in my head seem to maelstrom.

“I… Don’t understand,” I admitted, sliding my attention back to my mother for a moment. She was barely containing her rapturous joy. “You want *me…* To return to *Hogwarts?*”

“Well, that’s the purpose of this hearing. I’d like to hear your thoughts on the matter.”

My mind was whirling, spinning out, and I felt like the centrifugal force of myself had slipped through my fingers. *Oh, no… No no no, if I go back… I can’t! I can’t go back, not after everything I did, and even if they force me, that means I’ll have to see* him *again!* I started shaking my head, eyes wide and heart pounding when another familiar feminine voice cut through the silence.

“What a *wonderful* idea, Minister! Headmistress, thank you *so*–”

“Narcissa,” Shacklebolt interrupted, shooting her a stern stare. “I’m asking Draco.”

I was going to be sick. Of *course,* mother was ecstatic at the idea of me being accepted back into polite society and furthering my education, but was there really a point? Even if I scored perfectly on my NEWTs, I couldn’t see my *job prospects* as anything other than bleak. Who wanted to hire an ex-Death Eater but those involved with shady dealings? I couldn’t go back, I just *couldn’t*–

“W-why?” I began, voice thick as I swallowed and tried again. “Why are you asking me? You could just tell me what to do and I’d have to do it.”

My hands started to ache, and I realised I’d been gripping the armrests of the chair quite tightly. It took more effort than it should’ve to pry them off, but they only returned to fists in my lap as Shacklebolt went on:

“Yes, I suppose I could. But you are an adult, Draco, and given all you’ve gone through, I’m willing to let you choose your own fate. Not been given many choices in your life, have you?”

My throat tightened painfully, and I tried to swallow again, but I couldn’t. *They’re… Actually letting me choose? I could just… Fuck off into the ether?* My heart swelled as I imagined being *finally* free of all the chaos of my life, but my eyes reluctantly slid over to my mother again. She was giving me that *don’t-even-think-about-it* look, and I felt a frown forming on my brow.

A few pews behind her and to her right, though, I happened to glimpse a shadowed face, nearly swallowed in the hood of a cloak and my gut twisted. It was probably just a trick of the light, my anxiety and fear working me up to think those eyes were *green,* but…

Realising Shacklebolt was still waiting for my answer, I turned my frown down at the floor. If I tried to navigate adulthood without even having properly graduated, I’d end up on the streets. It wasn’t a matter of likelihood, but of eventuality; even if I moved away somewhere no one recognized my name or my face, the evidence of my involvement was permanently branded into my arm. My frown bent into a grimace as I eyed my left sleeve, picturing the skull and snake that lay beneath. *If he's offering this as both penance and 'rehabilitation'...*

“I-I’ll go,” I said quietly. *Fuck. Shit. Fuck me, this is so messed up.*

“Sorry?” Shacklebolt asked, and I cleared my throat. Could hardly hold his eye, but I turned my face up out of my lap to repeat myself.

“I’ll go,” I said a bit more loudly, but the waver in my voice was only made that much more evident. And I felt the hollow space between my ears suddenly fill with pandemonium. Hushed gossip spread through the crowd, and I didn’t want to see the relief I knew I’d find on mother’s face, so I kept my eyes trained on the Minister. Who eased back off his podium with a satisfied smile.

“Very good. Very brave of you, Draco.” I really was going to be sick. “Excellent. Minerva, I presume you’ll handle the letter explaining the details?”

McGonagall smiled wistfully, easing back down onto her pew. “Of course, Minister.”

“Well! Since that’s settled, do you have any more questions for me?”

*Why do you trust me? Why do you care what happens to me? Why worry about my education? Why me?*

“No, Minister.” I felt so fucking small. Put under a magnifying glass and splayed out prone. I didn’t know who to direct my anger at, but it was festering something *fierce* in my chest as Shacklebolt took his gavel into his hand.

“Very well. I wish you a very productive final school year, Draco. Dismissed.”

The gavel came down on the block, and the room flew into chaos. I stood on hollow limbs, feeling very faint, when suddenly mother was back at my side and wrapped around my arm.

“Can you believe that! Oh, Merlin, I think I just about passed away from shock!” she hissed next to my ear. I don’t know what happened between there and the Atrium, but when we’d returned to the wide room, witches and wizards of all varieties were crowding to get to me. I didn’t really see what the bloody big deal was, but then a voice that confirmed all my suspicions cut through the commotion.

“You’re coming back to school?”

I froze. *You’re fucking joking. Not a chance in hell.* Despite how lightheaded I’d been feeling, the very real and very familiar sound of Harry’s voice seemed to root me right back into my body, and straight down onto the marble as I hesitantly twisted to see him.

It *had* been him. Why had he come? What was he fucking *doing* here? I wanted to run so far away no one would even remember my name, but I couldn’t fucking *move*.

The crowd parted reverently for him as he stepped up to me, looking more than a little unsure but eyes bright. Bright as the fucking sun, that goddamn *green* sparkling like he’d just seen the stars for the first time.

“What’re you doing here?”

*He must really, REALLY hate me to show me his face after I’d already told him I’d die on the spot if I ever saw him again after that! I can’t fucking believe him!*

Harry shrugged noncommittally, some semblance of that idiotic fucking stupid bloody painfully lopsided *grin* tugging at his lips.

“It’s not every day Draco Malfoy gets summoned to court,” was his weak excuse, and I could’ve cut glass with the gooseflesh that rose on my skin for hearing my full name in his mouth. *Pack it in, don’t think about his fucking mouth, especially not right now! Not here!*

“Dunno what all the fuss is about, honestly,” I muttered as I cast a *help me* glance toward mother, but she was being all *respectful distance* and allowing me to have this polite little public conversation with the love of my fucking life. *I’m going to kill them both.*

“Well,” he began again, clearing his throat. A subtle flush crept into his cheeks, sending my gut upside down as he shifted his weight. “I-I just wanted to say that… Y-you’ll be very welcome back at H-Hogwarts.”

Just as I was tripping over his *annoying* fucking (adorable) stuttering, he extended a hand to me and suddenly it all made sense. He was throwing his weight around for my sake, making a scene to make sure the message came across: *Harry Potter’s got Draco Malfoy’s back.*

*What the fuck?* I almost said it out loud, so stunned was I. *I don’t need your fucking help,* is what I should’ve said, but the dulcet pink in his cheeks was quickly resolving into something more like poppies in the snow as he waited, and before I could make this anymore agonisingly awkward, I reached out to accept his handshake.

“Th-thanks,” I uttered quietly. And was quite pleased when it didn’t come out as a question. Still, what the *bloody fuck?* He smiled at me, full and bright and genuine, and it was the first time I’d ever been on the receiving end of *that,* so I couldn’t be blamed for any noises the sight might’ve startled out of me. Nearly knocked me to my knees, so forcefully did it hit me.

*Jesus Christ, I preferred him with the disfigured face. I should find a way to make that permanent so I don’t have to look at his bloody disgustingly beautiful face ever again–* The thought cut off short, so instinctive to leap to antagonising him but reluctantly changed now. He was bloody gorgeous, and I was going to swan-dive off the Astronomy tower if he ever smiled at me again.

“So I’ll…” He dropped my hand awkwardly, shuffling his feet like a thirteen-year-old girl. “See you at school?”

My fingers tingled from where he’d gripped them, but I only dropped them back to my side.

“Y-yeah. Bye.”

I stiffly rejoined my mother’s side and nearly dragged her away from Harry, not bothering to side-step any onlookers as we retreated back to one of the many Floos that lined the walls. She was speaking to me, I was sure, but I couldn’t hear her over the whooshing in my ears and it wasn’t two steps back through the green flame that I collapsed onto the rug of our receiving room.

---

*He ran from me. Again.* All the efforts I’d taken to try to be inconspicuous had been for naught – Mulpepper’s hadn’t had any Polyjuice potion, and the prices I’d found once I resorted to Knockturn Alley were outrageous, but found my social status had come in rather handy. I frowned down at my hand as I walked back to Grimmauld Place ambulatorally. The late-Summer weather was easing up closer to Autumn, and I needed to clear my head anyway.

*I hope I did the right thing. I know he said he never wanted to see me again, but if we’re just gonna be seeing each other at school again anyway, it couldn’t have been that bad, right?* I watched my hand curl into a fist as I recalled the pure, unmitigated horror I’d seen on his face. As if he’d been afraid I’d hex him again. It was amusing in some ways, and I reckoned it served him right for everything he’d put me through (including dropping that bombshell on me) but I couldn’t even pretend a smile at the memory.

McGonagall had visited me personally to ask me to come back, had nearly *begged,* but of *course* it had to be a Ministry-official ordeal to ask *Malfoy* to come back. After all, they were keeping a pretty close eye on him from what I could gather.

I wasn’t keen on revisiting the site of the battle any more than I’m sure Malfoy was, but McGonagall had made some indisputable points. The first of which obviously being my missed NEWTs (and most of the year in general), and the second being… If everyone else was returning, they’d all be looking to me to set the tone, right? I couldn’t deny seeing me alive and well in those well-washed but once-bloodied halls would probably do loads of people loads of good. So I’d agreed.

*Malfoy* agreeing, though… I hadn’t expected that. As Kingsley had said, it was… *Very* brave of him. So uncharacteristic of the coward I knew him to be, but clearly, he was making leaps and bounds if he was coming back *and admitted that he fancied me to my face.* My steps paced a little faster back home.

*But he still ran from me.* It didn’t particularly bother me, seeing him weaving through the crowd and watching his blond head disappear through a Floo. But it scratched at something inside my gut to envision what school life was going to be like for us now. Kingsley and McGonagall had both called it a ‘supplementary term.’ Which I’d come to learn meant a more personalised curriculum, since everyone had sort of left off at different points. That could only mean…

*Classes for just the seventh-years. Eighth-years, I suppose. Bloody hell, all of us packed into a room together.*

I finally reached the door just as I’d begun spiralling about what I’d done. *Why* I’d done it. It’s not that I *fancied Malfoy,* hell, I’d *hated* the git until a week ago, but… Something bitter washed over my tongue as I recalled all the pitying glances I’d caught on my friends’ faces since the war. Like some silent acknowledgement that I was Not Okay. And a grimace twisted my mouth as I considered just how hypocritical it was of me to have done the same fucking thing to Malfoy.

As the door swung shut behind me, I began an angry ascent upstairs. I couldn’t bring myself to actually pity Malfoy, pitiable as he was, simply because I knew how fucking *dehumanising* it felt to be pitied. Even if I still hated him, I wouldn’t want him to feel pitied. I definitely sympathised with him, but it wasn’t like there was any chance we’d get on as *mates,* let alone–

*He fancies me.*

I paused on the last step, the knowledge barrelling into me all over again. It was bloody *mental* is what it was. And I needed a fucking shower, immediately. The scent of bureaucracy was clinging to my robes and I couldn’t shake the feeling of his fucking *hand in mine.*

*Just what the fuck am I doing??*
