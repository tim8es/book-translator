## Chapter 14: Chapter 14

Chapter Notes

I slept luxuriously late, nestled comfortably in quilts and pillows and *gratitude* that it was the weekend. Lucky for Harry, he’d have a few days where he wasn’t *obligated* to see me and possibly work through all the no doubt *shocking* discoveries he was making.

A sleepy grin spread across my face as I stretched, recalling just how alarming it’d been when *I’d* realised I was gay. Hell of a wake-up call for any offspring of Lucius Malfoy, and a break-neck course in lying. I wondered if he’d stay in the closet, since there wasn’t much chance he’d get bullied for it being the *saviour of the world.* But not necessarily feeling one way or another about it.

There was nothing else I could do. I’d left the ball in his court, as it were, and now there was nothing left to do but wait and see. I knew I’d be anxious about it later, but I was just so fucking *pleased* with the way things were going that I just yawned and curled onto my other side, content to stay right where I was as long as I damn well pleased. No annoying staff to come tell me to get out of bed or insist I eat breakfast. *God,* I didn’t miss the manor.

I caught a few more hours of blessed sleep, the first time in what felt like *years* that I’d actually slept *enough,* before my growling stomach inevitably pulled me from my bed. There’d be no point changing into my uniform, but being seen in my pyjamas felt a bit too intimate for the general public, so I knelt to withdraw some of the precious few casual clothes I’d packed from my trunk.

Another yawn stole over me as I selected a pair of black jeans and a muted grey v-neck and laid them out on my bed. Once I’d stood to shed my sleepwear, a shadow crossed the square of sunlight on my floor, drawing my attention up to the window just in time to see Ulysses swooping up to land on the sill. I frowned, scratching my head as I went to let him in.

He hooted softly, an envelope in his beak that I accepted cautiously. Bloody bird was known to nip at fingers, but he just released the letter and took off back out into the sky. A pleasant breeze swept into my room as I turned the envelope over, so I decided to leave it open as my mother’s familiar scrawl registered on the front.

A sigh eased through my lips as I sat on the edge of my bed, deciding to just get it over with as I ripped the paper open easily. I hadn’t really thought much of mother since I’d been back, having been fully preoccupied with a certain brunette. But as I unfolded her letter, a frown creased my brow.

‘*My dearest Draco,*

*I hope your first week back has gone smoothly. If anyone’s been cruel to you, be sure to let the professors know. Remember, everyone needs a little help here and there, and there’s no shame in reaching out. Hopefully Harry’s been good on his word and you’re feeling welcome back in the castle.*

*I miss having you round the manor. I know our relationship has been under a bit of an adjustment in your father’s absence, but you’re still my son and I love you very much. How would you like to visit the villa over the holidays with me? We haven’t been back since you were a boy, and I know you made some friends last time we were there.*

*I would love it if you would write back. There’s not much to do now that you’re not around for pissing off, and I do miss that precious little scowl. Take care of yourself, my love.*

*All the love in the world,*

*Mother’*

I read it thrice. Mother *never* swore. Was this some sort of code? Was she in trouble, and trying to communicate it by writing like a *stranger?* It was honestly alarming, but something in my chest went tight as I finished my third read. She’d written to me like a *person,* and though it’d made me scoff to read *‘your father’s absence,'*it was like she was a real human being. *So fucking strange.*

Shaking my head to clear it, I tossed the letter onto my bedside table and resumed getting dressed. *Hopefully Harry’s been good on his word. She’d have a stroke if she knew.* A little amused laugh broke on my lips as I tugged my shirt over my head.

As I walked down the steps into the common room, I decided I *would* write back. If only just to say ‘*who the hell are you and what have you done with my mother.’* I’d keep the details of my update to a polite minimum, though, because if we were gonna try to have a normal mother-son relationship, we had a long way to go before I was going to broach the topic of my love life. And it wasn’t until I’d almost reached the Great Hall that I realised I’d been *humming.*

*Christ, I’m gonna make myself sick.* I couldn’t stop smiling. Thankfully, the Hall was nearly empty as I sauntered over to the Slytherin table. Remnants of lunch still lingered, and I helped myself to what I could reach. *Harry’s not straight, mother is alright, father is in Azkaban, the Dark Lord is dead.* *How the hell is my life going so well all of a sudden??*

A couple third years stopped dead in their tracks on their way out of the Hall, but even the terror in their eyes as they broke into a run couldn’t ruin my mood. I was on top of the fucking *world.*

After eating my fill, I returned to the common room to start my letter back to mother. But as I sat down to write, having conjured a quill and parchment, my thoughts strayed back to Harry. *I hope he’s alright. Should I check on him? No, it’s too soon. Maybe I can get Granger to check on him. Would that be weird?*

I shook my head again, lowering my quill to the parchment laid out on the table before the couch. The angle was a bit awkward, though, so I slid off the edge to sit cross-legged at the table. And began.

*‘Mother,*

*School’s fine. Harry is, indeed, being very accommodating. If not for him, I probably would be having a much harder time, but here we are.*

*The villa sounds great. I doubt any of the ‘friends’ I made will still be around, or want anything to do with me, but a change in scenery would be nice. Do you think you could send me that dark blue jumper you gave me for Christmas a few years ago? I thought I packed it, but it must still be hanging in my wardrobe.*

*Not much to report yet, sadly. It is only the first week after all, but I’ll write if anything terribly exciting happens. Honestly, I hope this year is the most boring one yet.*

*Love,*

*Draco’*

Feeling quite pleased with myself, I sat back to reread what I’d written. And as I did, I was dimly aware of the door opening and a few of my peers flooding through. What had been a casual conversation hushed to whispers as two pairs of footsteps seemed to tiptoe behind me to the stairs, but I didn’t turn. Didn’t really care much, honestly. I folded my letter in half and tucked it into my pocket when their footsteps retreated up to the dorms, decidedly putting it from my mind as I started my trek to the Owlery tower.

*She’s gonna fucking keel over when she sees I’ve written back immediately.*

---

My foot dangled from the sill where I’d sat, having thrown open the window to let some fresh air in. It was a gorgeous day, all things considered, with just the implication of Autumn in the breeze, and I briefly contemplated summoning my broom to go for a fly. But then *that* left the possibility open of running into Draco, which is precisely why I’d stayed in my room.

*If you really want me, then suck it up and make a move.* I couldn’t get those words out of my head. For sure, I *had* in the moment, but did I *want Draco?* The same way he wanted me? A disquieting voice in the back of my head whispered *it’s happening all over again. Someone takes an interest in me, and I just go along with it, no questions asked.*

Of course, I wasn’t *really* just ‘going along with it.’ I only kind of accidentally dipped my toes in. And bloody hell, it was so different this time, because it was *Draco fucking Malfoy.*

He didn’t treat me like a mental patient. Or like the crown prince of magical heroism, like most people tended to do. Granted, he’d been a hell of a lot kinder towards me, but in a way, I supposed he was just… Treating me like he always had. Which was a goddamn *blessing.*

It was why it felt so easy to talk to him. Why he’d received my confession about dying, because even though he’d *hugged me,* apologised and very nearly cried, I hadn’t seen a second of pity in his eyes. I *wanted* him to know everything about me, because if he was gonna waste time loving someone like *me,* he might as well know what he was getting into.

Considering all the hell he put me through only to learn it was because he was in *love* with me and shit at showing it only added to the growing list of Things Harry Potter Doesn’t Understand. I was gonna *have* to ask him why, and get some real fucking answers one of these days. And I curled my bent knee closer to my chest at the idea that we’d have to *talk.* After *that.*

The possibilities all seemed suddenly very fucking *real* now that I’d been slapped in the face with non-heterosexuality. And every single one terrified me. He’d said being gay was *rubbish,* and I was starting to understand a bit better why he’d said that. I’d thought being *straight* was awful, but it was easier to wrap my head around *women* because I never had to wonder what my role was supposed to be. With another *bloke,* though…

I swallowed uneasily, unwilling to admit that there was really only one person I knew of that I could ask about it. And that person had every right to hate me, but *somehow fucking didn’t.*

*If you really want me, then suck it up and make a move.* Lack of self-awareness aside (lack of *any fucking* awareness) I was once again reminded that I’d spent *two whole days* thinking about kissing him. Going on a third, because his mouth on my skin *really* hadn’t helped in that regard. And I blew out a sigh, resting my head back against the window frame.

He was right about being shit at liking someone. But for it to have taken a *single fucking week* for him to completely upend my entire world made a small smile creep into my lips.

*ALRIGHT!* I almost shouted into the empty air. *ALRIGHT, OKAY, I FUCKING ADMIT IT. I like Draco bloody goddamn Malfoy. Fuck me.*

It was so fucking *irritating,* I eyed the long way down to the ground and dryly considered throwing myself out the fucking *window,* because here I was, hiding in my room and thinking about him and *smiling.* I was so damn *annoyed* with myself, I lowered myself to the floor to pace.

Two days ago, I hadn’t even known I *liked men at all!* But his long-winded, harrowing confession in the manor was just so fucking awkward and confusing, and seeing the way I made him *blush* and hearing the vulnerability in his voice when he told me about his horrific experiences during the war all amounted to one wholly and *incredibly* endearing man.

I wanted to understand him inside and out, to pick apart that bizarre fucking brain of his that told him to torment the person he liked, to figure out just *what he saw* in me that despite all we’d done to each other, he’d ended up *in love* with me. It just didn’t make *any fucking sense!*

*Seeing you miserable made me miserable.*

*Spent years fancying you all wrong.*

*I’m just fucking this up again.*

*All I really wanted was to be important to you.*

And of all the fucking people in the *world,* for it to be Draco *Worst-Bachelor-Ever* Malfoy falling in love with Harry *King-Of-Horrible-Boyfriends* Potter, was so damn *ironic,* and I stopped pacing.

*Ironic? Or sort of… Perfect?* A sinking feeling in my gut led me to the floor, where I sat in a pile of utter dismay.

*Shit… If we’re* both *terrible at this liking-people shite, what sort of bedlam could we expect if we* did… *If we…*

*If you really want me, then suck it up and make a move.*

*Easier said than done. Fuck me.*

---

The next morning found me quite a bit worse off than the previous one. I’d *known* I was gonna be fucking anxious as hell, and I’d been right. The bliss of learning Harry wasn’t straight had worn off by now, and as I groaned into the daylight, I couldn’t help but worry.

*What if he never talks to me again? What if he’s too humiliated to even look at me anymore? What if he goes back to hating me?*

In the rational part of my brain, I knew it’d only been *one fucking day,* but every second that ticked by was driving me spare. I wouldn’t debase myself even more than I already had, though, by visiting his room again. Absolutely not.

I worried my lip with my teeth as I considered what he was likely thinking, what he was feeling. Was he angry at me for getting him all worked up and then leaving him high and dry? Was he gutted to realise another man had gotten that kind of response out of him?

Heaving a sigh, I slowly tore myself from the bed. No point laying about fretting all day. As I was dressing myself, though, I suddenly recalled how I’d left *all my fucking shower products* in his bathroom. And now I was dependent on him playing his hand, whatever it might be.

*Fuck me,* I groaned internally as I pulled my trousers up. Truthfully, I half-expected all the bottles to be piled up at my door once I left, but when I swung it open, I was shocked to see Harry stop mid-pace in the hallway, cheeks suddenly going *violently* red. And swooping relief warred with the dread that gripped me by the throat.

His mouth opened like he was about to speak, but I was just stunned silent. His lips shut, and then opened again, and I was about six seconds away from just saying *fuck it* and closing the door again when he finally got the balls to *talk.*

“H-hi.” Cleared his throat. “Y-you wanna, erm… You l-*l**eft* your, uh…”

I couldn’t believe what I was hearing. But I didn’t give myself a chance to question it, because he was just *standing* there, shifting his weight and stammering with the most *disarming*fucking blush, and he’d fucking *anticipated my needs?* *Fucking hell.*

“Yes, please,” I agreed quietly.

“Great. I-I mean *good.* I mean, not… Not *good* like, *good,* just… Jesus *Christ*.”

He turned away from me just as an indulgent grin was spreading across my lips, and I pressed them together as I cautiously followed him to his room, shutting my own door behind me. He didn’t turn back to me as he let us in, just went to the window and folded his arms over his chest, a thumb shoved between his teeth.

“Are you alright?” must’ve been the wrong thing to ask, because his shoulders bunched up around his ears like a wince and he shot a grimace at me.

“Can we…? Not… I m-mean, not *yet…* Just… I-I’m… Er...”

*Bloody hell,* I’d never seen *anyone* so lost for words. It was actually the funniest thing I’d ever seen, but I wouldn’t let myself laugh at him. Not when I was still unsure how he felt about… Everything.

“Right.”

And with that, I excused myself to his washroom. *At least he’s talking to me. Letting me use his shower again. I’ll fucking take it.*

After the fastest shower of my fucking life (but still not skimping on my hair routine), I redressed and left the towel I found hanging on the rack as I prepared myself to face him again. He might’ve been a stuttering *wreck,* but it was one of his most adorable features. And him being awkward was just another fucking Sunday, so I had to believe the fallout of Friday evening hadn’t been *too* awful.

He was pacing again on the opposite side of the bed when I opened the door, and he froze. Wouldn’t look at me, and obviously, I knew fucking why.

“I’m-I’m not… Sorry if I…”

I swore under my breath as he cast me a cutting stare, hand snapped out to make a ‘mouth-shut’ gesture.

“*Shut*… The fuck up. Don’t *apologise*, fucking hell. *I’m* the one who should apologise.”

His words hit like a smack round the back of my head, sending it into a chaotic whirlwind. *Why would he apologise?* He started to pace again, cheeks flaming red as he held himself with one arm and gesticulated with the other.

“Y-you never would’ve said *anything,* if you knew we… A-and *I* just… And *then,* I went and… *Fuck,* I know you’re dodgy on the topic, but you *really* are gonna have to give me a proper answer one day and explain it to me like I’m a fucking toddler because you should hate me. You should *hate*me!”

He was spiralling, faster and faster out of control, and making so little sense that I started towards him, even at the risk of him lashing out at me.

“Harry, why would you think I ‘should’ hate you?”

The glance he sent me then was verging on chaotic desperation and I had the vague sense he was about to start hyperventilating and I couldn’t just fucking *stand there* and do *nothing.*

“Why *wouldn’t* you? I-I treated you like a fucking *joke,* like this was some kind of fucking *game* b-before I… Before I-I mean… *Christ,* why is this *so fucking difficult!?*”

“M-maybe you should sit down…?” Hell, I was getting dizzy just *watching* him.

“Can’t. Can’t fucking sit down, can’t *sleep,* can’t *relax* because I don’t even know how to *start* to fix this, and *you…!*” I came to a halt at the foot of his bed as he jabbed a finger in my direction, waiting for the accusation. The insults and the vitriol I expected, braced like it was about to fucking *wreck* me, because I was sure it would. “Have *really* shit taste in men.”

My eyebrows flew up so high they nearly popped off my face. After a brief moment of shock, the amusement I’d been trying to swallow back bubbled to the surface and I *lost* *it.*

“Don’t fucking *laugh!*” Harry whined, but I was too far gone. I bent over to brace myself against my knees, because I’d been *so ready* for him to hate me for what I’d done. And the relief I felt that he was only being his perfectly on-brand, *honourable* self loosed the floodgates. “Oh, sod off!”

“I’m sorry–” I started to say, before being cut off by my own laughter again. Harry huffed a sigh and sat on the edge of the bed. “Y-you’re fucking *mental,* you know that?”

Wiping tears out of my eyes, I managed to straighten up again to see him fidgeting uncomfortably in his pyjamas like it itched just to *be him.* And he fixed me with a scowl.

“*You’re* bloody mental! I’m *apologising* to you and you’re *laughing at me!*”

“Sorry, sorry! I *swear* I won’t laugh anymore. I just… Thought you would hate me, honestly.” His scowl softened somewhat.

“I don’t h-*hate* you,” he assured quietly. “I’m *irritated,* because *you’re* bad at this, I’m *horrible* at it, so it makes *absolutely no sense* why you would l-like me when I…”

I lowered myself to his floor slowly, crossing my legs as a smile spread across my lips.

“Hated myself, too, when I first realised that I was… Well…”

Harry’s eyes went wide, his cheeks *somehow even redder,* as he stiffened where he sat. And I sighed.

“It’s *fine,* honestly. If you’re gonna beat yourself up that badly about it, we can just pretend it didn’t happen. Start over or-or whatever.” Some tension left his shoulders as he curled in on himself slightly, and I tried to offer him a reassuring smile. “I’ll even go back to being a prick, if you like.”

That, he smiled at, though it was a small one. *Christ,* I was so in love with him I wanted to rip my fucking eyeballs out.

“I… Have no fucking idea what I’m doing.” His throat worked for a moment as he glanced down at his lap. “A-and I… I have *no right* to ask this, but… D’you think… *Maybe,* you’d consider, erm… H-helping me f-figure it out…?”

I blinked up at him. He glanced back at me, worrying his lip between his teeth.

“You… Want *me…* To help you figure out what *you’re doing?”* Harry gave a small nod, and I quirked an eyebrow. “What do you *want* to do?”

A *multitude* of emotions crossed his face, from bristling to cringing to exasperation to resignation to terror, I couldn’t keep up with them all as he slowly lowered himself to the floor before me. And even more slowly scooted closer.

“I-I think… Given the fact that-that I haven’t been able to stop *thinking* about it for *days* now, I… Probably want to kiss you.”

His knees touched my shins and everything in me down to the last *cell fucking froze.*

*Oh.*

*Fuck.*

I swallowed reflexively as he peered up at me, hands fisting on his knees as he watched my reaction.

“Is that something you need my help with?” My voice sounded weird to myself. Hollow.

“Y-yeah. Erm… C-close your eyes.”

I studied his face a moment more, reading nothing but awkward shyness, perhaps *sheepishness,* as he waited for me. My eyes slid shut, and I suddenly couldn’t fucking *breathe.*

The sounds of him shuffling closer were abnormally loud to my heightened senses, but then I felt trembling hands on my face, angling my chin up, and one thought chimed through the emptiness of my head:

*I’d rather it be me than anyone else even if he is just trying new things.*

His breath washed across my lips, and they parted slightly on their own. For what felt like an eternity, it seemed like every event of my life had led to this one singular fucking moment, and a delicate brush against my upper lip dragged a tiny, reflexive gasp into my lungs.

And then Harry kissed me.
