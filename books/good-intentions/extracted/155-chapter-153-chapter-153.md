## Chapter 153: Chapter 153

Chapter Notes

Just like back at the villa, Draco seized me in this inescapable *control,* and I was helpless to do much more than lose myself in those gorgeous eyes. Felt *thrilling* to just give in, to *submit,* that I forgot to even worry about… What *had* I been worrying about? This was easy as *flying.*

A smile warmed Draco’s face.

*What made you decide you could trust me? Back then.*

I felt my head cock to the side to consider it, studying his face like the answers lay hidden in those invisible pores somewhere. For weeks after his confession, I’d fixated on unpacking everything he’d said, and reworking my perception of him. Having learned his most well-kept secret seemed to put his personality and *behaviour* into perspective, a *new, dizzying* perspective. Like a single, *vital* puzzle piece that changed him from an enemy to… Just a normal fucking bloke with trauma and struggles of his own.

*You could’ve said nothing. Just took your wand back and let me walk away. I… I chose to deliver it personally because I felt like it might give me some… Closure, I guess? And while it might’ve driven you mad with regret for never saying anything, your life would’ve gone on without me, we would’ve gone back to school and never spoken another word to each other. But you decided to tell me.*

With the very essence of my being so firmly in his grip, I was only aware of the string music through *his* senses, like I was just *encased* in him and hearing the song vicariously. But it was pleasant. Far more *moving* to hear how *he* heard it. I’d never heard music the way he did, and it swelled in him as I went on.

*I guess that was the first domino. Drove me absolutely bonkers to realise you’d earned my respect, because it was so fucking Gryffindor of you to admit it to my face when I’d done nothing but hate you, and seeing this new side of you let me understand you. And I only came to understand you more and more, the more you spoke to me, the more you let me know you. You taught me what being in love really meant, what it meant to be loved. Learning you were capable of love like that, how could I not trust you?*

*Learning you were capable of love like that* echoed in his head, and I saw his eyes go a little wider. *Even though I was shit at showing it?*

My cheeks split into another grin, recalling all the times he’d *bullied* me, all because he’d been in love with me and *hated* that he was in love with me.

*We were kids. Still learning how to love from scratch, and you do a helluva lot better now.*

Draco’s features warmed with another implication of a smile, just a softening of his eyes, a subtle lift to his lips.

*You taught me how. I don’t think I’ve ever properly said it, but… Thank you, Harry. Thank you for letting me love you, helping me get good at it and trading your heart for mine. It was quite literally killing me to live without it, and you’ll never, ever know how grateful I am to have yours.*

A gentle little *squeeze* around my soul had me sucking in a gasp, and I *knew* we were just doing that unsettling, silent *staring* again, but I just couldn’t *possibly* resist taking his face in my hands and pulling him into a *devouring* fucking kiss. It was just my luck the song had ended and we weren’t barrelled into by the next pair of dancers as he pulled me into his arms and *dipped me.* Snogged me to *cinders,* right there on the dancefloor, and I felt myself *burn.*

He was getting better and better at withstanding the furnace of my soul, because even though I sort of *assumed* some part of me was *glowing* (eyes, best guess) he just gripped it *tighter.* Like he *wanted* to be set ablaze, to share the ferocity of my magic, and I let him. Let *myself ignite* in his grasp as he fucking *ravished* my mouth with his own.

*So easy, loving you.*

---

When I finally drifted back to my senses out of Harry’s mouth, my head was still *swimming* with the implications that he– I was *easy to love?* Seemed like a pleasant *lie,* but it’d come from within his own blistering *soul,* still securely encased in mine, so I couldn’t really *doubt it.* But still… *Easy?*

I’d thanked him because I *knew* it couldn’t have been easy. To love such a creature as I *must’ve* been difficult. Complicated, if anything. But he found it *easy…?*

Shining, green eyes blinked up at me as I held him in the dip I’d twisted him into. Just sort of *stared* at him for a moment, but the applause for the musicians died down enough to reveal a subtle *whispering,* and Harry’s stomach *lurched.* I righted us, unwilling to be torn out of our pleasant reverie, but–

*Your eyes are glowing.*

I blinked down at him. *Heard* him alright, but… Not really processing as he stared at me. And as I glanced around, *‘âmes soeurs’* reached my ears, and the hair on the back of my neck stood on end. On *Harry’s,* too, as the rest of the dancers, the *hundreds* of witches and wizards attending the *soirée* looked on with curiosity, disbelief, *shock.* I laced my fingers through his and led us off the floor.

*Soulmates,* they’d called us. Seeing our matching sets of glowing eyes, the kiss, the *dancing.* Of course there’d be talk, and I didn’t doubt a few photos had been snapped of us, but as the crowd parted before us, I couldn’t spare a breath of a thought for the consequences because Harry was *panicking.*

*Writhing* in my grip, and I tightened it, just like my hand around his as he let himself be led. I found a staircase, ascended it to a hallway, and marched down it. Away from the crowd, *let them talk,* and to… I didn’t know where.

“Sorry,” he said softly, and I halted. He nearly bumped into me, so suddenly did I stop, but I felt him *wilt* behind me. “I-I *really* don’t care what they say, what they think, I j-just… The, the *attention* got to me, and I–”

*Shut up* reflexed out of me, and he did. Swallowed as I turned to face him. So *guilty* looking, I clicked my tongue.

“You’ve nothing to apologise for. It’s their own bloody business what they choose to give a shit about, and *we’re* not doing anything *wrong,* so they can all shove it.” *I’m with you, and you’re with me, and that’s all that matters.*

Harry’s cheeks pinked as he peered up at me, and I *felt* the embarrassment rising in his chest, even though he agreed with me. So I cast my gaze around us, found a door, and tried it. Locked, so I hauled him further down the hallway to the next one. Also locked, but the *third* one…

We burst into a… A *sick room?* A room with a bed, shelves of healing potions and a water font. Took only a moment for me to realise these rooms, this floor we’d come upon was for people who drank too much and needed to recover. And as Harry clicked the door shut behind us, the lights of the dancefloor illuminated the glass wall opposite us.

I led us towards it, curiosity pulling me to find it was a one-way mirror down onto the scene we’d just escaped. And the next song had already begun, dancers spinning each other across the floor as onlookers lingered in the wings.

“Oh,” Harry exhaled, coming up beside me, but I was categorising everything we’d just been through and the environment we now inhabited, all manner of scenarios playing out in my head. And I felt him look up at me, so I turned my attention back to him. “H-hi.”

A smile broke across my face, and his eyes, now their usual sheen, dipped down to mark it.

“Hi.”

Harry’s lips spread into a grin as his attention snapped back up to mine.

*“Hi,”* he said, imitating our inside joke, and I was just so *wholly fucking gone* for him that a swallow forced the spit my mouth had accumulated down my throat.

*Hi, husband.*
