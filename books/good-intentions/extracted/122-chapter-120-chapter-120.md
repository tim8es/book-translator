## Chapter 120: Chapter 120

Chapter Notes

I *might’ve* milked Draco’s recovery a bit much. Might’ve let on that he was still *recovering* when he was perfectly fine, because I *certainly* didn’t want to return to the classroom, and I could pick up enough to tell that *he* was *perfectly fine* hanging about the room starkers with me for as long as I wanted.

We *did* study in the privacy of our room… Occasionally. Mostly, we were balls deep in each other, but even saviours and their boyfriends needed time to recover between bouts. So we *did study.* But by the end of the week, I was feeling untouchable, confident in my abilities to pass my NEWTs. And then Winter Break began.

On the day we were expected to depart, I was dragging Draco through the halls. Deaf to his protests, his questions and curiosity, I led him to the art classroom. And once we reached the door, I rounded on him. Held a finger up to silence him, and he shut up.

“I’m only giving you this now because I can’t think of a good way to transport it. But I don’t want *your* gift until *Christmas Day.* Deal?”

His mouth snapped shut as his eyes went wide, and he nodded gravely. A subtle flush had crept into his cheeks, and I felt mine warm similarly, whether it was because of our newfound link or because I was *mortified* to show him the pitiful art I’d created, but I led him into the room with a sigh nonetheless.

“Sit down and shut your eyes,” I instructed as I released his hand. Felt… *Wrong* not to be touching him after an entire week in a room with him, but he did as he was told, and then I was rummaging through the drying racks to find what I’d crafted.

*Christ,* it looked *even worse* than I remembered as I finally withdrew it from its rack. Eyed it with a grimace before hesitantly turning back to Draco. He was sat on a stool, expectant and *bewildered* as I slowly closed in on him. Turned the canvas around to face him and took a deep breath.

“Y-you can look,” I said quietly. And his eyes opened.

For a long moment, he just *stared.* And I held my fucking *breath,* because what if he *laughed?* What if he *hated* it? Or… Or what if he’d gotten me something so much *better,* and this was like a fucking *consolation prize??* I was winding myself up, I knew, as his eyes finally drifted back up to my face.

The *swell* of emotions he felt washed over me as he slowly stood, reaching hesitantly for the painting.

“I-is that… A fucking M-*Marmite…?”*

I grinned sheepishly, and shifted my weight.

“Y-yeah.” *At least he got that right.* “It-it’s your favourite sea creature, and I… I just, well… I wanted to *try–”*

Before I could get the words out, he’d seized my face and pressed a *bruising* kiss to my lips. I yelped my muffled surprise, but easily melted into his mouth. Could never *not.*

*Thank fucking god.*

---

Mother had prepared a private coach for us. A carriage pulled by Thestrals with ostentatious filigree, not to mention the rather obvious M that decorated the doors. I rolled my eyes with a sigh as Harry and I approached outside the Hogwarts gate, trunks in hand and Harry wrapped around my arm.

“Even *now,* such a fucking *pureblood.”* I spat the title as I’d once spat the slur, and sort of chuckled at myself as we hoisted our trunks into the cabin. How *different* things were now.

Harry’s painting was carefully tucked under my other arm, wrapped in brown paper and tied with twine, and I brought this in most cautiously. Couldn’t bear the thought of ruining it, but equally couldn’t bear the thought of being *without* it, because it was so quintessentially *him,* so *perfect* to note all the different things I loved and find a way to weave them into one. It was *easily* my favourite present I’d ever received.

As we climbed into the carriage, I set it reverently on the seat across from us as Harry settled in next to me. We shared a grin, and then the door clicked shut and the Thestrals took wing. I’d never get used to that *lurching* launch into the sky, but Harry held me just as tightly as I clung to him, and an elated sort of giggle escaped my lips as we soared into the afternoon.

Harry was *fascinated,* and I was just *enamoured* with him. The way he parted the window curtains to peer out at the world below us, the way he clung to me as we lurched across the sky, the suggestion that we do something risky in the bloody *sky. God,* I was so in love with him.

“So this… *Link,”* I began carefully as wide, green eyes returned to my face. “You… You have *no idea* what you might’ve done?”

Trepidation fluttered in his chest as his eyes guttered, but he answered with a swallow.

“N-not the foggiest.” A nervous chuckle. “Are you… Did I…?”

“No! No, I… I *love it,”* I answered, because I did. *Hung* on every fucking nugget I learned through our link. “Just… Just wondering if there’s any, like… *Precedent* for this sort of thing.”

Our carriage lurched again, and he lunged into me. A quiet chuckle escaped me as I held him against me, and then our flight evened out. *God, we’re going to France. To our villa in Provence. To see mother.*

“I-I haven’t done any, like… *Research.”* He swallowed again. “B-but I can! I’ll scour the whole fucking Malfoy *library*… If you want.”

In that one single statement, I stopped caring. An easy laugh breezed through my lips as I turned a kiss down onto his hair.

“It’s fine. I like feeling you inside me all the time.”

He twisted an implicative smirk up at me, and I smirked right back.

“You *do,* do you?”

I hummed in agreement, and pulled his face up to mine to bury a *blistering* fucking kiss into his mouth. And he accepted it gladly.

*So fucking weird,* being able to feel him like this. To *sense his emotions* like this, so influential on my own. But as strange as it was, it felt… *Right.* Like *this* was what I’d been looking for the whole time. And all at once, the anxiety of breaking the news to *mother* resurfaced. Harry pulled away from my lips, studying me carefully as I breathed a sigh.

“Mother loves you,” I began. Forced a swallow down my throat. “It-it’s still just… It’s not *easy,* you know?”

I cut another sigh, and Harry’s eyes softened. He leaned up to press one more lingering yet *passing* kiss to my lips.

“You’ll feel silly for worrying once you see it’s fine,” he insisted, and I snorted.

“Yeah. Probably.”

*God, I hope he’s right.*
