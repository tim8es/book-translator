## Chapter 22: Chapter 22

I *was* eating, but I was more so watching Harry. Harry, as he selected a variety of dishes onto his plate. Harry, as he systematically took a bite of every item he’d gathered. Harry, as he worked backwards from least-liked to most. And he caught me grinning, cheeks going flushed.

“W-what?” he said around a mouthful of broccoli.

“Just the way you eat,” I admitted. “So fucking cute.”

With no one around to hear or watch us, I didn’t mind being sickeningly honest. And the way he stiffened in his chair, my grin only spread wider.

“Fuck off,” he murmured, but a grin of his own threatened his lips as he forked another floret. “Eat your food. And stop *watching me.*”

I didn’t. The sandwich that’d fallen slack in my hand lifted back to my grin as I obligingly took another bite, but Harry couldn’t stop *smiling,* and it was *enthralling.*

He ate his broccoli begrudgingly, then moved on to his rice, and left the steak medallions for last. Savoured each bite. And I couldn’t look away from his *fucking mouth.*

*Fuck, I’ve got it bad. I don’t wanna scare him away, even if he does like me.*

“You don’t like vegetables,” I said, and he cast me a sideways glance. “Except tomatoes. You know, I once watched you eat a whole fucking *raw tomato?* Really questioned my sanity, and *yours,* because who the *fuck* just *eats a tomato?”*

Sudden laughter burst out of him, and he covered his mouth with a hand.

“T-tomatoes are a *fruit,* idiot.”

“Semantics,” I said, and he shook his head, still grinning. “You never, *ever* cut your steaks into pieces. Like it’s a personal *affront* to you, you just hoist the whole damn thing up on a fork and rip *bites* off like a fucking *animal.*” He choked on a laugh again, folding over his plate as I continued. “Surprised no one’s called the fucking department of magical wildlife on you.”

He was laughing *so hard* his *throat* was turning red, and I couldn’t help but laugh, too.

“You always save your drink for the very end and down it in one. Rarely ever have dessert, but when you do, you take your sweet time. Like it’s an excuse not to leave the table.”

I was reading out an intangible fucking *love letter* to him, I knew, but I couldn’t fucking stop. Seeing him eat at such close quarters was *ridiculously* charming. And when he finally reined his humour in, he turned such a *wondrous* smile at me, I lost my breath.

“Why have you paid such close attention to my *eating habits?”*

*As if he doesn’t know.*

“Spent a lot of time watching your mouth,” I admitted. And he sobered slightly, flush reaching his ears as he straightened again. “That, and being *slightly* in love with you and unable to ignore *anything* you fucking do.”

He huffed a strangled sort of laugh, ran a hand through his hair and returned to his meal. Just sort of moved it around on his plate with his fork, though, and I waited for whatever he was gonna say.

When he finally looked back up at me, there was this raw fucking vulnerable *awe* on his face that made him look so *young*that I froze.

“I-I… I’ve never been l-*loved* like… Like *this.*” He shook his head, like he was having trouble believing it. “You… You love me for the *weirdest* fucking reasons. Like, do you even *care* that I saved the world?”

I didn’t really know how to respond. I mean, of *course* I *cared,* but it didn’t really have anything to do with why I loved him. So I shrugged and decided to say,

“Somebody had to.” Seemed straightforward enough, but Harry huffed an incredulous little laugh and shook his head again. “Never really been a *fan* of the Chosen One. Just of you.”

His cheeks went red, and I glimpsed something in his expression gutter just before he dropped his attention back to his plate.

“Y-you…” He swallowed. “You tell everyone you’re left-handed, but you’re actually ambidextrous.” I felt my face go slack, because how the *hell–?* “You don’t like sitting with your back to the door, and y-you like… Books about marine life.”

Harry turned a sheepish glance my way, and I think I was gaping at him, because he was reciting back to me things I’d *never told anyone.*

“You always pick the blueberries out of your muffins, even when there’s plain ones, and I-I think it’s… Because you like the flavour, but not the texture. You’ve always got… Random shit in your pockets. Like you just go about collecting things you like and carrying them around with you.”

I frowned, and curiously delved into one of my jeans pockets. *There’s no fucking way…*

My hands closed on something I didn’t remember putting in there, and when I withdrew it, I found a badly crumpled starling feather. Just sort of stared at it for a second, because how the *hell* did he know that when *I*wasn’t even aware? As I blinked up at him, he just gave me this smug little smile and forked another medallion into his mouth.

“How do you know all that?”

The things I’d been listing off were traits of his anyone with a *brainstem* could’ve picked up on. But I almost *never*wrote with my right hand, didn’t tell anyone about the marine biology books, and the thing with having my back to the door…

Harry swallowed his mouthful thickly, turning back to his plate with red cheeks.

“S-sixth year,” he said quietly.

*Ah. Yeah, that makes sense.* I’d noticed how closely he watched me sixth year, obviously. There was very little he ever did that I didn’t notice, but for him to pick up on things like *that…* I eyed the feather in my hand again.

“You suspected my involvement with Death Eaters… And thought it’d come in handy to watch me *pick blueberries out of my muffins?”*

When I lifted my incredulity back up to him, I saw him pushing his food around on his plate again. His cheeks still hadn’t lost any colour, and something was gathering in my chest.

“S-suppose I… Was a bit, er… *Obsessed,*” he admitted quietly. I couldn’t believe what I was fucking *hearing.*

“Harry… Potter. Did you… Have a *crush* on me?”

His spine shot pin-straight, and he turned wide eyes on me.

“Th-that… I did *not–*” I pressed my lips together, trying *so fucking hard* not to burst into laughter because even as his mouth flapped around empty excuses, I watched in real time as his fluster turned into something like *horror*. “M-maybe I *did…*”

Suddenly dropping his fork, he leaned over his plate on his elbows, hands threading into his hair, and a snort made it past my resistance.

“I-I… *Fuck,* I didn’t even know I was *gay* until like… Three fucking *hours*ago, maybe I…” I clapped a hand over my mouth, trying to give him the space to work through his thoughts as his admission sank into me.

*He’s… Actually gay. Holy shit, Harry’s full-on gay.*

A pitiful little groan left him as he pushed his plate away to lay his head on his arms, and I tried to hide the next snort that ripped out of me with a cough.

“There, there,” I teasingly consoled, replacing my hand from my mouth to pat his back. “I always *knew* you were dense, but bloody *hell,* Harry, you noticed things about me *I*didn’t even know.”

“You mean you just swipe random shit off every surface you can find on *accident?”*

“Yeah,” I laughed. *Fuck,* my heart was *pounding* in my chest. “God, I wonder what we can find in my *other* pockets.”

Harry groaned again, lifting himself to scrub at his eyes beneath his glasses.

“Feel like such a *twat…*” A burst of laughter barked out of me, and suddenly my hand on his back wasn’t *enough.* He let me pull him into my arms, burying his crimson face against my chest as I pressed a kiss to the top of his head.

“This is so fucking *ironic,*” I chortled, and he huffed an affecting cough of laughter. “*God,* you drive me fucking *mad.*”

*How many times has he seen me pocket shit without realising? Watched the way I orient myself in a room to know I keep the door in my line of sight? How long has he been* paying attention *to know me so fucking well?*

Harry’s arms wound back around my waist, and my throat went tight to consider *Harry had a crush on me. And he likes me again now. And he just admitted to being proper*gay.

*I… I just might actually have a chance.*

*Holy shit.*
