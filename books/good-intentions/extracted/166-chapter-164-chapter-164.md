## Chapter 164: Chapter 164

Chapter Notes

Once Seamus and Dean sat beside Ron, the eighth years had officially reassembled. And our meal was interspersed with at least three conversations at once for a bit as everyone recounted their holiday. I could feel Harry sort of waiting for a lull to hit, but less and less confident that one *would* as stories kept pouring forth out of the many mouths around us. When Hermione brought up their plans for a summer wedding, though, he finally perked up. And I paused to watch him as he sent me a glance.

“I-I…” He swallowed again as Hermione let him interject. “W-we actually have an announcement, too.”

She *instantly* straightened on the bench.

“Hold on, let me just…” Whipping out her wand, she conjured a quill and parchment and scripted something onto the top, flat against the table before returning a *mischievous* smile back up at us. “Alright, go ahead.”

Harry rolled his eyes, but took my hand, and I lifted it to my lips. A silent encouragement as his cheeks filled with blood.

“Well… Over the break, we sort of…” With a glance down at our clasped hands, he twisted them to make his ring obvious, and she followed his gaze down to it. “Er…”

That head of brunette curls twirled as Hermione was about to brag again to her fiancé, but as I laid my left hand over Harry’s left wrist, I could almost see the gears turning as she turned back to us.

“Wait,” she said quietly, frowning eyes dancing between our two rings.

“Oh, you’re engaged? How lovely,” Luna congratulated from Harry’s right, but Ron was the first on the uptake as he slowly stood at the table, eyes locked on our hands.

“No, no fucking way,” he breathed, fiancée *gaping* at us by his side. “No *fucking way!* I… I won.”

*Oh?* The ginger pointed at our hands as he twisted his shock back to Hermione, drawing her similarly shocked, wide eyes up at him.

“They’re *not* engaged like you thought, they’re *fucking married!”* An astonished chuckle huffed out of him, and Harry snorted quietly beside me.

*That’s a twist,* he mused as Ron cast his gaze around the table as if to say *‘is everyone seeing this? Hermione lost a bet!’*

“You’re fucking *lying,”* Neville supposed as he snapped a hand out to take Harry’s, which I reluctantly released for him to hold up to the light. “You two e-*eloped??”*

Hermione dragged his hand out of Neville’s to see for herself, muttering something about *not possible,* but Ron was just on top of the bloody *world* as she studied Harry’s ring.

“W-well, erm… I mean, h-he *proposed,* and I said yes, and… Well, the opportunity to sign the p-*paperwork*presented itself, so we… Just… Did?”

“We’re still planning a wedding– well, *mother* is, but I suspect it might fall around the same time as yours is why Harry’s bringing it up now.”

Hermione extended her other hand for mine, and beckoned it just as the bureaux woman had, and I acquiesced. Let her hold both our hands and frown down at them, but as she studied them, the crease on her brow eased.

“Oh my *god,”* she exhaled shakily as Dean and Seamus angled closer to get a good look.

“Oh my god is *right,* holy *shit!”* Ron agreed as he sat again. “I… *Genuinely* didn’t think I’d win that one, but when you set the *stakes…”*

Our hands were suddenly released as Hermione went *vividly* red, and slowly turned back to blush at her fiancé, who was grinning for all the *world* like he’d just caught her in a snare.

“R-Ron, let’s be *reasonable* here, it’s not like you bet on this because you *knew* it would happen, so if anything–!”

“Aht, aht! You’ve received *every galleon* you’ve wagered, and I’ll not have you backing out just because you bet something you didn’t want to.”

Harry and I exchanged a curious glance, but she just muttered something to herself and fidgeted with her hair awkwardly across from us. When Ron found us waiting expectantly, he smirked.

“I owe you one, truly. Anything you want, it’s yours. You’ve just made me the happiest man on *earth.”*

When Hermione swiped a hand out to swat at Ron’s chest, enough context clues gathered to know we *did not* want to know what he’d just won. And as he cackled to himself, Harry cleared his throat, twined his fingers back with mine and took a deep breath.

“Th-that’s not all… Though,” he began hesitantly. And then he glanced up at me with those big, green eyes, and I internally *swooned* at how bloody *gorgeous* he was, at how deeply he *loved me,* so much more than I ever thought I deserved. And he *smiled.* “We’re soulbound.”

If the news fell like a lead weight on our friends, I couldn’t tell. I was *lost* in Harry, in that fervent source of love that pooled around his soul, the eddies of his frankly *preposterous* magic that swelled the more I sank into them. Felt my own smile spreading across my cheeks, the pleasure that warmed them, and only returned to myself when Luna said,

“Oh! That’s one for me, then, isn’t it?”

Blinking out of the rapidly *devouring* centre of Harry’s soul, we both peered over at her. Pleased as *punch,* eyes wide with expectation as we glanced back at Ron and Hermione.

“R-*really?”* Hermione asked, and Harry nodded. “That’s… That’s *ancient* magic. That’s, like… *Extinct magic.”*

I snorted. “That’d explain why it’s so bloody hard to find any information about it.”

Ron seemed too stunned to even *blink,* but still managed to fish a single galleon out of his pocket for Luna, and slid it across the table to her.

“You’re… *For sure* soulbound?” And Hermione paid her loss the same.

“Got it in writing and all,” I answered. “Sent the French bureaux into a tizzy, but… Here we are.”

“That’s *mad,”* Seamus finally spoke, drawing our attention as he gaped at us. “I thought shite like *soulbonds* were focken’ *made up.”*

A chuckle coughed out of me.

“So did I.”
