## Chapter 143: Chapter 143

Chapter Notes

I think I was in shock as the sun steadily rose, igniting the room in rosy reds and fiery oranges through the windows as I curled up with Draco on the couch. Mugs empty and discarded on the tray as Narcissa sat with us.

The other side of the record I’d played was still sweeping soothing jazz into the sitting room, and by now, I’d started *fretting.* Fretting because Draco had given me the most *perfect Christmas ever,* and all I’d given *him* was a lousy fucking painting that I’d–

*Fretting?* his silent voice asked in my head, and I glanced up at him to find a *smirk.*

*Eager to tie me up again, aren’t we?* I mirrored his smirk back to him, and he dropped a kiss onto my scar.

*Haven’t been able to stop thinking about it,* he answered. And a chill went down my spine as the hair on the back of my neck stood on end. *Love having my way with you.*

*Forever. Yours forever.*

I felt him smile against my brow as his lips lingered, my *husband.* The consequences of this morning were taking *forever* to sink in as Narcissa hummed along to the music. Not only were we *soulbound,* but suddenly, seemingly in the blink of an *eye,* we weren’t just engaged. Engaged for less than a *day* before we were… *Married. I’m fucking married.*

A giddy little flurry of *joy* fluttered in my heart, and Draco pulled me tighter into him.

*Harry’s husband,* he mused to himself, and I couldn’t *possibly* smile any fucking *wider.* I reached the hand that wasn’t laced with his up to massage my aching cheeks. In my mind’s eye, I recalled how I’d wondered if he’d ever scripted *Draco Potter* into any of his notes, complete with little love hearts all around it and a small huff of laughter escaped me. The image in my head must’ve been clear enough that he saw it too, because he stiffened around me.

“Sounds weird,” I said quietly, and the clanging emotions tearing through Draco forced a small chuckle of his own through his nose.

“What does?” Narcissa wondered, and we both looked over at her in unison. And Draco cleared his throat.

*“Draco Potter,”* he answered. And hearing it *out loud,* I… My heart did *backflips,* because despite how *ridiculous* it sounded, to be able to give him something only *I* could, something that’d only ever been *mine* and one of the few things in the world that made me feel so *separate* from everyone else… To tear the crown of thorns Lucius had forced onto Draco and trade it for the laurel wreath of *my name…* “Not as silly as *Harry Malfoy,* but here we are.”

A reflexive giggle bubbled out of me, and I felt him twist a smile down at me, so I mirrored one back up for him. Narcissa might’ve been having another *moment,* but I was drowning in beautiful grey eyes, alight with the crackling fireplace and *love…*

*“Mister Potter,”* I breathed, throat going tight even as I teased him. How many times I’d been reprimanded with that title, and now there would be *two of us.*

*Two living Potters. Not alone anymore.*

Draco’s chin wobbled a bit as Narcissa piped back in with a thick:

“Y-you’re *still* going to have a *wedding…* Right?”

Those beautiful, shining, wet grey eyes blinked hard as he sucked in a gasp, and chased it with a long-suffering sigh.

“You’d never forgive us if we didn’t let you throw a bloody great party for us, would you?” he supposed, rolling his eyes back over at his mother. My *mother-in-law.* And just *imagining* the sort of event Narcissa would plan made my *head spin.*She cleared her throat.

“Glad we’re on the same page.”

It was then the music finally faded back to silence, and she took the opportunity to rise from her chair to go put on a new record.

*Never heard music in the house until this holiday. Father always thought it undignified and distracting. Figures he ever had me take piano lessons at all.*

My eyes snapped back up to Draco’s face, and I felt his wince as it flashed across his face.

*“Please,* no,” he begged as quiet Christmas music began. But I sat up straighter, grappling with this new knowledge.

“You play *piano?”*

A groan as he released my shoulders to let me swivel and face him. *Draco plays piano.*

“No, I *don’t.* Knowing how to do something and actually *doing it* are two different things.” *Always hated lessons. I’ll never forgive that fucking teacher for taking something so beautiful and ruining it with discipline. Though I suppose discipline was always the purpose of the lessons at all.*

I only deflated *slightly* as Narcissa rejoined us, imagining the painstake it must’ve required to be under a musician’s tutelage. But also, picturing a young Draco sat at a *piano,* even if he was wearing a permanent scowl and had bleeding knuckles from being rapped for mistakes so often was… *Delightful.*

*One day. One day I’ll hear you play.* He gave me a despairing sigh, but my determination was distracted as Narcissa set something on the ottoman in my peripherals.

“You’ve gotta be fucking *joking,”* Draco moaned as he recognised what she’d procured. With an innocent, *wicked* little smile, she reclaimed her seat as my eyes lighted on what we’d been presented with.

*A photo album? Don’t tell me this is…* I reached for it, and found it to be *heavy,* but the moment I leaned back with it, eyeing the gold filigree around the edges, Draco snatched it out of my grasp.

*“Oi!”* I sighed as he held it out of my reach, and scowled at him. He scowled *right* back at me.

“Is this *really* necessary? Like, *vitally* important? Can we just… *Not?”*

I reached for it again, but his arms were longer than mine, and I was *this close* to just–

“Oh, go on, Draco, first Christmas with the *new husband!”* Narcissa tutted, and my eyes narrowed at Draco’s exposed ribs.

“We’ve known each other since we were *eleven,* isn’t that enough?”

I *lunged* for his sides, pinning him down as I straddled his lap, and he *squealed.* With only one hand to defend himself, he was completely *vulnerable* to my attack, and sent me *cackling* with the way he twisted and writhed and *giggled* under my assault. Eventually he discarded the album onto the couch to wrest both my wrists away from himself, and I was just *howling.*

“Fuck– fucking *prick,”* he gasped, and it was just then Narcissa’s quiet chuckling reached my ears. I *was* trying to wriggle my hands free to reach for the album again, but was too entertained with this play-fighting to put any real effort in as Draco forced my wrists together to wrap one long hand around them. “See how *you* like it.”

He earned a *yelp* as he twisted me back towards the not-photo-album end of the couch, pinning me onto my back in one hand as the other reached beneath my jumper.

“No no no *Draco–!”* My protest was swallowed in the laughter he earned as he dealt his revenge, with all the advantage his longer limbs provided.

“Shouldn’t dish it out if you can’t *take it,* eh?” Abs *aching* with laughter as I writhed beneath him, and Narcissa was just *losing it,* watching us.

*Stop, please, we don’t have to look at your precious baby photos, please–*

Thankfully, he did stop. Leaned over me with a smug little grin as I gasped for air, and dropped a *smack* of a kiss to my cheek.

*Reckon if I pinned you to this couch much longer we’d end up in a rather compromising situation anyway,* was his silent retort, and I felt my cheeks *burn.* A quiet snort was his only reaction as I swallowed and let him pull me back up to sit proper again. And very, *very* reluctantly took up the photo album again.

“If you so much as *think* the word *‘aw,’* I’m going to castrate you,” he levelled a warning as he slowly offered it to me. Cautious fingers accepted it, and I *barely* bit back a smirk of my own as I silently shot back:

*They’re yours to do with what you want, but I reckon you’d miss them,* and he rolled his eyes.

The giant tome landed in my lap as I twisted it to orient it correctly, but *nothing* could’ve prepared me for the first page. The very first image, a swaddled baby in a crib, face all screwed up and *red* as it screamed what looked like *bloody murder.* Next to it was a younger-looking, *softer-* looking Narcissa, cradling a sleeping blonde bundle as she rocked gently in a chair. My heart fucking *melted.*

“Oh my *god,”* because beneath that was a sleeping Narcissa, curled up on a couch with a similarly sleeping baby in her arms and breathing softly. So *peaceful* looking, she looked *so much* like him at that age, and I felt my throat go tight.

“I’ve taken all the ones with your father out,” the woman in question intoned quietly. Draco hummed noncommittally, and I felt his attention honed in on *me,* but was led to flip the page like a *summons.*

There on the back was Narcissa again, trying to entice a *fussy* little baby with a stuffed toy dragon. Hair all out of sorts, looking rather *exhausted,* but nothing but adoration in her eyes. And it was only then that I felt Draco’s attention shift off of me to the pages. Beside this one was a *bewildered-*looking blonde baby in a little white frock on the grass, peering around like he’d never been *outdoors* before. Just *astonished* with the enormity of the world.

The opposite page bore photos of Draco a little older, peeking around a corner of a wall and *scowling.* *God,* that little scowl was *so Draco,* and a wet little laugh coughed out of my lips. Snapshots of his childhood accumulated before my eyes, and I had to wonder if any baby photos of *me* existed.

*Maybe in Godric’s Hollow,* I mused as I turned the pages. Draco was a toddler now, resisting being preened, covered in food in a high chair, and pitching a *fit* at being cleaned up. There were several rather obvious blank spaces where photos had been removed, and I was honestly glad for it.

A bit further in, he’d reached the age he’d been in my dream. In the cupboard, when we’d been locked in together. And recalling this dream fed it straight into him, because he stiffened. An arm around my waist, pulling me tighter to his side as I turned the pages. It gripped my heart in an iron *fist* to see how *rare* a smile was on his little face in these photos, almost always scowling or *glaring,* but when he finally reached out to prevent me turning a page, I paused.

Long, slender fingers slid up to touch a corner of a photo, and I studied it, wondering what about *this one* was significant. It was the first one featuring him in his Slytherin robes, clearly first year, but compared to the one next to it where he was fidgeting in generic Hogwarts robes…

*This was after I’d first met you.*

And it clicked. In the first, he’d looked *nervous.* Reluctant, if a bit *sour,* but in the span of a *year…* There was this *calm* about his expression, carefully assembled but unable to fully conceal the *brightness* of his eyes. For the first time since getting my hands back on the album, I tore my attention from it. Peered up at Draco, and he met my eyes.

Not for the first time, but no less *dizzying,* our entire history together crashed into me. All the tormenting and the cruelty, the *importance* of our relationship, even if it had been antagonistic. And knowing he’d only paid me so much attention because he *liked me,* and had no way of knowing how to *show it…*A swallow forced itself down my throat.

*Loved you for so, so long,* he thought, eyes dipping to my mouth once. *Since before I even knew what love looked like.*

Knowing how Lucius had treated Narcissa told me everything I needed to know. I’d wanted to pick apart his brain, learn why he thought to *torment* the person he liked, but the answer had always been there. Just beyond my thick, *stupid* perception, but my heart *knew,* and it was like in this moment, seeing that *brightness* in his eleven-year-old eyes after our first year together, that I finally, *finally understood.*

I leaned up to kiss him. A gasp sucked into his nose as our lips met, but only a second later, a hand cradled my face. And it fucking *destroyed* Draco to learn I *forgave him.* Up until now, I’d been content to just *let go* of how he’d treated me in school. To acquaint myself with this new version of him, but had never really found it in myself to *excuse* his behaviour. But to actually *see* the difference I’d made in his life, just *meeting* me, a fellow victim of childhood abuse, of *course* I forgave him. Sort of boggled my mind to realise it hadn’t been until *now,* but Narcissa didn’t know the *half* of what she’d given me with this photo album.

Meeting me, being curious about me, being *rejected* by me and then all the following months of *obsession* that came with his first rejection. Growing to admire me, to *envy* me, all the while returning to Lucius’s household each summer. How parallel our lives had been and I’d *never known.*

*God,* what fucking *torture* to be in love with the person you were meant to hate. If our roles had been reversed, my second-year attempt would’ve been much more successful. How he’d managed to bear it each year, being bounced back and forth between the *Manor* and *me,* I would never know, but even despite the fucking *war,* he’d fallen in love with me. Saved my *life* when I’d been caught, and even *confessed after.* I’d never, *ever* be able to say how much he meant to me, and was suddenly all the more *grateful* for the soulbond. Meant I didn’t *have* to articulate it, because I could just *feel it,* and that meant he could, too.

A stuttering gasp out of my mouth was the interruption I needed to realise we were *both fucking crying. Jesus fucking shit.*Bloodshot, teary grey eyes blinked down at me as a thumb stroked my cheek gently, and a quiet throat-clear registered somewhere in the peripherals of my perception.

“Breakfast,” Narcissa invited gently, and I heard a chair creak, and footsteps. But I was just fucking *lost* in memories.

Joining the Slytherin Quidditch team *as a Seeker* to get closer to me. Hurling insults at my friends because he was *jealous* that they were important to me. Directing all of his anger, *all* of his *hurt* at me because he had no other outlet and had been taught that *kindness* was *weakness.* In a topple of dominos, I fell in love with *every other version* of Draco that I’d ever *known.*

Product of an unhappy marriage, I couldn’t relate, but almost *literally everything else…*

*Wasted so much time,* I whinged internally, and a corner of Draco’s lips twitched in a smile.

*Never again,* he answered. Sniffed, and withdrew his hand from my cheek to swipe at his own. *Never wasting another second with you, Harry.*

*Wedding. Cottage. Goats and chickens and horses. Forever.*

He nodded tearfully, and touched his forehead to mine. And my eyes slid shut.

*Breakfast first.*
