## Chapter 92: Chapter 92

Chapter Notes

It was just like the Ministry to give me no time to prepare, and I couldn’t help but wonder *why now? It’s just a few weeks in, surely they don’t think I could’ve somehow fucked up already.* But I didn’t speak a word as McGonagall led me to her office Floo.

A ‘follow-up hearing’ is how my mother had phrased it in her letter. And as we disappeared into green flames, I started backtracking everything I’d done since the start of term. Looking for anything I could be in trouble for, any reason they might conjure to pry me out of school. We arrived in the Atrium before I could come up with anything.

Reporters instantly swarmed us, but their words fell on deaf ears as McGonagall braced an arm around my shoulders and guided me through the throng. Harry’s name managed to break through the persistent silence in my head a few times, but *would they really kick me out for being late a couple times? God, I wish Harry was here.*

How I’d *loathed* seeing him here last time. Felt like a personal punch to the gut to see him so soon after my confession. And then to have been invited *back to school* had just been so *unfathomable,* maybe the Wizengamot was rethinking their decision to leave it up to me? Maybe it didn’t even *matter* if I’d been a *model student,* or that I’d somehow ended up dating their precious saviour, because there was already plenty of red in my ledger. The Minister himself seemed to be one of the precious few who thought I deserved a second chance.

We finally reached the courtroom antechamber, and McGonagall’s grip released my shoulders. She hesitated before opening the inner doors, though, and I glanced up at her to see a sympathetic frown on her brow.

“If it’s not too improper of me to say,” she began, facing me a little straighter. “You’ve been made to do a great many difficult things in your life, Draco. But I’m betting this won’t be one of them.”

I almost snorted. *Easy, just walk in, be put on the spot and grilled in front of dozens of strangers who could easily strip me of my wand and lock my arse up in the worst hell on the planet. Child’s play.*

“Thanks, Headmistress,” I heard myself mutter, but my voice sounded far away. She gave me a stiff little pat on the shoulder and then pushed the doors open.

---

*CHOSEN ONE INVOLVED WITH DEATH EATER?* My leg bounced beneath the desk as I leaned over it on an elbow, forehead in my palm as I forced my eyes to move past the Prophet’s dramatic headline. Not that the publication ever had anything clever to say, nor did I really *care* what the general public might think, but this time it wouldn’t just be *me* affected by public opinion. I’d put Draco on the *bloody front page,* and wanted nothing more than to rip the paper apart with my fucking *teeth.* But I made myself read.

*‘Harry Potter, recently re-enrolled at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, was spotted in Hogsmeade hand-in-hand with none other than Draco Malfoy, only son of known Death Eater Lucius Malfoy. While the latter is serving his deserved life sentence in Azkaban Prison, his son has been given free rein to return to the very same school.’*

*‘Free rein.’ As if he’s been ‘unleashed’ on us.* The rest of the article was just more and more irritating as the author hypothesised Draco had *bewitched* me somehow, like I’d *never* hold his hand unless I wasn’t in my right *mind.* Took jabs at Kingsley, too, for his judgement. And I was inching nearer and nearer to insanity as my eyes drank in the *nonsense* that’d actually made the ‘news.’ *Ridiculous publication, all they care about is shock and awe. Maybe I*should*write an autobiography.*

When the article went so far as to suppose Draco was only getting in my good graces to *finish the job* killing me, I stood suddenly. Couldn’t sit here and read this shit, especially when I’d *already* broken everybody’s quills once. I was going to do far worse if I kept spiralling, so without a word of explanation, I kicked my chair away and stalked out of the room.

Where I was going, I couldn’t be sure. Straight to the Ministry? To the bathroom? McGonagall’s office? Started to run like I knew where I wanted to be, and the *rage* burning in my chest pushed my legs into a sprint. And as I followed hallways and descended stairs, I realised where my feet were carrying me.

The doors to McGonagall’s office burst open before I even reached them, and I barrelled straight through. Found it empty, but right in front of her desk sat Draco’s bag. A heap on the floor like he’d just *dropped*it. I span where I stood, searching for any clues where he might be, when my eyes snagged on a portrait that hadn’t been there before.

I hadn’t been in this room since before the war, and was sort of *dumbfounded* as a very grim, slightly wary *Severus fucking Snape* blinked down at me.

“You’re fucking *joking,”* I breathed, and he sat up a bit straighter. *Indignant.*

“Language, Potter,” he drawled, earning a reflexive scoff. I couldn’t decide if I was amused or annoyed or *furious,* but ended up just standing there as we regarded one another. The entirety of my history with the Potions professor slammed into me as he cleared his throat. “Something I can help you with?”

“Where’s Draco?” was out of my lips before I’d even caught my breath. And Snape raised an incredulous eyebrow as he eyed me down the crook of his nose.

“I imagine he’s at the Ministry with the Headmistress,” was his answer, and my heart sank. *I fucking knew it.* I wracked my brain for some way to reach him as I wandered over to a stool beside the tucked-away Pensieve. Flopped down onto it, defeated but *not giving up. There has to be a way.* “Suppose hell must’ve frozen over if *you two* are on a first-name basis.”

Snape sniffed impassively as I reluctantly glanced back up at his portrait. He was studying his nails like he wasn’t *at all* curious why I’d used Draco’s first name. And another beleaguered scoff coughed out of me as I leaned forward, elbows on knees to worry my hands together.

“Dating, actually,” I admitted without a second thought. *If they’re at the Ministry, then his mum must’ve been right about that follow-up hearing. So it’ll just be, like, a progress report. Not like I haven’t broken into the Ministry before, but I’ll bet security’s tighter since–*

My thoughts all screeched to a halt as Snape… *Laughed.* Not a biting, sadistic laugh either, but *genuine amusement.* And I lifted a *bewildered* frown back up at him.

“Poetry,” he chortled to himself, curling a fist before his lips as he shook his head. “Your father would’ve *loved* this.”

The scoff I gave this time was almost as amused as he was, and I sat a bit straighter.

“H-he *does,* actually.” Snape sobered somewhat as I forced a swallow down my throat. “I-I introduced them… With the Resurrection Stone.” The frown that’d been growing on his brow eased somewhat as he lowered his hand. “Had a right good laugh that Draco’s Patronus manifested as a s-*stag.”*

A chortle of my own tumbled through my lips to recall the way my dad had *lost it.* Bittersweet as my throat tightened, but then Snape’s expression shifted. Ever so slightly, something like *awe* shone in his eyes. And I shied away from it, returning a frown down to Draco’s bag as I gnawed on my lip.

*He’s with McGonagall. So he’ll be alright… Right?*

“It’s a *powerful* sort of love that can affect someone’s Patronus,” he said softly. I glanced back up at him, felt my features soften to recognise what he was saying. And he twitched a *smile* at me.

“Yeah.”
