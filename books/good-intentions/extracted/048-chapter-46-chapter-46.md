The sun was warm and the breeze was mild as we approached the Quidditch pitch. It was abandoned, being the weekend, but it still made me slightly nostalgic to see. Despite all the *shit* I’d endured during my Quidditch years, Quirrell manipulating my broom first year and most notably the Dementor that had us lose the only game Gryffindor had ever *lost* with me as their Seeker, my lungs went tight to see the towering stands.

“Alright?” Draco asked quietly as we came to a halt. And I swallowed.

“Y-yeah. Just…” I turned my gaze back over to him, read the concern in his features. He was so easy to read now. “F-feels like another life.”

His smile was all sympathy as he reached out to ruffle my hair. It was pleasant, but I still couldn’t help casting my gaze around to make sure we were alone. When I was satisfied that we were, I inched closer to him and laid my head against his shoulder. *God,* it felt so good just to *touch him.*

He eased out a sigh, like touching me was equally blissful, and I reached into his pocket to take his hand. His fingers wrapped easily back around mine.

“Wish we could just skip the whole *coming-out* bit,” I admitted quietly as an ease I was steadily growing used to settled over me.

“Me too,” he agreed. Laid his head against mine. I wasn’t sure about sitting on pure fucking *ground,* but thought this a nice enough place to relax for a while, but just as I was deciding to attempt to lay in the grass, the sound of footsteps approaching tore me from my reverie. I ripped myself from Draco to whirl on our visitor, only to find the *last* fucking person I wanted to see.

Of *course,* it had to be *fucking Ginny* stalking towards us, frowning decidedly at the grass in full Quidditch gear, broom in hand. A breeze whipped her hair around her face as she finally lifted her attention to us, and I saw the moment her expression went slack. And Draco finally turned with me to see who’d joined us.

*“Shit.”*

She took one long look at us, shock hardening into a grimace, and then pivoted on her heel to stalk back towards the changing rooms. And I glanced at Draco, already wearing sympathy as he jerked his chin towards her.

“Go on.”

I full-on *sprinted* after her as she marched towards the girls’ changing rooms. *Not like this, please god, FUCK!*

She’d just pushed through the door when I came barrelling after her, and my head *swam* at the notion that I was *in the girls’ changing room,* but then she threw her broom to the floor and rounded on me, arms folded in that signature *Weasley* way as she pierced me with a stare.

“What?” she demanded, like *nothing* was wrong, like *nothing* had just happened and I’d pursued her into the girls’ changing room on a *whim.*

“*Fuck,* Gin, can you just h-hear me out…?” I swallowed as she leaned into her hip, and just like that I knew *exactly* how bad this was about to be. *Shit.*

“Go on, then. *Explain* why you’re in the girl’s lockerroom right now, why you’ve been chumming it up with *bloody Malfoy,* and for *god’s* sake, this better be good.”

I swore under my breath, passed a hand over my mouth and tried to assemble my *tumultuous* fucking thoughts.

“I-it’s not… *We’re* not… Like…” She raised her eyebrows, jutted out her chin like *not what?* *What the fuck am I supposed to say??* Seeing her wait so expectantly, and feeling the *multitude*of emotions flooding through me, my brain sort of short-circuited and my mouth fucking *abandoned* reason. “I’m gay.”

Ginny’s brow eased in shock. Her entire fucking *body language* shifted as I internally cursed myself.

“Y-you’re *what?”*

*Christ, this is why I’m not cut out for girls.*

“I-I didn’t know until, like… *Last Friday,* but… Y-yeah.” My heart sank into my *gut* as her face fell. Arms falling to her side as her gaze slipped past me, sort of working through my admission, if I had to guess. So I tried to talk again, like a fucking *idiot.* “It’s not, like *anything* to do with you. I-I mean… *Fuck,* I mean like you didn’t *do this* to me, I just… *Honestly* didn’t know. I *promise* I didn’t fucking know.”

Ginny turned towards a row of lockers, hands on her hips as she leaned her forehead against the metal. And I wanted to fucking *cry.*

“I’m s-*so* sorry,” I began again. I just couldn’t fucking *shut up.* “I’m *so sorry,* Gin. I n-never meant to– I mean, I still l-*love* you, I just… *Fuck me,* if there was some way for me to fix this, I would. I *swear,* I never had *any intention* of–”

She lifted her head suddenly, cutting off my words with a *steely* fucking glare. And I swallowed again. *Christ,* she could be fucking terrifying when she wanted to be.

“You’re *gay?**”* I just gaped at her. Like the *idiot* I was, all I could do was nod. And she sighed, pinched her brow as she rotated to lean back against the lockers. “*Course* you are.” She huffed a weak sort of laugh, scrubbed at her brow. “*God,* I feel like an *idiot.*”

Sort of numb, I shook my head. Stumbled a step closer to her.

“No… *No,* Ginny, I… *I’m* a fucking idiot. None of this is *your fault.”*

Her voice broke on a sobbing sort of laugh as she met my eyes again, and in that moment I was, just for a second, sixteen again. Awkward(er) and *hopeless*, with *no fucking idea* what I was doing with my life. Just sort of following where it lead me. But then she sniffed, pushed off the lockers and cracked her neck.

“Alright.” She squared her shoulders towards me again, arms back around her waist as she levelled that *deadly* fucking stare at me. And I shrank a bit. “I’m not mad at you. *But…”* she interrupted my internal celebration as she went on. “You owe me a fucking explanation. Fucking *Malfoy??”*

I swallowed again, offering a sheepish smile. But she *wasn’t mad at me.* And I was *drowning* in love with Draco. So I straightened my spine and began the explanation she fucking *deserved.*

*God, this woman… Scary as they are, women are fucking incredible.*

I waited for Harry out on the pitch so long I almost fell asleep. The sun sank low in the Western sky as I laid back on the grass, hands behind my head. As determined as I was to give them the time they needed to sort this out, I was just about to go looking for him when I heard footsteps crunching on the grass approaching me. So I remained perfectly still where I laid.

Eventually, a body dropped into the grass beside me, hissing and then adjusting and giving a *great* fucking sigh. And I turned my head to look at him.

Harry was laying beside me, staring up at the sky with a sort of *dumbfounded* sort of look on his face. Which I couldn’t fucking interpret. Was it good or bad? Had things gone as horribly wrong as I’d predicted, given his *way with words?* Or… Had they been resolved painlessly? The latter seemed highly unlikely, but I just waited for him to speak as he exhaled another sigh.

“‘S fine,” he eventually choked. Cleared his throat, swallowed, and began again. “Sh-she’s not mad.”

“You told her?” I couldn’t help but ask as I rolled onto my side, lifting my head to prop it on a fist as he glanced over at me with a wince.

“Y-yeah.” All of a sudden, he looked as awe-struck as I *felt.* “Told her *everything.*”

I quirked an eyebrow. “*Everything?”*

He nodded, returning his attention to the sky as he worked through his thoughts. And I only waited patiently.

“Think she’s a bit g-*gutted,* but… Not mad at me.” Swallowed again. “Nor you,” he added on hastily. “That’ll be a conversation for another day, but… *Yeah.*”

I couldn’t fucking believe it. He told her *everything??* I suddenly wished I could’ve been a fly on the wall to hear how his horribly mangled confession had come out. *God,* I wanted to see his *face* when he told her. Wanted to see *her* face. I leaned down to kiss him, and he melted like *fucking butter* under me.

*This is it. She was the last hurdle before our public debut.* I couldn’t deny the implications of fucking *Ginny Weasley* knowing about us, and it sent my heart fucking *racing* to know we were that much closer to never having to hide again.

God, the *hiding* was the worst part. Wasn’t horrible, considering all the things we could *do* in private, but just the thought of being able to kiss him in *public…* Harry groaned against my mouth, and it rattled my fucking *brain.*

*“Dra–”* he managed to whine into my mouth, and I agreeably pulled off him. Reluctantly, but agreeably. And he blinked up at me for a long moment, long enough for me to reach out and stroke his cheek with a thumb. Saw his throat bob with a swallow. “Th-this is probably gonna *suck.”*

*Yeah,* I wanted to say. *But I’d come out with you a hundred times over if it meant never having to hide again.*

“We’ve survived worse,” is what I ended up breathing against his face. And his eyes guttered up at me, that *impossible* fucking green. I was suddenly aware I was smiling, because he twitched a smile back up at me.

“Yeah,” he agreed. Seemed to take heart from my words as he adjusted on the grass beneath me. *Fuck,* I was so in love with him I wasn’t sure I wouldn’t do *anything* he wanted me to do. Like a fucking *Imperius,* like a *love potion,* I was purely fucking *gone*for him. “Yeah, it’ll be f-fine.”

I nodded, still smiling like an idiot as I lowered another kiss to his brow. And spoke against it.

“Long as I’ve got you, *everything is perfect.”*

And I meant it.
