## Chapter 201: Epilogue 1

The slideshow of photographs from the wedding was starting to make me wonder if I’d even *been there.* I’d missed so much, so wrapped up in Draco, which only made me more grateful for the album illuminating the wall.

“Oh, *god,”* Ron groaned from the other end of the couch as his face, red and weepy and *contorted* with emotions lit up the wall with the next image. Must’ve been the moment he’d seen Hermione in her dress, and I snickered thickly to see just how *wrecked* he’d been. The witch herself hummed warmly, leaned a little further into her husband’s side next to me.

“Told you you’d fall apart,” Draco muttered from my other side, an arm over my shoulders as the next image replaced Ron’s weeping face. Now it was *his,* wide grey eyes full of rapt fascination as he first laid eyes on me. “Bloody hell, now *I* look like the arsehole for not *immediately crying.”*

I laughed a bit easier, because he’d cried *plenty,* but recalled how long it’d taken for it to sink in. Sort of reminded me of the expression he’d worn when I’d first asked him to bed me. Even though it’d been *months* before the soulbond, I could *easily* believe he’d been just as numb with *awe.*

“Oh fuck off,” Ron grumbled, lifting his beer bottle back to his lips and sighing into it. It was so nice to not only have Draco and my friends on speaking terms, but on *bantering*terms. I tucked my legs a little further under me beneath the blanket as the slideshow continued.

Ron and Hermione swaying in each others’ arms, and I heard her tut affectionately. Draco and I *staring* at Teddy in his arms as he mimicked a feature each of ours, and I pulled my glass of white wine back up to my lips to chase down the tightness that grew in my throat at the sight.

He and I still hadn’t spoken about it. Felt enough of his *apprehension* for forming new connections, but neither of us could deny how *taken* we were with the kid. Outside of the disarming attention of those big, curious eyes, Draco was still rather *reeling* at the idea of becoming any sort of paternal role in a child’s life. Didn’t want to become Lucius in *any way,* and I knew without even trying that nothing I’d say would change his mind.

Still, Andromeda *had* offered to let us *babysit…*

Draco’s arm tightened slightly around my shoulders as a moving photo of us cracking up over something by the catering tables illuminated the wall next. His sleeves cuffed up to the elbows, an ease to his posture, to his *laughter* that drew a smile onto my face. The next was Neville and Luna getting *silly* on the dancefloor, and I barked a laugh.

Hermione’s unoccupied hand blindly sought out my own, and I gripped it back readily. I shot her a sideways grin, which she mirrored back to me, because *god,* this had been a *great idea.* Granted, their apartment *was* just as tiny as she’d said, but it wasn’t… Uncomfortable.

After the slideshow finally ended, Hermione requested Draco’s help in the kitchen to help with dinner, leaving Ron and I mulling over the wedding in silence.

“Wish I could marry her all over again,” he said airily, quietly as he twisted his beer bottle against his knee. And I smirked over at him.

“Yeah. Me too.”

A part of me wondered if Draco had gone behind my back to orchestrate this moment with Ron, but there was *no way* he could pull something like that off with the soulbond in place. All the same, I straightened slightly on the couch and cleared my throat.

There was a difficult conversation to be had, after all.

“Ron,” I began, and he seemed to pull himself out of some internal reverie as he glanced over at me. “I… I’m a bloody hypocrite.”

His pale brow pinched a frown as I turned more fully towards him, delving into my trouser pocket for the salve to the unspoken wound I’d inflicted on our friendship. But as my fingers curled around it, I hesitated.

“How’d you mean?”

I swallowed. Braced myself, but this was *Ron.* My best friend, through thick and thin.

“I… I summoned *S-Sirius* after the wedding,” I finished quietly as I pulled the stone free. Held it out in my palm as his frown turned down to my hand, and his brow went slack. “It’s n-not *fair* of me to just… Justify its use only for myself. And, I mean, rather think I *earned* it, so… S-so if you *want,* I just…”

He hesitated a hand towards mine, eyes trapped on the stone as I held still for him to take it. But inches before he could, his attention snapped back up to my face.

“A-are you *sure?* I mean… Took me a while to wrap my head around, but this… This is sort of *cursed…* Isn’t it?”

I eyed the stone contemplatively. And Sirius’s allowance rang in my ears as I debated.

“I-I *think…* It’s probably no more cursed than the cloak or the wand. It all comes down to who uses it, and for what purpose. So long as you’re only *visiting,* not *clinging,* I think it’ll… *Probably*be fine.”

Ron’s blue eyes drifted back down to the stone as he finally palmed it. Turned it up to study it, and I awkwardly drew my own hand back into my lap.

“That’s not to say you shouldn’t be *careful.* I mean… Maybe George could use some *supervision,* but…” I shrugged with one shoulder. “I dunno. I don’t really see the *harm* in just *visiting,* so long as it doesn’t become an *obsession.”*

A wince pinched Ron’s features, likely envisioning his brother’s reaction to seeing his *twin,* but I had to admit, even *George* deserved this. To the untrained eye, it might’ve seemed like he’d just been his normal, mischievous self, but Ron and I knew better. Bloke was a *husk* of his former self.

And maybe this would only do more damage. Maybe it’d give him back a part of him that he’d *lost,* but… In the end, he was an autonomous adult, just like us. And he deserved just as much choice over the matter.

“Thank you, Harry,” Ron breathed like he couldn’t trust his voice as his fingers closed around the stone. And he sought out my face again, the pinch around his eyes now a bit more like *pain* as he slid across Hermione’s empty seat to pull me into his arms. Sort of shocked me frozen for a moment for him to *initiate* a hug, but I returned it eventually. “I… Can’t tell you what this means to me.”

I huffed an awkward, thick little chuckle as I clapped him on the back a few times.

“You’re my best mate. And I’m the *saviour of the fucking world,* so… ‘S bound to come with some *perks.”*

He echoed my tight laughter, a bit *wetter* than mine, but gave me one last *squeeze* before releasing me.

“Just to borrow, yeah?”

I nodded, relief in my smile as I exhaled a breath I hadn’t realised I’d been holding.

“Every once in a while, yeah.”

The land was coming together nicely. We’d done away with our garden fence entirely, and already the horse stables, chicken coop and goat house were erected and waiting for occupants. Harry had finally glimpsed our tenant rabbits emerging from their warren, had even planted an array of vegetables for them to snack on at the edge of our garden, but as I watched the contractors walk the field, blueprints in hand, Harry’s mind was *elsewhere.*

Going round and round Ron’s confession of his interest in pursuing *Auror.* And my generous, self-sacrificing husband was worrying himself into a *frenzy* about rubbish like *duty* and *expectations* and… And *responsibility.* I sighed.

*You said it yourself, you needn’t concern yourself with the run-of-the-mill ne’er-do-wells. You’re Harry fucking Potter, and you don’t owe the world one more breath out of your gorgeous body.*

I felt him echo my sigh, resurface through the bath water and push his long hair out of his eyes. *God,* I’d never seen him with hair so *long,* and even as mine could now fit into a ponytail, he was the most stunning thing I’d ever *seen.*

*I know you’re right. Just can’t stop thinking about the reporters and the fucking articles that’ll come out. ‘Here’s Weasley, Where’s Potter?’*

An amused chuckle slipped out of me as I turned to peer up at our bathroom window, squinting against the scattered sun rays bleeding through the clouds.

*They can all eat a dick. Though do feel free to continue fretting.*

Amusement of his own, laced with a bit of *adrenaline* answered this thought as I turned back towards our acres of land.

*Maybe I should bring it up to Morty tomorrow. He keeps encouraging me to start journaling, though I know if I do I won’t ever be able to be fully honest. Anything I write or record will just end up in some bloody museum somewhere.*

I pulled out one of the wrought-iron chairs we’d outfitted our back garden with to settle down into it, one leg folding over the other as my arms folded similarly across my chest.

*Poor little legendary hero. Not easy being the stuff of myth, is it?*

Harry snorted, and finally started to rise from the cooling bathwater. He’d begun to prune, but I’d long since gotten used to his… *Moods.*

*All I want is to give you a good life. I really don’t care what anyone else might expect of me, but I… Wish it didn’t affect me like this.*

My jaw twisted to one side as I considered his feelings. He was steadily drying and redressing himself to return to my side, and it wasn’t like I didn’t *understand* why he felt the way he felt. It’d’ve been so much easier for him to drift into anonymity if Ron had chosen to do the same.

But going for *Auror…* Putting his name down like that assigned a sort of *identity* to his life. No doubt he and Hermione would go down in history as well, but for him to take up the mantle like this, it made perfect sense why Harry now felt like he wasn’t doing *enough.*

And though I *completely* disagreed, it still made sense. At the end of the day, it’d come down to Harry’s own choices, though, and I’d support him no matter what those ended up being. Even if it was for the wrong reasons, and I *greatly* wanted to talk him out of it, if he decided to follow suit and take on *Ministry employ…*

*Just promise you won’t insert me into the equation. I’ll always have your back, no matter what. Don’t take my opinions into account.*

A *booster shot* of love dragged a gasp through my lips, and I released it with an elated little chuckle as I felt Harry descending the stairs to join me.

*You’re my favourite fucking person in the world, did you know that?*

I sniffed, all smug self-satisfaction as his words sank into me.

*I certainly hope so, or you married the wrong bloke.*

Saoirse was a perfect *peach* with Teddy. Patient with his ungentle affection, and gentle enough for them *both* with her paws and playful gnawing. It was so *perfectly disabling* to watch the two together, and he’d learned how to play with her rather quickly because her toys were strewn *everywhere.* The first time he’d picked one up, she’d been keen, and Draco and I were gifted a *delightful* giggle as he realised she’d dart after it whenever it escaped his little grip.

And *Draco…* Draco was the most agonisingly *precious* bit of it all. Watching them with open fascination, playful abandon and soft words, I just fell *more and more* in love with him the more I watched them interact. Though we were only *babysitting,* though he was still *hesitant* about any sort of *modelling* role in a child’s life, he was just as enamoured as I was.

Unfortunately for him, (though I counted it only *fair,* since Saoirse had bonded so inextricably to him) I was Teddy’s favourite. He’d reach for me any time I was in the room, beg to be picked up and carted around and I was only too happy to show him all the little bits and bobs of our home. It’s not like I had much else going on, and I found it oddly nostalgic to be a part of his early childhood. So much of my *own* childhood was lost to me, and I counted it a singular gift to be able to experience it through Teddy.

At the time, I’d only fulfilled my role in the prophecy *because* there was a prophecy. It was the role assigned to me from birth, the only future I’d ever bet on, but now in the aftermath, holding a giggling *baby* in my arms, I realised… It’d all been for *him.* For the next generation, and every generation afterward to be able to grow up in a world lacking a *villain.* I’d protected a person I’d only just *met,* and I’d have done it all over again to see that adorable little smile.

Granted, I hadn’t been able to protect *Lupin.* Not *Tonks,* or *Sirius,* or any of the other family Teddy might’ve had. But just being able to be in his life *like this,* I was giving him a much better chance than I’d ever been offered. Surrounded by love and safety and *protection,* and it *fucking killed me* to get to witness his perfect innocence.

When Andromeda would finally come to claim him, he’d always pitch a little *fit,* and it ripped my heart in two every single fucking time. Never got any easier, and he always seemed slightly *bigger* when we saw him again, but it’s not like we were equipped to handle a *child* living under our roof full-time. Not when we were still getting our bearings on how to take care of *ourselves.*

The seasons grew colder, the forest at the edges of our land changed colour, and time marched on. Draco had taken to wearing little silver rings through his hair, his *braids,* and I only reluctantly allowed him to braid *my own* as we both grew it out. Where he’d worried he’d only look more like Lucius, it gave him a sort of *elven* quality that I was *egregiously* attracted to. So he allowed it, and played with my *own* hair so much more often that I allowed it as well. We were damn-near fucking *hippies* as Autumn approached.

I shifted my chin against his blond tresses as he distantly considered the response we’d received today from the farm looking to rehome a clutch of baby goats.

Being with him, being *his* was as easy as *breathing.* Never a question of who was the more dominant or submissive one, because we were both *both.* We *were* each other, and a gnawing little anxiety crept into the back of my mind, because even if we kept trading off roles like we *always* had, there was still the warnings from the book about soulbonds whispering on the fringes.

I pressed a kiss to the top of his head, felt the way he was drifting closer and closer to sleep, such utter *peace* in my arms. Perhaps it was our role-switching that kept his soul virile and defined? Thankfully, my fretting wasn’t strong enough to keep him awake as he fell asleep against my chest, lungs pulling even and slow as his fingers stilled in mine. Maybe it was the way he *shared* my magic? Pulled it into himself, traded off and equal, he still felt just as uniquely *Draco* as he ever had. But there was a chance the evolution was just too *gradual* for me to notice, and as I danced with the idea of *genuine* concern, I–

Probably *more* careful than we needed, but even just *imagining* how he would’ve responded if he’d felt me worrying like this ripped a short scoff out of me. Fucker was *always* just *pining* for a reason to string me up.

He was my peace. My home. My safety, my anchor, my *gravity.* My whole fucking heart *and soul.* And as sleep finally crept up on me, I released the last vestiges of any *worry* that we were treading dangerous waters, because I was a *god,* and I’d never do *anything* to harm the person I loved.

Draco was waiting for me with a *smile* on a sunny beach as I finally slipped under.
