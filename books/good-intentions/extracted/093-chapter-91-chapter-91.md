## Chapter 91: Chapter 91

Chapter Notes

We missed breakfast, but found our throng of peers drifting to the classroom on time. And they all seemed unfazed, if a bit *suggestive* of our absence at breakfast. Harry and I held hands the whole way.

As we settled in to work, my head went sort of fuzzy at the notion that we’d established a new baseline. Forged a new *normal,* because nobody said shit, nobody asked where we’d been or even marked our absence, and it was like a fucking paradigm shift to realise the path we’d somehow laid out before us. Into the future, to that cosy little cottage with a cat and all at once, I could fucking *believe it.*

I hadn’t been out in the world long enough to *pinch* anything new for Harry into my pockets when the door to our classroom creaked open. Like we’d rehearsed it, the entire class twisted to see none other than the *Headmistress* in the doorway. She cleared her throat, lips pursing disapprovingly as she folded her arms before her.

“Ah, Headmistress,” the Bloody Baron greeted as he drifted towards the back of the room. “What can I do for you?”

My palms went fucking *slick* as I considered the *only logical reason* she’d have for visiting us. I was in trouble.

“I’m afraid I need to steal Mister Malfoy away from you,” she confirmed, eyes snapping onto me as a thick swallow worked its way down my throat. “Can you spare him?”

I would’ve glanced back at Harry, but I already knew what I’d see. And this was only to be expected, my own *mother* had predicted this, and I felt the blood rush out of my cheeks.

“Yes, of course. Er… When can we expect him back?”

McGonagall straightened slightly, adjusted her shoulders beneath the layers of black robes that rippled off of her.

“You’ll know as soon as I.”

I felt the attention of the entire room shift to me as I numbly started collecting my things. *Fuck. Shit.* There was no chance in *hell* this wasn’t a Ministry-authorised decision, but once I’d folded all my effects back into my satchel, I finally cast my attention over at Harry.

And nearly fucking *broke.*

Pure *panic* was screaming in his eyes, but when he met my gaze, he still tried to smile at me. His lips betrayed his dread, though, and the half-smile I quirked back at him likely had the same effect, but then I slung my bag strap over my shoulder and stood.

As I did, my attention was drawn to the other sets of eyes watching me. Hermione, Seamus, Luna, Neville… And it wasn’t until that very moment that I really even *recognised* them as my friends. *My friends.* Another uncomfortably thick swallow forced itself down my throat as I followed McGonagall out.

It was a silent procession out of the classroom, down the halls and through the castle to the Headmistress’s office. And once we ascended the stairs, she finally broke the silence with a *heavy* fucking sigh.

*Shit.*

“Apologies for prying you from your studies,” she began as she rounded her desk to filter through parchment upon it. Glanced up at me over her glasses as I shifted my weight, probably further away from the desk than I should be. “I assume you know why, though.”

I twitched a nervous grin, gripped my satchel strap and braced myself.

“I-I’ve been summoned.”

She nodded gravely, displeasure pinching her features again as she dropped her gaze back to the desk. Plucked a thrice-folded parchment from the surface and glanced back up at me.

“You’ve been doing exceptionally well, Draco, despite a few counts of absence.” *Fuck.* “No fights, no violence of any kind, and I believe that’s what will make all the difference.”

She came up to me, offered me the letter in her hands, and I lifted a trembling appendage to accept it. Just like the first summons, the letters blurred before my eyes as I turned my attention down to them. And McGonagall sighed again.

“Personally, I find it ridiculous that the Ministry should require an update on your progress this soon into the term. Reflects poorly on the school, but what say do I have? I’m just the *Headmistress.”* My eyes snapped back up to hers as she scoffed, surprised to hear her opinion on the topic. But when she met my gaze, her features sobered. “I feel you should know your mother will be there. I received a fairly outraged communication from her several days ago, but she is of a similar mind in that this is all simply *performative.* Do you understand?”

Numb as I was, I managed to nod. Swallow, even speak. Maybe my nerves were wired enough to break through the numbness.

“Sh-Shacklebolt needs to prove himself a f-fitting Minister.”

McGonagall’s imperious stare softened like *approval.*

“Precisely. It’s all paperwork and procedures, Draco. You’ve nothing to fear.”

*Nothing to fear.* I couldn’t help but doubt it, but I folded the letter into my pocket nonetheless. Sniffed, straightened my shoulders and dropped my school bag.

“When do we go?”

She quirked an eyebrow at me.

“Now.”

---

Every eye in the fucking *room* was trained on me as the door clicked shut. And I didn’t know what the fuck to *say,* how to react, what to do. So I just met each set of eyes as hopelessly as I felt.

“Right, well,” the Baron began. “No excuse to fall behind, eh? Carry on.”

I strove to redirect my attention back to my work, but *who was I kidding?* Not my fucking self, because Draco’d mentioned how his mother had supposed he’d be called in for another hearing. And if McGonagall herself was sent to summon him, it could only mean one thing.

*Fuck. I need to find a way to get myself into that hearing. Think, Harry, I’ve got the Cloak, but if I’m gone in the middle of the week, it’ll be sure to raise flags. Perhaps Hermione will cover for me? Say I’ve taken ill? If I can get the whole year to confirm I was sick, maybe I could–*

Every quill in the fucking *room* snapped at the same time, earning a round of groans as all the eyes turned back to me. Some accusing, some sympathetic as I curled into myself.

“S-sorry,” I murmured, lowering my brow to a palm as I took stock of everything I knew.

Draco was being watched by the Ministry. They’d summoned him for a follow-up hearing. He hadn’t done anything *wrong,* if I had any say in the matter. I wondered if they’d take my testimony into account if I *skipped school* to attend. A screeching chair behind me broke my reverie, and I glanced backwards to see Ron crouching towards me with something in his hand. As he thrust it onto my desk, I returned my frown back down to it, and then I understood.

It was today’s release of the Prophet. Loathe as I was to read a single word of their drivel, my eyes honed down on the front-page image. Draco and I, hand in hand in the main strip of Hogsmeade. Me, wincing, and turning towards the camera. Draco leaning into me to speak softly, and me glancing back at him.

*Christ.*
