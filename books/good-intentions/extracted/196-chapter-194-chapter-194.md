## Chapter 194: Chapter 194

Chapter Notes

Adjusting to life with a *cat* was unlike anything I could’ve anticipated. Draco was *besotted* with Saoirse, and as adorable as it was to see them play together, snuggle together while he read, I admit it made me a bit… *Jealous.* But at the same time, she was just as eager to interrupt *my*day-to-day, and she was always a bright spot.

We’d given her free reign of the garden, stern enough with preventing her terrorising the rabbits that she mainly roamed the back garden, but anytime I left to tend to the plants, a habit of leaving the door cracked formed. Leaving *every* door cracked for her, because this was her home, too.

When I’d pictured us having a cat in my imagined future for us, I hadn’t expected her to be such a *large part* of our lives. Even in the scenario I’d shown Draco, the cat was only napping by the fireplace. But Saoirse was prone to all *sorts* of shenanigans, and we were *endlessly* amused. It was… *Really* nice, having a little creature to care for, who demanded attention and would *literally climb us* if we didn’t acquiesce quickly enough. In the end, I fell just as hard as Draco, and it was like we’d adopted a *daughter,* of all things. Granted, the stakes weren’t quite as high, but it just felt so *domestic* and *lovely* to both love and nurture the same animal together. Like we were growing our *family.*

She took to sleeping with us. Curled up between us in bed, or nestled at our feet, but another aspect I hadn’t anticipated were what Draco referred to as the *‘midnight zoomies.’* Saoirse was seemingly *predestined* to wreak havoc in our cottage in the wee hours of the morning. Sleepily grumbled assurances that it was *‘just her kitten phase’* had me easily forgiving her, but *blimey,* what a *handful.*

Before long, I would’ve forgiven *any* infraction of hers. Even when she knocked shit over, broke vases and glasses and spread parchment off the study desk, she’d always scurry off like she’d scared *herself,* and I just *couldn’t* stay mad at her. Not with that little face, those little ears and nose and *meows.* *God,* I was just *weak* for the little thing.

And seeing Draco attach himself to her was a gift within itself. *Feeling* the way he *fawned* over her, doted on her, even if it was for something other than *myself* was just perfectly fucking *debilitating.* Any time I sensed them snuggling somewhere in the cottage, I *had* to find them. Even if just to watch from a distance, though more often to participate and wrap them both up in my arms wherever they were. And it had me all the more eager to expand our *sanctuary* of animals.

Saoirse changed our lives, to both Morty and Fern’s delight. Morty more so for the *therapeutic* benefit of having an animal, but *Fern* was just as in love with her as we were. And Saoirse, I was convinced, would *never* meet a stranger, because the first time they met, she was in Fern’s lap within *minutes.* Laying down and stretching out and *purring* as our handler cooed and gushed over her.

Lately, I’d been feeling like my heart could explode at any *moment* with the sheer *bliss* of the life Draco and I were carving out for ourselves. Music to Fern’s ears, but *Morty* seemed… Unresolved. Sure, he was pleased we were getting on so well, learning to take care of ourselves and *making progress,* but he seemed to have a unique focus on whether we were *distracting* ourselves or not. Honestly, it seemed like he *wanted* us to suffer, but I couldn’t deny it made sense.

We had everything we wanted. A home, a cat, each other, all the time in the *world* to lounge and read and fuck, eat and sleep and be out in the sunshine, but there were *things…* Things neither of us wanted to deal with. Things we avoided, shied away from, ignored. Saoirse proved a fitting distraction, as did everything else that occupied our time, but I tried to remind myself he was *only doing his job* every time he asked how we were coping. We *weren’t,* really. We were doing anything but.

One night, Draco and I sat down to really talk about it. I’d felt the imbalance within him from our session that day, and couldn’t refute I felt similarly, so when I settled in with him in bed that night, we both heaved great, simultaneous sighs as Saoirse happily purred at our feet.

“I *really* don’t wanna do this,” I muttered to anyone listening as I folded my arms over my stomach. And Draco scoffed gently.

“Me neither. It’s like, why dredge up old shit when everything is going so perfectly?”

“Exactly!” I agreed, frowning as I scooched a bit closer to him. Close enough to lay my head on his shoulder as we both watched Saoirse’s fur rise and fall with her purrs.

I screwed my eyes shut, wanting to just *get this over with* as I pulled Draco into Fieridem, and didn’t miss the little gasp he gave as I swallowed him up. This was the easiest way to have this conversation, after all. If we could skip all the fumbling language and just *feel* with each other, surely we’d make more progress, right?

Draco hummed happily, leaning back against me as we flooded into each other.

“This is so nice,” his little lilting soul-voice said. And I hummed my agreement. “Love this. Missed this.”

“Dangerous,” mine replied, and I felt him tense up. “Would do it all the time if there weren’t risks…”

A little disgruntled allowance passed between us, wasn’t sure whose, but in Fieridem, it didn’t *matter.* We both knew the risks, and neither of us could argue that there *wasn’t.*

“Feel our feelings,” Draco’s soul parroted Morty’s recommendation. “The fuck is that even supposed to mean?”

“Think about it?” I guessed. “Let ourselves feel it, go through it, suffer.”

A *strong, bitter* resistance lashed through us, and I groaned. Threw an arm around Draco to pull him closer into me, and he easily let me.

“That’s a professional’s advice? Suffer?”

I snorted. “I know, right?”

When I gave it a bit of thought, though, my soul was *already* in *agony.* As I’d already discovered through Draco’s exploration, *dozens* of little rips and tears with *names* were bleeding out of me. Not to mention the deeper wounds, the *softer* ones that I’d’ve done *anything* to avoid thinking about. And Draco wasn’t much better off.

My arm tightened around him as I took a good, hard look at the state of his soul. Not like it was anything I could describe in *shape* or *colour,* but I perceived nonetheless. *Father* was still the most prominent wound, but around it were various wounds ranging from insignificant to *probably* an issue, names like *Bellatrix* and *Crabbe* and *Noodle.*

“Noodle?” I asked, because I’d never really allowed myself to scope out his wounds like this before. And I felt him huff a laugh against me as an *onslaught* of memories poured into me.

“Tried to rescue a cat once. Father killed it.”

“Jesus.”

A montage of a little mangy white cat played behind my eyelids, and I watched. Young Draco, perhaps seven or eight, finding it behind a bin and bringing it food, water, milk. Building a little shelter for it until it trusted him enough to let him bring it inside, where he hid it in his wardrobe. The inevitability of his father finding out, and the *gruesome* aftermath.

“I think I could kill him from here,” I mused, not even really joking, because what sort of fucking *monster*would–

“Death would be too kind,” he answered, and I conceded. He wasn’t wrong, after all. Fucker *deserved* to live out the rest of his lonely days in a dank cell in the middle of the *fucking–*

I felt a gasp sail into my lungs just as my attention speared outward. And I knew Draco was brought along with me, because he clung to my arm as the landscape raced behind our eyelids. All the way off the coast, to a miserable little island that housed a towering structure, and through a barred window–

Unsure how I was even *doing this,* or *if* I was doing it, my soul went sort of *quiet* to find *Lucius Malfoy* in his cell. Hair matted, beard overgrown, dark circles under his eyes. The image of *piteous* as he sat crumpled in a corner of his cell. He didn’t seem to notice us, but there was this *defeat* in his eyes that nearly had me–

Draco jolted. Fieridem was broken with my concentration as he sat up in bed, and I hauled myself up with him.

“Oh my god,” *oh my god,* he thought. “Oh my… My *god.”*

*Shit, sorry,* I reflexed, unable to discern Draco’s emotions any better than *he*could, but he still–

“No, no,” he half-twisted back to glance at me, eyes *bright* with curiosity as he cracked a half-grin at me. *“Fuck!”*

I wrapped my arms around him as he lowered his eyes down into the heels of his palms, curling over his lap as he huffed another incredulous, sob-like chuckle. Worry if I’d done something wrong was quickly gathering within me, but the fucking *relief*he felt…

Another breathless, thick chuckle escaped him as I pressed a kiss to his shoulder, and it was just then his *incomprehensible* emotions seemed to assemble themselves into something *tangible.*

*Fucker’s getting everything he deserves.*

I smiled. Curled my arms tighter around him and pressed another kiss to his shoulder. Even though it was *hatred,* pure and bright and *burning* in his core, I leaned into it.

*Let him rot.*
