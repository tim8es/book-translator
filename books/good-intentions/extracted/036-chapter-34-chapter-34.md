## Chapter 34: Chapter 34

Thorough contentment nearly had me asleep atop Draco as he held me. I’d really only meant to thank him for sitting with us at lunch, bearing Hermione’s nattering, but the ease I now felt wrapped up in his arms and smeared like a *pastry* forced me to accept that I might’ve, *maybe* been *slightly* more in love with him than I’d thought.

*Fuck.*

Aeons later, he pressed a kiss to the top of my head, and I eased off of him. Could tell he wanted to talk the moment I’d opened the door, but there had been far more pressing matters. I leaned over the side of the bed to fish through my clothes for my wand, intending to clean us up as I heard him shift behind me.

“So,” he began as I triumphantly withdrew my wand and sat back up. He’d propped himself up on his elbows as I cast several cleaning spells over us both. “H-how’d I do today?”

I grinned sleepily, recalling the meal we’d shared with Ron and Hermione.

“Brilliant. I… Realise in hindsight, we didn’t really set it up to look very *organic,* but… I-it made me *so fucking happy*.”

Draco smiled, passing a hand through his hair as I laid back down next to him. We both instinctively curled onto our sides, hands finding each other like *magnets*. Like muscle-memory.

“Love making you happy,” he whispered as he pressed a kiss to my knuckles. “Worried it… It’ll be too *obvious…”*

I nodded, pulling my lip to worry between my teeth. He *did* make me *stupid* fucking happy, and despite Ron and Hermione’s secrecy, it’s not like I could expect everyone else in our year to be as painfully fucking dense as I was. A little petulant resistance thrashed in my chest as I pictured just *ignoring* Draco in class. Censoring my happiness.

“R-really only worried about *Ginny,*” I confessed, studying the way Draco reacted. He hummed on a nod, gaze sliding past me into a middle-distance. “Don’t want people thinking she, like… *Did this* to me.”

A nervous laugh wriggled through my lips as Draco returned a grin to my eyes.

“Are you implying *I*did?”

My laughter at that was a bit more genuine, but the anxiety lingered in the corners of my mind.

“That’s gonna be the name of my autobiography: Draco Malfoy Loved Me Gay.”

He barked a laugh at that, and pulled me into him, nestling my head beneath his chin. A content sigh eased into my hair as I threw an arm over him, a leg over his. *God,* it felt so good to be this *encompassed* in him.

“Prophet’s gonna have a *field day,”* he muttered, earning a groan that only drew more laughter from his lips. It rumbled around me, and I nestled into it.

“So fucking tired of *fixing* things, I wish just nobody *cared.* Wanna Obliviate the *world* of me.”

Draco stroked my back, and said nothing. And it was so fucking *nice* to just be able to *vent* without solutions or reason being offered back, I hadn’t even realised I’d let myself whinge to him because I’d gotten so good at censoring myself. It was just that fucking *easy* to talk to him, and it was with a sudden shock that I realised if we could talk like this *all the time,* I’d… Probably be a better boyfriend to him than I ever *had.*

“What?” he asked, as if he could read my mind. But I noticed I’d stiffened in his arms, and tried to force myself to relax again.

“E-er… Just, it’s… *Really* easy being your boyfriend I guess.”

His hand paused on my back, and I heard him swallow. When he pulled back to take my face and look at me, I was worried I’d said something wrong, because he was just studying me like there was *no way*I’d just said that. And I frowned.

“W-what?”

He shook his head, almost in a daze, and asked: “Is this *real?”*

A grin stole onto my lips, easing apart my frown, and I nodded.

“I bloody *hope* so,” I chuckled. “Would be just my luck I’m actually strapped up in a padded room at St. Mungo’s.”

He echoed my chuckle, still sounding a bit *incredulous,* before pressing the gentlest, *sweetest* kiss to my lips. My heart seized in my fucking chest at the tenderness of it.

“Being your boyfriend is the easiest thing in the *world.”*

The next morning, after being woken up in the most *glorious* way, Harry and I had breakfast together at the fucking *Gryffindor table,* before any of our peers had joined us. I was just as quick to agree to it as he was to justify that now people knew we were on ‘good terms,’ this wasn’t so… Unusual. Honestly, I would do just about *anything* to make him smile.

“Kinda wish he’d play music or something while we worked. The silence in that room is gonna drive me fucking *mad.*”

Harry snorted as I picked apart my blueberry muffin – for *precisely* the reason he’d guessed – and swallowed his bite of sausage he’d been munching on.

“Doubt I’d get much of *anything* done if he did. It’s distracting enough sitting next to *you.*”

I felt a smirk pull at my lips as I offered another little pinch of muffin into my mouth.

“He’d probably reseat us if you asked,” I supposed. He looked *affronted*that I’d even suggested it, and I chuckled at the sheer outrage on his face.

“I would *never,”* he insisted like he didn’t know I was joking. “And besides, you’d still distract me from across the bloody *room,* git*.*”

He took another pointed bite of his sausage, and I chuckled again.

“You’re not foisting your failures onto *me,* Scarhead, not when that witch *friend* of yours knows how to throw a punch.”

It was just enough old-Draco that Harry broke into hysterics, nearly choking on his mouthful as he covered his lips with his hand. The thought *I shouldn’t be this happy* echoed in my head, but seeing Harry laugh like that was just too enrapturing.

His laughter died suddenly, though, as I saw him looking over my shoulder like he’d had the breath knocked out of him. Seeing his cheeks lose colour like that, I followed his gaze to find an equally pale ginger had halted in the mouth of the Great Hall. And I swallowed.

Ginny took a heartbeat to assess the situation, me and Harry sat next to each other, before hastily stalking towards the middle of the Gryffindor table. Far away from us. And Harry sighed.

“I know, I know, it’ll be a *thousand* times worse if she figures it out on her own or hears it from someone other than me, I just…” I eyed him sympathetically as he pushed his breakfast around with his speared sausage link. “I’ll n-never get used to letting people down.”

“You’re welcome to blame me for your homosexuality all you like,” I offered as I took another bite of muffin. And he scoffed, but the dourness of his expression lifted somewhat.

“Only if you *really* piss me off, Ginny can be sort of… *Scary.*” I snorted at the little performative shudder he gave.

“Women, man.” He turned a sparkly-eyed smile up at me.

“Yeah. *Women.*”

We both faked a shudder, which dissolved into laughter as we continued our breakfast.

*God, he’s so easy to love.*
