Harry slept heavy that night. I’d expected as much, just as much as I’d expected my *inability* to sleep. My head was just as heavy as his, but where it’d knocked him out, mine kept me awake.

So much had happened today. From the wedding to the revelation with *Teddy* to *Sirius…* I was still racing to catch up to it all as he slept in my arms, soft breaths tickling my chest.

He’d cried *so much* after letting Sirius go, I was worried he’d be sick. My gut still felt full of *broken glass* even with his evenly rising shoulders beneath my arm. Hadn’t had the time to mentally prepare myself for how it’d *destroy* him.

I’d been decimated all the same. I laid in *tatters* beneath our quilts as I listened to his heartbeat. Some measure of comfort, of *ease* as he slept like a log against me.

Knowing how *Lucius* had hated him (bastard didn’t deserve the title father anymore) only made me respect him more. Anything that shitstain deemed *unworthy* was instantly at the top of my list of people to admire. And though I’d never met the man in life, meeting him in *death* was…

I had too many thoughts. Too many feelings, and I needed to *sleep.* I felt *dense* with it, but couldn’t quite shut my mind down like Harry had. How I envied his rest.

At the end of the day, though, after all that’d *changed* today, my name, my notion of what a *father* could be, my opinion about *Sirius,* I realised… Everything I used to know was dead. Gone, divorced from myself. I felt like I could finally *breathe* without the Dark Mark on my arm, and as sleep finally started to beckon me, I breathed an easy sigh because I couldn’t help feeling like…

The story of our lives, the war, who we’d been forced to *be* had come to an end. A happy epilogue, and before us stretched an *infinite* eternity. A prologue to a *new* story.

And I eagerly turned the page.
