## Chapter 163: Chapter 163

A bittersweet nostalgia swelled in me when I finally spied the lights of the castle in the evening light. The sky had just faded from fiery sunset to rosy dusk moments before, and knowing it might be the last time I came here for who knew *how* long had me staring out the window again, even as Draco laid his head in my lap on the edge of sleep. Thestral-drawn carriage rides were a bit more on the *exciting* side than the *peaceful* side, but I couldn’t help feeling like I’d just begun the last chapter of a story.

This whole *life* I’d lived, the story of the first eighteen years of my life felt like it was drawing to a close. I hadn’t known what to expect after the war ended, hadn’t really let myself set my sights *ahead* until Draco had come into my life. Well… He’d *been* in my life, but he hadn’t been a part of it. And as I peered down at the drifting blond, a sort of *wonder* bloomed to replace the nostalgia.

After all I’d lost, all I’d been through and *survived,* to have been granted a prize as precious as *him* was just… So much better than I’d expected. He gave me something to look forward to. He *wept for me* when I was sad. Taught me how to *use* my power instead of it using me. What *love* was. And even if I *never* returned to Hogwarts after the NEWTs, even if my friends and I saw each other less as they began careers and families of their own, I already *had* mine. My own family, a *partner* unlike anything I’d ever had before. Someone who saw me at my worst and still *loved me.*

I delicately threaded my fingers through his hair, back off his brow to appreciate his peaceful face. I’d never been afraid of dying. And with Draco by my side, in my head and heart and very *soul,* I… I wasn’t afraid of *living* anymore, either. Didn’t fear a vast, empty future with no purpose or direction because *he* was North now. A little hint of a smile warmed the corner of his lips.

*Can you love me a little quieter? I’m trying to sleep.*

A soft chuckle huffed through my lips as he squinted up at me.

*We’re nearly there anyway.*

Draco took the opportunity to stretch what little his slender length *could* in the cabin before he hoisted himself up off my lap. And though I felt a little colder for his loss, he just sent me a smirk and reached around me to draw the curtains away from my window, putting him decidedly in my space as he peered out into the evening.

*You nervous at all?* he asked, and I felt him sort of rifling through my head to assess my state, but I wasn’t. Excited to see my friends again and break the news, maybe, but as I turned a smile up at him, all I felt was *happy.*

*Me? Nervous? Never.*

His lips curved into a smile just before he leaned in and pressed them to mine, and I let him. Our kiss was brief, though, as the carriage suddenly began its descent, *lurching* my stomach with the abrupt drop as we swooped down towards the castle.

Most everyone else had taken the train, so we didn’t see many people on our trek into the Great Hall. Seemed like we were a bit early, too, as we left our trunks to be taken up to our rooms. It was so strange to walk through these halls, knowing it might be the last time. Drifting past familiar portraits, ghosts, statues and suits of armour. And I sort of played with the idea of hiding the Marauder’s Map somewhere in the castle for the next generation to find. Not like it’d do me much good once I’d left.

*God, the next generation,* I mused as we finally strolled through the doors to the Great Hall, hand in hand. *The world continues to turn.*

The hand in mine squeezed gently, and I glanced up at Draco.

*Thanks to you,* he implied, and I smirked.

After *weeks* of *exquisite* French cuisine, the first dinner back at Hogwarts was… Disappointing. Delicious, to be sure, but such *simple* dishes that I realised I’d developed a taste for the *complex.* An image flashed across Draco’s mind as we loaded up our plates in comfortable silence, and I sort of froze to attend it.

He and I in our little cottage kitchen, wearing *aprons* and coated in a fine dusting of baking powder. Crowded around the counters and reading from a French cookbook as we learned how to recreate the dishes I loved *together.* Giggling and getting each other messy and being absolute *bollocks* at it, and a pleased flush rose to my cheeks as it flickered dark.

“You wanna learn how to *cook* with me?” I asked incredulously, and he grinned over at me.

“I wanna do *everything* with you,” was his answer. And my throat went tight at the sheer *possibilities. Everything together. Forever.* Draco dropped a tong-full of roasted asparagus onto his plate. “Sky’s the limit, Mr. Potter.”

Blushing, I resumed assembling my own plate as my mind latched onto this idea and *danced* with it. Learning new hobbies together, maybe I’d paint for him again since he liked my first one so much? Making *horrible* art for him, because he loved art and he loved *me,* and cringing when he wanted to put it on display in our house. Filling the rooms with so much love it was *impossible* for guests to have a bad time when they visited. Entertaining our friends, our *family,* getting pets together and finding *new ways* to love every day.

I couldn’t create sentences to put into words as we ate, and was only distantly aware of the room filling with more and more students until my reverie was shattered by two familiar people dropping onto the bench opposite us.

“Happy New Year, you two!” Hermione met us as she and Ron settled into the Gryffindor table. And I hastily swallowed my mouthful, pure *glee* for seeing them nearly having me lunging *across* to take them in my arms.

“Happy New Year!” I returned once I’d choked it down. “How was Christmas?”

Ron grinned at me, pure *Weasley* as he angled up to collect his own dinner.

“You were definitely missed, but I *wish* you could’ve seen the look on mum and dad’s faces when I told them where you were.”

I felt myself blanch slightly, recalling Lucius and Arthur’s antagonistic history. Bloke had *cursed Ginny,* after all.

“O-oh,” was all I could come up with, paired with an uncomfortable cough. And Draco snorted beside me.

“It was *fine,* honestly,” Hermione interjected as she loaded her own plate similarly. Something about her seemed… *Different,* but I couldn’t put my finger on *what.* “Ginny was a peach about it all, even defended you, bless her. Oh, remind me later to give you all your gifts. Even your *absence* doesn’t seem to deter Molly from that tendency…”

A chuckle coughed through my lips as Ron *dove* into his meal. Ravenous as always as I took another hesitant bite of peas. *Ginny defended me?*

It was at this moment Luna pranced up to the table, and took her usual seat on my other side.

“Hiya Harry,” she said brightly as she adjusted her robes around the bench. And I pulled her into an impromptu sideways hug, which she easily reciprocated.

“Hey, Luna. Happy New Year.”

“Last year of the nineteen hundreds! Quite exciting, don’t you think?” as we released each other, and I chuckled again.

“Sure.”

She leaned forward against the table, peering around me.

“Hiya Draco,” she echoed, and he paused. Swallowed and sent her a smile.

“Hi, Luna. Have a nice holiday?”

She nodded enthusiastically, blond curls bouncing around her.

“Very nice. My father and I went to Romania to search their caves for undiscovered magical creatures! Those caves are rather treacherous, so they haven’t been fully explored yet, but we found…”

Luna’s voice faded into the background as Neville joined us. Asked her to recount her story where she’d begun, and I was just *awash* in the pleasure of being with my *friends* again. It was the first family I’d ever *made,* and while it hadn’t been bound in blood or *writing,* it was still just positively *lovely* to be with them all again. *Ginny* even joined, sending me a subtle smile as she inserted herself into the conversation, and I was so totally *grateful* for them all that I just let myself be overwhelmed with it.

*Love you,* sounded quietly in my mind, and I glanced over at Draco. He was half-listening to Ron and Ginny argue back and forth about how some event had occurred at the Burrow, but spared a moment to shoot me a little *smile.* And as I returned it, I realised we’d yet to make a *single announcement.* Nobody had yet commented on our matching rings, *or* the fact that they were on our left hands and not our right. I was gearing up to redirect the conversation to make said announcement when who should amble up to the table but *Seamus and Dean.*

Hand-in-hand. Flushed as *sin,* and the table went quiet. Draco and I shared an implicative glance as Dean cleared his throat and shifted his weight.

“H-hey,” he began. Sent us a glance, but only straightened taller as he spoke for them both. “W-we, er… Have an announcement to make.”

As if it wasn’t already *plainly obvious,* several of us shifted to face him more directly, Seamus *beet-red* and slightly hiding behind his shoulder, and I saw Hermione stiffen in her seat across from me. *Here we go.*

“We’re d-*dating,”* he exhaled in a rush before any of us had the chance to ask. The table stilled for a breath, but what inevitably broke the silence was *Ron,* heaving a *defeated* fucking sigh as Hermione latched onto his bicep and *rocked him.*

*“See?? See??* Oh my fucking *god,* have I *ever once* been *wrong??”* She dissolved into peals of laughter as her fiancé delved into his pocket to withdraw galleons, and I couldn’t help but snort.

“Congratulations!” Luna adulated beside me, and Neville unsuccessfully choked back laughter of his *own.* “You two look very good together.”

Dean laughed nervously, and I saw him cast a hesitant glance at *Ginny,* who I turned towards to read her reaction similarly. She was *Weasley red* but only shock sang in her wide, chestnut eyes as they danced from Dean to *me,* to Ron, to every other person at the table before she curled in on herself.

“Fuckin’ *finally,”* Draco breathed, and I turned back to watch him fork another asparagus into his mouth.

*Real classy,* I teased, and he sent me a quirked eyebrow as he chewed.

*Won’t be the biggest announcement at this table, you know.*

And I swallowed.
