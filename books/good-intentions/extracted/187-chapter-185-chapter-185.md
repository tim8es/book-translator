## Chapter 185: Chapter 185

Chapter Notes

After somewhat settling in, Harry decided to take a turn about the garden. I let him go in favour of perusing the kitchen, the pantry, so I could begin to compile a list of things we needed to buy to stock our house. *Our house.*

Winter’s chill lingered in the air, and I felt Harry shiver all the way from the guest room. He wasn’t of a mind to come back inside just yet, had bent to admire a little rabbit warren beneath a bush at the edge of the fence when I yielded a sigh and pulled the comforter off the top of the guest bed. I’d allow him to be quiet and moody as long as he wanted, but like hell was I letting my husband freeze to death.

He didn’t turn when I stepped out onto the stoop, blanket folded up in my arms. The sunshine was barely starting to fade over the Western horizon as I shut the door behind me and made my way to him along the little stone pathway that led to the front gate. It was gorgeous land, to be sure, and the garden had been kept just wild enough to evoke a certain sense of whimsy, but for Harry, it was the fresh air, the wide sky above him that he’d sought out. A new kind of freedom he was dipping his toes into as I came up behind him to wrap him up in the comforter, and my arms.

A content sigh eased out of him as I rested my jaw against his temple, and he leaned back into me. Lifted a hand to hold one of my banded arms around him, and I couldn’t help but smile. What a corny display we made, if there were anyone around to observe us. The isolation, however, was *exactly* what we both needed.

“It’s not bloody fair that I’m more comfortable in dire situations. Every bone in my body is screaming at me to relax, to enjoy the safety, but I just know I’ll sleep fitfully for the first few… Well, I don’t even know how long. I was just starting to accept that I could trust *us,* that *this* wasn’t going anywhere anytime soon, and now…”

I pressed a kiss to his hair, and wrapped him up tighter in my arms as he sighed slightly less contently.

“Perhaps your handler could refer you to a Healer. Maybe they could refer me, too, actually.” Harry groaned, and I grinned.

“God, I’d *forgotten* we’d made that deal with Kingsley. Do you *really* think I need a babysitter?”

Resisting rolling my eyes, I echoed his sigh.

“I *think…* That a show of good faith couldn’t hurt after he pulled those strings to let you off the hook. You know I’ve always got your back, but… I’m not exactly the most well-adjusted person, either, or hadn’t you noticed?”

He chuckled, nuzzled the side of his head against my cheek. The sky was finally darkening to a pleasant violet overhead as our breath started fogging in the chill air.

“Maybe seeing a Healer couldn’t hurt either,” he assented quietly. I was just considering how we’d both been able to access wounded corners of each others’ souls, how we’d tried pouring *love* into them and how it’d helped… A bit, when a soft *hoot* reached our ears, and we twisted our heads in unison to see none other than Ulysses swooping through the sunset towards us.

*This ought to be good,* I thought wryly as he came to perch on a branch of the tree just to our left. Dropped the letter he bore on the frosted grass and gave us a hair-raising *screech* before alighting back into the sky.

“Bloody hell,” I muttered under my breath as I disentangled myself from around Harry to go retrieve it from the ground.

It was from mother, of course, and Harry drifted up to my side as I straightened to open it.

*‘Hello my loves,’* it began.

*‘Congratulations on your new home! I hope all goes well getting settled in. I was curious to know when would be your earliest convenience for me to visit? The young miss Granger (soon to be Weasley) reached out to me recently with questions for a joint ceremony, and assured me this is something you two consented to, but I’d like to discuss it in person if it suits you.*

*I’m back in the Manor now, so if there’s anything of yours you’d like me to bring, Draco, let me know before I come see you. And before you get your knickers in a twist, I won’t be staying long. I’m sure you two need time alone with each other in the new place.*

*I love you both very much, and I already have some ideas for a venue, colour stories, etc. Eager to hear back from you, and to see the new house!*

*All my love,*

*Mum’*

*Bloody woman doesn’t waste any time,* I mused internally as the letter began to shake in my shivering hands. Harry shared his warmth with me, wrapping half the comforter around my shoulders and winding his arms around my waist. I lifted one of mine to lay over his shoulders as I drew it around me with the other, and felt Harry look up at me, so I peered back down at him.

“Maybe like, a week?” he suggested, gnawing on his lower lip, and I smiled.

“Sure. Sounds perfect.”

---

A week, it turned out, was a fucking *eternity.* Draco penned his mum back, we explored the nearest town and stocked our kitchen and pantry, but I was slowly losing my bloody *mind.* The restless nights bled into exhausting days and I hardly had the mental faculties to even *enjoy* the novelty of our beautiful cottage, but something did occur in that week to break the wretched monotony. The Ministry finally sent me my… My *handler.*

Three days after Draco responded to his mother, a woman came knocking on our gate. Warded as our property was, that was all it took to rouse me from my sleepless stupor on the couch when the gentle rap of her knuckles echoed through the house. The most patient, caring, *loving* man in the *world*found me approaching the door as he pocketed his wand, curious as I was, and we answered together.

“Good morning,” the slight redhead greeted us brightly once we’d opened the gate for her. Quite short, but clearly fairly *bubbly,* or maybe just nervous to meet us as Draco and I exchanged a glance. “I’m Fern Lovell, and I represent the Department of Mysteries. I’m here to chat with you, Mr. Potter. Get your written consent to my monitoring and, well… Start off on the right foot.”

She extended a manicured hand, which I hesitantly accepted.

“Pleasure, er… Department of *Mysteries?”*

“Indeed!” she nearly shouted as she released my hand. *Definitely nervous.* “You yourself are quite the mystery, if I say so myself. But to be honest, the Ministry really doesn’t *have* a specific division for your, er… *Particular* situation.”

Her throat bobbed with a swallow, and something about her blue eyes twitched like a flinch, as if she realised she might’ve *offended* me. And instantly, I was at ease. A chuckle escaped me as Draco laid a reassuring hand against my lower back gently.

“That’s… Fair, I suppose. Won’t you c-come in?”

“Please,” she grinned widely.

Draco and I showed her to our living room, pressed a kiss to my temple and left to make us some tea. Well, tea for them, coffee for myself as Fern and I sat opposite each other with the short ottoman between us. She smoothed her skirt beneath her before she sat, and crossed her knees, eyeing me up like she wasn’t sure what to expect. Like I might *explode* at any moment, and I bit back a little thrum of irritation as she laced her fingers together over her knee.

“So!” She cleared her throat. “How-how’ve you been adjusting? I mean, after leaving Hogwarts and… Y’know, *married life* and all that.”

My cheeks puffed out with a sigh as I started to nod, but realised if I was to actually *gain* anything from her company, I owed it to myself to just be *honest.*

“Not… Not great, actually, erm… Draco’s been wonderful, of course, he’s the best part of my *life,* but…” I studied Fern as I felt Draco’s chest tighten at my confession, wondering how much of her curiosity was professional and how much was… *Personal.* “I-I s’pose I’ve had a bit of a hard time, er… M-moving on…?”

Fern’s open expression tightened in contemplation as she nodded.

“It’s to be expected, truly. Everything you’re feeling and going through is only *natural,* I mean, bloody *he–”* Her words died in her throat as she censored herself, cheeks suddenly going unnaturally pale and eyes wide, and I had to laugh.

*“Please,* I can’t stress this enough, I would prefer you just speak to me like a normal bloke.”

An awkward chuckle coughed out of her as she straightened and tugged on the bottom of her vest.

“I’ll… I’ll do my best, Mr. Potter, while… While still representing the Ministry.”

“Harry, please.” Fern’s surprise eased into a small smile as some colour returned to her face, and I saw her shoulders visibly relax as she cut another nervous laugh.

“Of course. Harry.” I smiled. “So you’re… Struggling to adjust?” My smile fell slightly, but Fern blazed on. “Any recent, er… *Outbursts?”*

Of course, this was what she was here to assess. Was I a danger to myself or others?

“Nothing too alarming. I… Shattered a mug yesterday, but no one was hurt.” Fern produced a small notebook and a quill from her vest and scribbled something down with a hum, and I sent a glance towards the kitchen. And then scooted forward to the edge of my seat, elbows on knees. “Actually… I dunno what your *role* is, really, but…” Her bright blue eyes lifted back to me curiously as I paused to worry my lip. “I– *We* were actually thinking it might benefit us both to perhaps… Well, we were thinking we might see a *H-H**ealer.* To help with the, y’know… *Moving on.”*

My hands wrung together as my request sank into Fern.

“Of course!” she nearly shouted again. “Of course,” she repeated slightly quieter as she set her quill and notebook down beside her on the couch. “I was thinking much the same thing, and I actually have…” She delved into one of the pockets of her vest and produced a business card, of all things, and extended it to me between two fingers. “I can look into other options, but this is the Ministry-selected Healer I’m obliged to recommend.”

Hesitantly, I reached forward to accept the card. One Mortimus Worten, complete with an address, business hours, and qualifications. It was just then Draco returned with a tray of mugs and accoutrements, and set it down on the ottoman to sit beside me. Leaned in to study the card with me.

“Obviously, it’s *completely* your decision who you choose to consult with, but perhaps I can arrange a meeting? To, like… Feel things out?”

Draco and I exchanged a glance, and the mental equivalent of a *shrug* passed between us before we turned our attention back to Fern.

“That sounds great. I… Really appreciate your candour, Ms. Lovell.” She waved a hand before her face with a downward smile.

“Please, call me Fern. If I’m to call you Harry, it seems silly to keep addressing me so formally.”

*I like her,* Draco noted, and I chuckled. Set the card down on the tea tray to trade it for my mug of coffee.

“Alright, Fern. What else can I do for you?”
