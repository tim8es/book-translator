## Chapter 152: Chapter 152

Chapter Notes

Watching how gracefully all the dancers twirled with their partners across the floor, a little flutter of anxiety tightened my stomach, and Draco wrapped an arm around my waist. We’d discarded our empty plates, chatted through the first dance and I *wanted* to dance with him, to let my husband spin me across the dancefloor wearing this *ridiculous,* sparkly blazer (which didn’t seem so out of place amongst the other attendees) but… The second song began before I could steel myself.

*Fret much harder and I’ll have to find a dark alcove,* he teased me, and I frowned up at him.

*It’s different with people watching. Reckon I’d’ve had my ears talked off by now if not for you.*

He scoffed back down at me, eyebrows raised incredulously.

*Are you saying my presence makes you unapproachable?*

My frown split into a wide, self-satisfied grin as I nodded.

*Yep. You’re my bodyguard.*

He mirrored my smile, and pulled me in to press a kiss to my temple.

*Like you ever need protecting.*

Just then, a certain sort of *dread* swelled in Draco, and I frowned up at him. He was staring past me, though, eyes going wide and… Sort of *pale* as I turned to follow his line of sight. Through the hedging crowd of non-dancers, Narcissa was spearing straight at us with a familiar face in tow…

*Oh my god,* flashed across my mind as I pushed off the pillar we were both leaned against. She looked positively *mischievous* as she carted none other than *Andromeda fucking Tonks* with her, all decked out in violets and golds and glittering jewels, and I felt my throat tighten.

*This was the ‘friend’ she disappeared to find,* Draco realised, but I was just *gutted* to see Tonks’s mum. After our brief introduction, to know she’d *lost her daughter* in the war, and having heard how fondly *Sirius* thought of her…

Narcissa led her right up to us, still so *strikingly* similar to Bellatrix but… Like, the *softer* version, we just beheld each other in silence for a moment. A peripheral perception of Narcissa glancing back and forth between us registered, either in my mind or Draco’s, as Andromeda and I stared at each other. And then she spoke.

*“This* is your new son-in-law?” Incredulous as *hell,* and I felt like I might *throw up* as she turned those wide, dark eyes back over to her sister.

*“I know,”* she extolled, pure *brimming* with pride as she eyed us, and I felt Draco’s mind *whirling* to adjust to the situation. The music played on, the dancefloor alive in the corner of my eye, but Andromeda returned her attention to me, and I just fucking *broke.*

She swept right up to me, smelling slightly of that *milliner’s shop* Narcissa liked so much, and *yanked* me into a tight hug. *Sirius’s cousin. His family.* My *family now.* Hesitantly, I wrapped my arms back around her. *Tightened* them to consider all the loss we shared, all the grief and the *trauma,* the same faces likely burned into the backs of our eyelids everytime we tried to *forget.*

*“So good* to see you, Harry,” she uttered over my shoulder, and it was like a brand new *adoption.* *Another one,* after the Weasleys and *Draco,* and I hacked a quiet sob.

“Y-you too, Andromeda,” pushed my *limits* of verbal communication, and I felt Draco lean into my soul with his. Propping it up, almost, just a silent presence to *support.* Even though his own mind was going *haywire.*

*Mother’s never mentioned my aunt. No idea what their relationship is, but must be decent if they’re on speaking terms like this.*

*Probably because your mother’s considered a blood traitor now* made sense to me, and I felt Draco’s curious agreement as his aunt finally released me from her grip.

“You’re… You’re doing well, are you?”

A smile crept across my face as the music finally cadenced. And I nodded.

*“Very* well. I… Can’t tell you how *glad* I am to see you, but…” The back of my neck prickled at Draco’s nearness as I pulled him into me, even though he was *reluctant* to draw Andromeda’s attention. His hand came to rest on the small of my back as her sincere eyes flicked up to him once. “If… If it’s *alright* with you, I… I owe my husband a *dance."*

The crowd of dancers gave the musicians a round of applause, and she *smiled* at me.

“Of course. I’m… I’m so happy for you, Harry.” She sniffed, and it was *so strange* to see Bellatrix’s twin blinking *tears* out of her eyes as they swept back up to Draco. Who stiffened behind me. “So happy for you *both.”*

I twisted back just in time to see Draco clear his throat, swallow, and stiffen.

“Th-thanks.”

---

Harry was a *maelstrom* of emotion as I pulled him towards the dance queue. Hearing how the new song had started, I was *immensely* grateful that it was a waltz, because that was all I’d even *taught* Harry, and as the pairs of dancers drifted onto the floor before us, I caught his eye.

*Ready?*

He swallowed. Offered me a shaky smile, and then glanced around. Likely to seek out curious onlookers, but I pulled his attention back to me with a *jerk* of the soulbond. And he snapped wide, hesitant green up to me.

*Hey. Tonight is a celebration. An excuse to have fun, to dust off all the gowns and finery we can’t otherwise justify and just enjoy ourselves. Don’t think about anything but me.*

*Easier said than done,* was his reflexive reply as the queue advanced, and we stepped forward. This… *Might’ve* been a more difficult circumstance for him to contend with than just practising in the ballroom, so we took another step forward and then I took his face in my hands, forcing his attention up at me.

*Listen to me, Harry. You’re mine to do with what I want. And I want to dance with you. So you’re going to fold like the bitch you are and let me twirl you around for everyone to see. Everyone is watching us, and I know you love being watched. Let me show you off. Submit.*

Praise and encouragement only made his palms sweat, so I *knew* I had to be firm with him. To *command,* and his thick lashes fanned up at me just like I wanted as he slipped back into that well-known pocket of his soul where he *softened.*

*Yes,* he answered, and I pulled him along with me as the queue shortened. *Anything for you, daddy.*

Like *honey,* his words eased the descent into *daddy* and I assumed the position. Took his hand in one, his shoulder in another, and *grasped* his attention to hold it onto me as we stepped up to lead. The triple rhythm sank into my bones, and I spied our spot in the corner of my eye as I locked into his soul like a *vice.*

*Follow me anywhere,* and we twirled out into the chaos.
