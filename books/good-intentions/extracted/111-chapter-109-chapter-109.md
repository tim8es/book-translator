## Chapter 109: Chapter 109

Chapter Notes

Days passed, the first snow fell, and Christmas drew nearer. I was sat across from Draco one evening in the kitchens, because we’d both been unable to fall asleep and a midnight snack seemed to suit us both. I’d been agonising what gift (or gifts) to get him, since he’d lived a life of luxury and seemed to want for *nothing,* when he sleepily delved into his pyjama trouser pockets and slid across the very answer I was looking for.

It was a bit of green ribbon, frayed nearly all the way down and braided. *The colour of my eyes.* The colour of *Slytherin.* I replaced one hand from my mug of hot cocoa to accept it, and he leaned his chin into a hand as he watched me.

Alright, so in itself, it wasn’t really *the answer.* But all at once, inspiration hit me. What did Draco love?

One: me (obviously). Two: marine life. Three: *art.* I’d never forgotten the expression on his face when I’d taken him to the museum. It was the same face he’d made when he would explain something about magical sea creatures. Open, bright, and genuine. It made me smile to recall as these three aspects braided themselves together much like the little ribbon I now pocketed.

Course, there was also *potions,* but I couldn’t hope to match his level of skill enough to present him with a *potion.* Not for Christmas, not when he could undeniably do a much better job. What potion would I even *concoct?* No, I knew *exactly* what to give him. He seemed to pick up on some subtle body language about me as I sipped my hot cocoa again, because he frowned.

“What?” I shook my head, unable to stop *grinning* as I came up with my plan.

“Just love you a lot.” And it was true. He echoed my smile, reached his free hand across the table, and I took it. Laced our fingers together and set my mug down.

“Love you, too.”

*This’ll be tricky to pull off.*

---

I’d asked Harry what he wanted for Christmas *loads* of times, and if he wasn’t giving me some ridiculous, made-up answer in hopes for a laugh, he was evading or flat-out denying he had *any clue* what he wanted. Which only made me *more* anxious.

Bloody hell, I’d never even *tried* to select a *thoughtful gift.* Of course, I loved Harry more than words could say, but if this was to be another instance where *showing* could suffice for *telling… Christ, what could ever amount to enough?*

I wanted it to be *perfect,* but didn’t think he’d know what to do with luxury brands, glittering jewels. He accepted anything I found in my *pockets* with the brightest smiles, even little nondescript *rocks.* Really didn’t make it any easier on me, but one Saturday a mere *week* before the holidays, who should come to my rescue but one Ron bloody Weasley.

“Harry, can I steal your sentient, sarcastic *mouth* for the afternoon?” Our lips broke away as we both glanced at the redhead, and I quirked an eyebrow.

“You… Wanna hang out with *Draco?”* he asked incredulously, and I was about to elbow him in the ribs for it, but couldn’t deny I was equally sceptical.

Ron stiffened in his chair as even *Hermione* sent him a quizzical frown.

“W-well, *yeah.* We… Th-there’s some… *Stuff* we need to talk about.”

He sent me this *stern* fucking stare, and it was then I realised what he was getting at.

*Oh thank god.*

“R-right. Yeah, we… You two keep each other company, yeah?”

I disentangled myself from Harry, and Ron gave me an approving jerk of his chin as we made to depart, but just before I left his side, I sent Harry one last glance. He looked so *perplexed* and *sad* to see me go, I almost couldn’t. So I dropped one last, *bruising* parting kiss before hastening away from the table. Before I let myself be drawn into his *powerful* fucking magnetism.

“Hogsmeade, yeah?” I asked as I caught up with Ron, and he huffed a chuckle as he wrapped his scarf tighter around his neck.

“You have somewhere else in mind?”

---

I sort of… *Deflated*, watching the back of Draco’s head as he and Ron disappeared through the Great Hall’s twin doors. But quickly realised this was the *perfect opportunity.*

“Hopeless, both of them,” Hermione chuckled as she returned to her meal. I caught her admiring her ring instead of *using the spoon* in her hand, but I couldn't waste a single *moment* if I wanted this to be perfect.

“S-sorry, Mione, I… I’ve got to…” I started to stand, and she swept her brown eyes up to watch me, full of confusion. “Sorry.”

I raced away from the Gryffindor table to her parting *“Bye??”* and straight to the arts wing.

I’d never been, since these were *elective* courses and I’d never… Well, *elected,* but tugged the map out of my bag as I went just in case they’d decided to relocate themselves within the castle. I knew the general direction, though, and marched confidently.

*This is going to be absolute shite, and Draco’s gonna lose his* mind. I grinned as I followed spiral staircases and narrow hallways down to where the *visual studio* waited. The door wasn’t locked, thank god, and I sent a silent apology to whoever stocked the supplies as I unloaded my bag and map onto one of the large tables.

*Parchment.* Easy enough to find, but as I selected a leaf from the stack on a shelf, the quality rubbed my fingers *wrong.* So I replaced it and span where I stood, looking for sturdier material.

*Canvas.* Lunging across the room, I selected a decently small canvass from the rows leaned up against the opposite wall, and set it on another table. *Paint.*

Draco’s favourite sea creature. It was a fairly obscure one, one he’d only ever mentioned in passing, but I knew enough to infer it was his favourite. From how *often* he mentioned it to the light that would flicker in his eyes, the way he’d suck down his enthusiasm anytime it came up, it *had* to be his favourite.

There were bins and *bins* of paints, varieties of colours and types and I was *supremely* out of my depth, but I grabbed a handful of blues. A fistful of greens, a white, some purple. Didn’t know what the *hell* I was doing, but I was driven by *love,* so I was confident whatever horrendous result I yielded, Draco would *love it.*

I brought my armful of paints over to the table I’d set my canvas on, and was just about to seek out *brushes,* when who should stroll through the unlocked door but our *fantastic* guest.

“Oh. Hi, Harry.”

*Thank fucking god.*

---

Ron and I made an unusual pair, to be sure, but he seemed just as anxious about what to get Hermione as I was for *Harry,* so I took a bit of comfort for not being *alone* in my fretting. *I wonder if Harry is fretting, too.*

*God, I hope he’s*fretting.

“So… Er… Mione doesn’t really like *impractical* things, or so she’ll tell you, but she’s also so *easily fascinated* by little magical trinkets.” We strolled up the main road between shops, cold air ruddying our cheeks as we *‘shopped.’* “It’s these *raised-by-muggles* type that always finds such inane shit *interesting,* y’know?”

*Raised by muggles. That’s right, there’s a whole load of things Harry’s never experienced.* Ron gave me an idea, and I swerved sideways towards Zonko's Joke Shop.

“Oi! Doubt you’ll find anything worth buying *there,”* he groused, but followed me anyway.

The shop, like the street outside, was *bustling.* Felt strange to be here without Harry, to be *anywhere* without Harry, but I made myself study the shelves as I pressed into the throng. Plenty of people still leapt out of my way, *fear* shining in their eyes, but I hardly noticed. *What would Harry like?*

Ron clapped me on the back, and might’ve said something about finding me in some amount of time, but I was deep in thought. *Course, I want him to* like *whatever I give him, but it also has to* mean *something. Has to* show *rather than* tell. *Fuck me.*

I scoured every *inch* of that store, but nothing really stood out to me. Nothing screamed *Harry* at me, and it was then I realised I was going about this all wrong. *What reminds me of Harry?*

Green. Red. Warmth. Quidditch. I flipped through all my associations with him, but every option fell flat. I wasn’t going to get him a gift because of its *colour,* no Quidditch gear he could buy for himself. Couldn’t show out with jewellery or finery of any kind, because it’d mean *nothing* to him. By the time I found myself back at the front of the store, though, Ron caught up to me and we headed back out into the cold afternoon.

*What reminds me of Harry? What would Harry like?* Ron was nattering on about Hermione, but I was *still* going about this *all wrong. I love Harry. I want to show him how much I love him.*

*What would make him feel loved?*

We stopped outside an antique shop. I followed Ron in mechanically, considering all the ways I’d thought to *love* Harry by now. Fucking him *senseless,* penguining him, learning his mannerisms and quirks. Being with him every hour of the fucking *day,* indulging all his kinks and preferences. These still didn’t feel like *enough.*

My eyes landed on a case of jewellery, and while I’d already written the option off, a single, simple gold band caught my attention. A *wedding band.* My centre of gravity shifted, and I swayed where I stood.

*That’d be too soon, for sure. God, I’d never survive if I proposed and he* rejected *me.*

My entire *world* refined to a point as the possibility played out behind my eyes. *Making his coffee every morning, finding a job at an apothecary and coming home smelling like rosemary, wearing each others’ clothes, sharing a life together…*

I leaned heavily against the glass case, glove *squeaking* as I gripped a fist and tried to settle myself. He’d already *planned* on forever with me. I’d seen it myself, in his head. And as the yellow glint of metal swam before my dizzy eyes, Ron’s proposal to Hermione echoed in my head.

*In the interest of not wasting any more time…*

A hand clapped onto my shoulder, startling me back into my body as I remembered where I was.

“See something you like?” he asked, and I glanced over at him. Blue eyes were scanning the case like he might find something for *his fiancée,* but I only pushed off the glass.

“N-no, just… Got *dizzy* for a moment. Let’s go.”

*I want that,* he’d said.

*Forever.*
