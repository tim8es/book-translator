## Chapter 130: Chapter 130

Chapter Notes

Draco gave me a more comprehensive tour of the villa, and then it was lunch time. Courses upon *courses* of the most *delicious* food I’d ever *tasted.* We napped in the afternoon, and I tried on all my new clothes for him once we’d woken up. Dinner, bed, and sleep.

The next day, we returned to the… *Place* *Cachée* (Draco took his time helping me pronounce it) and he immediately led me to an apothecary. I smiled to see him pawing through ingredients, potions and instruments. After he’d talked himself out of a *good* number of purchases, he still ended up with a hefty bag as we strolled back out onto the street. Narcissa seemed more agreeable to letting us part ways today, and drifted into the crowd, likely to her smelly millinery as Draco led me in the opposite direction.

Right to a… A *pet shop.* My heart swelled to see the rabbits, and the lizards, and the various rodents. Draco even managed to convince one of the shop owners to let me *hold a gerbil,* and I almost fucking *died* when it licked my fingers. How he’d known I’d *swoon* over this stop, I hadn’t the foggiest, but was pretty much resigned to how well he knew me at this point.

When he finally pried me from the pet shop, I was almost in *tears*. Leaving all those fuzzy and scaly and fluffy creatures made my heart cinch, but he pulled me in by the waist and pressed a kiss to my head as he led me to a cafe. And I consoled myself in a *real* cup of coffee.

“You wanna go back to the vault later?” he asked on the end of a sip of his own tea. And I considered.

“Eh, I… I mean, wealth is fun to *play* with, but I… I dunno what I’d even *do* with any of that stuff.”

He smirked as he lowered his mug back to its saucer, and it was then our croissants arrived. Mine with simple ham and butter, and his with tomatoes, olives, peppers, anchovies and *tuna.* My nose curled to hear him describe what he’d got, but he seemed *very* pleased with the results.

After our lunch, he took me to a sweet shop. And though I was full to *bursting,* I let him pick out a selection of sweets for me to try later. When we left, we had *two* heavy bags in tow.

Another day of shopping led us back to the villa, likely ahead of Narcissa as we trudged up the stairs. Full from our meals, weary from the walking, and *stimulated* beyond our capacity. Another nap claimed us after we’d dropped our bags to the floor, and it was the same familiar, *astonishing bliss* in his arms as we fell asleep.

The next time we awoke, it must’ve been near dinner, because Draco was a bit *frantic* to usher us from bed. And indeed, Narcissa had already finished her last course by the time we joined her. Muttered apologies fell on deaf ears as we were plated, and fed, and *nourished. Jesus fucking Christ,* every new dish I tried was *delectable.*

After dinner, Draco led me back down to the vault. It’d been left open for us, and I was just sort of perusing for my amusement. He was *really* hoping I’d find something I liked, I assumed, because he just… Backed away. Let me wander the aisles and shelves, the racks and cases and trunks and I was just *entranced* with the sheer, staggering *opulence*held within this one room.

When I finally found him again, I was still empty-handed, and he offered me a knowing smile as I returned to his side. He led me back up into the villa, to the library, where he let me explore their titles. I wasn’t much of a *reader* (when I could help it) but I guess he must’ve picked up on my curiosity when he’d given me a tour. And I couldn’t deny the larger tomes, the *engraved* ones drew my attention.

The door to the library clicked open, and I peered around a row of books to see Narcissa pacing her stabbing heels sharply onto the clean marble. Distant voices reached me, but I wasn’t interested in Draco’s conversation with his mother. So I turned back to the shelf before me.

Books as tall as my *torso* were leaning against each other on the top shelf. I couldn’t hope to reach them, but I endeavoured to appreciate the fine script on their spines, the gilding in the titles and the inlaid little gems. I’d known Draco had grown up in the lap of luxury, but *seeing* it with my own eyes was… Something else. A surge of *warmth* pulsed down the bond, and I paused to let it soak into me. Whatever Draco and Narcissa were discussing, it was affecting him. And I resolved to ask him about it later tonight.

But for now, I was perhaps the *most interested in books* as I’d *ever been.*

---

My hand was fiddling with the rings in my pocket as Harry disappeared into the library. I tried not to fret, for fear of him feeling it, but when mother strolled in through the doors, it became a *triple* effort to remain neutral.

“Draco, love! Find anything you like in the vaults?”

A hesitant glance back towards where Harry had vanished, and I swallowed.

“E-erm, yeah… Actually…” Tremblingly, I withdrew the rings from my pocket. Two I’d spied on our first trip, and then actually managed to swipe during our second. Two gold bands, one with a little curved indentation in one spot, an engraved crescent moon around the hole in it, and another fitted gold band with a *sun* that fit *right* in the hole. I eyed them a moment in my palm before holding them out to my mother.

Curious eyes fell on my hand. Stared, went wide, and then snicked back up to mine. And I swallowed again.

“Oh, *Draco…”* She reached for my hand, and just when I was worrying she’d snatch them from my grip, she… *Closed my fist* around the rings in both of her hands. Peered up at me with wet, blue eyes, and I was *frozen* in shock. “You… You’re going to give me another s-*son?”*

I coughed a laugh. “If he’ll have me.”

A curiously *overwhelmed* sort of smile lifted the corners of mother’s lips moments before she pulled me into a hug. And for the first time, I… I didn’t *flinch.* My heart *swelled* as I wilted into her. Brought my own arms back up around her, and it… It felt… *Right.*

*“I-I’m so… H-happy for you,”* she wept, and my throat went tight. A sob broke against my shoulder as my grip on her tightened.

“I… I love him *so much,* mum,” I said, similarly tight as my eyes pricked with moisture. Another sob into the shoulder of my shirt.

*“Th-that’s all I’ve ever w-wanted for you. Just want you to be happy.”*

*Christ,* it was so fucking *strange* to be sharing *tears with my mother.* But I couldn’t deny how *incredible* it felt to have her blessing.

*“Y-yeah,”* was all I could manage.
