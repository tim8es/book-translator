## Chapter 35: Chapter 35

The Baron sent us to the library to work today, which was as good as giving us the day off, despite Hermione’s insistence we use every minute of our *lives* for academia. It took all of one glance between Draco and I for us to bail, leaving Ron staring after us mournfully. But between studying with Hermione and being our third wheel, it wasn’t really a choice for him.

We decided to roam around the grounds, because it was a beautiful day and everyone else was in class so there weren’t throngs of onlookers for us to ignore. The sun was warm and the shade was cool and the breeze was *perfect,* and as we walked sort of aimlessly, our casual conversation hit a lull. Neither of us really seemed to mind, and it was a blessed relief to share comfortable silence with another person.

“You know,” I began as I balanced along the low stone wall hedging a short garden of asphodel outside the Greenhouses. “Seeing as I’ve been gay this whole time and never knew, I s’pose I’ve never been in love before.”

Draco hummed noncommittally, sort of sideying me to track my progress.

“Which makes a lot of sense why it *didn’t* make sense that being with Ginny never worked. Like, I was *sure* I loved her, but I wasn’t… *In love* with her. And I didn’t really know the difference.”

I glanced over at him, caught a glimpse of the wariness in his eyes. Whether it was at my current test of my relationship with gravity or at the direction of my thoughts, I couldn’t say.

“H-how did you… Realise you were in love with me?”

Draco cleared his throat as I hopped down the end of the flowerbed to the grass, and though I *thought* I was putting on a good show of appearing casual, I was hanging on his every word.

“Suppose it was a multitude of things. I knew I was gay when I was, like… Ten? Tried broaching the topic with my father, and learned how unacceptable it would be for a Malfoy not to have offspring, so began a long, *long* string of lies and secrets. But *you…*”

Forgetting my feigned indifference, I swivelled to face him, walking backwards and *again* testing my relationship with gravity as he smiled at me.

“I knew my father hated you *way* before I ever knew he hated *queers.* Claimed you’d ‘set the Wizarding World back twenty years,’ though I wouldn’t know what he meant until… Later. So, knowing how he felt about *me,* even if he didn’t know it, made me curious about the other thing he supposedly hated. I wanted to know what could be so bad about you, honestly, the amount of times I heard my father rag on you, your father, Siri–” My stomach dropped just a *tad* as Draco’s words halted in his mouth.

“‘S alright. Go on,” I tried to encourage. He looked a bit hesitant, but he sighed and continued.

“You didn’t seem so bad, honestly, ‘cept for the way you rejected me. I was a right *priss,* and being told ‘no’ was… *Unthinkable* for eleven-year-old me.” I smirked, mirroring Draco, as he crossed his hands behind his back. “Thought you were full of it, and then you were sorted *Gryffindor,* and I *knew* you were.”

My hand itched for his, but with all the windows that might’ve betrayed us to the student body, I just fisted my hands in my pockets and turned back around, lagging so Draco would catch up to me.

“M-my father’s *rhetoric* was… Pretty influential on such a young mind, and I guess I sort of latched onto it in hopes it’d help me *fit in.*” He glanced at me, seemed to sort of *wince*. “I’m… Rambling, aren’t I?”

“You’re fine,” I assured him with a grin. “I wanna know everything about you, so I don’t mind.”

“Y-you asked how I *realised* I was in love with you. There’s a long and aggravating history of *why,* but I suppose… I didn’t start to suspect my feelings until… Until the war began.”

We were coming up on the Black Lake, just sort of following where the other one led, and neither of us were really leading. But we ended up wandering towards the bank.

“All of a sudden I was hearing my father’s rhetoric from multiple sources, piecing together that their general *disdain* for m-muggleborns was a bit more like *murderous intent,* and realising the community I’d tried to chameleon myself into was… Wasn’t…”

“Wasn’t where you belonged,” I offered quietly. And he flinched a smile at me.

“R-right, erm… S-so when I began hearing plots involving *you… Your* death, it just…” Draco went slightly pale as he halted, a few metres away from the lake’s edge and just sort of staring out over it. I paused with him. “Brought things into focus at a *nauseating* speed.”

Since we weren’t really headed anywhere, I decided to drop into the grass right there by his side, and he eased down with me, slightly stiff but still went on:

“I couldn’t help thinking *none of this would be happening if they could just meet Harry and see he’s not that bad.* But obviously that’s *ridiculous,* because it didn’t matter to them what sort of person you were. And I realised it mattered to *me.* I couldn’t convince myself anymore that purebloods were the ‘good kind’ of wizard, because I *saw…* I *saw* what kinds of things they did. And I saw what kinds of things *you*did.”

Draco’s eyes turned suddenly sincere as he swivelled towards me, attention *rapt* on my face.

“And then the Snatchers caught you, and brought you to the Manor and asked *me* to identify you, and of course, *of course,* I’d have recognised those *ridiculously* green eyes *anywhere* and it just sort of hit me all at once. All the bits and pieces of you I’d learned over the years and thought I *hated* about you, were suddenly pretty obviously the things I *loved* about you, and although it fucking *shook* me to my core to realise I’d been slowly falling in fucking *love* with you all that time, seeing the look in your eyes like you were *daring* me to rat you out, like you *knew* I recognised you, you just…” He took a stuttering breath in, like he’d been forgetting to breathe in his tirade. “You inspired me. I put my life on the line right fucking *there,* not just because the thought of them killing you was *intolerable,* but because you… You made me want to be brave.”

The rest of his breath left him in a *whoosh*, and he looked like he might have more to say, but was waiting for my reaction. I… Didn’t know *what* to say, honestly. *I… Made him want to be brave?* Inspired *him…?*

“Does…” he began again when I took too long to respond. “Does that answer your question?”

---

Harry looked… Well, a bit bloody *awestruck* as I waited for him to answer. I was fairly sure I’d cocked up my words, *again,* but all I could do was wait and hope. And eventually, he swallowed.

“I… *Really* wanna kiss you right now.”

Laughter burst out of me unexpectedly, and it was then I realised he’d not just been asking this because he was curious. He was trying to figure out if he was in *love.*

In love with *me.*

“Reckon if you *did,* I’d end up taking your virginity here on the fucking *grass,*” I chortled, and he coughed a laugh of his own. “And someone would *definitely* notice that.”

Regardless of the exposure of our current position beside the lake, he leaned forward on his hands, just *barely* into my personal space as my laughter died.

“It’s yours,” he breathed. Eyes sparkling like they did in the sun as a few ducks drifted past us on the water, a wren calling in a nearby tree. “*I’m* yours.”

*Really easy being your boyfriend* echoed in my head, which I found to be suddenly vacuous. *Yours.*

My breath hitched in my throat as I felt my cheeks warm, and I was *this fucking close* to just tackling him back to the grass and making good on my word when the first bell chimed, drawing Harry’s affectionate attention away from me back towards the castle. The sounds of students filing out of the Greenhouse had us leaning apart, but there was this *knot* in my throat, like I’d just swallowed *sand,* so I was grateful for the interruption because there was likely no way I could even *speak*right now.

*Holy shit.*
