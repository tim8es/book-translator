## Chapter 94: Chapter 94

The second the Minister’s gavel came down on the block, the room erupted into chaos. Just like after my hearing, it was clear the Wizengamot disagreed with itself but my wand was safely back in my pocket as I stood on unsteady legs. Both McGonagall and mother were weaving through the pews to get to me, and I couldn’t do much more than stand at the moment.

I wondered if I looked just as fucking *humiliated* as I felt.

“Draco!” Mother reached me first, elbowing people out of the way uncharacteristically gracelessly as she rushed to my side. I swallowed just before she threw herself at me, arms pulling me into an unsettling embrace. And I tried not to flinch from it. “It’s so good to see you, darling. You’ve been keeping out of trouble?”

“Y-” My voice squeaked, and I cleared my throat before continuing. “Yeah, ‘m fine.”

McGonagall reached us then, glancing anxiously between us and the commotion around us. But clearly trying to give us a moment.

“Good, good,” mother said, finally pulling herself off me to hold me at arm’s length. My face was nearly feverish as she studied it. “I haven’t been reading the papers, like you said. You’re… Still coming for the holidays, yes?”

I nodded, and happened to glimpse Shacklebolt weaving through the crowd behind her. He gave me a small, if awkward smile, and paced out of the room. I was infinitely grateful he’d decided to dispose of the evidence his Wander had produced, but couldn’t deny a bit of confusion that he hadn’t at least dropped a subtle suggestion to McGonagall to keep a closer eye on us. *Suppose it must have something to do with not being proper students. Or he’s just as embarrassed as me and is about to go have a stiff drink to forget what he saw.*

*Fucking kill me.*

Mother passed a hand over my burning cheek, bringing my attention back to her as she pinched a smile.

“You *must* write to me more, Draco. I… I worry about you.”

I nodded again, weighing the likelihood that Shacklebolt *knew it was Harry.* If everyone here knew Harry and I were together. *Whatever happened to that photo?*

“Apologies, Narcissa, but I really must return Draco back to the castle.”

Mother nodded, pulling her hands off me somewhat reluctantly.

“I-I’ll see you over break,” I managed in parting. And with that, I let McGonagall usher me from the room.

The Atrium was pure *buzzing* with reporters and curious onlookers as we ducked through the throng back to the Floos. I could hardly make out any of the words being said to me, again sort of stiffly numb, but just barely cognizant enough to confirm this time, *they’re saying Harry’s name. Are they… Asking me about him?*

Dread sank in my gut as we reached the Floo wall, McGonagall’s grip around my shoulders tightening as we stepped into one. I just hoped they’d leave my mother alone, but knowing what sort of people reporters were, I didn’t count on it. As green flames engulfed us, the sight of dozens of faces crowding us to demand answers burned itself into my retinas.

*This is what Harry goes through all the time.*

---

I reverently knelt where Draco’s bag still sat, Snape’s portrait now empty behind me as I plucked it off the floor. Hermione was immediately striding past me to rifle with the parchments scattered across McGonagall’s desk, but I was no closer to a solution than I had been in the library.

“Reckon the Floo would let us through?” Seamus wondered behind me as I pulled the strap of Draco’s bag onto my shoulder. What I was doing with it, I couldn’t say.

“Pretty sure you have to have an invitation to use it,” Ron answered as I rounded on them. Luna came up to join Hermione’s efforts, the rest of us all looking pitifully fucking lost as we studied the last place Draco had been.

“Could always leave the Apparation wards and just *go,”* Dean supposed, leaning over the glowing Pensieve to peer down into it.

“*Or,* we just pinch a few brooms and–”

Ginny’s suggestion was cut off as the Floo burst into flames against the wall. My throat stopped up as my eyes adjusted to the sudden green light, so like the curse that’d sunk into my chest and grazed my brow. And once it died, I blinked against the spot it’d left in my vision, hoping against hope…

“The *hell…?”* The bag on my shoulder dropped to the floor as I lunged towards the voice I recognised, even though my sight hadn’t returned to me yet. A warm body slammed into me, a familiar scent filled my nostrils and I just about had a *heart attack.*

Sighs of relief were a distant backdrop as I held Draco to me. A surprised sort of chuckle coughed in my ear as he wrapped his arms back around me, and it felt like fucking *bliss* in the wake of the agony I’d been in. *He’s here. He’s back. He’s alright. Is he alright…?*

“‘M alright,” he murmured in my ear, had I asked this question out loud? Or was he just reading my mind? Didn’t really matter either way, because I could finally *breathe* with him in my arms.

“Seems you’ve caused quite a stir, Mister Potter,” McGonagall observed as she stepped around us. I couldn’t quite pull myself away from Draco, but felt my cheeks warm as I traded my arms from his neck to his waist and twisted my face to peer at her. Hermione and Luna were conspicuously inching away from her desk as the Headmistress approached it, and pulled out the chair to sit. “I have to assume this isn’t unrelated.”

I nodded, frowning, because I’d also come to the same conclusion. And the notion that *I’d* been the reason Draco had been summoned gripped my lungs in my chest.

“What’s related?” he wondered, and I heard the familiar rustling of paper behind me. Buried my face in Draco’s chest as he released one of his arms around me to accept a copy of the Prophet. “Oh.”

The arm still around me tightened slightly, and I inhaled deeply of his scent again. Couldn’t drink it down fast enough because he was *here. He’s alright, he came back to me.* I’d been on the verge of ripping apart the entire *world* to have him back in my arms, and here he was. Even if I’d’ve had to go back on the run, I would’ve.

Draco swore quietly as he scanned the article, and a sudden flare of anger had him dropping it with a yelp as it burst into flames.

“Shit, sorry.” I pulled myself out of his arm to take his hand in both of mine, searching for any harm I might’ve incurred. Ron brandished his wand to extinguish the burning pages on the floor as I splayed Draco’s palm before me.

“You’re alright. Nothing worth reading, anyway.”

Finally, I let myself look up at him. His expression was a conflict of a smile and a worried frown, but when our eyes locked, he pulled his hand from mine to stroke my cheek gently. I wanted to ask him what happened, but a sudden *need* to feel his skin against mine had me tugging him from the office by a hand. Just to *feel* his steady heart beating against mine, like this wouldn’t be real until we were flesh against flesh. Just before leaving, though, I paused to address the room again.

“Th-thanks, guys,” I said, and swallowed. I *really* didn’t like showing weakness like this, especially to my friends who were prone to *worrying* about me, but all I was met with were smiles. They were willing to follow me *anywhere,* and I felt infinitely fucking grateful to each and every one of them. My eyes lingered on Ginny, though, because she’d been the *last* person I’d expected to want to help reunite us. And she was just smiling the same as everyone else.

“Go on,” Ron allowed as he stooped to scoop Draco’s bag back off the floor. And I didn’t need any further invitation.
