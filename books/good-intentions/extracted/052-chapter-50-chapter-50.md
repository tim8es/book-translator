## Chapter 50: Chapter 50

Harry was a ball of fucking *anxiety* the rest of the day. And I honestly had half a mind to fuck it out of him, but just knowing he was preparing to *out us,* to tell his *friends* about us twisted me all up to *fuck*inside. And I just couldn’t separate myself from his anxiety.

*What if they give him a hard time? What if they reject the idea of us?* I worried my thumb between my teeth as he paced in my room that afternoon. I *desperately* wanted to be there, to hear what they would say, what *he* would say, but if he wanted me gone, I’d be gone. I trusted him enough, right?

They were *his friends,* after all. And I didn’t want to impose. But I couldn’t release my thumb from my fucking teeth as I fidgeted on my bed.

“R-Ron and Mione will back me up,” he supposed, but sounded suspiciously like he was trying to convince *himself* as he paced back and forth. “N-no one’s gonna question *my judgement,* after all. They *have* to believe me.”

Harry swore under his breath as he ran both hands through his hair, scratched at his scalp. And I remembered how lovingly I’d kneaded my hair products into that scalp.

“Yeah,” I assured him. “You’re *Harry Potter.* They’re not gonna call you *mental* if you tell them everything.”

“Yeah,” he said. Halted. “Yeah.” A bit more sure as he turned towards me, hands on his hips. And my anxious fucking heart *melted.*

*“Harry,”* I began as I shuffled off my bed to approach him. Took his hands off his hips, held them *tight* as I touched my forehead to his. “It’ll be *alright.”*

Whether I believed that or not, I wanted *him* to. And he exhaled a long sigh as his eyes slid shut.

“Yeah. Thanks.” He didn’t sound convinced, so I pulled him into my arms and pressed a kiss to his hair. Stroked his shoulder with a thumb.

“The worst that can happen *isn’t even that bad.”*

He went sort of stiff in my arms, and I briefly wondered if I’d said something wrong. But then he twisted in my grasp to press a kiss to the underside of my jaw. Another to my neck as he exhaled another sigh against it.

“Wish you could be there,” he breathed against my skin. And I choked back a fucking *sob.*

“I’ll be here waiting when you’re done.”

He buried his head against my chest, sounding near the edge of fucking *tears* as I held him.

*Fuck, I wish there was something I could do to help.*

My descent to the common room was slow. Uneasy, hesitant. But I eventually wound up on the landing, and all the eyes of those who’d gathered turned towards me. Ron, Hermione, Neville, Luna, Seamus, Dean, even *Ginny* as my eyes danced across the familiar faces. And I swallowed.

“H-hey,” I began. “S-so I… I have an ex-*explanation* to give.”

Ginny cleared her throat, leaning back against an armrest of a couch as Seamus straightened slightly, worrying his hands together before him.

“Yeah,” Ron answered, and I shot him a *grateful* fucking glance as he met everyone’s eyes. “We deserve an explanation, right?”

A chorus of agreement echoed through the room, and I stiffened as Ron turned back to me, nodding encouragingly. Not that I *felt* any more encouraged, but I’d take it. And swallowed.

“S-so most of you *know* I’ve been, erm… H-hanging out with D- *Malfoy* lately…” Several eyes on me turned incredulous, sceptical, even *outraged* as I endeavoured to continue. “There’s, like, a *wealth* of backstory to how we wound up here, but… Basically, I learned some… *Things…* About him, over the summer, and came to learn he’s an alright sort of guy, and we’ve been h-hanging out, talking, and I…”

Luna twirled her hair in her fingers, *maybe* intrigued, but it was hard to tell with her. And I tried to say what I *meant* as my attention franticked around the other attending faces.

“H-he’s alright, okay?” I eventually said, arms postulating outwards. Had I been holding myself? “I l-like him… A lot.” I swallowed.

Ginny met my eye at that moment, and I recalled the confession I’d made to her. And it tightened my throat, but Draco was *upstairs,* far away from this, so I could be honest… *Right?*

“I… I actually just realised I’m g-*gay…* Recently… And I…” Seeing the bewilderment and shock and *awe* on my friends’ faces just fucked up my trajectory, and I just burst open with a: “I-I’m in fucking *love with him, alright?”*

Somehow, in the midst of all this, I’d lost my breath. And tried to gasp it back as I hastily catalogued the reactions from my friends. My guts started to twist their way up into my fucking *ribs* when no one said anything, but eventually, Hermione, my *saving grace,* stepped forward.

“Anyone *you love…* Has *got* to have some redeeming qualities.” I could’ve *kissed*her. And breathed a bit easier as I assessed the rest of my friends’ reactions to my words.

The first to follow up was obviously *Ron.* And though he looked *loathe* to make a statement, he joined Hermione’s side, taking her hand and straightening his shoulders as he spoke.

“Yeah. R-reckon he had a h-*hard time,* too, so… Er…” Could’ve kissed *him, too. My best friends.*I offered them grateful smiles, but noted the way Dean sidled up to Ginny’s side in my peripherals, slid his hand into hers. And I didn’t mind.

“So *what?”* Seamus began, tearing his finger from his teeth as he shoved off the wall, eyeing the rest of the gathered assembly like they were *lunatics.* “We just… *Accept* a fock’n *Death Eater* into the group?”

Against *all fucking odds, Neville* stood out from the group, scratching behind his ear bashfully as he glanced between me and Seamus.

“Y-*yeah,”* was his answer as he came to a halt at the forefront. And straightened. “I m-mean… If *Harry* sees something in him…”

I couldn’t fucking *believe* what I was seeing. *Did this… Actually fucking work…?*

I waited for Harry as long as I could *stand* in my room. But when I recalled his Invisibility Cloak hung reverently against the side of my wardrobe, I paused my pacing. Eyed it, arms dropping to my sides. *Do I dare…?*

Seems I did, because I approached my wardrobe and snatched it off the hook. Threw it around me and went to the door. Took a deep breath, but eventually pulled it open and cautiously descended down the staircase. I just *had* to know what he said.

The stairs creaked quietly beneath me as I approached the common room, but I must’ve arrived at the right fucking moment because the first thing I heard was a familiar voice saying:

“I… I actually just realised I’m g-*gay…* Recently… And I…” I froze. Leading with *actually coming out* was just so fucking *Gryffindor* of him, and pride swelled in my throat. But then he said “I-I’m in fucking *love with him, alright?”*

*...What?*

If I’d thought I’d frozen before that, I was fucking *ice* as the conversation continued. I’d not even made it all the way down the stairs, but hearing *that…*I felt my eyes well with tears, and couldn’t fucking move.

Like my attention was on a *timer,* the next thing I heard, in a thick Irish accent was:

“We just… *Accept* a fock’n *Death Eater* into the group?”

Ice sluiced through my veins as the following silence stretched. But then a voice I *hadn’t*expected answered.

“Y-*yeah.*” *Longbottom.* After his confrontation with us during dinner, I was shocked to my fucking *core* that he was the one defending us. “I m-mean… If *Harry* sees something in him…"

I turned and fled, as *silently* as I fucking could back up the stairs. Straight to my dorm where I slammed the door behind me and threw the cloak off me. Collapsed to the bed in fucking *tears.*

*Harry loves me.*

Disgruntled acceptance was actually the *best case scenario* I could’ve hoped for. So, after a bit more of an explanation, I left my peers to their gossip to return to Draco. And an ease that didn’t really feel *deserved* settled over me. I approached his door, feeling altogether fucking *drained* as I shouldered it open. And fucking *froze* when I found him face-down on the bed, sobbing his fucking *guts out.*

“Draco!?” Alarmed, I shut the door behind me and rushed to his side. Knelt and watched, reached. *Jesus,* what the fuck could’ve *happened* in the twenty fucking minutes it’d taken me to break the news??

He curled his face further into the bed, sobbing all the harder, and I fucking *panicked.* Had he received ill news from his mother? Had he broken a fucking *bone??* Any and all possibilities flooded my mind as I stood and brushed my hands over him like I’d find the solution in his *body.* All the way to his fucking ankles, I searched, but then he hitched a gasp in and coughed. Seemed to settle somewhat, and I came to rest back by his head on his floor, carding a hand through his hair like a *mother* worried about a *sick child.* *Christ, I love him so fucking much, it's not fair.*

He stuttered his breaths for a moment, occasionally breaking on a sob before he finally, *finally* twisted his head to look at me. Eyes red and puffy, I was relearning what anxiety felt like as he measured his breaths.

“Y-you…” he began, and I waited with bated breath for him to continue. Hand paused in his hair, I hung on his every fucking *word. What the fuck is wrong??* “You l-*l**ove me…?”*

*Oh.* I went altogether still on his floor. *He heard that.* My heart lurched in my chest as a swallow ushered itself down my throat.

“Y-*yeah,”* I eventually confirmed. Drew my hand back into myself, settled back on my heels. *God, here it comes. I have no fucking right to love him as much as I do, given how torturous his love for me has been, and here I am just making it look fucking*easy–

Draco stuttered in another gasp, and reached out for me. Hesitantly, I laid my hand in his, unsure what this reaction was. He was *crying,* for chrissake–!

“R-*really?”*

*Fuck.* I smiled, tears welling in my own eyes.

“Yeah. *Really.*”

He broke down in tears again, turning his head into his pillow even as his hand tightened around mine. And I was compelled to lean forward, to lift and close the space between us as I buried my face into his shoulder.

“I l… *Love you,* Draco.” He hacked a *gross* fucking sob. “I *love you.”* More sobs as he drew my hand into himself, curled it beneath his throat. “Love you *so fucking much.”*

My prickling eyes finally offered tears, and I let them release down my cheeks as I nestled my face as far into his neck as I could fucking *reach. God,* I loved him so much it should’ve been *illegal.*

*“I love you,”* I whispered into his neck as he shattered apart, and even though his sobs wrought my heart into fucking *bits,* they were all his. Every fucking shard had *Draco Malfoy* stamped onto it, and I let myself cry, too. Sank into the overwhelm that drew tears to his eyes as I broke a-fucking*-part.*

*Fuck me, nothing ever happens like I want it to.*
