## Chapter 114: Chapter 114

Chapter Notes

Despite the way they’d left their Apparation signature so obviously unconcealed, I silently awarded them props for setting up anti-Apparition wards around the facility it led me to. I popped them like a fucking soap bubble, but it was a good try.

I wasn’t afraid to die. Never really had been, after my attempt at twelve years old. I’d walked to my death on my own two legs five years later, after all, but if anything, *anything* had happened to Draco, I wasn’t sure what I’d do. I was so fucking terrified, so *furious* that I was calm as I followed the dimly lit corridor to the door that seemed to be softly singing my name.

*Harry… Harry, I’m here…* There was no one to stop me, no one who *could* stop me as I stepped right up to the dilapidated metal frame and braced myself. If there was even a *scratch* on Draco, we’d have to go back into hiding because I was going to *kill whoever else was there.*

The door burst open with a *BANG* and my eyes drank in the room beyond. Poorly lit, but I saw all I needed to. A host of startled individuals, all staring at me, but my attention narrowed to the one in the centre, twisting atop a stool as he stood to appraise me.

“The *hell…?”*

As he rose, his movement revealed the last thing I ever wanted to see, and the *one* *thing* I’d come here to *find.* A slumped blond, bound to a chair and *smattered in red.* It took all of two seconds for my magic to swell to *impossible* size before it *exploded out of me.*

*Not dead. He’s not dead, he can’t be dead.*

All the lights in the ceiling burst, every piece of furniture leapt to the walls of the room as we were plunged into darkness. A scurrying of bodies, swearing, but I was of singular, calm focus as I bled into the black. Some of them might’ve tried to lunge for me, but I was *un-fucking-touchable* as I approached the hunched figure at the pinnacle of the room. And as I absently kicked the stool before him aside, I realised I could *see.* But didn’t spend much mental power wondering how or why.

My foot happened upon… Something as I stepped up to him. I paused, and peered down at my shoe as I twisted it away. Two halves of a *wand* laid discarded on the floor, and I heard the single door behind me slam shut.

There was an awful red patch on the back of Draco’s head. Leaking down his neck to stain his jumper, and as I lowered myself to a knee, I saw the way his *brow* was similarly red.

“D-” I tried to say his name, but it lodged in my throat. There was *pandemonium* behind me, but I couldn’t spare them any attention. A hesitant hand reached up to press him back to the chair by a shoulder, dread *sluicing* through my veins like *ice* as he eased back.

His jumper had been torn open across his chest, coat nowhere to be seen, a faint line of *red* down the centre next to an even thicker trail of red from his opened forehead. Head lolled to the side as I marked the split lip, the purpling side of his jaw, the faint lift and fall of his chest.

*Breathing. He’s alive.*

Sudden *blinding light* erupted out of me as I rose back to my feet. Took his head carefully in my hands and squeezed my eyes shut. If there was *any way* I could repair the damage here and now, I would, but he *needed blood. I have to make this quick.*

I felt my magic push out of my chest with every heartbeat, down my arms and into my hands, and for a brief moment, it wasn’t murder in my heart. Wasn’t *hate,* it was pure, *blinding love.* Blinding as the light that still shone through my eyelids. But when I squinted back down at him to see his wounds slowly receding, the contrast of his pale skin with the glistening *red* on it drove murder right back into my heart. And I released him tenderly.

Still just as calm, just as *violently livid,* I rounded on our audience. All pressed up against the door with just the one standing before the rest, and *heaving.*

“You… You *ruined everything,”* he said, but his voice wavered. *Terror. Good.* I started towards them, idly wondering *just how much murder* I could reasonably get away with. “W-we… He–!”

*Silence.* Just a short passage of thought, and he shut up. Held a hand to his throat and *choked* as he fell to his knees. It was then my attention caught on a familiar face, and, not breaking my stride, I stepped right up to Theo. The rest of our *guests scattered* like *flies* to the edges of the room as he drew panicked gasps.

“Was it worth it?” I asked as the man on his knees collapsed to the floor. Theo was weeping, fluttering eyes full of *horror* as he sank to his own knees.

“H-he’s worth *everything,”* he muttered on a sob.

*Worth brutalising? Worth torturing?* Red leaked out of Theo’s nose, and his features pinched in pain. He reached for me, but there was a firm boundary that forbade his touch as he collapsed similarly.

*Two down.*

---

I was fairly used to pain. Whether it be from father’s beatings, the *literal* Torture Curse, or Harry’s Sectumsempra, it was familiar to me. So when I awoke, roused by touch to find not pain but *weakness…* I was confused.

“Draco? Draco, love, can you hear me?”

*Harry.* A smile pulled at my lips as I blinked up into near-blinding light. Details were *swimming* before me, sort of dragging as I squinted around and then I found his face. Still blurry, but… *Bloody hell, am I hallucinating…?*

Harry’s eyes were *glowing green.* *That’s* what the light was. And in the illumination they shone from his face, he was…

*Drenched in blood.*

“Kn-knew you’d find me,” I managed to whisper, unsure if this was real or what my dying mind had decided to conjure on death’s doorstep. A kiss against my temple.

“You’ll be alright,” he said, but his voice sounded strange. Like he was speaking underwater. He spoke again, but I couldn’t make it out. I was *so tired…*

---

Draco went under again, his pulse was *so faint* beneath the skin of his throat, and I panicked. Hands slicked red, I tapped his cheek.

“Draco? Draco!” He wouldn’t stir. Head lolled again, mouth agape and my calm finally *snapped.*

*This isn’t happening. This isn’t fucking happening.* I could conjure *storms* with my *bare fucking hands,* but I couldn’t *save his life??* A steady chant of *‘no’* marched through my lips as I stood to round him. Found the cuffs around his wrists, *also* slick with red, and made quick work of loosing them. Held my hands around the sticky skin and pushed *love* into them.

*Love.* Endless, *fathomless*, *impossible* love. And it was then the singular door to the room burst open again. A gasp sailed into my lips as I released Draco’s wrists, and he began to slump forward, but I banded my arms around his chest to hold him to me.

A *Lumos* lit the room at the end of a wand, and I breathed easier to recognise who’d found us. *McGonagall.* As well as a *host* of other Hogwarts professors, some Aurors, even *Ron and Hermione.* But I still couldn’t fucking *let Draco go.*

“What… In *Merlin’s–”* Our Headmistress stepped gingerly into the room, over bodies, over *puddles* of red, before she lifted her attention to *us.* “Oh, *dear.”*
