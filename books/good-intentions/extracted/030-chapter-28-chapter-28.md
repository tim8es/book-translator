## Chapter 28: Chapter 28

*Maybe they’d keep it a secret if I asked them to.* Hermione had been eyeing me all during lunch, and I’d started to work through the logistics of just *telling* her, because that look was driving me *nuts,* and I just didn't like lying to my friends. But if I told her, she’d definitely tell Ron. And if I told them *both,* they’d bully me into telling *Ginny,* and it was all still just *so new,* I didn’t want it to get out just yet and knowing Ginny, half the school would know by the next day.

So I just ate in silence, bearing the discomfort of her gaze on me, and trying not to lock eyes with Draco across the Great Hall. The eighth years had sort of claimed the edge of the Gryffindor table closest to the back entrance, which was *fine,* because it meant all the rest of our house left us well enough alone toward the front of the Hall, but it also meant anyone walking in or out passed perilously close to us.

Either Hermione or this *morning,* or something Draco had said or done was making me particularly sensitive to the stares today. Not that people didn’t *always* stare, but the more I thought about it, the more I wished he *would* just come out. See how people would react, if anyone would care. Obviously, I’d have a bigger impact because it’s *me,* but if I could just be shown how…

*Rather a lot of things he’s teaching me how to do.* My ears burned.

“Harry?” I looked up from the plate of food I realised I’d only been pushing around with my fork to see Ron sort of waiting expectantly.

“Sorry, what?”

He and Hermione exchanged a knowing glance, and I frowned.

“Mum’s invited you both for Christmas,” he said slowly, like maybe if he repeated himself slowly, I’d listen. I hadn’t *meant* to ignore him, really, I’d just been… Lost in thought.

“Oh. Right.” It was then I noticed the letter open in his hands, and how the *hell* had I not noticed bloody owls swooping overhead with mail? “Sure, sounds great.”

Ron shook his head like I was just being as thick as always, but *Hermione…* There was something about her eyes, like *shining,* or something. Like *anticipation.*

“What?” I asked, checking myself to see if she was waiting for me to realise I’d spilled food on myself.

“Oh, nothing. Nothing at all,” she promised, lifting her cup of water to her lips with a barely concealed smile. My eyes narrowed.

*There’s no way. Bloody hell, I know she’s perceptive, but there’s no* way *she knows* already!

I hummed, unconvinced, but returned to my own meal and actually *ate* this time. Vegetables first, always, fibre or grain second, protein last. And as I was recalling the meal I’d shared with Draco in the kitchens, I happened to glance up and see him unwrapping a parcel. Curiosity piqued, I simply *forgot* not to stare as he withdrew a… *Jumper?*

Even across the hall, I could see he was pleased. He folded it up into his lap, polished off his drink, and left. And I, for some reason, wanted to chase him down and ask what it was. *Why* he’d received a jumper in the post, and who it was from. But I rooted myself to the bench, determined not to give Hermione any more cause to look at me so *suspiciously gleefully.*

*Jesus, Harry, calm down. You can ask him later. I wonder if he’ll spend the night again.*

My heart gave a little flutter at the thought, and I rushed through the rest of my meal like that’d somehow speed the day up.

I’d just been finishing up my letter to mother when a knock came at my door.

“One second,” I called out as I scrawled my name at the bottom and laid it out on my nightstand to be sent off in the morning. And my cheeks broke into a grin when I saw who was waiting outside. “Hi.”

“H-hi. Um… U-usually you’re kissing me by this time of night.” He peered up at me sheepishly, like he felt silly for seeking me out when I hadn’t immediately went to his room, and my stomach flip-flopped.

“Apologies for keeping you waiting, Mr. Potter. Coming in?”

He nodded eagerly, relief washing over his features as I stepped aside to invite him. And as he looked around the environment, I realised just how long it’d been since he’d last been here. Only made sense, since *his* was the room with its own bathroom, but as I shut the door behind him, my chest tightened to see him in *mine.*

Which was *ridiculous.*

“I was writing to my mother,” I explained as he hesitantly went to sit at the foot of my bed. “Surprisingly.”

Harry hummed, and sort of looked around, rubbed his hands together. *God,* he just chose the *worst* moments to be so painfully fucking *adorably* awkward.

“She sent you that jumper?” he asked, and I chuckled. Of course he’d been watching.

“Yeah, yeah she did. Wanna see?” He nodded, but I was already on my way to the wardrobe to withdraw it for him. It was one of my favourites, after all, because not only was it a gorgeous deep midnight blue, but because within the stitches was a subtle silver threading. Not to mention it wasn’t the least bit scratchy. I withdrew it from its hanger and turned round to hold it out for him.

“It’s nice,” he said, and stood to take a closer look. “Ooh, I like the sparkly bits.” *Sparkly bits.* A grin spread itself onto my face as he reached a hand out to feel it. “What’s the occasion?”

“Just asked her to send it to me is all. Thought I’d packed it, but turns out I didn’t. Wanna see it on?”

“*Obviously.*”

I handed it to him, and he took it almost *reverently* as I stripped my pyjama top off. *God, I’m giving my boyfriend a fashion show in my room. Everything about this is incredibly gay.*

Once I’d tossed my shirt behind him onto the bed, he offered the jumper back to me and I pulled it over my head. Tucked my arms into the holes and fixed my hair. It’d only frizzed up a *bit* in the absence of my full routine by the end of the day.

“What do you think?” I asked, gave him a rotation to appreciate it. Felt like a bloody *loon,* but Harry was smiling, so I did, too.

“Looks… *Incredible* on you.” He reached out to place a hand on my chest, and I held very still as he lifted himself onto his toes to kiss me. “You know, I love that little birthmark you have.”

His hand slid up my chest to the back of my neck, and my lungs swelled in my chest to know another facet of me he’d noticed.

“Yeah?” He nodded, used his hand to pull me back down for another kiss, and it was just so damn *sweet,* I think I stopped breathing altogether. Pulled him into me and drowned in his lips for a moment. “I love the freckles in your eyes.”

Said eyes fluttered up at me, and something like *mischief* shone in them.

“I love how one of your molars is a bit shorter than the rest.”

*Oh, it’s like that, is it?*

“I love the little dimples you get when you laugh.”

“I love the little crease you get between your eyebrows when you scowl.”

“I love the way you stick out your bottom lip when you pout.”

“I do not pout!” I laughed, enjoying this game *far* more than I had any right to. “I love the way you stir your tea even when you don’t put *anything in it.*”

“I love how you hold the last bite of something really tasty in your mouth before you swallow it.”

“I love how absorbed you get in reading something about *fish.*”

“It’s *more* than just fish, you know,” I tutted, and he grinned at me. “I love how terrible of a liar you are.”

“I love your smile.”

“I *love you.*” I knew it was a risky card to play, and I expected Harry to pull that petulant little pout like he *knew* I was cheating, but he… *Beamed* at me.

“Gotcha.” Looking so bloody *pleased* with himself, I realised it’d been his intention from the start to get me to say it. So I took his face in my hands, tilted his head back to match our eyes more straight-on, and said it again.

“I love you, Harry.” It was getting easier and easier to say, *especially* when I knew he wanted me to. So I added on one more, for good measure, in a whisper. “I *love* you.”

By way of answer, he closed the inch I’d left between our mouths. *God,* I just *knew* I was gonna die young from the way he made my heart skip beats so often. Some cardiac issue down the line in my early thirties, even if this ended up going *nowhere.* Because I’d never, *ever* forget being *Harry’s boyfriend.*

As much as I *loved* snogging the *spit* out of him, it seemed he wanted to talk, because he eventually pulled away, tugged me towards my bed, and sat. So I sat with him, because just *talking* to Harry was bliss of its own.

“S-so I’ve been thinking…”

“Don’t hurt yourself,” I cut in quietly, and he shoved at my shoulder playfully, clearly trying not to smile.

“Y-you said you were gonna… Come out?”

*Oh. This isn’t the direction I thought this would go.*

“I did,” I confirmed suspiciously. And waited as Harry gnawed on his lip.

“W-what exactly… H-*how?”*

I hadn’t really thought about it. Figured I’d just start being openly gay, but there wasn’t much about my mannerisms that I ever thought to hide. Barking nobody had picked up on it yet, but maybe straight people really didn’t *look* for the signs. So I shrugged.

“Probably start with my mother,” I answered. “I don’t really *talk* to anyone but you, so I’m not sure many people would *care,* but…”

Harry was looking increasingly distressed, and I wasn’t sure why. *Did I say something wrong?*

“Right… Not like you’d just… Stand up in the middle of class and just go ‘I’m gay!’ or something.” He laughed nervously, and then swore under his breath.

“Are you… Worried what people might think?”

“No! Well, *yes,* but… N-*not…* ” A sigh as he passed one hand through his hair. It was then I realised I’d taken his other one, twined my fingers through his. *Huh. When did I do that?* “I-I think we’re doing this out of order.”

*Out of order?* “How d’you mean?”

He swallowed, pink cheeks losing a bit of colour, before he went on.

“I… Think we should tell R-Ron and Hermione.” He grimaced at the thought, but the fact that he wanted his *friends to know…* “About us. Start there, *swear* them to secrecy, and en-enlist their help, y’know… Bringing you into the *group.*”

I tried to picture it, and I couldn’t. The rest of our peers had been understandably cold to me, not necessarily *cruel,* but chumming it up with the whole bloody *year?*

“I… Don’t know if that would work, Harry.”

*Even if he is the Chosen One, bringing an ex-Death Eater into the throng is sure to cause ripples.*

“It *would,*” he insisted, but sounded suspiciously like he was trying to convince *himself.* “Th-there’s the, ahm… O-obstacle of *Hermione,* because you two… I mean, everything that– Y-you know what I’m saying. The *point* is, you’re fucking *brilliant,* and I don’t want my friends to hate you.”

My heart caught up to his meaning before my brain did, and *lurched* in my chest. *He… Wants his friends to like me? Wants me to be included… So it’s that much easier when we stop being a secret.*

*He wants his friends to know about us.*

“Okay,” I said thickly. “If… If that’s what you *want.*”

His eyes lit up. “Really? I-I mean, I *know* you like k-keeping to yourself, but… It would… *Really* mean a lot to me not to have to do all the work, y’know? Because, like, the *only* other solution I could come up with was to just *come out first,* and I-I don’t even know where to *start.”*

Harry was babbling in that *painfully* adorable way, and though it came out all kind of a *mess,* I got the point. He was planning ahead, laying the groundwork for us to *publicly be a couple.* He was *thinking about our future.* And I was all at once on the verge of fucking *tears.*

I took a deep, slightly shaky breath and blinked *hard.*

“Okay. Let’s tell your merry band.”
