## Chapter 66: Chapter 66

Chapter Notes

Harry and Ron took their sweet time coming to lunch. It was nearly over by the time they appeared, and Harry looked *frazzled.* Fucking *jittery* as he came up to the table, but the one time he met my curious gaze, he just jerked his head, giving me this stern look that said *later.* So I let him be.

Hermione was doing this… *Completely* unconvincing pretending-not-to-notice thing as Harry stuffed his face like someone might snatch the food right out of his mouth. Even *Ron* looked so focused on eating, red as *fuck* in the cheeks, that I almost just asked. Felt strange to be left out of the loop when the rest of them seemed like there was some secret they all knew but couldn’t talk about, like I was once again on the outside looking in.

I might’ve bristled a bit at the silence, might’ve taken it a bit *personally,* but lunch was over before I could work myself up into a strop. Ron and Hermione left us at the table as Harry downed his glass of water, and I waited with him expectantly. As soon as the couple were out of sight, he set the cup down so forcefully I worried it’d *shatter,* and twisted towards me.

“Ron’s planning a surprise birthday party for Hermione and I wasn’t supposed to *know,* because I’m a *shit* liar AND,” he took a deep inhale and blazed on. “He’s planning on *proposing* to her which I *definitely* wasn’t supposed to know because she fucking knows *everything always,* even *years* before anyone else and now I don’t know how I’m supposed to interact with her now because I’m just going to *ruin everything!”*

Another deep inhale to replace the rushed explanation of everything that’d just happened. And I was sort of stunned silent for a moment.

“Oh.” *Shit.* “Shit.”

“Yeah.” Harry shakily stood from the table, face slightly blanched as I joined him. “I m-mean, she knew about *us two years ago!* What the *fuck* am I gonna *do?”*

My sigh harmonised with his groan as I laced my fingers through his, and we started back towards the classroom.

“I’m… *Pretty* sure she already suspects what’s going to happen for her birthday. And if she assumes your pitiful *lying* is to do with that, she won’t assume it’s anything more than that, right? Did he say *when* he was going to pop the question?”

“No, just that he couldn’t imagine life without her.” A flush rose to replace the pale in Harry’s cheeks as he shoved his other hand into his pocket. “Now that *I* know, though, he might just ask at the party. Get it over with before my transparent arse gives it away.”

*Can’t imagine life without her.* Until recently, I hadn’t been able to imagine a life *with* Harry. But after what he’d shown me… I couldn’t help but lean into the idea.

“Well,” I cleared my throat. “I’m not sure about typical Wizarding marriage customs, but for purebloods, it’s generally arranged. *I* was meant for some Greengrass bitch or other, but, well… S’pose my mother’s to thank for us being considered *blood traitors* now*,* because we’re not really beholden to pureblood customs anymore, thank *god.”* Harry glanced up at me, eyebrows lifted with shock, and I smirked back.

“You were gonna be *arranged* into *marriage?”*

My chin jerked a nod, grimace curling my lips as I considered what sort of hell *that’d’ve* been.

“Technically had a *fiancée*from the age of *ten.”* A shudder coursed through me. “But all that to say, isn’t… I mean, it’s not *ten,* but isn’t eighteen a bit *young* usually?”

Harry hummed contemplatively as we turned the final corner towards the classroom.

“I-I think it really depends on the people. Reckon they’ll end up hitched at some point anyway, so I don’t… Don’t think it’s too fast, at least for them. Watching them dance around each other for six fucking years was *agony.*”

*He knows them better than I do, I suppose.*We finally reached the eighth year classroom, and wove through desks together towards our own. And as we settled in to work, my mind strayed back to Ron’s rationale.

*Can’t imagine life without her. Seems a decent enough reason to get married, but bloody hell, that’s a big decision to be making at this age. Must be madly in love to be so sure.*

I glanced over at Harry, whose attention was torn between his own work and the back of Hermione’s head, frown firmly on his brow. *Madly in love with history.*

All the years I’d spent watching the three interact from the sidelines were a hastily stitched-together scar down the centre of my heart. *So much* I’d missed out on Harry's life. So much I still *envied* of his friends. I’d never really wanted anything as badly as him. Even my father’s love and approval was *leagues* below Harry’s heart, because now that I had it, it seemed to pervade every facet of my *being.* So much so I actually hadn’t spared my bastard of a father a *thought* in… Too long to even remember.

My head went sort of light as I turned my attention back to my own work. Leaned my forehead over in a palm as I fought against the dizziness, because *really realising* how entirely I now *belonged* to Harry was… I mean, against the laws of *nature,* because he’d ruled my every thought, every choice and breath and action for *years,* and now… It was like he was actually a *part* of me. An organ I’d never known I’d lost, reinserted.

*Can’t imagine a life without her.* Problem was, I *could* imagine a life without Harry. It was the only life I’d ever *known* until recently. A special sort of hell, seeing him every day and yet being so *unattainably* outside my reach. Just like–

I whipped a fresh sheet of parchment out of my bag like I’d been *hexed* to, dipped my quill into my inkwell and began penning a letter. Caught Harry’s attention as I did so, sudden as my movements were, but he was, for once, a peripheral detail as I lowered my quill to the page.

*‘Mother,*

*There’s much to report, but first I’d like to propose my invitation of a plus one to the villa. Apologies if this information is ill-timed, but it seems I’ve found myself in a relationship, and I’d like to introduce you. I’ll explain more when I see you, but just know I’m the happiest I’ve ever been, and would like to include you in that happiness. I can’t wait to see your face, honestly.*

*As I’ve implied, my final stretch here at the castle is going far better than I could’ve ever hoped. And I promise I’m not saying this at wand-point. I’m just as shocked as you.’*

My hand paused as I recalled the photograph snapped of us in Hogsmeade. How it’d probably be making the rounds to media outlets any day now, if it hadn’t already. Mother still hadn’t mentioned it in her letters, but I factored in the possibility of her already knowing who I was referring to as I resumed writing.

*‘Don’t read the papers before I visit, if you can help it. Just trust me on this.*

*Love,*

*Draco’*

I dropped my quill back into the well with a sigh as I eased back in my chair. A glance sideways at Harry told me he’d read enough to get the gist, because he was blushing guiltily down at his textbook. But must’ve felt me looking, because he peered sheepishly over at me. I lifted my eyebrows and gestured to my letter, asking *what do you think?*

And he smiled. Uncurled his shoulders a bit and nodded. Mouthed *‘love you.’*

*“Love you, Harry,”* I whispered back as I leaned forward onto my elbows over the desk.

*Never knew how much I was capable of with this organ. How much of a handicap it was without it.*

*I need Harry like a fucking lung.*

---

After class let out, Draco was *blistering* to go mail off his letter. Of course, I bid him off, and he dropped a *sweet* fucking kiss to my lips before he whirled with all his shit and *bolted.* I snickered under my breath as I followed the easier tide of my friends back to the Great Hall.

I happened to catch Ron’s eye as we left, and he gave me this pointed sort of look, gestured with his chin, and I followed to see Hermione chatting amiably with Luna. *Distract her,* I took it to mean. And sent him back a firm nod.

She was already well distracted with Luna as he paced off in an unknown direction into the castle, but when Neville stole her away from Mione, I dropped back to walk with her.

*Fuck. Shit. Now’s the time to draw up my inner-Slytherin, I suppose. Was meant to be a Slytherin anyway, no big deal.*

“Hey,” I began. *Fuck me.* She eyed me suspiciously, but wound an arm around mine.

“Think you’ll pass your NEWTs?”

*Of course,* ever the academic, her first topic was fucking *schoolwork.* I had to snort.

“I’ll have you know I *am* actually working in class. Besides, who’s gonna fail the *Chosen One?”*

She slapped my chest, *right* on the scar she didn’t know I had, and I winced.

“Rely too much on your *hero status* and see where you end up.” A hesitant laugh as I rubbed at where she’d hit me. “But nevermind that, are you…? Is something going on I should know about?”

*She’s been hanging around Luna too much,* I realised as her bluntness stunned me silent. Could only gape at her as I grappled for an excuse, *any* excuse for my weirdness since learning all Ron had planned. *Hurry the fuck up, Ron!*

“E-erm… W-well…” My head *raced* as I tried to come up with something to say, and suddenly remembered how Draco was sprinting to the Owlery tower. “D-Draco’s invited me to go to France with his mum over the holidays, and I’m just… Sort of vexed over how to break the news to Ron.”

Hermione slumped slightly against me, features rearranging themselves from smug to… *Disappointed?*

“Oh. Well.” She cleared her throat, reset her shoulders as I suppose she *contended* with the idea that I wasn’t anxious about her *surprise birthday party. Holy shit, have I just successfully pulled of a lie…?* “I’m sure Molly and the rest of them will be disappointed, but… We *do* have every Christmas for the rest of our *lives,* so I’m sure you missing one will be fine.” She sniffed. *Definitely disappointed. Holy shit.*“Can’t imagine Ginny’ll be too disappointed. She might invite Dean if you’re not there.”

A cackle left me, and I tried to cover it in a cough into my fist. Picturing *Dean Thomas* in the midst of the chaos that was the *Weasleys* was just… *Phenomenal.* Honestly, I hoped he’d go.

“Yeah… That’d be a *riot.”*

Hermione echoed my cackle, and we just set each other *off. God,* it felt so good to laugh with Mione. Like old times, like *normal.* Like any semblance of normal I’d ever clung to in the midst of my insane fucking life.

“Yeah,” she agreed. And then sobered suddenly, glanced sideways at me. “Doesn’t bother you, does it?”

I smiled at her. “Nah, she’s brilliant. I mean, if Dean ever does her dirty, he’ll find out what a *war hero’s* capable of, but…” Hermione’s hesitant eyes turned admonishing, and I grinned. “She deserves happiness.”

Her scolding frown eased back into a smile.

“Yeah. God, if he and Seamus ever end up together, I’m gonna be *rich.”*

I fucking *lost it.*
