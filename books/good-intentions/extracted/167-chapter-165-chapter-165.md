The group had a few questions about the soulbond, which we tacitly answered before I sent a hesitant petition to our token bookworm.

*If anyone can find something, it’s Hermione.*

“Mione, would you actually…”

Her eyes *sparkled* with intrigue as she vanished her quill and parchment.

“Already on it.” She smacked a kiss to Ron’s cheek, stood, and was racing out of the Great Hall before I could even complete the thought. And Neville scooted closer to replace the absence she’d created.

“So it’s like… *Full-sentences* mind-reading? Like *all the time?”*

“Yep,” Draco answered him. “Thoughts, feelings, I can even access a bit of Harry’s raw magic.”

*“Wicked,”* Seamus breathed, and I was surprised to see *Ginny* leaning around Luna to join in.

“You mean *you*can do the… The silent, wandless thing, too?"

I glanced up just in time to see Draco shooting her a smirk before he opened his opposite hand before us, and I felt him drawing on my core. Pulsing from my soul into his, and I didn’t doubt it stoked the glow in my eyes as his lit similarly. Not a breath later, though, little blue sparks like tiny *fireworks* erupted from the ends of his upturned fingertips, earning appreciative coos and *ahhs* from our audience.

“Have you declared to the Ministry yet?” Ginny wondered, and earned the table’s attention as Draco’s sparks fizzled out. “I mean, since you were married in France. I dunno anything about our *soulbond* laws, but if you haven’t already heard from them, I think you might.”

It was sort of curious to me that she should bring this up, and I eyed her a moment as I considered her interest in the *Ministry.* *Could that be where she seeks a career?*

“Shit, we probably should. Don’t want a repeat of Christmas morning.” An elbow gently jostled my side as I swept my gaze back to Draco.

*Such drama we’ll cause, waltzing through the Ministry hand-in-hand to declare our marriage.*

*Not to mention our soulbond,* he shot back with a grin.

An image arose in our joint imaginations of just such a thing, and it was with a little flutter of *glee* that I realised causing waves and being the centre of the media’s attention no longer *bothered* me. Eyes on us were *welcome,* because I *wanted* everyone to know how in love we were. How *immutable* our bond was, and as warmth swelled in Draco’s chest, I realised we were doing that *silent staring* again, and he chuckled.

“S’pose we’re to expect *more* of this… Unnerving behaviour?” Ron wondered as he munched on a chip. And we finally looked away from each other to respond.

*“Unsettling* was the term mother used,” Draco supplied, and I coughed into my opposite hand. “We’re… Still sort of getting *used* to it, but at least brace yourself for how *obnoxiously* in love we’re about to be.”

This earned a cackle from me, and both Seamus and Neville *groaned.*

“Bloody hell, though. I think I’d go *mad* if I had *this one* in my head at all times,” the former nudged Dean good-naturedly, and was met with a smirk. One that somehow earned a *violent* flush on Seamus’s cheeks as Draco snorted.

“‘S not for everyone, for sure,” he continued, and pulled my hand up to his lips to press a kiss against my knuckles. “But *we’re* suited *very*well.”

The conversation drifted away from our soulbond as questions were levied against Ron, concerning the wedding. *A party* that everyone was excited for. But when we were unable to come up with many answers about *ours,* I started to feel a subtle pull to bed. And Draco was way ahead of me.

We excused ourselves politely, offering well-wishes for the NEWTs before escaping away up to the dorms. And just how little time we had left had my chest tightening as we strolled through the familiar eighth year common room.

*There’s no chance you’d want to become a professor here, is there? Reckon you’d be the only one who could break the curse on the Defense position, honestly.*

I shot Draco a half-hearted smirk as we ascended the stairs to the boy’s hall.

*I’ll probably end up accepting an invitation or two to come teach a masterclass, but full-time? I’m not gonna go ahead and full send a yes, I want at least a few years to myself after fucking everything.*

He hummed in assent as we sought out our room, and it felt like a *lifetime* ago since we’d been here. In the room where we’d shared our first kiss, our first fuck, where I’d *declared* him my *boyfriend.* How different a life it seemed now with him woven into my *soul.*

*You said you wanted to put as much good into the world as you could. What does that look like for you?* he wondered as I released his hand to approach the window. Stared out into the cold night, the glittering lights of the castle reflected by the stars in the sky. Less than a week before this was no longer *my school.*

*I dunno. I’m still… Learning where my boundaries are as far as giving, but… I suppose, if ever there were another threat that was beyond the ministry’s capabilities, I would lend my services. If they wanted me to train new aurors or something, I dunno if they’d even let me, but… I’d like to create a self-sufficient world that doesn’t need a saviour. I won’t be around forever, after all.*

The bed creaked behind me as Draco sat, and I heard the familiar sounds of undressing as he stripped for bed. I touched my forehead to the cold glass, and peered down, recalling how I’d contemplated throwing myself *out* of that window when I’d realised I’d come to *like him.* Again, it felt like a *past life.* Like a memory of a *dream,* anything before loving him. Draco was a turning point, a fixed moment in my life that converted my life into *before* him and *after* him. And as I rotated to lean back against the glass, arms folded over my stomach, I saw he was stripping off his jumper, having already rid himself of his shoes and socks.

*Too noble for your own bloody good,* he teased as his face was revealed again. And I snorted as he dropped his jumper to the floor. Sighed heavily and pushed off the window to undress myself similarly.

“I’m *sure* everyone’s expecting me to go for auror, and it’d make *sense,* with all the-the *field experience* I have, hunting down evil wizards. But it’s…” I paused to consider as I pulled my own jumper up over my head. “Fucking *exhausting,* honestly. Not like every evil wizard is on *Riddle’s* level, but…” A cold wash of *shock* fed down the soulbond at hearing me use Voldemort’s given name so casually, but I couldn’t be bothered to amend myself. “It’s also not like they’ll need *my help* with run-of-the-mill ne’er-do-wells. I trust the ministry to handle the small fries, and if they ever find someone big and bad enough to call on *me,* well…”

Draco gave me a half-smile as he unbuttoned his trousers and lifted to feed them down his legs.

“You’ll hate them so hard their *heads*will explode?”

Embarrassment flushed my cheeks as I began unbuttoning the shirt beneath my jumper.

“R-right… Er, something like that.” He chuckled under his breath as he scooted back up on the bed, pulling the covers out from beneath him to wriggle under. “Real fuckin’ *elegant* with that one, wasn’t I?”

My own trousers came away easily, having already toed my shoes and socks off as I joined him.

“You never really *explained* how you got away with that. I mean, *sure,* you didn’t use your *wand,* but it’s still fucking *murder.* Not that I’m *complaining*, but… Didn’t–”

“I-I think…” I swallowed, accepting the invitation beneath the covers as Draco held them away for me. “The, erm… The *aurors* that found us likely defended my actions as *self-defense.* It was McGonagall, Ron and Hermione, and a few of the Ministry’s that found us, s-so they… Y’know, they *saw* what had happened, and… Their word must’ve been reliable enough that they were *believed.*I… I’ve had the same thought, and it’s the only thing that makes sense.”

Draco hummed in the neutral as I let him throw the covers over us, and I nestled into his side. Familiar, *home,* as his mind fragmented and then came back together to take in this knowledge.

*God, I hope they’re not unkeen to our soulbond. I know we were abroad when it solidified, but I’ve gotta be on some sort of watch list with how I did all that wandlessly. And now with you accessing that power…*

He pulled me closer into his arms, and pressed a kiss to my hair.

*Please, please fret about it.* I chuckled, anxiety easing slightly. *Oh no, will they send their own saviour to Azkaban? Will they force us to break the soulbond? Oh dear, oh golly, oh my.*

More laughter spilled from my lips as he *teased* me, and I gave a retaliatory *bite* into his chest, which earned a *hiss* and a sudden *flash* of desire as my mouth softened to *suck.* Even just feeling his skin between my teeth invited the memory of him all *decorated* in love-bites, and I felt a spark of desire light in me to answer his.

*Not much longer we’ll have this room. Should make memories in these walls before they disappear.*

Slurping off my mouthful of his skin, I sent a mischievous smirk up at him.

*What did you have in mind?*
