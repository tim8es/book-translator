## Chapter 116: Chapter 116

I was in and out all day. Kept being woken up by Pomfrey to be fed another dose of *revolting* blood-replenishing potion, chased by water, and then back under. When awareness finally seemed to take, it was dark outside. And a hand was loosely laced with mine.

Thankfully, I wasn’t in pain anymore. Still *unbearably* weak, but as my stomach twisted into knots, I chalked it up to hunger. I hadn’t eaten since breakfast, after all.

Peering up at the ceiling, a bright light before me drew my blinking attention, and I lifted my aching head. There, on my chest and lap, laid a curled-up, glowing *stag.* As if my waking roused it from sleep, it twisted its head up towards me. And I smiled.

*His Patronus waited for me to wake up.* Seeing me breathing and conscious, it rose onto its legs, gave a *long* stretch, and lept to the side. I let my head roll to follow it as it sank into Harry’s chest. He was soundly asleep in a pulled-up chair, neck at an awkward angle against his shoulder and arm resting against the armrest to hold my hand. And I smiled wider.

The next thing my eyes lighted on was a plate on the bedside table. A napkin had been laid over it, so it was likely cold, but my stomach *screeched* at the sight. So it was with careful precision I pried my hand out of his, and scooted back up against the pillows.

*He killed all those people,* Mione’d said. I eyed Harry’s sedate, lax expression, and what I’d thought to be a *fever dream* suddenly resurfaced behind my eyes with startling clarity. Harry, eyes *glowing* and… Fucking *dripping* blood.

*Jesus Christ.*

It wasn’t an image I thought I’d *ever* forget now that I remembered. He could’ve ended their lives cleanly, painlessly. But he must’ve *torn them to shreds,* the way he’d been *soaked through.* I put the image from my mind somewhat unsuccessfully as I reached for the plate. Plucked the napkin off and my mouth *salivated* at what laid beneath.

A fine array of all my *favourite foods* waited, likely cold on the porcelain. Asparagus almondine, twice-baked potato (with all my favoured toppings), and a *messy* fucking brisket sandwich. And set beside the plate, innocent enough, was… My throat went tight.

A *blueberry muffin.* With all the blueberries painstakingly removed. My eyes flicked back to Harry’s face, still blissfully asleep as emotion *swelled* in my chest. I coughed the *quietest* hack I could as I reached out to take the plate in trembling hands. Nearly didn’t *make it* back to my chest as I sat straighter against the headboard, but a fork was already laid out on the plate, and I dug in.

My throat was almost *too* tight to allow the first mouthful down, but as soon as my stomach registered *food*, it was easy. I *inhaled* what’d been laid out for me. My stomach ached at the intrusion, but it was welcome. And once I set my empty plate back onto my side table, Harry stirred.

Groaned in his sleep, and tried to shift. But the position of the chair he was in must’ve been uncomfortable, because he lifted his head. Smacked his lips, and blinked. And a smile pulled at my lips.

Bleary eyes found mine, and went *vivid.*

“Draco,” he croaked. Coughed into his fist and returned *rapt*attention to my face.

“I’m alright,” I said, similarly raw. He reached for me again, and I permitted a hand to grasp. Gripped his back, and smiled wider. “Two out of three Deathly Hallows must do the trick.”

Harry choked on a laugh, and leaned over to kiss me. I met him, of course, but my movements felt all… *Slow.* *Meager* as I kissed him back. And when he finally pulled away, I struggled to focus on his face.

Pain sang in his green eyes as they danced up to my brow. With hesitant hands, he reached up to unband my head. And I tilted my neck forward as much as I could to let him.

When he finally tore the bandage free, a strangled sort of cut-off sob choked out of him. As he dropped the length of fabric to the floor, trembling fingers reached for my brow. Delicately pushed my hair back, and those green eyes went *wet.*

*“Fuck,”* he breathed, and I swallowed. My brow wasn’t in any pain anymore, but seeing Harry so *distraught…*

“Said he wanted us to *match,”* I said quietly as Harry’s thumb stroked the corner of my forehead. His own brow tightened as he smoothed out my eyebrow, and my eyes drifted shut at the *bliss* of him touching me.

“I-I’m… I’m *so sorry,”* he croaked, swallowed noisily as I forced my eyes back open.

“Doesn’t hurt,” I replied quietly, but I knew what he meant. He was sorry this *happened.* Sorry he *let it happen.* And suddenly, my curiosity got the better of me. “What… What did you do?”

Absent anything like *accusation,* I wondered aloud. And Harry’s eyes went *vacant.* Suddenly fucking *empty* as he lowered himself back onto his chair.

“You want to know?” he asked, as if this might be a question I *shouldn’t ask.* But there was *nothing* I didn’t want of him, no facet I was *afraid of,* so I nodded. And he swallowed. “I… I followed them. Found you, tied to that chair and… *Bleeding.”* The vacancy in Harry’s eyes guttered, but he went on. “This… *Guy,* tried to talk to me, but his voice was annoying so I made him shut up. And he… I guess he *ch-choked?* Made him shut up too hard, and he… Erm, *collapsed.”*

*Jesus fucking Christ, I told them, I bloody* told *them…!*

“And then I spoke to Theo. His… His last words aren’t worth repeating, but he started to, like… Have a n-*nosebleed,* and then he collapsed, too.” Harry’s vacant gaze snapped onto mine then with such *clarity,* such *lethal*fucking attention that I went still. “And the rest, I just… Hated so hard their heads exploded.”

I blinked. Blinked again as Harry watched me. Slowly but surely, his vital observation softened to something like *wariness,* as I contended with this knowledge.

“You… *Hated* them *so hard…* Their h-*heads exploded?”* He nodded gravely. And I coughed a scoff. “H-how the fuck are you *here?”*

A corner of his lips twitched upwards, and I breathed slightly easier to see him *smile.*

“They Wandered me. Got off on a technicality, because *technically,* I didn’t use *any* spells. Didn’t break any *Wizard Laws.”* Harry’s smile curled wider into a grin as he went on. “Though I’m fairly sure Kingsley bent the laws in my favour.”

My head fell back against the headrest, eyes going slightly fuzzy as I wrapped my mind around this. *We’re… Alright? There’s no repercussions from this…?*

A hand reached for my face again, and delicately brushed my hair off my brow. And I was obliged to turn a fluttering glance back over at Harry, who looked *murderous.*

“Wish I could kill him again,” he said barely above a whisper. And I swallowed.

“I-is it *that bad?”*

Green eyes twitched down to mine, and the smile he offered me then looked fairly *forced.*

“I… I couldn’t h-*heal* this,” he answered just as quietly. Attention drifting back up to my brow as he traced a thumb against it again. “Too deep.”

My own hand lifted to feel the subtle rift of the scar across my brow.

“Show me.”

Harry twisted to seize a hand mirror off the next bed’s bedside table, and returned to press it into my waiting hand. And I brought it up to my face.

*Couldn’t heal it, my arse,* I mused as I prodded the angry red line with my other hand. By all rights, it *looked* like it should hurt, but as my eyes danced down the length from the left side of my forehead, through an eyebrow and down that cheek, I… I was *pleased.*

*We do match,* I realised as I traced its length across my eye. Thankfully, the socket hadn’t been grazed and I was seeing just as easily as I ever had, but to think this would be on my face *forever…* My vanity both wailed and *rejoiced* at my reflection.

“Bloody hell,” I managed in a whisper as I studied the scar. “Look like a fuckin’ *badass,* don’t I?”

A grin stretched my lips as I returned my attention to Harry’s face. And though I found it to be sombre, my words must’ve made a difference, because he smirked at me.

“Sure,” was his quiet answer. And I carefully set the mirror onto my lap.

“I *like it,”* I insisted, because if I’d have to wear Theo’s resentment every day for the rest of my life, I might as well enjoy it. I *matched Harry,* after all. And his smirk spread wider.

“I love you, Draco.”

So matter-of-fact, so *irrefutable* in the wake of the violence he’d wrought on my behalf, I felt my grin soften.

“I know.”
