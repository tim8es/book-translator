## Chapter 181: Chapter 181

Chapter Notes

“So, where will you two go?” Hermione wondered beside me as we lounged in the common room later that day. I eyed my other half where he sat apart, chatting in hushed tones to Ron as I considered how we both respectively felt about the places we’d once called *‘home.’* “Molly’s letting me stay at the Burrow as we look for a place of our own.”

My fingers idly found the subtly raised scar across my brow as she pulled her ankle beneath herself.

“To be honest, I’m not sure. I can’t picture him moving into the manor, however briefly, and he…” Glancing at the expectant witch as she twirled the ends of a curl between her fingers, I considered how much Harry would want her to know. “He’s got this… *Dream* that we’ll move into a cottage out in the country. A little private corner of the world. But until we find it, best guess? Grimmauld.”

Hermione hummed and twisted to peer over at our conversational boys. And just at that moment, Harry absently reached up to rub his eye only to collide with the lens of his glasses, and we both cracked a snort. He was a bit too involved in his current conversation regarding Ron’s potential career choice, but some subconscious corner of his mind might’ve picked up on my amusement, because his ears pinked.

“It’s none of my business, *at all,”* she began as she turned back towards me. *This should be good.* “So you don’t have to tell me, but I can’t help but be *curious…* You gave us the short and sweet version, but how the *hell* did you two end up together? Like, I *knew* he had a thing for you, and I know enough about *boys* to know they sometimes target the person they fancy, but in a *week flat?”*

I felt a smirk pull at my lips as I finally tore my fingers away from my scar.

“Well,” I sighed. “Guess my confession must’ve, er… *Softened* him somewhat, because he showed up at my hearing, you know. Found me on the train, came to my room, poor sod couldn’t work out *why* I fancied him. And… Seeing that sort of effort on his part, even though I was *convinced* it was because he wanted to *torture* me, kind of… I mean, I let myself *hope,* I guess. Agonising as it was, but he seemed to genuinely want to be closer to me, so I… Let him. Fuckin’ terrifying, but then he broke it off with Ginny, and he–”

My words died in my throat as I remembered how upset he’d been when he’d visited me after. Not to mention it wasn’t necessarily one of his finer moves, and Hermione and her sister-in-law were already thick as thieves, so I *might’ve* censored myself. A bit.

“Anyway, in the days that followed, I started picking up on little *hints* that he mightn’t have been straight for the first time. Or at least I let myself *acknowledge* the hints. I’d sort of resigned myself to breaking my own heart before he could get the chance by convincing myself there was *no way* he was gay, but then he… Er, I mean *I…”* Hermione’s eyes were *alight* with curiosity as I weighed the truth on my tongue. This one was a *glutton* for gossip, and the amusement of seeing her reaction eventually outweighed the risks that Harry would be upset with me for sharing… *Details.* So I leaned in conspiratorially, and she mirrored me as I lowered my voice to whisper: “I pinned him to a bookshelf in the library and told him to think about me *using his mouth.”*

Pure *satisfaction* for seeing her jaw drop like that. *God,* the things I could tell this woman.

“You *didn’t,”* she hissed as I leaned back, spread my arms across the back of the couch as a chuckle burst out of me.

“I guess you could say it was a sort of… *Experiment* at the time. Prod the gay in him and see if it responded.” My eyes drifted back over to the back of his head as he scratched behind his ear, like he could *feel* me watching. But still wasn’t paying much attention to our conversation. “And *god,* did it *ever.”*

A bemused sort of giggle bubbled through Hermione’s still gaping lips as she leaned back again. Her eyes were a bit vacant, like she could *picture* just how that night had gone down. But when their attention snapped back onto me, my chest went sort of tight as *pride* of all things gripped my heart in a fist.

“And then what happened?” she prodded, and I worried my lip for a breath before yielding.

“A bit more back and forth, and then… Harry kissed me.” If I had a mug of tea, I’d’ve taken a *very* self-satisfied sip as the witch gave a short little frowning head-shake like she’d misheard me.

*“Harry* kissed *you?”* I dipped my chin in confirmation, a grin stretching my lips.

“Among other things.”

She clapped a hand over her mouth to stifle the sudden outburst my admission earned, and seeing her so scandalised just *begged* my pride to divulge the *one secret* I was *sure* I could get away with, so I leaned in again and lowered my voice.

“Do you want to know something I’m not supposed to *ever* mention?” Hand slowly lowering from her mouth, she eased back in to nod with bright eyes. Half a glance at Harry and he was still engaged in conversation, so I tried to whisper it as fast as I could before he caught onto what I was about to tell her: “After our first kiss, he *nearly–”*

*Not. Another. Word.* My voice caught in my throat, and I internally *thrashed* against Harry’s control, to no avail of course, but he did let me scoff. Hermione twisted to see him glaring daggers at me.

*Oh, what’s the harm? We’re married, she’s your best friend, you know she’d love this.*

“Harry?” Ron wondered as the most heart-stoppingly *beautiful*brunette stood slowly from his seat. Hermione clicked her tongue in disappointment.

“Oh *come on,* Harry! You never tell me *anything* about your relationship! Just because *Ronald* doesn’t have the stomach for it doesn’t mean *I don’t.”*

Still forbidding my speech, he stalked across the common room, eyes honed in on me like a *threat.* A wild little burst of adrenaline shocked my heart at that look, but not… Not in a bad way.

“Good for you, but unfortunately, I promised*this one* I’d *kill* him if he ever told anyone what he was about to tell you.”

He finally came to a halt at the edge of my knee, and I met the warning in his eyes without a lick of shame.

*You could never kill me.*

*If you tell her, I’ll tell her how you proposed.*

Any semblance of a smirk I still wore vanished off my face. Slowly, *very* slowly, I felt my throat loosen, my tongue my own again, and Hermione’s insistence of *‘how bad could it be?’* was a distant backdrop to our staring contest. Harry was carefully following my internal battle as I weighed the pros and cons of exposing my *horrendously* cheesy proposal with the confession of his reaction to my kiss, because if *both* secrets came out, we’d *both* be in for a *lifetime* of teasing, but just seeing how serious Harry was reminded me of just how *proud* I was to be his partner, and if the whole bloody *world* knew how absolutely *gone* we were for each other–

“Nearly passed out,” I exhaled in a rush, and the silence that followed was *deafening* as Harry’s cheeks turned a *lovely* shade of scarlet.

*Bastard!*

“What, *literally?”* Hermione wondered, glancing between the two of us. And I quirked an eyebrow at my better half, silently calling his bluff. He gaped at me a moment too long, though, and I sent a smirk back at the witch.

“Went pale and woozy, the whole bit.”

“You–!” He sucked in a gasp, and spared both his best friends a glance. *“H-he* proposed in front of the bloody Eiffel Tower!” A finger jabbed accusingly in my direction, and I pursed my lips. “In the snow! On-on fucking *Christmas Eve!”*

“I need to lie down,” Ron groaned quietly as he did just that. And Hermione dissolved into *hysterics.*

*“You* initiated the *s**oulbond,”* I shot back, and he flapped his lips at me again.

“You *cried* when you met my *parents!”*

“You called my mum *mum!”*

“Wh– I– *Sh-she–!”* Harry ran an aggravated hand through his hair. “Y-you said ‘I love you’ first!”

“Because I *loved you first!”* I insisted, angling towards him as warmth rushed into my own cheeks.

Our back-and-forth came to a halt just then, and I was suddenly hair-raisingly aware of Hermione’s attention on me. So I looked back at her.

“You met his parents?”

*Oh. Shit.*

*Shit.*

I cleared my throat. “Y-yeah. Yeah, I did.”

Ron sat up slowly from where he lay on his couch.

“You kept the Stone?”

Guilt swelled in Harry as he met the ginger’s gaze, but just as quickly shut it down.

“I-I did. Ron, listen to me, it’s not some *miracle* that’ll allow you to regain lost loved ones, it’s an instrument of *torture.* You don’t want it, I *promise*you.”

“The *fuck* do you think you know about what I want?” he demanded, rising to his feet. I loosed a sigh as I exchanged another glance with the ginger’s fiancée. “Is that part of saviour status, knowing what’s best for everyone else?”

“Ron–”

“You’ve had it *all this time,* and never *once* did you think maybe *I–”*

“Of *course* I did! Of course I did! That’s *why* I never said anything!” Harry turned to face Ron more directly, and his swelling desperation swooped through me at *nauseating* speed. “Do you think I kept this from you out of *spite?* Out of *resentment?* You’re my *best friend,* Ron, and I *know* you. I know you’d use it once and let it consume you, hell, *I* only used it because, one, I was about to *die,* and two, I don’t even know my *fucking parents!”*

If Harry’s suddenly *intense* emotions hadn’t washed over me like my *own,* I might’ve flinched at the way his voice rose, just like Hermione did. The love of my life was at his limit, though, so I stood. Gently laced my fingers through his and pulled him towards the stairs, both by the hand and by the soul. And he reluctantly let me.

The last glimpse I cast over our shoulders as we left was Hermione cautiously approaching a simply *aghast* Ron as I led us back up to our room.

*Fuck, what’ve I done?*
