## Chapter 141: Chapter 141

Chapter Notes

Harry lead me from the room at a fucking *march.* Hand tight around mine, and my longer legs kept pace easily, but he still had to drag me because I wasn’t nearly *half* as eager for this confrontation as he was. I hadn’t been teasing him when I requested he refrain from *murder.* But to know he would go to *war* for me, even against the French *bureaux…* My throat tightened as he pulled me through the villa, right to the sitting room.

Sure enough, the doors burst open before us to reveal mother still in her robe, untangling curlers from her hair and sat across from two women in bureaux uniforms. Silver emblems pinned to their chests as they stood to face us.

Harry finally stopped, pure *seething* rage, as the two women exchanged a glance.

“Monsieur Potter, Monsieur Malfoy… Apologies for interrupting your holiday,” one of them began in a thick French accent. “Believe me, we don’t want to be here any more than you want us here, but…” Another glance between the two of them as mother finally gave up with a tangled curler and shifted on the couch to watch the proceedings with icy eyes.

“The fuck is going on?” Harry demanded, and I tried to send *peace* to calm him down, but couldn’t summon my own from beneath the weight of my dread.

“Why don’t you come sit down,” the same woman invited us to the couch where mother sat, and she agreeably scooted to one side. With an internal *huff,* Harry pulled me further into the room and dropped onto the cushion, never letting go of my hand but leaning decidedly more towards *irritated* than *livid.*

The two women sat again, and the one who had yet to say a word pulled a briefcase from the floor to set flat across the coffee table between us.

“Are you two aware of French laws regarding soulbonds?”

The entire *world* flipped on its head as Harry and I both went *rigid* on the couch.

*What?*he wondered.

*There’s no way, those aren’t real, or if they ever were, it was centuries ago.*

“N-no,” I volunteered, mind still *reeling* to catch up. Seemingly the *spokeswoman* for the bureaux scratched an eyebrow as she appraised us.

“I see. Laws differ country to country, but in *France,* there is a… A lengthy *legal* process a pair must complete before permitted to form a soulbond. To prevent, er… *Enslavement.”*

A spark of *humour* lit in Harry at that, but he didn’t laugh. Just eased back a bit in his seat, and I didn’t have to ask why he found that *funny.*

“Are… Are you saying *we…?”*

The woman glanced down at our clasped hands, and dipped her chin in a nod.

“Oui. Sometime around two in the morning, our wards picked up the creation of an unauthorised soulbond. Some, er… *Red tape* delayed our visit, but we have been sent here to confirm consent, to ascertain the circumstance, and possibly begin the paperwork.”

She ticked off three fingers as she listed their purpose, and turned them down to indicate the briefcase once she landed on *paperwork.*

*Holy fucking shit,* Harry thought as his anger evaporated.

*We’re… We’re soulbound?*

He coughed an incredulous chuckle as he glanced sideways at me, a grin quirking his lips and my heart *leapt* in my chest.

*Jesus Christ.*

“Sorry, I…” He turned back to the bureaux agents, just fucking *elated* with the knowledge that I was still processing. “We had no *idea.* I’ve… I’ve never even *heard* of s-soulbonds before.”

The two exchanged another glance, the silent one raising her eyebrows as if to say *‘your call.’*

“It’s very, er… *Unprecedented* for two people to create one unassisted. Much like marriage, there is typically an officiant, someone to cast the necessary spells at the appropriate intervals over a period of months. This is the department we represent, and… While I see you two are *engaged,* there are still a few measures we must employ to ensure this soulbond is mutually agreeable. Oui?”

Mother breathed a quiet French exclamation, but I wasn’t really listening. Harry and I… *That’s* *what's been happening. He’s been knitting our souls together.*

He glanced back over at me.

*Is that… Alright?*

I caught his glance for a moment before dropping my gaze down to our clasped hands. The ring on my finger. *Not just husbands. Soulbound.*

My heart swelled so full I thought it’d *burst* in my chest, and Harry sucked in a little gasp.

“Yeah. Yeah, just… Tell us what to do.”

“Bien.” She clicked open the briefcase and withdrew… I didn’t *know* what, but it looked like a weird, long plate with two runes carved into it on opposite ends. “Les mains, s'il vous plaît.”

She gestured a *come-hither* curl of her fingers with both hands, and I reached my unoccupied one back. Harry mirrored me, and she took them both, set them on the runes and withdrew her wand. A spell I’d never heard before, thick with her accent crossed her lips, and the runes beneath our hands glowed *green.*Green as Harry’s eyes as she slipped her wand back up her sleeve.

“Parfait,” she approved as she took the device from beneath our hands, and we eased back on the couch in unison. “This is just to confirm both parties are willing. Should be used at every casting over the extent of the process, but c'est ça. Since there is consent, and it was, as you say, an *accident,* this will suffice.”

As she spoke, she withdrew a stack of parchment from the briefcase and replaced the plate back into it. Leafed through the stack and set down three before both of us, as well as quills that had been tucked into the sleeve against the briefcase’s lid.

*We’re soulbound* echoed in my spinning head, and I doubted I’d be able to *read,* much less read *French* right now, but I’d sign *anything* if it meant I got to keep Harry in my head. In my heart, my *soul.*

“It’s a lot of reading, but in short, you are both of sound mind and body, oui?” We nodded. “This is a voluntary choice for both parties?” Another nod. “A few signatures anywhere you see a dotted line, and we can all resume our holiday as planned.”

She smiled pleasantly at us, and *holy fucking shit,* this was so much *better* than I’d feared it would be. Harry and I both reached for our respective quills in unison, *still* holding hands as we leaned forward to sign our *souls to each other.*

“Dieu merci, je veux rentrer à la maison,” the other woman muttered under her breath, and I coughed a laugh.

“Oui mais,” the first one answered. “If you like… We are both registered officiants for soulbonds, but I also happen to have the paperwork for marriage if you’d like to just, er… How is it, kill two birds with one rock?”

Harry and I froze at the same time, quills stilling as we looked back up at her. She was just smiling invitingly, hands clasped on her lap as she glanced back and forth between us. We shared a glance of our own, but didn’t even need to *think* our mutual agreement before returning our attention to the bureaux woman.

“Yes, please,” he answered for us.

“Très bien. It’s basically the same job, though it’s very rare we get to see any new soulbonds these days.”

We resumed our mutual signing as she reached back into the pocket where the quills had come from. I could *feel* mother *brimming* with the urge to pounce on us, but how fucking *appropriate* that she should be our only other witness to this signing.

*Merry Christmas, mother, here’s that other son I promised you,* I chuckled to myself, and *Harry* chuckled out *loud.* The more *chatty* woman sighed pleasantly.

“J'aime l'amour, non?” A grunt from the quieter one, and it earned her a *tsk* and an elbow in the side. “Arrêtez, vous aimez ce travail.”

“Moi aussi j'aime dormir,” was her muttered answer, and I *heartily* agreed.

“Désolé,” I murmured back as I traded the soulbond contract for the *marriage* one. And the second woman stiffened on the couch to the tune of the first one’s stifled chuckle. “Joyeux noël, Mademoiselle.”

A little *flutter* in Harry’s heart had me smirking at him, but he was studiously signing his name with ears like cherry blossoms.

“Joyeux noël, et félicitations,” as I slid both contracts back across the table along with my quill. And I smiled at her.

“Merci.”
