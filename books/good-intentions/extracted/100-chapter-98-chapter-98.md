## Chapter 98: Chapter 98

Chapter Notes

Stepping into Muggle London was… Well, like a different world. Nobody gawked at us, nobody even seemed to *notice* us as we appeared on a busy sidewalk. As if the Leaky Cauldron were just another pub behind us, not a portal to a world of magic. Harry just stood with me a moment, letting me take it all in, before he was tugging at my arm insistently with a

“Come on!”

I stumbled along, eyes still drinking in the tall buildings, the cars rushing by, the busy workaday Muggles traversing the sidewalks and risking their lives crossing the street. Talking into little handheld contraptions, walking in and out of bookshops and cafes and too many details for me to drink in. By the third bodily collision and muttered apology, I redirected my attention to *where he was taking me.*

My eyes landed on a tall structure at the end of the street that seemed to be his destination, and as we came to the end of the buildings, an enormous plaza opened up before us. Dominated by some sort of monument that reached taller than any of the buildings and bracketed by two large fountains behind it. I was all at once *astounded* that Muggles could accomplish such feats of architecture *without magic.*

We came to a halt on the corner, and waited to cross the street towards it, and that’s when Harry’s giddiness started to infect me. He was just *so pleased* to be carting me through the city that he was *giggling,* and I felt a grin of my own pulling at my lips. It wasn’t until we reached the edge of one of the fountains that Harry finally stopped to sit against it. Felt like an *idiot* for grinning so much, but was helpless to resist as I joined him.

The world just… Moved on around us. No stares, no whispers, and I inhaled deeply of the fresh air. Aromas of street food and trees, *water* registered in my nostrils and as I released a heavy sigh, I realised Harry had done the same thing because he sighed in unison with me. We shot each other grins, and I was compelled to pull him into my side.

“Isn’t this nice?” he asked, and I hummed, jaw resting against his head.

“Very.”

It suddenly made perfect sense why he wanted to come here. With everyone none the wiser about who we were, it felt like we could just… *Exist.* And even if I felt a little odd being in an environment purely devoid of magic, there was an irrefutable cluster of it in my arms. And Harry was *more* than enough.

“What do you wanna do?” he asked after several glorious moments of bathing in the sunshine. In the *normalcy.*

“You’re the one raised by Muggles, you’re the expert.” He hummed contemplatively and nuzzled his head further into me, arms banding around my waist. A few passersby did shoot us strange looks, but it wasn’t shock or recognition. It occurred to me they were likely just reacting to two blokes being affectionate, and for that, they could all kiss my arse.

“Well… There *is* a museum behind us that I’ve never been to. Supposed to have *loads* of paintings.”

I felt an eyebrow quirk in amusement.

“What interest do you have in *paintings?”*

He twisted a little scoff up at me, and I released him to grin back down at his performative offence.

“I like art!” A little tug at one corner of my lips as Harry yielded. “Well… It’s something to do that I’ve never done.”

My smile blossomed in full as I leaned down to kiss him. And just like *always,* he melted into me. I soaked him up like a fucking *sponge.*

“Alright, let’s go look at some art.”

---

The queue to get into the National Gallery was… Long. Chock full of tourists, but I was still buzzing with excitement. Had my wad of cash all primed in my pocket only to find it was free admission, and before long, we were *surrounded* by art. Most of it was nice to look at, but Draco seemed positively *enthralled.* So I spent most of the time watching him.

I was content to just exist in his orbit, trading hands every so often as he led me from piece to piece. A few he spent *several* minutes admiring, and curiosity to see what he found so interesting was the only thing that drew my attention away from his rapt expression. He was undeniably the most *beautiful* thing in the whole museum. In the whole *world.*

*Christ, I’m on a bloody museum date with my boyfriend. I’m so fucking happy I feel like I’m gonna be sick.*

Several hours in, we both sort of simultaneously realised we were *famished.* Luckily, the Gallery had a handful of restaurants to choose from. All relatively high class, but I was eager to blow some of the cash I’d withdrawn and the food looked *incredible,* so as we settled into a table, I didn’t even *look* at the menu prices.

It was difficult to choose just one dish because it all looked so good, but when I glanced up at Draco to ask him what he was getting, he was just… Leaning his chin in his palm and smiling at me. Warmth rose to my cheeks as I returned his smile, and then he was extending his free hand for mine, pulling it in like a magnet.

“I love you,” he said softly, thumb stroking the back of my hand. Even after *weeks*of hearing those words, they still sent my heart flipping in my chest.

“I love you, too,” I answered easily as I set my menu down. Leaned across it on the other elbow and *lost myself* in those clear, grey eyes. Never had I ever felt so *consumed* in another person, so totally *possessed,* even Voldemort’s occupation in my mind was a pitiful comparison. Draco was in my very *soul,* and instead of a violation, it was… *Warm.* I’d never felt less alone.

Our lunch passed blissfully. The food was delicious, the company exquisite, and then we were wandering back out into the museum. Draco was eager to gorge himself on art, and I was only too happy to be his satellite. This precious sort of childlike *awe* crept into his features as he feasted on painting after painting, and I was reassured that this was a *wonderful* idea. Trancelike as he was led through the halls, he never once let go of my hand. And seeing him like this, *loving* him like this, I…

*I’m so fucking glad I didn’t die.*

It was an alien thought that accompanied a familiar feeling. One I’d been feeling more and more frequently the harder I fell for Draco, but the thought had never manifested so clearly. For the first time in my fucking *life,* I… I was *grateful to be alive.*

The notion, so foreign and earth-shattering a concept, dropped from the nape of my neck right down into my gut, plucking a single cord in my heart on its descent. Everything in me went sort of still as the clear, crisp note sent ripples throughout my body, out into the air *beyond,* and it was then I realised Draco was saying my name.

“Harry?” I blinked at him, all at once sort of *blind,* because I’d… I’d *made the room brighter.* Like the clouds had parted to let the sun in, but as I focused on his face, it went dim again.

“S-sorry,” I muttered, noting a few museum staff checking the light fixtures in the ceiling. Of course, I hadn’t *meant* to make the room brighter, but as Draco faced me more fully, concern in his eyes, I decided to confess the realisation I’d just come to with a swallow. “I-I just… Had a thought I’ve… Never had before.”

He waited expectantly, lifted a hand to stroke my jaw, and I felt my cheeks warm.

“What thought?”

Smiling, I breathed: “I’m so glad to be alive.” Draco’s pinch between his brows tightened even as the muscles around his eyes relaxed. A subtle little shift in expression as I went on. “I’m so lucky I survived long enough to get to fall in love with you.”

Those soft, grey eyes moistened as Draco’s lower lip curled. Valiantly trying not to cry, he lifted his other hand to take my jaw in both and lowered his forehead to mine. It was like peeling away the last coat of old paint off my soul in one smooth tear, from crown to soles, leaving me spotless and *new,* raw and fresh and *totally* unaccustomed to the feeling.

“That makes two of us,” he whispered, and I was anticipating it this time when he closed the space between our lips. Out here where no one knew my name, my face, my scar. Where all they saw were two people in *love,* and as he pressed a lingering, *profound* fucking kiss to my lips, all the lights in the ceiling *burst.*

A dozen screams finally interrupted our kiss as the staff began ushering us out, and Draco and I stifled giddy little chuckles as we followed the throng out. Arm in arm, heads bent together as we alternated giggling and telling the other to *‘shush,’* all the way back out into the sunshine.

“Oops,” I finally said as we sauntered down the steps. “Sorry for interrupting your perusing.”

He grinned at me, nearly back at the fountains, and spun me into another kiss. And another. And another and *another* until I was a cackling *mess* in his arms. When he finally released me, my cheeks *ached* from smiling so hard.

“You can interrupt me *anytime.”*
