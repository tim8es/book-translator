## Chapter 5: Chapter 5

I’d woken several times throughout the night, and as such, I was fucking exhausted for the first day of classes. Having skipped dinner probably didn’t help, but I shovelled down as much breakfast as I could manage, silently grateful the elves had prepared coffee for us eighth-years. Merlin knew I fucking needed it.

Especially with the way we were all led from the Room to the West Tower. My peers were equally groggy, and I couldn’t help shooting Malfoy a glance or two (or five… Maybe ten) as we followed along, but the way the classroom was set up… Being that it was only this year’s returnees, and we were sat alphabetically, I was naturally sat next to Malfoy.

*Fucking great,* I groaned internally as I settled into the desk to his right. He’d been studiously avoiding my gaze all morning, perhaps just not looking at me entirely, but the proximity was unavoidable. And even though we’d all been given individual assignments ranging from reading to essays and the Bloody Baron himself was drifting down the aisles to help with questions, I found myself glancing sideways at the blond more than once. More than twice. I couldn’t *help it.*

*“Stop it,”* he eventually hissed at me, a flush rising to his ears and cheeks. And all at once, rage swelled in me like a *tsunami.* After all, this was all *his fucking fault!* I was only trying to be nice to him after he’d unloaded the most unbelievable fucking admission onto me, and now he expected me to just forget and *move on? Not bloody likely.*

I leaned across the aisle to hiss back, “This term is only gonna be that much more intolerable if you keep acting like a *little shit!”*

He shot a glare sideways at me, but I was fuming. And my anger only slightly guttered when I saw the way his glare fractured, the way his throat turned red as he turned back to his Transfig textbook and angrily flipped a page.

“Don’t talk to me,” he eventually whispered.

“Don’t fucking *like me!”* I spat back, barely beneath a whisper.

The next look he sent me was bordering on livid. *“You-!”*

“Quiet back there!” The Baron called, and we hastily turned back to our work. “Eyes on your own work, please.”

I scowled down at my Potions worksheet, not really seeing the questions. *Why* was he still being a prick to me when he’d admitted to liking me?? If he knew he was shit at liking someone, couldn’t he at least pretend to be friendly? I couldn’t make heads or tails of it, but as the implications of him fucking *liking me* leaked through my anger, I felt a sort of toe-curling discomfort.

*He’s a dick to me because he likes me. He doesn’t want to like me, but he does. And he hates me for it.*

Despite the eddying remnants of my rage, a grin quirked my lips. If I just did some simple fucking mental math, I could work out that he was *flustered.* Either at my proximity or something else about me, it was too late. I already *knew* *he fancied me.* There was no going back.

I turned my grin over to him slyly, trying not to make it obvious I was looking at him. Seemed he was having just as hard a time focusing on his own work, though, because I saw him swallow just before he cast another wide-eyed glance back at me.

*“What?”* he whispered.

Casting my gaze about to make sure no one was keen on our hushed conversation, I leaned in again to say *exactly* what I knew he didn’t want to hear:

“You pretending to hate me is *painfully*fucking cute, you know that?”

Malfoy *choked.* Spluttered, set his quill down and I eased back into my seat as he drew the attention of a few curious onlookers. Returned my attention to my worksheet, casual as anything, but the grin was plastered on my face.

It was so fucked up, but teasing him was… *Fun.* I was having fun with Malfoy, for the first time in my life, and I didn’t feel the least bit bad about it. When he finally got his coughing fit under wraps, he cleared his throat thrice and the last swallow was *loud.*

*“Fuck you,”* he shot back in the silence that followed.

*“Ask nicely,”* I returned. And he was instantly on his feet.

The Bloody Baron turned his attention back towards him. A few others twisted to see him, too, as he went *blindingly* fucking red. Finally, I looked up at him, a smug grin on my face as he cast all his *‘loathing’* into one glance down at me.

“Bathroom.”

And he ran. *Again.*

Feeling quite proud of myself, I dedicated myself to the worksheet before me in earnest. The answers came with a bit of trouble, having just upended Malfoy’s entire *world,* but I couldn’t bring myself to regret it.

*He fancies me.*

---

I was going to*kill him.* I was going to *kill* the Saviour of the Wizarding World. And I didn’t even know if I’d get Azkaban for it. Maybe they’d just kill me.

*It’d be worth it,* I assured myself as I thrust open the boy’s bathroom door to stalk to the sink. My reflection was discouraging, but I didn’t spend more than a moment on it before running the faucet and splashing my face.

*‘Ask nicely!?’ Fucking git, he’ll pay for that…!* The brutal humiliation and desperate fucking anger all sputtered to a silence as I realised he… He’d been *flirting* with me. He was *flirting back.*

I lifted my dripping face back up to the mirror, and saw wide eyes, pink cheeks, and a shell-shocked expression.

*What the fuck??*

There was no fucking way, I reassured myself as I gave my face another splash, bending over the sink. *You’re feeling guilty about… Last night, and he’s just fucking tormenting you, there’s no way he actually–*

My hands paused at my face, water sloshing through my fingers as the quiet hiss of the faucet echoed in the acoustic bathroom.

*He knows. He fucking knows, I fucking told him–!* My hands found the rim of the sink suddenly as I lifted what turned out to be a very bewildered frown up to my reflection.

*He likes that I like him.* The notion sent me reeling, and I saw as much on my face. Watching my own reactions in real time became suddenly too much for me to deal with, and I spun around the sink to press my back against the wall beside it, sliding down hopelessly as I considered the consequences of my actions.

*He likes that I like him. Holy shit.* Is *he gay?? Most straight boys get the ick when they find out a poof likes them.*

*‘Likes’* my mind oh-so-helpfully chastised. I shut that down immediately.

*Yes, I* like *him,* I insisted. *And yes, he knows.* My knees folded up to meet my forehead as I mournfully wrapped my arms around them.

*Oh, Christ, it’s barely the first day of classes! If he plans on holding this over my head and teasing me every chance he gets–*

I wanted to*cry,* honestly. But my impromptu bathroom break was only valid for so long before the Baron sent someone to check on me. So, wiping my face with the bottom of my jumper, I stood. And reluctantly made my way back to class.

---

Seeing Malfoy in such a state was enough to satisfy me for the rest of the day, so I didn’t say anything else… *Untoward.* I just watched him, which was probably equally as irritating, but knowing that he was irritated because he *liked me…* I felt myself slipping *dangerously* close to latching onto that, which I *knew* wasn’t a good idea. Because Ron had made it *abundantly* clear that Ginny was feeling neglected, and I could only tell him I needed time so many times before it became an issue. I knew better than to piss off the sole female Weasley by now.

Come night, though, I found myself pacing in my room. I *didn’t want* Malfoy to hate me. I didn’t want things to go back to the way they were, even if there were layers to it I hadn’t understood. So around ten, I was out in the hallway that led to all the boy’s rooms and found the one marked *DM.* And only hesitated for a breath before I knocked.

A few moments later, the door swung inward to reveal a very confused Malfoy. The confusion only lasted a heartbeat, though, before it turned to dread. And I might’ve been overly conscious of seeing him in his pyjamas for the first time, but before he could object to my imposition–

“It does matter,” I said. He blinked at me. “It matters, even if you think it doesn’t.”

His confused frown turned into a scowl and he stepped back, silently inviting me in.

I hadn’t been in any of the *normal* dorms, so I was briefly entranced with the interior before the door clicked shut behind me, and suddenly it all felt so fucking *real.*Malfoy perched on the edge of his bed, arms folded over his stomach as he frowned at me.

“Explain.”

*This is such a stupid fucking idea. I shouldn’t have come here.*

“It matters because it’s *you.*” I didn’t know how else to explain it, only that yes, him fancying me was a *huge fucking deal.* Because it was him. Draco bloody Malfoy. “Changes everything.”

His icy scowl shuddered slightly, and he’d just parted his lips to respond, but I barrelled ahead.

“I’m sorry, alright?” His frown fully replaced with surprise, I swallowed and started again. “I-I said I didn’t want to make you uncomfortable and then I did just that. It was…”

My voice trailed off as I recalled just how *fun* it’d been to tease him. How rewarding it was hearing him choke and splutter. *Fuck.*

“It was…?”

One pale eyebrow arched curiously as he waited for me to continue.

“Fun.” I finally finished, swallowing again. His eyes tracked the movement of my throat pretty fucking obviously, but I didn’t let myself balk. “I… I liked seeing you flustered.”

His cheeks pinked, and I’m sure mine did too at my own admission. *He’s so damn responsive to teasing, I couldn’t help myself.*

“I wasn’t f-*flustered,*” he lied, and I couldn’t help but grin again. Even a strained laugh choked through my lips.

“Positively *ruffled,*” I corrected myself. And the way he was blushing was really pushing my fucking limits. “If I’d known it was that easy, I would’ve–” My voice died in my throat as I realised he was watching my mouth. Very intently. I swallowed. “S-sorry, I came to apologise for doing that, and I’m just doing it again.”

His eyes flicked back up to mine, a sort of amusement shining there. And all at once I felt like the floor had been pulled out from under me. Draco stood slowly, closing in on my personal space, and I pressed myself reflexively against his door.

“You like getting me flustered?” he confirmed. My cheeks burned as I nodded shyly. “Is there a chance you *like* that I like you?”

My knees locked, my heart lurched, my stomach flipped. He was dealing me my just desserts, but I couldn’t help but feel like it was *unfair* somehow.

“P-possibly,” I allowed, and then his face was mere inches away and I suddenly couldn’t breathe.

“Why are you here, Harry?”

My brain took a little holiday as my name whispered out of his mouth. Joints going jelly, heart hammering in my throat as I tried desperately to put my thoughts into words into my mouth.

“Y-you can’t just drop a fucking bombshell like that on me and expect me to forget about it and act like nothing happened. And if we’re just gonna have to s-see each other every day, we might as well get on. So…” *Fucking Christ, get it together, Harry.* Malfoy was listening so intently, eyes wide and rapt like I’d never seen them before, and my cheeks were fucking *burning.* “I-I’ll… Keep the teasing to a minimum, but *you* have to start *acting like it.*”

“Acting like what?” he wondered, a little crease forming between his eyebrows. *He’s got little flecks of blue in his grey eyes. Fucking hell, I’ve never been this close to him. Shit.*

“L-like you like me.”

The crease of his brow went slack with shock, and he took a deep, settling breath.

“You’re so fucking *thick.* I *have* been acting like I like you this whole time!” He thrust his arms outward in an exasperated gesture. “I… I bloody *told you* I have no clue how to do this, so if you’re actually asking me for like, *specialised treatment,* you’ll have to be pretty fucking specific, because I’m fairly sure you don’t mean *this.*”

He didn’t give me a second to wonder what he was going to do before he lifted a hand between us, slowly enough that I could slap it away if I wanted, but I was frozen stiff. Couldn’t breathe as he raised it all the way to my forehead, where he carefully swept some hair away to expose my scar. There was something both blinding and gentle about his expression as he eyed my scar, the barest whisper of his thumb tracing the length of it down the side of my brow. When he spoke again, it was soft.

“What is it you want from me? To be sweet to you? Find excuses to talk to you? Smile every time I see you and hold your chair out for you?”

I couldn’t think, suddenly intensely aware of every tiny hair on my body. But as he listed his suggestions, I realised just how bloody mental it sounded. Honestly, I couldn’t picture him doing *any* of that. Couldn’t really picture him doing *this,* touching me with the lightest contact, like I was glass. Felt like I might shatter like it, too, if his minty breath kept washing over my chin each time he exhaled or spoke.

*Fucking Christ.*

“D-don’t run from me anymore,” I finally stuttered, hoping to break the pregnant pause. His eyes flashed back down to mine as he dropped his hand back to his side. “I-I’m not gonna give you a hard time, so...” A nervous swallow forced itself down my throat, and his gaze dipped to my mouth once. “J-just, like… Be normal around me.”

I was somewhat vindicated to see pretty obvious whiplash in his eyes as his frown returned, but something about the muscles around his mouth seemed to hint at… Something else. I couldn’t guess what.

“Fine. Then don’t flirt with me in class again.”

My mouth popped open. “I-I…! That wasn’t-"

He snorted, and I silently*begged* him to step back so I could breathe properly.

“You’re the one who called me *cute.*” My teeth snapped shut as I watched a grimace contort his features. “*Cute?*”

He seemed so unsettled by the word that what was likely a hysterical giggle burst from my lips.
