## Chapter 133: Chapter 132.5

Chapter Notes

Knowing Draco was willingly leading me to a *Muggle tourist attraction* only made me more curious. I wanted to know why he hated it, what *condemned* it in his opinion, but as I followed him into the snowy night, all I felt was *awe.*

*Awe* at the sheer *number* of Muggles that passed us, at the towering structure in the distance as he led me down streets and alleys towards it. Eagerly, I led him faster down the sidewalk. And when we finally came upon it, I was *stunned silent.*

*“Wow,”* I said, *again,* like I had any reason to still be *amazed.* But the tower, stretching up into the night sky, was *astounding.* Bedecked in twinkling lights that illuminated the snow falling around it and so preposterously *useless* that I couldn’t understand why *anyone* would hate Muggles. *Imagine taking all the space and effort and time to do this by hand just to make something nice to look at.*

The opulent majesty of the edifice was *entrancing,* but when Draco tucked our hands into his coat pocket, I glanced at him. Wanted to ask ‘*see? How can you hate something so beautiful?’* But he was just watching me with this… *Vulnerable* sort of softness that I didn’t know what to make of.

“Draco?”

His eyes fluttered, swallowed, and a little flurry of *anxiety* pedalled his heart. And I frowned. *What could possibly be wrong?*

“N-never get enough of you,” he said softly, a corner of his lips twitching like a *smile.* *Weird,* I thought as I faced him more directly. *Maybe he’s just as happy as me, and worried he’ll wake up at any moment.*His heart was *racing,* though and I tried to give him my most assuring smile that I wasn’t going *anywhere.* “C-can I keep you?”

*Try getting rid of me,* I was about to say, but then my fingers found something in his pocket. No doubt a little trinket he’d inevitably give me, so I extracted my hand from his and fished it out into the open air. The shape of it registered as soon as my fingers closed around it, and I was just pitying whoever’d misplaced a *ring,* but when I dropped my gaze to my hand, opened my palm and *saw it…*

I froze. Draco’s tremulous pulse was *thundering* in my ears, but I hardly noticed. It was a *gold band.* A… A *wedding band.* With a little delicately engraved sun reaching out of one side like a jewel. This wasn’t one of his accidental thefts, this was *planted* there on *purpose,* for me to find. The implications of what I was blinking down at wouldn’t quite register, though, so I turned my attention back up to Draco to clarify.

“I-is this…?”

He winced as he glanced down at my hand. And suddenly his *nervousness* made a bit more sense. Those clear, grey eyes wandered back up to my face, and he nodded solemnly.

*He’s… He’s not… No way.*

“Kneel,” I said. Not really *believing* it, and his eyebrows shot up with a scoff. A puff of warm breath amidst the cold, but with a wary downward glance, he… He actually *did.* Nerves *fraught* with apprehension, he dropped to a single knee and looked up at me. Like he was waiting for an *invitation.*

“Say it.”

I really didn’t expect him to. But I hadn’t expected him to *actually fucking kneel,* either, and as snow frosted his icy hair, he glanced down at my open hand again. Swallowed, and returned his pewter attention back up to me.

“Marry me,” he eventually said. I felt the pull, the *urgency* behind the words, and finally let myself acknowledge that this wasn’t a spur-of-the-moment thing. That he hadn’t accidentally swiped this ring off of some surface and decided to *roll with it* when I discovered it. He’d… He’d been *planning this.* “I-I’m serious about forever. And… And I think *you* might be, too, so… If-if you’ll *have* me, which I hope to *god* you will, I’d… There’s nothing I want more in the *entire fucking world* than to call you f-*family.”*

This wasn’t how I’d pictured it. Never would’ve *dreamed* he’d propose to me in front of the fucking *Eiffel Tower* of all things, *my god.* He wasn’t a *grand gestures* sort of bloke, but the sincerity in his eyes, the *painful* fucking terror fluttering around his heart… I collapsed to my knees before him, the *reality* of what he’d just said flooding me and filling me and *killing me* all at once.

*“Family,”* I repeated in a hush. *Family? My own… Family. A real family, him and me. Not borrowed from anyone else, not bound by anything but love, he’s… Choosing me to be his* family. Draco nodded, and I looked back down at the ring – when had I closed my fist again? – and it caught the lights of the tower like *starlight.*

*This is really fucking happening.*

Without a thought, I slid it onto the appropriate finger. Held it up to appreciate it, but I was watching a *montage* of every day of the rest of our *lives.* The… The *cat,* and the *kitchen* and the *garden,* every morning until we *died* waking up in each others’ arms, knit together with a *promise,* something *real* and *tangible* and *nameable.*

*Husband.* My heart fucking *exploded* as I smiled back up at my… *My future husband.*

“Took you long enough, *prick,”* I choked, and it was then I realised I’d started crying. Streaks of wet caught the cold down my cheeks, throat tight as Draco released a *flood* of relief into me on a sigh. And he sniffed, eyes wet and *shining* with the many glittering lights of the tower.

“S-sorry,” he replied, similarly thick with emotion as he took my hand. Withdrew his other from his pocket and slid a *second* ring onto my ring finger. Looking down at it, I saw it was mine’s *twin.* Like they were carved from the same piece of metal, a little indentation of a crescent moon hugged the curve of my sun. “I-I’m just a bloody *chicken,* I guess.”

As I stared down at them, realising how *bloody perfect* they were, a familiar green *sheen* grew to reflect in them. And I glanced back up at Draco, seeing the evidence of my eyes reflected in his own, and his lower lip wobbled slightly as he choked on a sob.

*Family. We’re family.*

Suddenly feeling like I couldn’t *marry him fast enough,* I pried the moon ring off my finger and took his hand to push it onto *his.* A subtle tremble of his hand made it slightly tricky, but it slid on like it *belonged* there. Like they’d been *made for us.*

*This is why he wanted to return to the vault.*

I wanted to stare down at our matching hands *longer,* at the thin gold bands shining around our fingers, but a sudden *swell* through the bond lifted my elation back to my *future husband.* And not a moment after I’d smiled, he *shattered.*

The *wretched fucking bliss* of it all pulled me right along with him as I gathered him into my arms. Buried my face into the side of my neck and *wept.*

*“Draco,”* I sobbed as he tightened his own arms back around me. *“Fuck,* I… Y-you’re *my family.”*

*Family.* One of the only things I’d ever craved *so badly,* that I’d been *denied* from infancy. The Dursleys were only family on paper, and *Sirius…* Sirius, I’d only had a meagre *two years.* But now I got to have a *family of my own.* Maybe even *children* at some point. Children that would have *everything I never did.*

It was too much. Too *overwhelming* to be offered the *one thing* I wanted more than *anything.* The only thing the Mirror of Erised had ever *shown me.* The *deepest desire of my heart.*

*“S-say yes,”* Draco wept into my shoulder, and I choked on a laugh.

*“Yes,”* was my instant reply. As if my acceptance wasn’t *obvious,* but I’d give him as much proof that *I wanted this* as he asked for. *“Yes, I’ll marry you. Yes, I love you. Yes, I want you f-forever, Draco.”*

Not like anyone ever enters into a relationship *planning* for it to fail, but to get to share *a lifetime* with him, to *promise forever…*

I’d never been so happy in my goddamn life.
