## Chapter 84: Chapter 84

Chapter Notes

Seamus tidied himself up in our bathroom as I pulled a black jumper over my head, and this time I didn’t have to ask why Harry was pacing.

“Could’ve *sworn* he and Ginny got back together,” he hissed under his breath, worrying a nail between his teeth. “She’ll be *gutted* once word gets out.”

“Oi,” I interrupted as I caught his wrist, snagging his finger out of his mouth. “Fretting?”

His face flushed, features going slack with shock as he hastily shook his head.

“Nope. N-not fretting, just… Ahm…” He shifted his weight between his feet, but it was just then Seamus decided to join us again. And he ripped his hand out of my grip to face our guest. “F-feeling better?”

He eyed us suspiciously, but offered an embarrassed smile.

“Y-yeah. Thanks for, erm… A-anyway, I’m *starving.”*

“Agreed,” I grumbled, and made to leave. When we headed for the door, however, Harry didn’t, and I twisted to frown at him curiously.

“Y-you two go on, I, erm… I’ll be right along.”

He nodded encouragingly, and I felt my frown quirk into a question, but decided to let him be.

“Come on, let’s hope we didn’t miss lunch.”

Seamus was stiff and awkward as we made our way to the Great Hall, and I found myself glancing at him a few times. Luckily, Dean wasn’t at the Gryffindor table as we sat across from each other, and I sort of resented the pain potion I’d taken because I couldn’t even *feel* the bruises on my arse anymore. But it was just as well, because I reckoned if I’d winced when I sat, even as deep in thought as he was, Seamus would’ve been set the fuck *off*. And I couldn’t help but sympathise with the git.

“D’you know how long?” I asked conversationally as I took a sip of tea. And he paused, fork full of shrimp lowering from his mouth.

“E-er… Not-not really,” he admitted, flush rising back to his cheeks. “Been a sort of *gradual* thing, I s’pose.”

I hummed, took another sip of my tea and a spoonful of mash. Seemed he wasn’t keen on conversation, which suited me fine because my eyes caught on a positively *stunning* brunette sauntering into the Hall at that moment. Couldn’t help but smile as he joined us.

“Alright,” he said as he settled beside me, and began assembling his meal like there was nothing amiss. Seeing both me and Seamus waiting expectantly, though, he sighed. “He r-remembers,” he finally uttered.

Seamus choked. “You *didn’t–”*

“I didn’t say *owt!* I *swear*, I just… Just brought him a bit of pain potion because I *knew* his hangover would be doing his head in, and made sure he hadn’t… Hadn’t *blacked out* or summat. That’s it!”

*Sneaky,* I smirked. *Very slick, almost Slytherin of him.*Harry glanced at me, and flushed to see the smirk I wore. And Seamus groaned, lowering his head to an arm against the table.

“*Fuckin’* ‘ell,” he moaned, and Harry sort of fidgeted beside me. Cleared his throat.

“Would’ve been *worse* if he didn’t,” he sort of grumbled, and I snorted.

“*Christ,* can you *imagine* if he didn’t? Pure fucking *punishment* for you to be the only one who knew what happened.”

“And *then* to have to *tell him,”* Harry continued with a shudder. Seamus lifted his head out of his arm hesitantly, and I forked a few asparagus into my mouth.

“R-right.” He righted himself. “Yeah. No, *course* it’s better that he r-remembers…” Swore under his breath and angrily stabbed a few shrimp to shove them into his mouth.

*Bloody hell, are straight relationships this fraught with drama or is it just queers?*

After lunch, blessedly absent of any unwanted *company,* Draco dragged me up to the Potions classroom to begin brewing more pain potion to replace the vial he’d *pinched off Pomfrey,* leaving Seamus to his own devices. He’d looked sort of panicked to see us go, but it wasn’t like we could fix things *for* him. So I let Draco pull me along with a parting wave.

“Reckon they’ll be alright?” he wondered as I kept pace with him.

“Eventually,” I decided to say. “Might… Might be a bit of an *adjustment,* but if they’re moving at any sort of pace like *we* did, reckon they’ll end up in bed together by tonight.”

He snorted, and sent me a smile. Eyes *glowing* with love, and I couldn’t help but mirror it back to him.

Up in the Potions classroom, I hoisted myself up onto the table beside the cauldron Draco selected. Went about plucking ingredients out of cabinets and sort of muttering to himself, and I was surprised to find he hadn’t even borrowed one of the extra textbooks.

“You know how to brew pain potions by *memory?”*

He threw a smirk back at me from the shelf he was perusing before returning back to it.

“What, like it’s hard?”

I rolled my eyes and settled back on my hands as he returned to the table with an armful of jars and little sacks and laid them out. Watching him work was sort of *entrancing.* He was so singularly focused, so practised with his movements and eyes just pure *shining* with enthusiasm. Like watching a master painter before his canvass.

“Think you’d ever want to work for an apothecary?” I wondered, feet swinging off the side of the table idly as he stirred. He grinned up at me.

“Be hard-pressed to find one that’d hire me,” he supposed, eyes tightening as he lowered them back to his potion. “Ministry’s pardon only goes so far.”

He dropped in a few lacewing flies with his other hand and reversed the direction of his stirring. So matter-of-fact, so *resigned* to the idea that he’d face prejudice around every corner of his life, it made me want to *cry.*

“Draco,” I began softly, and his grin faltered. “Does *my* pardon mean nothing?”

Draco huffed a short laugh through his nose, tapped off his spoon and set it on the designated cloth beside the cauldron. Withdrew his wand to wave it over the pot and finally met my eyes again.

“S’pose we’ll have to wait and see once that photograph hits the media.”

The concept of the backlash I’d possibly receive for going public with Draco sent my stomach swirling with dread, but I clamped down on it stubbornly. After all, it wasn’t like I *owed* the masses anything else. I’d given them *more* than enough of myself as it was, so I just fisted the front of Draco’s jumper in one hand and pulled his mouth down onto mine.

“If I have to *threaten* an apothecarist to hire you, I will. Don’t think I won’t.”

He chuckled against my lips, and pressed another kiss to them.

“Perhaps your *influence* is contagious, though,” he supposed as he pulled back to dust a pinch of some sort of powder into the pot, and resumed stirring. “Harry Potter triumphs over discrimination.”

A chuckle of my own answered his proposed headline, and I eased back onto my hands.

“Shouldn’t be hard. Done a lot of *triumphing* in my life.”

Draco smirked back up at me, but then his eyes were alight with focus again as he added a few more ingredients to the pot. And I was lost in visions of our future, flashes of him coming home from work and having his own potions lab set up somewhere in our flat. Or *house.* And I smiled to fill in the details of our life with *hope.*
