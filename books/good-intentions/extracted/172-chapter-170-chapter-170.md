## Chapter 170: Chapter 170

Chapter Notes

Luna and Draco helped me back to my feet, and my cheeks fucking *burned,* but I only offered sheepish smiles and quiet gratitude as we were led to where the eighth years had claimed a corner of the library. I felt a bit woozy, having just gone through *that,* but once we’d sat with our friends, I started feeling a little better.

Draco had found something I hadn’t even been *aware* of. Something that was implanted into me as an *infant,* that I’d had my entire *life* and never once noticed. I remembered feeling… Odd. *Different* once I’d slipped back into my body on the forest floor, but hadn’t thought about it much. I’d just come back to life, after all, something for which there was *no* precedent. So I chalked any side-effects up to the lack of research.

Hermione cleared her throat once we’d gotten settled with the rest of them, and I peered up at her as she leaned towards us.

“So… When you say *healing his psyche,* you mean…?”

Draco and I shared a glance, and I decided to take this one.

“Th-there’s, ahm… A bit of my soul with some leftover damage from… The Horcrux, and…” I swallowed to see the curious eyes on me, and felt a reassuring hand on my thigh. “I-I guess it was… Acting up? So Draco, he…”

*Fucking words,* I moaned internally, and he cut a short sigh.

“It’s… Sort of difficult to explain. I was trying to pour *love* into it, but it… I mean, *healing* *hurts,* too.”

Hermione leaned back with a frown as she mulled over our words, and from the other end of the table, Seamus piped up from where he sat, legs over Dean’s lap and a book in his own.

“Reckon it’s not the sort of thing you can take a potion for,” he offered quietly, and I thanked him with a smile.

“Yeah, not… Not really.”

“Oh, speaking of!” Our token bookworm hauled a tome out of her bookbag and laid it on the desk. “Would you like me to summarise, let you read for yourselves, or both?”

Draco leaned forward to eye the book’s title, and I heard him read it in his head. *'**On Soulbonds.' Bloody fuck, she wasn’t lying.*Reached for it, and she relinquished it to allow him to pull it closer for us both to see.

It wasn’t a *massive* book, thank god, but as he flipped it open to the table of contents, *hope* swelled in us like *excitement.*

“B-both, I think,” he muttered as our eyes danced down the list of chapters.

*1. Forming a Soulbond.*

*2. Dangers and Risks.*

*3. Immediate Effects.*

*4. Short-term Effects.*

*5. Long-term Effects.*

*6. Dissolution.*

*Dissolution? You can dissolve a soulbond?* The notion made our blood run cold, but then Hermione was talking, and we tore ourselves out of the book to listen.

“Basically, the process normally takes several months. But you said it took you two a little under a month, right?” Some hasty backtracking had us nodding. And she frowned. “So… I’m not sure *everything* in there will be accurate. It’s… Really not supposed to happen that fast. But, for the sake of *summarising,* I’ll say this: first is *intense* feelings, then subtler feelings as it deepens, telepathic communication, and then something called *Fieridem.* There’s a whole bit on Fieridem, but essentially it comes from the latin for *‘to become the same,’* and there’s a *mountain* of warnings about that stage. Ways to postpone it, to come back from it, cautionary tales, the lot. From this text, at least, it sounds like… If the two parties’ souls aren’t at least *roughly* the same size, or… Well, *‘wealth’* is the way it describes it, very old text, but… Basically, the worst case scenario is loss of sense of self for whoever has the *‘poorer’* soul. The s-*smaller,* or I guess, like… *Weaker…”*

Hermione’s voice faded to a murmur as she slumped back in her seat, glancing between us with something like an apology in the lines of her grimace. And I felt all the blood rush out of my cheeks as my face went *cold.*

*Shit. Fuck, this is why the bureaux took the time to make a house call on Christmas fucking morning, why they referred to it as enslavement, you should’ve never let me-*

*Harry,* Draco twisted towards me, taking my hands out of my lap where they’d fisted and *pierced* me with a stare. *I know who I am. You haven’t done anything irreversible to me. Well, nothing I’d want reversed. We’re alright, and now we know and we can be… Careful, I guess?*

I nodded, *yearning* for more details and supposed *‘cautionary tales,’* and he released my hands to let me draw the book into my lap. Flipped straight to the chapter titled *‘Dangers and Risks’* and sought out what Hermione’d referenced.

*‘Fieridem. Generally speaking, human souls are all roughly the same wealth, but in a few rare circumstances, a pair may find themselves Soulbound and faced with a difficult reality: one of the two may have a poorer soul, or one a richer soul. In these instances, things like autonomy and identity become weakened by the other. In one extreme case recorded in the year 1212, an unlucky witch entered a catatonic state from which she never recovered. When her Soulbound husband sought help from the presiding Healers of the time, it was determined that she could no longer function without his conscious will. (Healer’s Records, Neworth, 1282.)’*

I felt sick. We’d formed the soulbond without even *knowing* what we were doing, without an *inkling* of the consequences or *risks,* and I was suddenly back in that hospital cot, Draco in my arms and *pushing love* into his chest. If only I’d known just what we’d be chancing with that decision.

Draco read on, even though my vision blurred.

*‘For other, more evenly matched souls, Fieridem is described as a euphoric experience. An intimacy unattainable by any other means, even within the Legilimency arts, for these are a purely cerebral field. Pairs are reported to be able to share real-time vision, hearing, taste, smell, and sensation with Fieridem, as well as memories and dreams. A purer understanding of one another, even on occasion access to each others’ souls described as ‘a physical touch.’ (Healer’s Records, Upperton, 1408.)’*

*We’ve been looking for magical records of pacts and oaths, when we should’ve been looking through healer’s records.*

His acknowledgement sounded distant in my head as he returned his attention up to Hermione.

“Thanks, Hermione. This is perfect. We owe you one.”

I saw the tight smile she gave him, but her worried focus was *honed* on me, and I tried to blink up at her.

*Catatonic. I could crush Draco’s soul to nothing if I tried.*

A sigh to my right.

“Harry.” My eyes slid over to his, a wide *horrified* green that I saw through his own. He twisted to face me more fully, plucking the book out of my lap and laying it back on the desk before taking my face in his hands again and *lashing* my attention to him. “We’re *fine. I’m* fine. You won’t do that to me, not on purpose and not by *accident* now that we know it’s a possibility. We’ll just… Use caution and… Take it slow, yeah?”

I nodded dumbly, tongue thick in my mouth. It’s not like I *regretted* forming a soulbond with him, but felt like a goddamn *twat* for doing it *unintentionally.*

*We should’ve done more research early on. I just… I liked it so much I didn’t care. Didn’t care what was happening, only that we were getting closer and I never wanted to stop.*

*Neither did I. Neither of us is to blame, Harry. What’s done is done and I wouldn’t have it any other way. I love you so fucking much.*

I loved him *right back,* not in words but in *action.* And he leaned in to kiss me again, *swallowing* my horror for what we’d learned right out of me, until there was nothing left but *him.* *Draco,* here in a way that was *more*than here. *Fieridem.*

His soul felt just as lively and *distinct* as it ever had, and I considered trying to put a pause on our Fieridem, but the moment I pulled even a *fraction* away from him, he tensed. Broke his lips away from mine and tightened his grip on my head.

*Don’t go,* he begged. *Please, I’m alright, this is alright, I want this. Please don’t leave me alone in my head again.*

*Draco, it’s dangerous,* I warned, but his soul *wailed* inside him, so I let myself slide right back into that *here.* And felt him relax, his hands losing tension around my face as he exhaled a stuttering sigh. *The second I get the sense you’re forgetting who you are, we’re taking a break and getting you to a Healer.*

I felt a corner of his lips curl up, and he nodded against my forehead.

*Anything, just please, please don’t leave me.*

Felt a corner of *my* lips curl in response, and pressed one more kiss to his lips.

“Should’ve bet more than money,” came a soft, feminine voice that broke us from our silent conversation. And as we turned similarly to see Luna chewing on her quill, her lips broke into an easy grin. “I’ve got an *unfair advantage,* after all.”

One pale brow rose on her forehead suggestively, and I hacked a cough as warmth rushed *right* back into that cold vacancy my horror had left in my cheeks. Draco released my face with a chuckle of his own, pointedly ignoring Hermione’s confusion as he passed a hand over his mouth.

“How d’you mean?” the brunette wondered curiously, and Harry and I exchanged a glance.

“Oh, nothing,” Luna replied airily as she leaned forward over her own parchment. Lowered her quill back to the page, and sent us a *mischievous* little smirk before planting her attention back onto her work. “Nothing at all.”

Hermione crossed her arms over her stomach as she frowned at us, which was *plenty* of incentive for us to reach down for our bookbags and withdraw our own studying materials under her suspicious gaze.

Despite the *terrifying* discovery we’d just made, *pleasure* warmed my cheeks as I tried not to smile. Draco was just *giddy* with it, and I couldn’t deny it infected me. I wanted to read more of the book Mione’d found, but McGonagall’s request that we *actually fucking study* echoed in my ears, and I resolved to bring it to our room tonight to indulge my curiosity. For now, we had little less than *three days* before the most important exam of our *lives.* And I’d be *damned* if I let this be an obstacle between us and that future I’d envisioned for us.

*Cottage. Cat. Goats and chickens and horses. Home.*
