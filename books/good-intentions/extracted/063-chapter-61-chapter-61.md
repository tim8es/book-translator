## Chapter 61: Chapter 61

Harry reverently replaced the Resurrection Stone back in his desk drawer with all the other knicknacks I’d gifted him before he turned a weary smile back at me. And as he started dressing down for the night, I did the same.

“Everything’s… A bit *alright,* isn’t it?” I noted, pulling my trousers off my left ankle from the edge of the bed. And Harry’s cheeks pinked as he kicked his own off.

“Y-yeah… Guess it is.”

Our clothes formed a pile at the foot of his desk as he climbed back up onto the bed in nothing but glorious skin. He’d taken to wearing his scars proudly, or at least unabashedly, around me and I couldn’t have been more entranced with the brutal beauty of them. Couldn’t help tracing the jagged lines as he lay on his back next to me, quilts up to our waists as I leaned over him on one elbow.

“This might sound fucked-up, but these are so fucking gorgeous,” I risked the admission as his eyes went sort of wide up at me.

“Yeah…?”

I nodded, smiled as gently as I could and leaned down to press a kiss to his bedecked chest. And his breath hitched in his throat as I returned my head into my palm to peer down at him. He glanced down at himself once as my fingertips ran feather-light over the many lacerations he bore. But when he turned his gaze back up to my face, I saw curiosity shining in his eyes.

“You… You like this one, too,” he supposed as he lifted a hand to push his hair off his brow. My attention flashed up to that *gorgeous* fractal scar that ran the length of his forehead, arched down one temple and disappeared into his hairline.

“I *know* it’s, like… *Macabre* to appreciate, but…” My lips touched down to his brow as I pressed a kiss to *that* scar, too. “It’s *you,”* I explained, lips still touching his forehead. “Everything about you is *stunning.”*

Harry’s pink cheeks bunched with his smile as I leaned back again, and I smiled back just like I *always* would.

“Bit of a trademark of the Chosen One. Sure you’re not just a *fan?”*

I snorted, traded the hand trailing over his chest for his cheek as I stroked his blush with my thumb.

“Seem to recall *you* being obsessed with *me,*arsehole.”

Harry and I teased each other, chatted about France, and snogged until sleep finally claimed us. And it was so *easy,* loving him, being with him, that I dreamt of *normal.*

*Normal,* disagreeing over the layout of furniture in our apartment. *Normal,* tasting fucking *coffee* on his breath every morning. *Normal,* finding him in the office of our apartment wearing *my jumper.* Everything our life together could ever be.

And when I awoke, it was to the same bliss I’d dreamt of. In Harry’s arms, calm, warm, *happy.* So *nauseatingly* happy, I never wanted to *move.* But responsibilities drew us from the warm sheets to the shower to class. And life went on.

It was *so strange,* having all our peers aware of us. More than aware, but *alright* with us. I felt a sort of warmth I’d never, ever felt before in their presence. Seeing as I was the sole non-Gryffindor (besides Luna) to have accepted the invitation to return, their camaraderie created such an atmosphere of *warmth,* I couldn’t help but compare it to the environment I’d left behind.

Slytherin house had always been cold. Literally and metaphorically, because every student seemed determined to climb to the top of every ladder they could; social, academic, and otherwise, by *any* means necessary. Cheating, blackmailing and bargaining were never off the table in those endeavours. But the interpersonal relationships had always been cold, calculating, *manipulative.* And now to be roughed up with *kindness…*

Ron slapped a hand against my back as we left the Gryffindor table that lunch, sort of startling me, but when he pulled me into a rough one-armed sort of *hug* as we walked, I fucking *balked.*

“Reckon I don’t need to give you the *fuck-with-Harry-fuck-with-me* talk, do I?”

Said brunette was happily chatting with Hermione behind us as we exited the Great Hall, and I strived to appear casual as I righted myself beneath the ginger’s arm.

“Reckon you don’t *want* me to fuck with you like I fuck with Harry,” I shot back, and earned the exact sort of groan I hoped to. But he didn’t rip his arm off my shoulders.

“You *know* what I mean,” he insisted. And I peered down at him, eyebrows quirked imperiously as I took his meaning.

“Being saviour of the entire fucking *world*, wandless nonverbal magic and all, no, I reckon you don’t. If he ever has a problem with me, I expect dire consequences.”

Ron smirked up at me, and it was *so strange,* and yet somehow so fucking *normal* to interact with him like this, because he was nothing if not an extension of Harry, and if I could love *Harry…*

“Right. Just so we’re clear.”

His arm finally loosened around my shoulders, and we fell into easy step together as I fixed my collar.

“Draco,” a distinct voice behind me as my arm was claimed in the space between Ron and I. And I smiled down at Harry. “What’ve you got for me today?”

My smile turned into a grimace as I sent both hands into my pockets, and came back with… A *bit of chalk.*

*“Fuck,”* I groaned, handing it over to Harry as I turned my pocket inside-out. It was coated in white dust, and I shook it out with another curse. *“Shit.”*

Harry chortled, pocketing the chalk like it wouldn’t stain the inside of *his* pocket, but I supposed it didn’t matter much to him. He dusted off his lap as I shook chalk dust onto him, and I loosed a sigh.

“Fucking sticky fingers, fucking *wandering hands…”*

He leaned his lips close to my ear as I clapped chalk off my hands, and whispered:

*“I like your wandering hands.”*

And I went fucking *rigid.* We were nearly back to the classroom, surrounded by his friends, and as he eased away from my ear, I sent him a smirk.

“Not my sticky fingers, though?”

His returning grin turned implicative as he glanced down me. All the way down, and all the way up, and I nearly stiffened in my fucking *slacks.*

“Those, too.”
