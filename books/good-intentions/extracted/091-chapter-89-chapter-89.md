## Chapter 89: Chapter 89

Chapter Notes

*Don’t cry, don’t fucking cry,* I chanted over and over as I knelt with Harry. His sobs were pure *wrecking* me, though, as I held him. Tried to be the stable one, the safety net to catch him, to offer *any* sort of comfort. Which meant I *would not cry.*

Didn’t have much to say, either. What could be said? Harry wilted into me slightly, and I shifted my weight around to let him bury his face in my chest. My throat was growing increasingly tight as he clung to my jumper, and I hastily blinked away the tears prickling my eyes. *Don’t fucking cry!*

I tried to remember how James had just *lost* it at the thought of what my *father* would think of this. How unexpected that’d been, especially to hear an adult *swear*. But I supposed being dead had a way of rearranging one’s priorities. I counted myself singularly lucky to have been able to meet them, though it’d been brief. The history behind the stone reluctantly reminded me of Harry’s… *Attempt* second year, though, so I didn’t have to wonder why the conversation had been short.

Even just *that much* had absolutely *destroyed* him. He was just falling to *pieces* in my arms, and a sneaky fucking tear escaped my restraint. *Dammit.* I buried my face in his hair, breathing in his familiar scent, Harry-and-Draco, and tried to breathe evenly. I could do this much for him. Be the steady hands he crumbled apart in. But when he sobbed a curse into the fabric of my jumper, my throat went *painfully*tight.

*‘Fuck’ is right. Jesus, there’s plenty of people I miss, but to miss people you barely even know… It’s gotta be a different level of grief.*

As Harry’s bawling wails quieted to sniffling sobs, I was surprised to feel a gentle sprinkling of… *Rain?* Washing the inside of the room in soft sheets. My confusion only lasted a moment before I realised it was *Harry* making it rain, and I gripped him tighter. My heart was full of *nails* in my chest.

*He’s so sad he’s making it rain.*

*Fucking hell.*

“Oh *fuck off,”* he moaned into my chest, and just like that, the rain stopped. We were little more than damp, my hair dripping into my eyes as I blinked the water away. “So fucking *annoying.”*

A cough of laughter escaped me on a sob, and I kissed his wet hair.

“Thanks for introducing me,” I whispered, sure if I tried to vocalise I’d regret it. He nodded against me, still hacking wet little sobs as he scooted closer. My core engaged to keep us upright, as hard as he was leaning against me, but I’d do it as long as he needed. The dripping corners of his desk formed small puddles on the floor, the only other sound as he wept to exhaustion.

When Harry finally began extricating himself from my arms, there was a wet spot on the front of my jumper and my abs were *screaming.* But he offered me a tearful, wavering smile, and nothing else mattered.

“S-sorry,” he stuttered, like an *idiot,* like he had *anything* to apologise for. I could only shake my head at the moment and return what I hoped was a far more sure smile than his. “Never… N-never got to introduce anyone to my p-parents before.”

My lungs lurched like they’d force out a sob, but I caught it in my throat. Brushed away the single traitorous tear that’d escaped on my shoulder and took his face in my hands. He sniffed hard, and blinked away a few more tears, and then something shifted in his expression. Like he finally recognised how *barely* I was holding it together.

*Don’t cry.*

“Draco,” he said softly.

*Don’t fucking cry.*

I tried to smile, swiped my thumbs over his cheeks, because this wasn’t even fucking *about me.* It’s not like *my parents* had died before I could remember them, wasn’t like I had to summon them from the fucking dead to introduce them to my boyfriend. I wouldn’t let myself cry because I didn’t have any fucking *right* to, when Harry’d lost so much more than I ever had.

“Hey,” he insisted, fingers wrapping around my wrist as his eyes carefully searched mine. “It’s alright. I know, seeing me miserable makes you miserable.”

He tried for a smile again, chin quivering as his heartbreakingly green eyes sparkled with tears. *That’s right.*

Even *years* ago, when my infatuation had first begun evolving into something *more,* I had been like a fucking *sponge* to his moods. If he was having a good day, so was I. If he was pissy, it set me off. And when he was sad, it ripped me to shreds. That hadn’t changed in all this time, and now, Harry was…

“I’m so fucking sorry,” I finally managed to strangle through the obstacle course of my emotions in my throat. “I-I *wish* I could go back in time and change things for you. Give back everything that’s been stolen from you. You deserve everything, *everything,* Harry, and I–”

My inhale hitched on a gasp, and Harry pulled me into his arms by the neck. *Fuck.* Squeezed me like he was squeezing all of his *enormous* feelings into me, and I flooded over with them as tears finally burst out of me.

“You’re enough,” he choked into my ear as I carefully wrapped my arms back around him. How *desperately* I wanted to disagree, because how could *I ever* be enough? I was sure the litany of names that knelled through my head rang just as clearly through his, and there was no way I would *ever* make up for all he’d lost.

*I’d do anything to make up for what he lacks.*

---

I wasn’t really sure what made me decide tonight would be the night. It was even harder of a conversation than I’d braced for, and I’d braced for it to *decimate* me. Maybe it was just the undeniable proof that Draco’s heart, his fucking *soul* belonged to me, and no one singular person had ever been so vitally important to me that I’d even *thought* to introduce them to my parents. But as he wept with me, *mourned* with me, I felt that insurmountable burden of pain and grief start to lessen. Even though we were in *tears,* I felt… Lighter.

My friends understood what all I’d lost, maybe guessed at what all I’d suffered, but for someone to actually *reach into my fucking heart* and rip some of the pain out to share it was… Was just, like, more powerful than any magic I’d ever *known.* Never known another person to… Cry *for me.*

In a world with ridiculous things like supervillains and dragons and prophecies, I had to wonder if corny shit like *soulmates* were real, because knowing– *seeing* Draco mourn a loss that wasn’t even *his* was just so deeply *incomprehensible* to me that I had to attribute it to some sort of magic. Maybe even *older* than magic, and it was then I realised I’d stopped crying.

Not just because I had no more tears to shed, not for any *biological* reason, but because I… Just didn’t need to anymore. The heaviness of my heart that I’d grown accustomed to, that’d dragged it through the mud with the weight everywhere I went and only seemed to grow heavier throughout my life was just… Gone. Like someone had plucked it out of the mire and held it in the palms of their hands, right in my chest where it belonged.

Felt weird. Unfamiliar. *Is this what normal people feel like?* I was still adjusting to it as Draco’s sobs in my ear stuttered out to hiccoughs.

*I love you* was on the tip of my tongue, but it stopped short at my lips because it felt too *empty* for what I felt. Too *casual* and *light* for what I really wanted to say. So I did a hasty reworking in my mouth and pleaded to whatever gods might be listening that I wouldn’t cock up my words this time and said:

“You’re *everything*. Everything I never knew to want, everything I never knew to *hope* for, everything I never thought I deserved. I’d do it all over again for you.”

Still not right, but close enough, and Draco damn near broke my fucking *ribs* as he tightened his arms around me. Felt like we were one complete person for a second, despite the fact that I couldn’t fucking *breathe,* because his pulse was hammering against my chest like it was my own fucking heart, lungs breathing in sync and I *melted* into him.

*Can’t imagine my life without him.*
