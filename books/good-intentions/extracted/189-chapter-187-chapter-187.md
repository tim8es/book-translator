## Chapter 187: Chapter 187

Chapter Notes

“So how’re you settling?” I heard Narcissa ask from the living room as I loaded up the tea caddy for the three of us. Draco was tentatively pleased to see his mum, and I personally felt a modicum of relief at having a proper adult in the house, but as I steadied myself with a breath, I tried to remind myself that we were *capable.* Only freshly out in the world, but we’d survived worse than *living on our own.*

“Fine. It’s… Definitely a learning curve, but it’s a far cry from prison.”

There was this dry, empty weight to Draco’s answer as I rejoined them, Narcissa having pulled her bare feet up onto the couch she’d claimed and leaning over the armrest on an elbow. For someone so prim and put-together, it was odd to see her so relaxed, but it was… Nice. I much preferred Lucius-less Narcissa.

“W-we, erm… We have a handler from the Ministry who’s been keeping an eye on us,” I chimed in as I set the caddy down on the short tea table between the couches. And joined Draco across from her, sending him a glance. “But yeah, it’s… *Weird* not having anything to do. Keep feeling like I’m forgetting something.”

Narcissa hummed and leaned forward to pour a little cream into one of the mugs and pull it back with herself. Draco mirrored her, sans cream, as she took our measure silently.

“Well, the weather’s not really suited for gardening, and I suppose you two aren’t really in a position where you *need* jobs, but… I understand. Staying inside all day for months on end will wear anybody down, even with company. You should consider getting out in the community.”

Draco crossed one ankle over the opposite knee as he reclined back, an easy arm stretched out behind me against the back of the couch as I reached for the only cup of coffee I’d brought out with a hesitant smile.

“We’ve been out… A bit. A spot of shopping here and there, but…” We exchanged a glance as I took a sip of my coffee. “Well, let’s just say I don’t have high hopes for *community involvement,* considering who we are.”

I couldn’t help but agree, in such a remote part of the country we were some of the rare few magical folk, and even if the muggles wouldn’t recognize us, it wasn’t like either of us were interested in forming relationships (and therefore obligations) to our elderly… Neighbours. Draco and I were of the similar mind that we had given enough of ourselves to causes beyond our own needs and were well and truly *done* owing anyone anything anymore.

Narcissa sighed. “People can surprise you, you know. But I hear you. You deserve all the time you want to just… Relax.”

Indeed, *relaxing* had become easier and easier since Draco and I had reestablished intimacy. I scooted a little closer to his side, and his hand came up off the couch to run through my hair on the back of my head. Sure, things were new and I missed the familiarity of the castle, the structure of routine, but not having to do it *alone…*

“Thanks, mum,” I said quietly into my mug with my next sip, and she smiled.

---

Hours after mum had finally Flooed back to the Manor (unsurprisingly disappointed with our lack of input on venues and such) it started to snow. I’d drawn my robe around myself after an evening shower, and then found Harry curled up on the bay window bench facing the back garden, staring out into the winter scene.

“Alright?” I asked as I joined him, and he turned a smile up at me.

“First snow in our new home,” he said quietly, and I felt my lips pull up into a matching smile. Reached out to stroke my thumb against his ankle.

“With the love of my life and all is right with the world.” Harry’s smile spread wider into a grin as I echoed his Legilimency vision back to him. He reached back down for my hand to take it off his ankle, laced our fingers together and *looked* at me.

This was it. The future he’d envisioned all those months ago when I’d first confessed that I wanted him *forever.* I felt my eyes gutter as emotion swelled in my throat, because Harry was just *staring* at me like he was memorising this moment. Sitting in it with me, *saturating* in it and we both drew deep breaths in unison. Such a small moment, sitting together by the window as it snowed outside and just *breathing* together. But it made all the difference in the *world.*

We were missing a bottle of wine and a cat, and I was *far* too dressed, but some invisible weight lifted off of Harry as he pulled me into a kiss. Every time he reached for me, I’d be there. Every need, I’d supply. Even if what he needed was *space,* even if we found ourselves in careers that put responsibilities back on our shoulders, Harry would always, *always* be my priority. And I let this sentiment flow into his mouth as I cradled his jaw in my free hand.

I felt him lean into me, both physically and at the soul-level as the wind whipped quietly outside. And with this weight off of him, brand-new ideas sprung to life in his head, as if the wick of his soul had finally dried out enough to burn again. I nearly wept for the sensation of it.

An art gallery, where I could house all my favourite paintings. A piano, similar to the gallery at the villa, a little stream through our back garden like the greenhouse. Dressing the house up in bright colours, plants, a phonograph to fill the air with music. Setting up a half-sized Quidditch pitch out on our expansive land for whenever we wanted to play Seeker’s games, or invite our friends over for casual games. Going horseback riding together out into the woods, planting orchards of fresh fruit for us to pick. On and on and on, cascading, kaleidoscope ideas blossomed and flourished in his head, and I was nearly dizzy with them all by the time he finally released my lips to touch his brow to mine.

Bright, glowing green eyes blinked up at me, alive with *possibility* as I struggled to swallow the emotion in my throat.

*Love doing life with you,* he said voicelessly, and I smiled.

*What a life.*

---

I felt… Weightless. Buoyant, even, as I summoned a bottle of wine from the kitchen. Just because I *could,* because we were *adults* in our *own home,* had bought it with *our money,* and I just wanted to. As Draco had internally recognised, the situation was slightly different from how I’d imagined it, but I figured getting him naked and silly wasn’t so impossible.

He was still slightly lost in me as the bottle drifted into my waiting hand, because he didn’t realise what I’d done until I glanced at the cork and it popped off, startling him slightly. I couldn’t help but giggle at his surprise as I reached out a hand to touch his knee.

“Sorry,” I muttered, and raised the bottle in toast. “To the end of a story, and the beginning of a new one.”

I threw back a mouthful straight from the neck, and grimaced slightly at the shocking bitterness. Draco had slightly more experience with wine than I did, which was to say *any,* but I could confidently say we hadn’t the foggiest idea what we’d been buying when we’d purchased this bottle. Just that it looked fancy and it had a French name. Draco chuckled as I lowered it from my lips and passed a hand over my mouth. And shuddered as the thick flavour slid down my throat.

“Corny,” he teased as he accepted it when I offered. Took a beat to contemplate what *he* should toast to, eyeing the label dubiously, but eventually settled on: “To making happiness.”

My cheeks flushed with pleasure as I watched him neck a few gulps of his own. And it was *far* more palatable on his tongue. But I still couldn’t fight back the chuckle of my own at his reflexive shudder as he swallowed.

“I love you *so much,* Draco,” I said earnestly as I took his hand. Because fuck it, if we were going to be *sappy,* I might as well lay it on *thick.* But instead of snorting, his eyes went sort of… Soft. “There’s no one I’d rather be here with.”

A flicker of what he’d expected for his life flashed behind his eyes. Tumbling from seedy job to seedy job, living on the streets and falling into all manner of bad habits to cope with his pitiful post-war existence.

But here I was. *Smiling* at him, wearing matching wedding rings in a home of our own. *Family.* Family and love and warmth, and he felt such a *surge* of emotion that it stopped up *my* throat. And hastily plucked the bottle back out of his hand to chase it back down.

Where my presence in his life had shone a *light* into his dark imagination of his future, *his* presence had painted *rainbows* onto the blank canvass of my *own* imagination. His eyes dropped to our clasped hands, and he thumbed my ring sentimentally. His own personal sun, he considered me. Content to soak me in, to reflect me, to be a… A *poor imitation.*

*That’s not what you are,* I corrected him with a frown. He still didn’t look back up at me, though a small smile pulled at his lips. *You’re my peace. My calm in a noisy world. My anchor.*

Draco sniffed, laced our fingers together, and finally peered back up at me. His pale lashes were decorated with glistening*wet,* the red of his eyes making the grey seem so much *bluer* as he tried for a smile.

*Gravity,* he called me, chin wobbling. *Home.*

*Fuck,* he was *killing* me. I pulled him into my arms almost on impulse, propped my chin against his shoulder as he buried his face into the crook of my neck. Wrapped his own around my ribs and *squeezed* a tight little sound out of me.

And there, in his arms, I took another deep breath. Reflexive, likely to contend with the *tsunami* of emotions washing over us both, but as I released it with a sigh, I… Went quite. Very still. *Soft,* as I finally, *finally* started to *actually*believe it was…

It was all over.

And if soulbonds and romance and *hope* really existed, maybe happily ever afters did, too.
