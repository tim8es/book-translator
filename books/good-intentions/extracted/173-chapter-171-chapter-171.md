## Chapter 171: Chapter 171

Studying with Harry was… *Easier* than I’d feared it’d be. Even though we were reading different things, at different paces, I never confused who was reading what because I didn’t have to cram it all into one head. We had two between us, and though we were both aware of the other, the words never got jumbled together. A fucking *blessing,* because the few times I paused to check in on Harry’s progress, he was… Well, academia had never really been his strong suite.

Which only made me that much more determined to absorb as much information as I could. There wasn’t anything new at this point, thank god, we were just reviewing things we both already *knew,* but it sort of amused me and… And *pleased* me to think I was about to find out what sort of test-taker Harry was. Did he panic and blank when the exams were laid out before him? Did he recall information easily? How did he store information in that gorgeous head of his?

I think it must’ve been the presence of our peers, all silently studying around us that ended up being the reason we *actually did, too.* We could’ve easily chattered away to each other about the soulbond, because *that* topic was undoubtedly festering in the backs of our heads, but… Seeing everyone so determined to *pass* made it that much easier to focus.

When the bell signalling lunch finally tolled, my neck was *aching* from hunching over the desk for so long. Harry had been leaned into an elbow against the surface, arm bent up over his head as he read sideways, so it was curious to me that *his* wasn’t similarly hurting, but when we got up to eat, we just left all our shit there. Not like anyone would mess with *our things, our table.*

The other eighth years chatted quietly as we went, comparing theories for what would actually appear on the NEWT, and Harry and I just walked in silence. Fingers laced together and hunger drawing us to the Great Hall. First day back and it’d already been *quite* a day. And it was only half over.

“Harry,” Hermione sidled up to us just as we were turning down the last hallway to the doors, and we both peered over at her. “D’you think… I mean, *I* wouldn’t be opposed to a joint ceremony, but… D’you reckon *Narcissa* would?”

My better half glanced back up at me with a question in his *stunning* eyes, and the imagination of *Hermione and mum* working together to coordinate a *joint wedding* sent me *cackling.*

“That bad?” she supposed as I tried to reign in my humour.

“She’d probably agree, but… I dunno how well you two would get along, especially with an event that’s equally important to you both. She’s a very, er… *Flamboyant decorator,* my mum, quite the *maximalist,* and you can *both* be very stubborn, but… S’pose there’s the incentive of the *Malfoy estate* to foot the bill, so… You two *might* just be able to find a compromise where necessary.”

Hermione was silent for a while as she considered this. Long enough for us to take our usual seats at the Gryffindor table and start loading up our plates before she spoke again.

“I know she… She doesn’t really *know* me, but… *Shit,* I haven’t even *asked*you what you thought of a joint wedding.”

Harry and I shared a glance, and I silently decided to voice our answer.

“We’re already married. The specifics of the *ceremony* don’t really matter to us, so long as Harry gets to wear something *sparkly.”*

I shot a smirk over at his flush as he studiously forked sauteed carrots into his mouth. Hermione seemed to consider as I dove into my own meal.

“I… *Think* we can manage that. I’ve… Honestly, never really envisioned a massive ballgown or anything, but… I mean, if *you two* are agreeable to the idea, I guess I’ll just have to hear what Narcissa thinks.”

A noncommittal hum as I chewed soundly, but bending my head down to take my next bite sent my neck *singing* with pain, and I let out an agonised little sound. And Harry was immediately attending me.

Glanced up at me once, dabbed his lips with his napkin, and stood. And not a moment later, his hands were on my neck, pouring *warmth* into them in just such a way I *knew* they were glowing, and I groaned as it fed into the taut muscles. Let my head droop forward to allow his gentle massage.

*“Bloody* hell,” came Dean’s opinion as Harry *flooded* ease into my neck. *God,* it felt *heavenly.* “Do… Do I *wanna* know?”

I felt Harry smirk up at him, still pressing his thumbs down firmly against either side of the nape of my neck. Working the muscles with small circles as they loosened under his touch. Just like in the hospital wing, it was *bliss.* An appreciative sigh escaped my lips.

“Just one of the *perks,* I guess.”

My arms folded over the table to brace against his onslaught, and ended up pushing my plate away to allow me to lower my brow to a wrist. I melted like fucking *butter* under his love, and when my aching muscles finally loosened to their extent, he dropped a delicate little kiss to the back of my neck. And returned to his seat beside me.

But I still couldn’t raise my head, such *heaven* radiating through me. Might’ve fallen *asleep,* so at-ease did I feel.

“Is he… *Alright?”* Hermione asked, and I felt gentle fingers threading through the hair on the back of my head as Harry played with it.

“He’s *wonderful.* You know… I never really knew love was a *verb,* an *act,* before him. Always thought it was some *passive* state, to *be in love.”*

His voice was soft, *gentle* as his hands as he brushed his fingers through my hair, this way and that, just sort of *playing* with it. And I felt myself smile.

“‘M still here,” I muttered into the table, and felt the chuckle Harry gave in response just as I heard it.

“You *better* be. You even *think* about leaving me, and I’ll kill you.”

A snort ripped out of me as I finally found the wherewithal to raise my head. And I *knew* my hair was all askew from his affection, but didn’t bother fixing it as I turned a lazy grin up at him.

“You could *never.”*

He grinned right back at me, hand coming to rest on the back of my neck again as he eyed me subtly.

“No. Killing you’s the one thing I could *never do.”*

The rest of the day passed by in a blur of words, dates, facts, names, and my head felt overfull when we finally found ourselves in our dorm. I was eager for sleep, but as soon as I fell down onto the bed in all my clothes, my curiosity piqued to recall the book in my bookbag. And as always, Draco was way ahead of me.

He plucked it out of the discarded satchel, toed his shoes off, and came to sit on the edge of the bed as I twisted to sit up with him. The book opened easily in his lap, and he flipped to the table of contents again.

*Surely there’s more dangers and risks other than Fieridem.*

The pages turned under his long, slender fingers as he sought out the second chapter, and I rested my chin on his shoulder to attend him. This chapter’s introduction was a brief but *firm*warning against forming a soulbond if either party were unsure, but Draco barely skimmed it.

*Emotional influence* was the first of the warnings. A partner’s sour mood could sour the others’. Shite like that. *Unintentional sharing* was the next. Supposed accidental memory-sharing, or dream-sharing, or anything else one might’ve liked to keep private from the other. A warning about the risk of the *depth of connection,* but again, we dismissed it.

This third warning was about *overstimulation.* An overwhelm of the senses for sharing everything so deeply. Said to cause stress, anxiety, even *panic* when it became too much. A fair point, I conceded, but again, the notion didn’t bother either of us. We’d had enough experience at this point to know how to temper each others’ feelings, and the thought that it could ever *overwhelm*us seemed silly.

And the last was Fieridem. *Rather short chapter,* I noted with a measure of relief. And Draco twisted a smile down at me on his shoulder.

*Short, yes, but informative. We should be careful about this.*

I grudgingly agreed, turning a frown down at the book as I adjusted against him. And he agreeably flipped back to the table of contents. His longest, middle finger slid down the chapter titles and reluctantly paused on the last. *Dissolution.*

A *violent* fucking shudder shook me at the thought, and he snapped the book shut.

*No. Never, never ever want to know how to end this.*

*Me neither. God, it feels fucking wrong that it’s even possible.*

I agreed *heartily,* and felt a wave of relief when I heard the book drop to the floor. We were out of our clothes in record time before scooching beneath the quilts to cradle each other.

*Never, ever, EVER want this to end.*
