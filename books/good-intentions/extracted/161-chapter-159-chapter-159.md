## Chapter 159: Chapter 159

Back in the villa that night, sleep claimed Harry and I like an *imperative.* After all the excitement, the dancing, the *fucking,* it was no wonder we were exhausted. And our sleep was surprisingly dream-free as we succumbed together.

1999 found us tangled together between the sheets, just like we had been for months now, and no less *utterly blissful.* As my mind roused to consciousness, though, the first thought I had was how little time we had left in France. How soon our return to Hogwarts, to our peers, to… To the *NEWTs.* But Harry was still blessedly under, so I let myself sink into his warmth for a while more.

*My husband. My forever. My family.* It was so strange, to have had my definition of *family* so completely overwritten by him. Felt like a fucking idiot for not considering how that word would affect him, because it’d been so *instinctive* to ask him not to be my husband, but to be my *family.* And my lips curled into a sleepy grin to recall the way that word had sunk into him when I’d proposed.

Always, before him, the concept of *family* was laden with obligation, with expectation and duty, *responsibility.* But with him, I just felt… *Home.* Which, objectively speaking, is likely what *family should’ve* felt like. But it never had until him.

He stirred in my arms just then, moaning quietly as he shifted, and I squinted into the rays of dawn illuminating the room. Two more days until we were expected back at school to take our exams to *graduate, Jesus Christ, we’re finally about to graduate… Hopefully.*

Thankfully, this thought didn’t seem to wake him further as he twisted to face me, buried his face in my throat and exhaled contentedly. His breath warmed my chest beneath the covers, and I wondered if he could even really *breathe,* but found I wouldn’t have to wonder for long as he turned his head up to press a clumsy kiss to the underside of my jaw. And I smiled, again.

“Morning,” he whispered into my skin, and I curled him tighter into me as those lips drifted down my throat.

“Morning, husband,” I whispered back similarly. Seemed my introspection about our NEWTs had infected him, though, because I felt a little flicker of *anxiety* in his chest about returning to school. More than just the prospect of exams, but *debuting as husbands,* revealing our soulbond to our friends, and I understood. Was proper *excited,* but understood his nerves nonetheless.

Harry sighed into my chest, nestling further against me, and I tracked his thoughts as they latched onto mine.

*God, I’ll be so fucking humiliated if I still don’t graduate, after every string McGonagall pulled to have us repeat seventh year. I was feeling confident when we left for the hols, but what if some of that knowledge slipped through the cracks by now? Not to mention Hermione, god, I’ll never hear the end of it if I don’t pass…*

*Oi,* I interrupted. *Between the two of us, I’m sure you’ll be alright. Just ask me anything you don’t know, and I’ll do the same, yeah?*

His head lifted off my chest, confusion knitting his brow as he blinked at me.

*Oh yeah. Soulbond.* I couldn’t help but chuckle as he recalled that he also had free, *legal access* to *my* mind now. All the information *I’d* retained. And his frown eased into a guilty little smile.

*We’ll get through this together.*

And it was just then a subtle knock came at the door, indicating breakfast was served.

---

Narcissa had *post* for us once we met her in the dining hall. Our official, French notarised *marriage and soulbond licences.* Complete with seals and our signatures, the date, the *lot.* And I really should’ve been more prepared for how it made my throat tighten to see. She had them taken away by the staff to be properly framed and preserved, but to actually *see it in writing… God,* it was fucking with me.

Breakfast was potently distracting, though, and I think I’d found my new favourite type of food because there was *nothing* French I didn’t *salivate for.*

“So!” Narcissa clapped her hands together as I sipped my espresso, and Draco raised an imperious eyebrow as he lowered his spoon from his lips. “I was thinking a late-June, early-July wedding. Right between both of your birthdays, but in order to set everything up in half a year, you two should start thinking about the guest list *now.”* Grey eyes slid over to me as she postulated enthusiastically, and I just grinned back. “I’ll handle the invitations of course, and if you two don’t have a venue in mind, I know a *lovely* handful of locations that are just *darling* in the summertime. As for the *colour scheme,* I was thinking…”

Draco extended a hand, which I took as Narcissa’s gushing sort of faded into the background.

*I can tell her to shove off if you’d rather not,* he offered, but honestly, the idea of a big party for us, gathering all our friends and family in one place sounded like *heaven.* Especially if it gave me a reason to dress up all *sparkly* again.

*It’s alright. If anything, we should sign the name-changing documents, and that’s an occasion to celebrate, isn’t it?*

He smiled at me, stroked the back of my hand with his thumb and dipped his chin a fraction.

*Of course, Mister Potter. Anything you want.*

“Or perhaps we can have *strippers* for the guests, goody bags are so *dated* at this point, honestly.” Our joint attention snapped back to Narcissa, and she raised her eyebrows incredulously. “Oh, you *are* listening! Splendid.”

“Sorry, m-mum,” I chuckled nervously, and her eyes softened. “Still… Still an *adjustment* being in two heads at once.”

She tilted her head back, seemingly mollified as she glanced between us once.

“Of course, dear, I understand. You… Don’t really care about the details too much, I gather?”

Another glance back at Draco as I asked *is this a loaded question? Will I get chewed out no matter the answer?* So he answered for me.

“I’m sure we’ll have a good time regardless of… Of *floral arrangements,* mum. All we want is to celebrate the forging of a new *Potter* in the world, surrounded by all our loved ones.”

I squeezed his hand, and he squeezed mine back as Narcissa seemed to settle back into her chair.

“I *think* I can make that happen.”
