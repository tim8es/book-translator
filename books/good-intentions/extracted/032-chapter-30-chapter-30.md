## Chapter 30: Chapter 30

Chapter Notes

I paced in my room, Draco cross-legged on my bed as I went fucking *mad.* Despite his *generosity* earlier today, I felt like my bones were trying to crawl out of my *body* as I waited for nine P.M. And he sighed, *again.*

“Just fucking *go,*” he insisted as I worried a nail with my teeth. “She’ll fall asleep at this rate, if you keep faffing about.” As I turned back to him, hand dropping from my mouth, the pure fucking *exasperation* on his face made me square my shoulders.

“Right. I’m *going,* and I’m– I’m going.”

*Fucking Gryffindor,* I moaned internally as I swung around to haul my door open. Out into the hall of the boys’ dorms, down the steps to the girls', right up to the door marked *H.G.* And I froze.

*Just fucking go* echoed in my mind, and I swore under my breath as I knocked on her door. A moment of silence. *Nothing.* And after what was probably *way* too fucking long, I realised she *wasn’t in her room.*And swore again as I made my way back to the boys’ corridor.

*R.W.* I stood outside and *reluctantly* fucking listened. If they were *busy…*

Silence. I knocked.

A moment later, a very flushed and *out-of-sorts* Ron opened the door, and although it made me slightly sick, a swell of relief rushed through me that the doors were sound-proof.

“Harry,” he began, clearing his throat and running a hand through his hair.

“B-bad time?” I hoped, *prayed* to whatever fucking wizard god might hear me, that he’d turn me down, but to my dismay, he said:

“Nope! No, not a bad time. What–what’s up?”

I peered around him, because he’d *opened the door,* so clearly nothing especially *remarkable* was happening, but I was still loathe to see anything even *close* to Hermione-

“I-is Mione here?”

He swallowed, face losing some colour as he glanced behind himself. Couldn’t see what he looked at, but he seemed to brush up as he turned back to me.

“Y-yeah. Yeah, what, er…”

“C-can you two come with me to my room? I… I need to t-*talk* to you.”

*Christ,* I was *already* fucking this up, and I hadn’t even *begun…*

Ron stiffened, glanced behind him again, and turned back to me with a nod.

“Sure. Give us *one…* Second.”

And he shut the door in my face. *Desperately* trying not to think about what they’d been up to, I began to pace in the hallway. My own door was only *three away,* after all, and regardless of the silencing that might’ve been placed on all our dorms, the fact that *so few walls* had separated us was–

The door swung back open, Ron *fully* red and Hermione behind him, blushing similarly as she pulled her curls into a messy bun*.* And I swallowed.

“Brilliant. Just brilliant. Follow me.”

I turned down the hallway, too deep to back out *now,* and hesitated with my hand on the knob.

“J-just… *Fuck.*”

And I threw the door open, one arm crossing around my waist as I rested the other elbow against it, hand on my brow as I stalked into the room. Unable to even *look* at Draco, as Ron and Hermione hesitantly followed me in.

I sent Draco a final *I’ll-kill-you-if-you-say-anything-untoward* glare, and then finally turned back to round on them. And my door eased shut.

They both looked slightly… Well, *dishevelled,* but I decidedly put it from my mind as they glanced warily at Draco. Out of the corner of my eye, I saw him wave half-heartedly and a sigh huffed out of my lips.

*Now or never, Harry. Gryffindor.*

“S-so you know how I’ve been… Er…” Another glance at Draco. “*Out-of-sorts* since the Manor?”

Hermione’s face grew all of a sudden bright, and it threatened to derail my confession, because what the *fuck* did *that*mean?

“W-we… He, erm… Well… C-care to explain?” I sent another *desperate* glance over at Draco, and he returned it with a *you’ve-got-to-be-kidding-me* sort of exasperation. But sighed and returned his attention to Ron and Hermione nonetheless. And cleared his throat.

“So *basically,* I… Er…” A pause so long I tore my attention *back* to him, and saw a sort of resignation on his face. “Confessed my undying affection, confident we’d never see each other again.” Gnawing on my thumb, I turned back to watch Ron and Hermione’s reactions, and only had *more fucking questions,* because Ron’s face fell in tandem with Hermione pressing her lips together like she was trying not to smile. *There’s no fucking way.*

“Explained why I’d been such a *git* to him, because it took me way too fucking long to realise I was in *love* with him, and thanked him for saving the world. And ran.”

The silence that followed his words nearly ripped my *heart* out of my mouth, but then Hermione nudged Ron with an elbow.

“He really did run. I saw him leaving, and he was at a *dead sprint.*”

Ron shot me a *please-say-psyche* kind of look, and I bit down on my thumb harder.

“So yeah, we came back to school, and… Well… Harry?”

An *aggrieved* fucking sigh escaped me as I cut my gaze back over to him, and saw an implicative shrug that said *“this is all you”* in response. So I tore my thumb from my mouth, turned back to my *best friends,* and said:

“We’re dating.” A swallow forced itself down my throat as I watched their *dichotomous* fucking reactions, really and truly wondered what the *fuck,* but went on. “W-we got to talking once we came back, hung out loads, and… *Yeah.*”

Draco wasn’t really a *master wordsmith,* but I still shot him a glance to see if I’d done alright. And despite the smile he sent back at me, Ron’s groan tore my attention back to them.

“I’ll be… *Right* back.”

And he left. Before I had a moment to wonder what the fuck *that* was about, Hermione sort of wrapped her arms around herself and rocked back and forth on her feet.

“So… You two…”

“I’ve been in love with him for *nearly* a fucking decade, and he’s just now decided to acknowledge it,” Draco interrupted, clearly just *done* with this interaction as I glanced back at him. “Does that clear anything up for you?”

Hermione coughed into her hand, but I recognized it as a *snort,* and my mind sort of flipped backwards. *This is not what I expected from her.*

“Sure,” she allowed, lowering her hand and pressing her lips together. “Got it.”

Just then, Ron returned, looking put-out as *anything* as he rejoined her side. And he grimaced at me.

*“Malfoy? Really?”*

Just as I was formulating a sheepish response, Hermione unfolded her Ron-side arm and held her palm out. And he looked over at it, looked up at her with affectionate *loathing,* and withdrew a fistful of galleons from his pocket. She accepted them, curled her fingers around the coins, and pocketed them.

“Thank *you,*” she cooed pleasantly. And my mouth fucking *fell open.*

Draco’s snort registered in my ears, as did the following *genuine laughter,* but even that rapturous sound couldn’t tear my shock away from my *best friends* as Ron winced, and Hermione seemed so bloody *pleased* with herself.

“You… You’re fucking*joking,”* I supposed incredulously as Hermione turned a defensive sort of expression towards me. “Did you lot… *Bet*on me??”

“What? It’s not like we’re *hurting* you,” she insisted petulantly as she adjusted her shoulders. I couldn’t fucking shut my mouth, even though Draco was *losing it* on my bed.

“I expect this from Ron, but *you…”* I couldn’t *describe* the sheer fucking *betrayal* I felt. “*Mione!”*

She clapped a hand over her mouth as Draco cackled behind me, trying to *rein in her amusement* at her just *winning a bet* about him and I. I couldn’t fucking *believe* what I was seeing, and my mouth still wouldn’t close.

“Unbelievable,” I breathed as Ron shifted his weight uncomfortably. “Un-*fucking-believable.”*

“Sixth year,” Ron answered my unspoken question as he scratched the back of his neck, defeat clear in his eyes. “Woman’s *unmatched."*

Hermione broke into fucking *peals* of laughter as I gaped at the two of them. And in the back of my whirling mind, I heard the bed creak. Felt arms wrap around my waist, laughter bubbling in the chest pressed to my back.

“You *bet with Hermione?”* I accused Ron, and he lost a bit more colour.

“W-well… *Look at you!”*He gestured at Draco and I, wrapped around my waist and chuckling into my ear.

“My *best friends…* *Jesus Christ.”*

Hermione was nearly on the fucking *floor* as she swiped tears from her eyes, and Ron sent her an adoring glance. *God,* I was gonna fucking *kill them.*

“Must be nice to be so *known,*” Draco breathed into my ear. Reminding me how *awfully* this could’ve gone. I wasn’t *quite*done yet, though.

“You saw me tailing him, watching for any hint about what the Death Eaters were planning, and just *assumed* I was *into* him?”

Hermione regained a bit of composure to respond.

“Harry, you were *stalking him.* Like, literally every chance you *got.* And it’s not like I was *wrong!”*

Ron groaned again, passed a hand over his face. “Christ, I owe *George*money, too."

Draco laughed a bit more, shoulders shaking against my back as my mind raced to catch up. Hermione was now *shaking* Ron’s arm, exuberant triumph on her face.

“Y-you’re not *angry?**”* She sobered a bit and cast a hesitant glance back over at us. “I m-mean… I-I just realised I was gay, like… *Three days* ago, but… But *Ginny–*”

“Reckon she’ll be relieved, honestly,” Ron grunted, shoving his hands in his pockets. “She’s been going round the bend about ‘*what she could’ve done differently,’ ‘is this her fault.’* Nowt to do about a bloke being gay.”

A kiss behind my ear, but I barely registered it.

*This is not at all what I thought would happen.*

Harry was sort of frozen stiff in my arms, and I wanted to whirl him around and *shake him* because this was going… *Far* better than we’d both feared. There was a bit of horror on Weasley’s face seeing me being affectionate with Harry, but if they were gonna know about us, I wasn’t torturing myself anymore by keeping away from him. And Granger looked… Positively *vindicated.*

“God, I *knew* it! You thought I was *mad* for thinking Harry might be gay,” she accused with a series of flirtatious little pokes into the ginger.

“Alright, alright! Enough, you!” he chuckled back as he playfully swatted her hands away. “You already got your money, leave it!”

“Never. Never, *ever* letting this go.” She glanced back over at us, and concern flashed through her amusement. “Harry?”

I let him go, pocketing my hands just as his legs started to stumble forward and he threw his arms around the pair of them. Smiles all around, it seemed, as a sob shook Harry’s shoulders.

“I’m gonna*murder you,*” he choked from between them, but they only chuckled and returned his embrace. My heart tightened to see them embrace, but it didn’t hurt like it used to. I wasn’t jealous of them anymore, because I was *his boyfriend.* And reconciling with his best friends made him happy, so it made *me* happy. Harry’s shoulders shuddered on another sob.

Suddenly feeling strange for watching, I returned to my place atop his bed and folded my legs beneath me. Tried to give them a moment of privacy, because I was really only here for moral support. This was about Harry. But as I watched myself pick at my cuticles, I couldn’t help but overhear.

“Y-you can’t tell anyone. I mean… Not yet. I-I just–”

“*Course* not, Harry. We would never *out you,* this’ll be on your terms.”

“Th-thanks.” A sniff. “Sorry for losing you money.”

A gruff scoff. “Wouldn’t be the first time, honestly.” More shared laughter. It was… Strange, being in the same room with them like this. I’d seen their interactions from afar throughout the years, and it’d made me *sick* with jealousy, but now it just made me… *Grateful.*

“So… M-my *plan* was to… Well, if you two wouldn’t *mind…* If you could help, like… *Include* Draco in the group? F-figure it won’t come as so much of a *shock* once everyone learns how incredible he is.”

My eyes shot up from my fingers like elastic, spine going rigid as my cheeks warmed. Harry was still watching his friends’ reactions carefully, but I saw the deliberation in their eyes as they glanced at me.

*“Incredible,* is he?” Weasley asked, and Harry finally turned to look back at me. Eyes red and wet, and a small smile on his lips.

“Fucking *brilliant,* actually.”

My heart was trying to escape my ribs again as I took a reflexive swallow. And he jerked his chin towards his friends, as if to say *on with it.* So I cleared my throat and stood.

“I-I’m… *Sorry*‘bout… Well, everything.” Years ago, I’d have rather eaten my own fucking wand than admit I was ever, *ever* wrong about *anything.* But for Harry, for that *smile…* “Dunno why this idiot is with me, either.”

It was a cheap play, being self-deprecating when I was meant to be *mending* things, but I couldn’t help but wince as I recalled all the ways I’d–

“I fucking *hated* you two for being important to Harry, and was a right twat about it because I really didn’t even *realise* it was because I was jealous.” *Fuck,* I was fucking this up, *again.* “B-but I, obviously… I mean… *Fuck.*”

“W-what he’s *trying*to say,” Harry interrupted. “I think… Is that things are different now that things are… Different.”

Granger coughed into her hand again, very *poorly* hiding more laughter.

“Blimey, Harry, you’re *both* just…” Weasley shook his head, a bit… *Bemused?*

“Equally inarticulate,” she continued. “But I think we get it. Sorry about punching you.”

I smirked, tension easing slightly. “I deserved it.” And Granger grinned.

“Yeah, you did.”
