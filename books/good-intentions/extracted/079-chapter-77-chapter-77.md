“Oi!” Instantly, all conversation died as eyes turned up to him. “For all of us *pitifully* single, in the wake of such *horrendous* fucking love,” a few chuckles. “I propose we play a game!”

Necked a *hearty* gulp from the bottle as Ginny clicked her tongue.

*“Christ,”* Draco moaned next to me, and I turned a smirk over at him.

“Hypocrite,” I chimed casually as I offered another finger sandwich into my mouth. He jostled me with an elbow, nearly setting me off-balance, but I just laughed around my mouthful.

“Right, shove that couch back,” Seamus instructed, suddenly taking the helm of *Spin the Bottle.* George and Luna teamed up to clear a space before the fire, and then the Irishman was sat with his back facing the hearth, eager as anything. “Bring that bottle over here.”

Dean hopped off the couch, passed off the bottle, and…*Sat directly across from him.*

*“Fuckin’ ‘ell,”* I murmured around my bite, and Draco snorted. “Poor *Ginny.”*

He choked on a laugh, bent over and wiped his mouth before twisting a sly grin my way. One I raised an eyebrow at.

“You not gonna play?”

My lips twisted sideways into a smirk as I swallowed my mouthful.

“Are *you?”*

“Will if you will.”

I shoved off the wall, set my plate down and squared my shoulders. *Valiantly* denying my legs from hurting.

“Fine.”

The circle was assembling itself as we approached, bottle passing from person to person to empty it before we could use it, and a herald of cheers welcomed our arrival.

“Alright, alright,” I granted as I carefully eased myself down onto the floor. Draco tossed me a pillow just as I’d lowered myself to my knees, however, and I smiled gratefully. “Couple of newbies in the circle, is it?” he wondered as Dean accepted the bottle.
