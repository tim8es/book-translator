## Chapter 113: Chapter 113

The more I painted, the less I hoped Draco would be able to recognise what it was.
*Surely the long tentacles give it away?*
My teeth were worrying a
*hole*
in my lip when I finally set my brush down. I’d spent
*hours*
at it, though, and my stomach was pulling me away towards lunch. Towards
*Draco.*
So I cleaned up as quickly as I could and gingerly set my wet canvass on a drying rack Luna had shown me how to use.

Nearly
*skipping*
down to the Great Hall, I grinned to myself to imagine how he’d react. I expected shock, at the very least, and I wondered what he’d picked out for
*me.*
I’d never been so singularly excited for an exchange of gifts.

He wasn’t at the Gryffindor table yet. I was slightly disappointed, but figured there was likely a pre-holiday rush, so the streets were probably crowded. Just as I was sitting down across from Ron and Hermione, though, I started worrying. Were people being cruel to him in my absence? How would he fare in public without me? I didn’t really taste the food I ate, but when Hermione asked,

“Where’s Draco?” it lifted me from my reverie.

“Out shopping again, I s’pose,” was my half-hearted answer. Even when I eventually drifted away from the table, though, a sour sort of anxiety began welling in my stomach.

*Would he be upset if I interrupted him? Is there some big, elaborate plan that he’s hoping to keep a secret from me? Fuck, but he’s been gone for*
hours.

I visited our room first, to check the map. Maybe he’d returned and was assembling his gift somewhere in the castle? As I spread it out on the desk, though, I came up empty. So it was with a short sigh that I plucked my coat out of the wardrobe and made my way to Hogsmeade.

*I’ll just get eyes on him and see how he’s doing. Won’t let him see me and be back in the castle before he can catch me following him. Bloody hell, here I am*
stalking
*him again.*

A wry smile tugged at my lips as I strolled out into the cold sunshine, but there was a permanent frown on my brow.

*Bastard, making me worry about him. He’s earned another bout of tickling for this.*

I ended up
*running*
the rest of the way to Hogsmeade. Just couldn’t walk normally, and eventually gave into the summons I felt to his side. Just as I’d predicted, the streets were
*packed.*
A quick scan of the main strip, but I found no blond head peeking above the rest. A few people tried to talk to me, but I was on a
*mission.*

*He wouldn’t leave Hogsmeade to go elsewhere, would he…?*

I checked every. Single. Store. No Draco. That’s when I started shaking. Trembling so bad I stumbled down a random alley to escape the crowd, lean against the wall and
*breathe.*

Something was wrong. Something was
*definitely, horribly*
wrong. I fumbled my wand out of my pocket to summon my Patronus, unsure what else to
*do,*
and said:

“Find Draco.”

My stag tilted its head at me curiously, paced a few steps further down the alley, and I followed it. Still using the wall to lead me step by heaving step as my head spun lighter and lighter, but when I saw it dip its nose to the ground and look back at me, I fell to my knees beside it.

There was…
*Blood*
on the cobblestone. Not a trail of it, just a singular, obvious dollop. Everything but that spot of red went dark in my vision as I came to the only conclusion I could.

He’d been
*taken.*
My stag lifted its glowing head to the sky, and I watched it sniff the air. It was only then I realised it’d started to get dark, but only distantly as my Patronus’s nose touched something… Odd. Like a crack in the air, a rip in space that only became apparent with its luminous snout investigating it.

I reached for it, fingers still trembling as I extended a few into the strange fracture. And when I recognised what I was feeling, my fingers stilled. Everything went still, quiet and calm. Steady legs lifted me back to my feet, and I turned back to my waiting stag.

“Go tell McGonagall that Draco’s been kidnapped. I’m not waiting for help, but lead them to me when they come.”

Just like that, it disappeared out into the encroaching dusk, and I faced the Apparation signature head-on. My eyes shut to hone my other senses as I felt for the edge of it. The little pull that I could follow wherever they’d gone. Wherever they’d
*taken him.*

*You’ve got to mean it, Harry,*
an insidious voice echoed in my head. Whether any part of Voldemort still lingered in me, I didn’t care. He’d only ever killed for
*power,*
had never known love like I did. That, after all, had been the ultimate difference between us that had given me an edge over him.

I took a deep, settling breath, and let myself slip through space with
*murder*
in my heart.

---

*Slow death it is,*
I mused silently as I held my right eye tightly shut against the steady trickle of blood that fountained down my face. I’d seen enough war at this point to know head wounds were fatal if left untreated, but as Theo’s ragged breaths echoed through the room, metal clanged to the floor and there were footsteps again.

*S’pose it’d be too much to expect a quick and easy death. They blame me for Harry’s victory.*

*How fucking ironic.*

“Feel better, Nott?” A short scuffle, followed by a scoff, and I peered my one good eye back up at the men before me. Theo was shoving off the first man’s hand from his shoulder and stalking back to the back of the room, but then the latter turned back to
*me.*
“Looking rather
*pitiful,*
Malfoy. Care to help me speed things along?”

He dragged a stool closer to himself by a foot, screeching against the floor before he sat facing me. And I managed to roll my head back to squint at him.

“This oughta be good,” I said, barely above a whisper. “You… You wanna know Harry’s
*weaknesses?*
A way to kill him when even the
*Dark Lord failed?”*

A weak huff of laughter slipped through my lips as the blood from my face dripped down my chest. And the nameless man before me sighed.

“You’d never do that, would you? Lovesick fucking
*idiot,”*
he groused, arms folding across his chest. “I could pull your
*teeth*
out, one by one until you gave me something. Carve you up nice and pretty until you
*scream.*
I find Muggle means of torture
*far*
more effective than any Unforgiveable, don’t you?”

*Absolutely mediaeval. Just like their ideologies.*

I spat more blood that had leaked between my softly wheezing lips.

“Do what you want. There’s no answer to your question. Harry doesn’t
*have weaknesses.*
I will give you something, though.” Groaning, I pushed off the back of my chair to lean my head forward, and the man mirrored me, bracing his elbows on his knees to frown at me. “In the months since the war ended, he’s gotten
*lethally*
powerful. Raw magic like you wouldn’t
*believe,*
even if I
*told*
you.” A smug grin pulled at my lips, and my cheek
*howled*
in protest. “And you’ve
*royally*
fucked up by doing this. You’re all gonna die
*horrible*
fucking deaths in this room.”

I was growing colder and colder by the second as my life dripped onto the disgusting floor, but if I was gonna die in such
*vile*
company, I was going down with scathing,
*biting*
sarcasm. Because Malfoys
*never*
begged.
*Well, not for just anyone,*
I amended internally.

The frown on the man’s brow twisted into sadistic glee, like he’d been
*hoping*
I’d put up a fight. But my vision was starting to blur, so even holding my smirk to my face was a task.

“He’ll never find us. And
*you’re*
as good as
*dead already.”*

A loud
*BANG*
outside, and everyone but me and my interrogator flinched to look towards the door.

“He’s already found you. And you’re
*already dead.”*

*Lame. Such lame last words,*
I grieved internally as darkness swelled up to swallow me again.
