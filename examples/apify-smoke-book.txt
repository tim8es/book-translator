The Lantern Window

Chapter One

Mara arrived before sunrise, when the station lamps still seemed brighter than the sky. She carried one small suitcase and a folded letter she had read so many times that the paper had softened at the creases.

The house stood at the end of a narrow street. Its upper window held a weak amber light, although the rooms below were dark. Mara stopped at the gate and wondered whether someone had been waiting for her, or whether the old lamp had simply been left burning through the night.

She did not knock immediately.

For a minute she listened to the wind moving through the bare branches. Then she put the letter back into her coat pocket and opened the gate.

Chapter Two

Inside, the hall smelled of dust, cold wood, and something faintly sweet. A clock ticked somewhere beyond the stairs, slow enough that Mara noticed the silence between each sound.

On the table lay a brass key and a note.

"You will understand the room before you understand the letter."

Mara read the sentence twice. She knew the handwriting, but that only made the message less comforting.

Above her, the amber light went out.

She looked toward the staircase and, despite herself, smiled.

"Fine," she said quietly. "One mystery at a time."
